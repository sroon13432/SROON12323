What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 23 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD® A8 3870 3,6 Ghz or Intel® Core ™ i3 2100 3.1Ghz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 660 run DARK SOULS™ II: Scholar of the First Sin at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The GTX 660 is compared against the published recommended GTX 750 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does DARK SOULS™ II: Scholar of the First Sin need?

DARK SOULS™ II: Scholar of the First Sin lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for DARK SOULS™ II: Scholar of the First Sin at 60 FPS?
