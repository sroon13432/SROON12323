Is 8 GB of RAM enough for Content Warning with a Steam Deck?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Content Warning, Steam Deck

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. Content Warning asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with Steam Deck the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 6 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5 @ 2.5 GHz or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

How much RAM does Content Warning need?

Content Warning lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Content Warning at 60 FPS?
