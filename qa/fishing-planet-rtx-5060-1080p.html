Can an RTX 5060 run Fishing Planet at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Fishing Planet, RTX 5060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RTX 5060 should land around 55-65 FPS in Fishing Planet, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 14 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3-10100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3100. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 5060 run Fishing Planet at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The RTX 5060 is compared against the published recommended RTX 4060 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Fishing Planet need?

Fishing Planet lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Fishing Planet at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 4060 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1050 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 470 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Fishing Planet need?

The store page lists Intel Core i3-10100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3100 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-12700 or AMD Ryzen 7 7700 as the recommended one.
