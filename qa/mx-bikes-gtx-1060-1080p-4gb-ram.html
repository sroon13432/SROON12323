What limits you first

  • Memory is not the limit. 4 GB covers the 4 GB this game asks for, so any stutter you see will come from the graphics card, a hard drive, or the CPU rather than from RAM.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 2 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 2 Ghz CPU. VR: Intel Core i5-4590 (AMD FX 8350) or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
