How many FPS can a GTX 970 get in Titanfall® 2 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Titanfall® 2, GTX 970

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in Titanfall® 2 once you step one preset down from what the GTX 970 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 970 sits a little under Titanfall® 2's recommended RX 480 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 660, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 45 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3-6300t or equivalent [4 or more hardware threads]. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 970 run Titanfall® 2 at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The GTX 970 is compared against the published recommended RX 480 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Titanfall® 2 need?

Titanfall® 2 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Titanfall® 2 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 480 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 230 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Titanfall® 2 need?

The store page lists Intel Core i3-6300t or equivalent [4 or more hardware threads] as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-6600 or equivalent as the recommended one.
