Riven on an RTX 4060 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Riven, RTX 4060

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-65 FPS at 1080p in Riven once you step one preset down from what the RTX 4060 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 4060 sits a little under Riven's recommended GTX 1080 Ti but comfortably above its minimum GTX 1070, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 30 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 7 2700X Eight-Core Processor (16 CPUs), ~3.7GHz; Intel i5 7000 series. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Riven at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-65 FPS. The RTX 4060 is compared against the published recommended GTX 1080 Ti (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Riven need?

Riven lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Riven at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1080 Ti as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1070 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 500 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Riven need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 7 2700X Eight-Core Processor (16 CPUs), ~3.7GHz; Intel i5 7000 series as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 7 3800X 8-Core Processor (16 CPUs), ~3.9GHz; Intel i5 11000 series as the recommended one.
