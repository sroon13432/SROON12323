Will a Steam Deck hold 60 FPS in Apex Legends™ at 800p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Apex Legends™, Steam Deck

Short answer: yes. At 800p on the High preset the Steam Deck should land around 95-115 FPS in Apex Legends™, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Steam Deck is well past Apex Legends™'s recommended R9 290: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the Steam Deck sits roughly 67% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 75 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD FX 4350 or Equivalent, Intel Core i3 6300 or Equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Steam Deck run Apex Legends™ at 800p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 800p on the High preset is 95-115 FPS. The Steam Deck is compared against the published recommended R9 290 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Apex Legends™ need?

Apex Legends™ lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Apex Legends™ at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists R9 290 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and AMD Radeon HD 7790 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 200 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Apex Legends™ need?

The store page lists AMD FX 4350 or Equivalent, Intel Core i3 6300 or Equivalent as the minimum processor and Ryzen 5 CPU or Equivalent as the recommended one.
