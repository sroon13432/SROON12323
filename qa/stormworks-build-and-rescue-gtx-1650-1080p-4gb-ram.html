Do I need more than 4 GB RAM to play Stormworks: Build and Rescue on a GTX 1650?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Stormworks: Build and Rescue, GTX 1650

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Stormworks: Build and Rescue asks for 4 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 1650 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 2 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-4440 / AMD Ryzen 3 1200. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

How much RAM does Stormworks: Build and Rescue need?

Stormworks: Build and Rescue lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 4 GB.

What GPU do I need for Stormworks: Build and Rescue at 60 FPS?
