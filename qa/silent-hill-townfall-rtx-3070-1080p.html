Is an RTX 3070 enough for SILENT HILL: Townfall at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — SILENT HILL: Townfall, RTX 3070

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in SILENT HILL: Townfall once you step one preset down from what the RTX 3070 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 3070 sits a little under SILENT HILL: Townfall's recommended RX 7800 XT but comfortably above its minimum RX 6600, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 75 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 3600 / Intel i7-8700K. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3070 run SILENT HILL: Townfall at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The RTX 3070 is compared against the published recommended RX 7800 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does SILENT HILL: Townfall need?

SILENT HILL: Townfall lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for SILENT HILL: Townfall at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 7800 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 6600 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 700 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does SILENT HILL: Townfall need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 3600 / Intel i7-8700K as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 7 5700X / Intel i7-9700K as the recommended one.
