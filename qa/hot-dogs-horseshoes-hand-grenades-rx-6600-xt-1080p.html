How many FPS can an RX 6600 XT get in Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades, RX 6600 XT

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 6600 XT should land around 55-70 FPS in Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 20 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i5-4590, AMD FX 8350 equivalent or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 6600 XT run Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The RX 6600 XT is compared against the published recommended GTX 1080 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades need?

Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1080 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and R9 290 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 420 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades need?

The store page lists Intel i5-4590, AMD FX 8350 equivalent or better as the minimum processor and Intel i5-6600 or better as the recommended one.
