How many FPS can an RX 580 get in Buckshot Roulette at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Buckshot Roulette, RX 580

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 580 should land around 235-260 FPS in Buckshot Roulette, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe RX 580 is well past Buckshot Roulette's recommended Any DirectX-capable GPU: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the RX 580 sits roughly 1025% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 500 MB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
