Can a GTX 1650 Super run BIGFOOT at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — BIGFOOT, GTX 1650 Super

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1650 Super should land around 60-75 FPS in BIGFOOT, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 10 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i3 8130U(2Cores, 2.3GHz) or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1650 Super run BIGFOOT at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 60-75 FPS. The GTX 1650 Super is compared against the published recommended GTX 980 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does BIGFOOT need?

BIGFOOT lists 2 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 4 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for BIGFOOT at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 980 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GT 730 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 240 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does BIGFOOT need?

The store page lists Intel i3 8130U(2Cores, 2.3GHz) or equivalent as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7 (64bit) or equivalent as the recommended one.
