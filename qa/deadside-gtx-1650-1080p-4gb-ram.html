Is 4 GB of RAM enough for Deadside with a GTX 1650?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Deadside, GTX 1650

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Deadside asks for 4 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 1650 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 25 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Core i3 / AMD FX. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
