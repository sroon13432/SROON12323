Is an RTX 5070 enough for ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE at 1440p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 27 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE, RTX 5070

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 35-40 FPS at 1440p in ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE once you step one preset down from what the RTX 5070 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 5070 is below ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE's recommended RTX 3070 while still clearing the minimum RTX 2060, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 150 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600X. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
  5. If 1440p at the recommended preset is short of 60 FPS, render at 1080p and let the monitor scale - the frame time gain is larger than the visual loss at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 5070 run ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE at 1440p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1440p on the High preset is 35-40 FPS. The RTX 5070 is compared against the published recommended RTX 3070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE need?

ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RTX 2060 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 620 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE need?

The store page lists Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600X as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-12700K / AMD Ryzen 5 5600X as the recommended one.
