Is 4 GB of RAM enough for Inscryption with a GTX 660?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Inscryption, GTX 660

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Inscryption asks for 4 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 660 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

RAM at minimum4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. The GTX 660 is below Inscryption's recommended RX 460 while still clearing the minimum GTX 550 Ti, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 3 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-760 (4 * 2800); AMD Athlon II X4 645 AM3 (4 * 3100). If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Inscryption need?

Inscryption lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 4 GB.

What GPU do I need for Inscryption at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 460 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 550 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 100 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Inscryption need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-760 (4 * 2800); AMD Athlon II X4 645 AM3 (4 * 3100) as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-3470 (4 * 3200); AMD FX-4350 (4 * 4200) as the recommended one.
