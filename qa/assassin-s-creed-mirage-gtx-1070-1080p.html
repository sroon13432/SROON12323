How many FPS can a GTX 1070 get in Assassin's Creed Mirage at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Assassin's Creed Mirage, GTX 1070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1070 should land around 55-70 FPS in Assassin's Creed Mirage, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1070 lands just above Assassin's Creed Mirage's recommended GTX 1660 Ti - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, INTEL® Core™ i7-4790K (INTEL® Core™ i5-8400 for Intel® ARC™ with Rebar) / Ryzen™ 5 1600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1070 run Assassin's Creed Mirage at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1070 is compared against the published recommended GTX 1660 Ti (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Assassin's Creed Mirage need?

Assassin's Creed Mirage lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Assassin's Creed Mirage at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1660 Ti as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and ARC A380 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 350 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Assassin's Creed Mirage need?

The store page lists INTEL® Core™ i7-4790K (INTEL® Core™ i5-8400 for Intel® ARC™ with Rebar) / Ryzen™ 5 1600 as the minimum processor and Intel® Core™ i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen™ 5 3600 as the recommended one.
