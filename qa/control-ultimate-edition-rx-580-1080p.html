Will an RX 580 hold 60 FPS in CONTROL Ultimate Edition at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — CONTROL Ultimate Edition, RX 580

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 580 should land around 55-65 FPS in CONTROL Ultimate Edition, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 42 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-4690 / AMD FX 4350. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
