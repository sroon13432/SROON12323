How many FPS can a GTX 1660 Super get in PEAK at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — PEAK, GTX 1660 Super

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in PEAK once you step one preset down from what the GTX 1660 Super is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 1660 Super is below PEAK's recommended RTX 2060 while still clearing the minimum GTX 1060, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 16 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5 @ 2.5 GHz or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 Super run PEAK at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The GTX 1660 Super is compared against the published recommended RTX 2060 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does PEAK need?

PEAK lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for PEAK at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2060 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1060 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 430 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does PEAK need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5 @ 2.5 GHz or equivalent as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5 @ 3.0 GHz or AMD Ryzen 5 or equivalent as the recommended one.
