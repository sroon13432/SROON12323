Can a UHD 620 run The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion® Game of the Year Edition (2009) at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion® Game of the Year Edition (2009), UHD 620

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the UHD 620 should land around 135-170 FPS in The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion® Game of the Year Edition (2009), and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 4.6 GB Sound: DirectX 8.1 compatible; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 2 Ghz Intel Pentium 4 or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
