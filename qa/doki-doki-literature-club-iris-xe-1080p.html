Is an Iris Xe enough for Doki Doki Literature Club! at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Doki Doki Literature Club!, Iris Xe

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Iris Xe should land around 80-100 FPS in Doki Doki Literature Club!, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 350 MB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 1.8GHz Dual-Core CPU. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Can the Iris Xe run Doki Doki Literature Club! at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 80-100 FPS. The Iris Xe is compared against the published recommended Integrated Graphics (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Doki Doki Literature Club! need?

What GPU do I need for Doki Doki Literature Club! at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists Integrated Graphics as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Integrated Graphics as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 60 meet the recommended figure.
