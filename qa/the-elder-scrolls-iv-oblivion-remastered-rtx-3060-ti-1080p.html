Is an RTX 3060 Ti enough for The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered, RTX 3060 Ti

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered once you step one preset down from what the RTX 3060 Ti is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 3060 Ti sits a little under The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered's recommended RTX 2080 but comfortably above its minimum RX 5700, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 125 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 2600X, Intel Core i7-6800K. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3060 Ti run The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The RTX 3060 Ti is compared against the published recommended RTX 2080 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered need?

The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2080 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 5700 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 600 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 2600X, Intel Core i7-6800K as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 5 3600X, Intel Core i5-10600K as the recommended one.
