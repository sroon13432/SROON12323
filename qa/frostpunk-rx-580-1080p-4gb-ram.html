Is 4 GB of RAM enough for Frostpunk with an RX 580?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Frostpunk, RX 580

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Frostpunk asks for 4 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with RX 580 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 8 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 3.2 GHz Dual Core Processor. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Frostpunk need?

Frostpunk lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 4 GB.

What GPU do I need for Frostpunk at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 580 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 225 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Frostpunk need?

The store page lists 3.2 GHz Dual Core Processor as the minimum processor and 3.2 GHz Quad Core Processor as the recommended one.
