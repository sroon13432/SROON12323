Is a GTX 960 enough for Lies of P at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Lies of P, GTX 960

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 40-50 FPS at 1080p in Lies of P once you step one preset down from what the GTX 960 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 960 is below Lies of P's recommended RX 6500 XT while still clearing the minimum RX 560, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 50 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 3 1200／Intel Core i3-6300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 960 run Lies of P at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 40-50 FPS. The GTX 960 is compared against the published recommended RX 6500 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Lies of P need?

Lies of P lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Lies of P at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 6500 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 560 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 190 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Lies of P need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 3 1200／Intel Core i3-6300 as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 3 1200／Intel Core i3-6300 as the recommended one.
