Is an Iris Xe enough for Lords of the Fallen at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Lords of the Fallen, Iris Xe

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Iris Xe should land around 80-100 FPS in Lords of the Fallen, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Iris Xe clears Lords of the Fallen's recommended RX 6700 by a comfortable margin, so the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target is reachable without cutting settings.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 45 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, intel i5 8400 | AMD Ryzen 5 2600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Iris Xe run Lords of the Fallen at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 80-100 FPS. The Iris Xe is compared against the published recommended RX 6700 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Lords of the Fallen need?

Lords of the Fallen lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Lords of the Fallen at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 6700 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 590 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 60.0 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Lords of the Fallen need?

The store page lists intel i5 8400 | AMD Ryzen 5 2600 as the minimum processor and intel i7 8700 | AMD Ryzen 5 3600 as the recommended one.
