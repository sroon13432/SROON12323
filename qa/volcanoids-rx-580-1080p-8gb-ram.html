Is 8 GB of RAM enough for Volcanoids with an RX 580?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 23 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Volcanoids, RX 580

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. Volcanoids asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with RX 580 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 6 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2500K / AMD FX-6300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Volcanoids need?

Volcanoids lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Volcanoids at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 580 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 470 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 225 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Volcanoids need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-2500K / AMD FX-6300 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-7700 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600 as the recommended one.
