Can an Iris Xe run Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5), Iris Xe

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the Very High preset the Iris Xe should land around 70-85 FPS in Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5), and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 110 GB; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core 2 Quad CPU Q6600 @ 2.40GHz / AMD Phenom 9850. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Use FXAA or MSAA 2x instead of MSAA 8x; 8x MSAA reduces performance by over 40% with little visible gain at 1080p+.
  2. Advanced Graphics settings (Extended Distance Scaling) should remain at default unless running an RTX 3080 or better.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Iris Xe run Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the Very High preset is 70-85 FPS. The Iris Xe is compared against the published recommended GTX 660 (1080p @ 60 FPS (Very High Settings)).

How much RAM does Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) need?

Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 660 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (Very High Settings), and 9800 GT as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 70 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) need?

The store page lists Intel Core 2 Quad CPU Q6600 @ 2.40GHz / AMD Phenom 9850 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5 3470 @ 3.2GHz / AMD FX-8350 @ 4GHz as the recommended one.
