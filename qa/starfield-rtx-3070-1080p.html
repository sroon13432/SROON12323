Will an RTX 3070 hold 60 FPS in Starfield at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Starfield, RTX 3070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RTX 3070 should land around 55-70 FPS in Starfield, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 125 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 2600X, Intel Core i7-6800K. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3070 run Starfield at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The RTX 3070 is compared against the published recommended RTX 2080 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Starfield need?

Starfield lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Starfield at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2080 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 5700 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 600 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Starfield need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 2600X, Intel Core i7-6800K as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 5 3600X, Intel i5-10600K as the recommended one.
