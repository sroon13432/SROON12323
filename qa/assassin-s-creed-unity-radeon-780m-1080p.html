Is a Radeon 780M enough for Assassin's Creed® Unity at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Assassin's Creed® Unity, Radeon 780M

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Radeon 780M should land around 55-65 FPS in Assassin's Creed® Unity, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Radeon 780M lands just above Assassin's Creed® Unity's recommended GTX 780 - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 50 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2500K @ 3.3 GHz or AMD FX-8350 @ 4.0 GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Radeon 780M run Assassin's Creed® Unity at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The Radeon 780M is compared against the published recommended GTX 780 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Assassin's Creed® Unity need?

Assassin's Creed® Unity lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Assassin's Creed® Unity at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 780 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 680 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 130 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Assassin's Creed® Unity need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-2500K @ 3.3 GHz or AMD FX-8350 @ 4.0 GHz as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-3770 @ 3.4 GHz or AMD FX-8350 @ 4.0 GHz or better as the recommended one.
