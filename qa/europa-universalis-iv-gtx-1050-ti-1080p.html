Can a GTX 1050 Ti run Europa Universalis IV at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Europa Universalis IV, GTX 1050 Ti

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1050 Ti should land around 185-225 FPS in Europa Universalis IV, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1050 Ti is well past Europa Universalis IV's recommended GTX 560 Ti: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the GTX 1050 Ti sits roughly 321% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 6 GB HD space Sound: Direct X-compatible soundcard; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core™ i3-2105 / AMD® FX 4300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1050 Ti run Europa Universalis IV at 1080p?
