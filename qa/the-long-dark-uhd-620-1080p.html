Will a UHD 620 hold 60 FPS in The Long Dark at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — The Long Dark, UHD 620

Short answer: not at that target. At 1080p the UHD 620 comes in around 30-35 FPS on the High preset, so the realistic route is 1080p, or 1080p with shadows, textures and upscaling pulled back well below what The Long Dark recommends.

Below targetThe UHD 620 falls short of The Long Dark's recommended GTX 555 and is around the minimum 512 MB VRAM card, so expect to run well below the recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 7 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Dual-Core Intel i5 CPU @ 2GHz+. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the UHD 620 run The Long Dark at 1080p?

Not at the recommended preset. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 30-35 FPS. The UHD 620 is compared against the published recommended GTX 555 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does The Long Dark need?

The Long Dark lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for The Long Dark at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 555 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 512 MB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 65.5 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does The Long Dark need?

The store page lists Dual-Core Intel i5 CPU @ 2GHz+ as the minimum processor and Intel i7 CPU @ 2.6GHz or higher as the recommended one.
