Is a GTX 1060 enough for Solasta: Crown of the Magister at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Solasta: Crown of the Magister, GTX 1060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1060 should land around 55-70 FPS in Solasta: Crown of the Magister, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD FX 4300 / Intel Core i5-3570K. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1060 run Solasta: Crown of the Magister at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1060 is compared against the published recommended RX 590 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Solasta: Crown of the Magister need?

Solasta: Crown of the Magister lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Solasta: Crown of the Magister at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 590 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 950 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 240 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Solasta: Crown of the Magister need?

The store page lists AMD FX 4300 / Intel Core i5-3570K as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 3 1300X / Intel Core i5-7400 as the recommended one.
