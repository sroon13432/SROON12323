Do I need more than 8 GB RAM to play Forza Horizon 5 on a GTX 1050 Ti?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Forza Horizon 5, GTX 1050 Ti

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. Forza Horizon 5 asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 1050 Ti the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 110 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i5-4460 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Forza Horizon 5 need?

Forza Horizon 5 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Forza Horizon 5 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 590 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and ARC A380 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 240 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Forza Horizon 5 need?

The store page lists Intel i5-4460 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 as the minimum processor and Intel i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 1500X as the recommended one.
