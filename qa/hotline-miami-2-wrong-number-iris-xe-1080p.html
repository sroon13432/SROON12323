Is an Iris Xe enough for Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number, Iris Xe

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Iris Xe should land around 185-230 FPS in Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 600 MB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 2.4 GHz Intel Core 2 Duo or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Iris Xe run Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 185-230 FPS. The Iris Xe is compared against the published recommended 512 MB VRAM card (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number need?

Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number lists 1 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 2 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 512 MB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 256 MB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 22 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number need?

The store page lists 2.4 GHz Intel Core 2 Duo or better as the minimum processor and 2.8 GHz Intel Core 2 Duo or better as the recommended one.
