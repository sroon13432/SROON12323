Is 16 GB of RAM enough for Squad 44 with an RTX 3070?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Squad 44, RTX 3070

Short answer: 16 GB meets the 16 GB minimum but falls short of the 32 GB recommended for this game. Squad 44 asks for 16 GB at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings, and with RTX 3070 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

RAM at minimum16 GB meets the 16 GB minimum but falls short of the 32 GB recommended for this game. The RTX 3070 falls short of Squad 44's recommended RTX 3070 and is around the minimum RX 6600, so expect to run well below the recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 16 GB against a 32 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 100 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i3 9th gen or AMD Ryzen 3 3300X. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
  5. If 1440p at the recommended preset is short of 60 FPS, render at 1080p and let the monitor scale - the frame time gain is larger than the visual loss at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Squad 44 need?

Squad 44 lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 16 GB.

What GPU do I need for Squad 44 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 6600 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 620 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Squad 44 need?

The store page lists Intel i3 9th gen or AMD Ryzen 3 3300X as the minimum processor and Intel i5 11th gen or AMD Ryzen 5 7600 as the recommended one.
