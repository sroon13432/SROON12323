Will a GTX 970 hold 60 FPS in Borderlands 3 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Borderlands 3, GTX 970

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in Borderlands 3 once you step one preset down from what the GTX 970 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 970 sits a little under Borderlands 3's recommended RX 590 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 680, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 75 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD FX-8350 or Intel i5-3570. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 970 run Borderlands 3 at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The GTX 970 is compared against the published recommended RX 590 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Borderlands 3 need?

Borderlands 3 lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Borderlands 3 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 590 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 680 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 240 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Borderlands 3 need?

The store page lists AMD FX-8350 or Intel i5-3570 as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen™ 5 2600 (Intel i7-4770) as the recommended one.
