Will a GTX 1650 hold 60 FPS in EA SPORTS FC™ 26 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — EA SPORTS FC™ 26, GTX 1650

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 40-50 FPS at 1080p in EA SPORTS FC™ 26 once you step one preset down from what the GTX 1650 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 1650 is below EA SPORTS FC™ 26's recommended GTX 1660 while still clearing the minimum GTX 1050 Ti, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 100 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 1600 or Intel Core i5 6600k. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1650 run EA SPORTS FC™ 26 at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 40-50 FPS. The GTX 1650 is compared against the published recommended GTX 1660 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does EA SPORTS FC™ 26 need?

EA SPORTS FC™ 26 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 12 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for EA SPORTS FC™ 26 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1660 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1050 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 320 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does EA SPORTS FC™ 26 need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 1600 or Intel Core i5 6600k as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 7 2700X or Intel Core i7 6700 as the recommended one.
