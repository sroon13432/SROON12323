How many FPS can an RTX 4060 get in Palworld at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Palworld, RTX 4060

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in Palworld once you step one preset down from what the RTX 4060 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 4060 sits a little under Palworld's recommended RTX 2070 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 1050, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • VRAM fits exactly. Palworld is written around 8 GB of video memory and that is what the RTX 4060 carries, so texture settings at the recommended preset are fine but the next preset up is not.
  • Install it on an SSD. The store page lists 40 GB SSD required. On a mechanical drive this game streams slowly enough that the hitching looks like a GPU problem when it is not.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-3570K 3.4 GHz / 4 Core. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Lowering 'View Distance' from Epic to High gives an instant 12-15 FPS boost near large pal bases.
  2. Turn off 'Motion Blur' and set 'Ride Camera Distance' to maximum for improved spatial awareness and smoother visuals.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Palworld at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The RTX 4060 is compared against the published recommended RTX 2070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (Medium / High Settings)).

How much RAM does Palworld need?

Palworld lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Palworld at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (Medium / High Settings), and GTX 1050 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 510 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Palworld need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-3570K 3.4 GHz / 4 Core as the minimum processor and Intel Core i9-9900K 3.6 GHz / 8 Core as the recommended one.
