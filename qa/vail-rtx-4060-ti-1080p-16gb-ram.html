VAIL with 16 GB RAM and an RTX 4060 Ti: playable or stuttering?

Answer from the CanMySpec database — VAIL, RTX 4060 Ti

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 16 GB against a 32 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Core i5-7600K / Ryzen 5 3600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does VAIL need?

VAIL lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 16 GB.

What GPU do I need for VAIL at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 6700 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 5600 XT as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 540 meet the recommended figure.

The store page lists Core i5-7600K / Ryzen 5 3600 as the minimum processor and Core i7-12700K / Ryzen 7 5800X as the recommended one.
