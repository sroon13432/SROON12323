Will a Steam Deck hold 60 FPS in GRIS at 800p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — GRIS, Steam Deck

Short answer: yes. At 800p on the High preset the Steam Deck should land around 235-260 FPS in GRIS, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 4 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core2 Duo E6750 (2 * 2660) or equivalent / AMD Athlon 64 X2 Dual Core 5000+ (2 * 2600) or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
