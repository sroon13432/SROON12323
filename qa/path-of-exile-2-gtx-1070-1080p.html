Path of Exile 2 on a GTX 1070 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Path of Exile 2, GTX 1070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1070 should land around 55-70 FPS in Path of Exile 2, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 100 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core™ i7-7700 or AMD™ Ryzen 5 2500x. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1070 run Path of Exile 2 at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1070 is compared against the published recommended RX 5600 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Path of Exile 2 need?

Path of Exile 2 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Path of Exile 2 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 5600 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 960 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 350 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Path of Exile 2 need?

The store page lists Intel® Core™ i7-7700 or AMD™ Ryzen 5 2500x as the minimum processor and Intel® Core™ i5-10500 or AMD™ Ryzen 5 3700X as the recommended one.
