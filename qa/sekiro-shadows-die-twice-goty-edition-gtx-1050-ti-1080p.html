Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition on a GTX 1050 Ti at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Answer from the CanMySpec database — Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition, GTX 1050 Ti

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-50 FPS at 1080p in Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition once you step one preset down from what the GTX 1050 Ti is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 1050 Ti is below Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition's recommended GTX 970 while still clearing the minimum GTX 760, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 25 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3-2100 | AMD FX-6300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1050 Ti run Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-50 FPS. The GTX 1050 Ti is compared against the published recommended GTX 970 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition need?

Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 970 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 760 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 210 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Sekiro™: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition need?
