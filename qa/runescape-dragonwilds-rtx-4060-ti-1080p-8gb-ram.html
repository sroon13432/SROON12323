Is 8 GB of RAM enough for RuneScape: Dragonwilds with an RTX 4060 Ti?

Answer from the CanMySpec database — RuneScape: Dragonwilds, RTX 4060 Ti

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. RuneScape: Dragonwilds asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with RTX 4060 Ti the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 50 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3-8100 or AMD Ryzen 5 1600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
