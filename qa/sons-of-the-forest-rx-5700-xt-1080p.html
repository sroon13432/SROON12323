Will an RX 5700 XT hold 60 FPS in Sons Of The Forest at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Sons Of The Forest, RX 5700 XT

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 5700 XT should land around 55-65 FPS in Sons Of The Forest, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 20 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, INTEL CORE I5-8400 or AMD RYZEN 3 3300X. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 5700 XT run Sons Of The Forest at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The RX 5700 XT is compared against the published recommended RX 5700 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Sons Of The Forest need?

Sons Of The Forest lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Sons Of The Forest at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 5700 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 570 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 430 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Sons Of The Forest need?

The store page lists INTEL CORE I5-8400 or AMD RYZEN 3 3300X as the minimum processor and INTEL CORE I7-8700K or AMD RYZEN 5 3600X as the recommended one.
