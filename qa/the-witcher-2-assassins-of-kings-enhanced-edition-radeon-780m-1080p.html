Will a Radeon 780M hold 60 FPS in The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition, Radeon 780M

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Radeon 780M should land around 165-200 FPS in The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Radeon 780M is well past The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition's recommended AMD Radeon HD 4850: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the Radeon 780M sits roughly 271% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 25GB Sound; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel 2.2 GHz Dual-Core or AMD 2.5 GHz Dual-Core. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Radeon 780M run The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 165-200 FPS. The Radeon 780M is compared against the published recommended AMD Radeon HD 4850 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition need?

The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition lists 1.5 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 3 GB at recommended settings.

The publisher lists AMD Radeon HD 4850 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 8800 GT as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 35 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition need?
