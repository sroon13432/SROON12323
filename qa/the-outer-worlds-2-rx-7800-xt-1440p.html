Will an RX 7800 XT hold 60 FPS in The Outer Worlds 2 at 1440p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — The Outer Worlds 2, RX 7800 XT

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 30-35 FPS at 1440p in The Outer Worlds 2 once you step one preset down from what the RX 7800 XT is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RX 7800 XT falls short of The Outer Worlds 2's recommended RX 6800 XT and is around the minimum ARC A580, so expect to run well below the recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 110 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 2600 / Intel i5-8400. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
  5. If 1440p at the recommended preset is short of 60 FPS, render at 1080p and let the monitor scale - the frame time gain is larger than the visual loss at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 7800 XT run The Outer Worlds 2 at 1440p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1440p on the High preset is 30-35 FPS. The RX 7800 XT is compared against the published recommended RX 6800 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does The Outer Worlds 2 need?

The Outer Worlds 2 lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for The Outer Worlds 2 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 6800 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and ARC A580 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 700 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does The Outer Worlds 2 need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 2600 / Intel i5-8400 as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 5 5600X / Intel Core i7-10700K as the recommended one.
