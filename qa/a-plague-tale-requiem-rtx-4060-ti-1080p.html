Can an RTX 4060 Ti run A Plague Tale: Requiem at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — A Plague Tale: Requiem, RTX 4060 Ti

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in A Plague Tale: Requiem once you step one preset down from what the RTX 4060 Ti is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 4060 Ti is below A Plague Tale: Requiem's recommended RTX 3070 while still clearing the minimum GTX 970, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 55 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-4690K (3.5 GHz)/AMD FX-8300 (3.3 GHz). If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 Ti run A Plague Tale: Requiem at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The RTX 4060 Ti is compared against the published recommended RTX 3070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does A Plague Tale: Requiem need?

A Plague Tale: Requiem lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for A Plague Tale: Requiem at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 970 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 620 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does A Plague Tale: Requiem need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-4690K (3.5 GHz)/AMD FX-8300 (3.3 GHz) as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-8700K (3.7 GHz) / AMD Ryzen 5 3600 (3.6 GHz) as the recommended one.
