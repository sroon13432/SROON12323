What limits you first

  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-6600@ 3.1 GHz or AMD R5 1600X @ 3.5 GHz or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3060 run Drive Beyond Horizons at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The RTX 3060 is compared against the published recommended RTX 3060 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Drive Beyond Horizons need?

Drive Beyond Horizons lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Drive Beyond Horizons at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3060 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1650 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 440 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Drive Beyond Horizons need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-6600@ 3.1 GHz or AMD R5 1600X @ 3.5 GHz or equivalent as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5 10400 3.1 GHz or AMD Ryzen 5 2600x 3.1 GHz as the recommended one.
