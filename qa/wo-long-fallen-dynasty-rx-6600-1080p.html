Can an RX 6600 run Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 26 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty, RX 6600

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty once you step one preset down from what the RX 6600 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RX 6600 sits a little under Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty's recommended RTX 2060 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 1650, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 60 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core™ i5-8400、AMD Ryzen™ 5 3400G. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 6600 run Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The RX 6600 is compared against the published recommended RTX 2060 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty need?

Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2060 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1650 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 430 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty need?

The store page lists Intel® Core™ i5-8400、AMD Ryzen™ 5 3400G as the minimum processor and Intel® Core™ i7-8700、AMD Ryzen™ 5 3600XT as the recommended one.
