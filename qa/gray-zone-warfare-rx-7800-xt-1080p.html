Is an RX 7800 XT enough for Gray Zone Warfare at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Gray Zone Warfare, RX 7800 XT

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 7800 XT should land around 60-70 FPS in Gray Zone Warfare, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core™ i5-8600 / AMD Ryzen™ 5 2600 (AVX2 support required). If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 7800 XT run Gray Zone Warfare at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 60-70 FPS. The RX 7800 XT is compared against the published recommended RTX 4070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Gray Zone Warfare need?

Gray Zone Warfare lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Gray Zone Warfare at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 4070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 5700 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 640 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Gray Zone Warfare need?

The store page lists Intel® Core™ i5-8600 / AMD Ryzen™ 5 2600 (AVX2 support required) as the minimum processor and Intel® Core™ i7-9700K / AMD Ryzen™ 5 3600X as the recommended one.
