Will a GTX 1650 hold 60 FPS in Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition, GTX 1650

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1650 should land around 60-70 FPS in Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 150 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i5-4460 | AMD Ryzen 3 1200. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1650 run Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 60-70 FPS. The GTX 1650 is compared against the published recommended GTX 970 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition need?

Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 970 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 770 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 210 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition need?

The store page lists Intel i5-4460 | AMD Ryzen 3 1200 as the minimum processor and Intel i5-8400 | AMD Ryzen 5 1500X as the recommended one.
