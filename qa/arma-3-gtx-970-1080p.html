Is a GTX 970 enough for Arma 3 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Arma 3, GTX 970

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 50-60 FPS at 1080p in Arma 3 once you step one preset down from what the GTX 970 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 970 sits a little under Arma 3's recommended RX 580 but comfortably above its minimum Intel HD 4000, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Install it on an SSD. The store page lists 80 GB available space (SSD) Sound: DirectX® compatible Other: Internet connection and free Steam account to…. On a mechanical drive this game streams slowly enough that the hitching looks like a GPU problem when it is not.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core quad-core or AMD Athlon quad-core. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 970 run Arma 3 at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 50-60 FPS. The GTX 970 is compared against the published recommended RX 580 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Arma 3 need?

Arma 3 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Arma 3 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 580 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Intel HD 4000 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 225 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Arma 3 need?

The store page lists Intel Core quad-core or AMD Athlon quad-core as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-7700K or AMD Ryzen 3 3300X or better as the recommended one.
