Is a GTX 1660 enough for METRO 2033 Redux at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — METRO 2033 Redux, GTX 1660

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 should land around 70-85 FPS in METRO 2033 Redux, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 20 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-4690, AMD Ryzen 3 1200, or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 run METRO 2033 Redux at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 70-85 FPS. The GTX 1660 is compared against the published recommended RX 590 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does METRO 2033 Redux need?

METRO 2033 Redux lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for METRO 2033 Redux at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 590 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 750 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 240 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does METRO 2033 Redux need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-4690, AMD Ryzen 3 1200, or better as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-8600, AMD Ryzen 5 3600, or better as the recommended one.
