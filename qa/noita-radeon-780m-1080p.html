Is a Radeon 780M enough for Noita at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Noita, Radeon 780M

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Radeon 780M should land around 135-165 FPS in Noita, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Radeon 780M is well past Noita's recommended 1 GB VRAM card: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the Radeon 780M sits roughly 189% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 2500 MB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Dual Core CPU, Intel i5 or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Radeon 780M run Noita at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 135-165 FPS. The Radeon 780M is compared against the published recommended 1 GB VRAM card (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Noita need?

Noita lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Noita at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 1 GB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 512 MB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 45 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Noita need?

The store page lists Dual Core CPU, Intel i5 or better as the minimum processor and The more cores the better as the recommended one.
