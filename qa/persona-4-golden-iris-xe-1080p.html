Can an Iris Xe run Persona 4 Golden at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Persona 4 Golden, Iris Xe

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the Iris Xe should land around 145-175 FPS in Persona 4 Golden, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Iris Xe is well past Persona 4 Golden's recommended GTX 460: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the Iris Xe sits roughly 217% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 14 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core 2 Duo E8400 | AMD Phenom II X2 550. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
