Will a GTX 1060 hold 60 FPS in Diablo® IV at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Diablo® IV, GTX 1060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1060 should land around 65-80 FPS in Diablo® IV, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1060 clears Diablo® IV's recommended RX 470 by a comfortable margin, so the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target is reachable without cutting settings.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 90 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core™ i5-2500K or AMD™ FX-8350. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1060 run Diablo® IV at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 65-80 FPS. The GTX 1060 is compared against the published recommended RX 470 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Diablo® IV need?

Diablo® IV lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Diablo® IV at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 470 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 205 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Diablo® IV need?

The store page lists Intel® Core™ i5-2500K or AMD™ FX-8350 as the minimum processor and Intel® Core™ i5-4670K or AMD Ryzen™ 1300X as the recommended one.
