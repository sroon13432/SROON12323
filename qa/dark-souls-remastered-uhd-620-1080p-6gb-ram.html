Do I need more than 6 GB RAM to play DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED on a UHD 620?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED, UHD 620

Short answer: 6 GB meets the 6 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED asks for 6 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with UHD 620 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 6 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 8 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2300 2.8 GHz / AMD FX-6300, 3.5 GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.
