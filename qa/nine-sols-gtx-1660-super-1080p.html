How many FPS can a GTX 1660 Super get in Nine Sols at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 21 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Nine Sols, GTX 1660 Super

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 Super should land around 80-100 FPS in Nine Sols, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1660 Super is well past Nine Sols's recommended GTX 970: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the GTX 1660 Super sits roughly 62% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 15 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Athlon X4 | Intel Core i5 4460. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 Super run Nine Sols at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 80-100 FPS. The GTX 1660 Super is compared against the published recommended GTX 970 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Nine Sols need?

Nine Sols lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Nine Sols at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 970 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 950 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 210 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Nine Sols need?

The store page lists AMD Athlon X4 | Intel Core i5 4460 as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 3 | Intel i5 Skylake as the recommended one.
