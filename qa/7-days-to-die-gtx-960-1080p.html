Will a GTX 960 hold 60 FPS in 7 Days to Die at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — 7 Days to Die, GTX 960

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in 7 Days to Die once you step one preset down from what the GTX 960 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 960 is below 7 Days to Die's recommended 4 GB VRAM card while still clearing the minimum 2 GB VRAM card, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 15 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 2.8 Ghz Quad Core CPU. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 960 run 7 Days to Die at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The GTX 960 is compared against the published recommended 4 GB VRAM card (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does 7 Days to Die need?

7 Days to Die lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 12 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for 7 Days to Die at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 4 GB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 2 GB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 170 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does 7 Days to Die need?

The store page lists 2.8 Ghz Quad Core CPU as the minimum processor and 3.2 Ghz Quad Core CPU or faster as the recommended one.
