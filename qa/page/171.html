How this board is answered

Every question here is answered by the CanMySpec Spec Desk from the publisher's own Steam requirement strings for the game, compared against the graphics card's position on our relative performance index. The questions are the ones players actually search for, written in the form they ask them; the answers are generated from the database and reviewed on every rebuild, not copied from another site.

Frame rates are estimates, not benchmark results: the model knows the card's relative performance, the resolution and the preset, but not your driver, your cooling or your CPU. Each answer says so and shows the band it expects. Open a game page to compare its requirement table yourself, or use the hardware filter to list every game your own card runs at 60 FPS.
