Will an RTX 4070 hold 60 FPS in Men of War II at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 26 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Men of War II, RTX 4070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RTX 4070 should land around 55-70 FPS in Men of War II, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe RTX 4070 lands just above Men of War II's recommended RTX 3070 - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 25 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5 2300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4070 run Men of War II at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The RTX 4070 is compared against the published recommended RTX 3070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Men of War II need?

Men of War II lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Men of War II at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 620 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Men of War II need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5 2300 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7 7700k as the recommended one.
