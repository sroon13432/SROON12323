How many FPS can a UHD 620 get in ASTLIBRA Revision at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — ASTLIBRA Revision, UHD 620

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the UHD 620 should land around 55-65 FPS in ASTLIBRA Revision, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 4 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Core i3-380UM 1.33GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
