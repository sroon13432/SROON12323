How many FPS can a GTX 1660 get in Half-Life: Alyx at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Half-Life: Alyx, GTX 1660

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 should land around 75-90 FPS in Half-Life: Alyx, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Core i5-7500 / Ryzen 5 1600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 run Half-Life: Alyx at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 75-90 FPS. The GTX 1660 is compared against the published recommended RX 580 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Half-Life: Alyx need?

Half-Life: Alyx lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 12 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Half-Life: Alyx at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 580 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 580 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 225 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Half-Life: Alyx need?

The store page lists Core i5-7500 / Ryzen 5 1600 as the minimum processor and Core i5-7500 / Ryzen 5 1600 as the recommended one.
