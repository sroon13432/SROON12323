How many FPS can a GTX 1660 get in Operation: Harsh Doorstop at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Operation: Harsh Doorstop, GTX 1660

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 should land around 60-75 FPS in Operation: Harsh Doorstop, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1660 clears Operation: Harsh Doorstop's recommended RX Vega 56 by a comfortable margin, so the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target is reachable without cutting settings.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-8320. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 run Operation: Harsh Doorstop at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 60-75 FPS. The GTX 1660 is compared against the published recommended RX Vega 56 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Operation: Harsh Doorstop need?

Operation: Harsh Doorstop lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Operation: Harsh Doorstop at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX Vega 56 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 760 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 270 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Operation: Harsh Doorstop need?

The store page lists Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-8320 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-7700 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600 as the recommended one.
