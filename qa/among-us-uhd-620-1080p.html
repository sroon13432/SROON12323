Is a UHD 620 enough for Among Us at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Among Us, UHD 620

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 40-45 FPS at 1080p in Among Us once you step one preset down from what the UHD 620 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe UHD 620 is below Among Us's recommended GTX 650 while still clearing the minimum Intel HD 4600, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, INTEL i3-4330. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the UHD 620 run Among Us at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 40-45 FPS. The UHD 620 is compared against the published recommended GTX 650 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Among Us need?

Among Us lists 1 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 4 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Among Us at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 650 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Intel HD 4600 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 45 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Among Us need?

The store page lists INTEL i3-4330 as the minimum processor and INTEL i3-4330 as the recommended one.
