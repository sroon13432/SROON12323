Can a GTX 1050 Ti run Tom Clancy’s The Division™ at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Tom Clancy’s The Division™, GTX 1050 Ti

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in Tom Clancy’s The Division™ once you step one preset down from what the GTX 1050 Ti is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 1050 Ti is below Tom Clancy’s The Division™'s recommended R9 290 while still clearing the minimum GTX 560, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2400 | AMD FX-6100, or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1050 Ti run Tom Clancy’s The Division™ at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The GTX 1050 Ti is compared against the published recommended R9 290 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Tom Clancy’s The Division™ need?

Tom Clancy’s The Division™ lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Tom Clancy’s The Division™ at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists R9 290 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 560 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 200 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Tom Clancy’s The Division™ need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-2400 | AMD FX-6100, or better as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-3770 | AMD FX-8350, or better as the recommended one.
