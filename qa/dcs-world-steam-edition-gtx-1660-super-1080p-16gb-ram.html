Is 16 GB of RAM enough for DCS World Steam Edition with a GTX 1660 Super?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — DCS World Steam Edition, GTX 1660 Super

Short answer: 16 GB meets the 16 GB minimum but falls short of the 32 GB recommended for this game. DCS World Steam Edition asks for 16 GB at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 1660 Super the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 16 GB against a 32 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 500 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel or AMD 3.0Ghz+ with 4+ cores. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

How much RAM does DCS World Steam Edition need?

DCS World Steam Edition lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 32 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 16 GB.

What GPU do I need for DCS World Steam Edition at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 8 GB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 8 GB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 300 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does DCS World Steam Edition need?

The store page lists Intel or AMD 3.0Ghz+ with 4+ cores as the minimum processor and Intel or AMD 4.5Ghz+ with 8+ cores as the recommended one.
