DOOM on a GTX 1660 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — DOOM, GTX 1660

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 should land around 80-100 FPS in DOOM, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1660 is well past DOOM's recommended R9 290: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the GTX 1660 sits roughly 60% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 55 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2400/AMD FX-8320 or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 run DOOM at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 80-100 FPS. The GTX 1660 is compared against the published recommended R9 290 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does DOOM need?

DOOM lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for DOOM at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists R9 290 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 670 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 200 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does DOOM need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-2400/AMD FX-8320 or better as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-3770/AMD FX-8350 or better as the recommended one.
