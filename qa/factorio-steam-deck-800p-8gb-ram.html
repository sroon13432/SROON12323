Do I need more than 8 GB RAM to play Factorio on a Steam Deck?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Factorio, Steam Deck

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. Factorio asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with Steam Deck the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 10 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Quad core 3Ghz+. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Factorio need?

Factorio lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Factorio at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1050 Ti as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Intel HD Graphics as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 160 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Factorio need?

The store page lists Quad core 3Ghz+ as the minimum processor and Quad core 4Ghz+ from 2020 or newer as the recommended one.
