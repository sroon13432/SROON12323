Do I need more than 4 GB RAM to play War Thunder on a Steam Deck?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — War Thunder, Steam Deck

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. War Thunder asks for 4 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with Steam Deck the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 95 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Dual-Core 2.2 GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does War Thunder need?

War Thunder lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 4 GB.

What GPU do I need for War Thunder at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 570 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Any DirectX-capable GPU as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 215 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does War Thunder need?

The store page lists Dual-Core 2.2 GHz as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5 or Ryzen 5 3600 or better as the recommended one.
