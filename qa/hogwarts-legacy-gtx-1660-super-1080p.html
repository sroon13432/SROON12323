Can a GTX 1660 Super run Hogwarts Legacy at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Hogwarts Legacy, GTX 1660 Super

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in Hogwarts Legacy once you step one preset down from what the GTX 1660 Super is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe GTX 1660 Super sits a little under Hogwarts Legacy's recommended ARC A770 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 960, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 85 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-6600 (3.3Ghz) or AMD Ryzen 5 1400 (3.2Ghz). If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 Super run Hogwarts Legacy at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The GTX 1660 Super is compared against the published recommended ARC A770 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Hogwarts Legacy need?

Hogwarts Legacy lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Hogwarts Legacy at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists ARC A770 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 960 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 400 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Hogwarts Legacy need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-6600 (3.3Ghz) or AMD Ryzen 5 1400 (3.2Ghz) as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-8700 (3.2Ghz) or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 (3.6 Ghz) as the recommended one.
