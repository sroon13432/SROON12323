Will a GTX 750 Ti hold 60 FPS in Neon White at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Neon White, GTX 750 Ti

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 750 Ti should land around 55-65 FPS in Neon White, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 6 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core 2 Duo E6750, 2.66 GHz | AMD Phenom II X3 720, 2.8 GHz (w/ at least 3-threads). If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 750 Ti run Neon White at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The GTX 750 Ti is compared against the published recommended GTX 750 Ti (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Neon White need?

Neon White lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 6 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Neon White at 60 FPS?
