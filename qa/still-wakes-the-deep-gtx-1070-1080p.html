Will a GTX 1070 hold 60 FPS in Still Wakes the Deep at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Still Wakes the Deep, GTX 1070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1070 should land around 55-65 FPS in Still Wakes the Deep, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1070 sits a little under Still Wakes the Deep's recommended ARC A750 but comfortably above its minimum Radeon RX 6000 series, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 9 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Quad-core Intel or AMD, 2.5 GHz or faster. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1070 run Still Wakes the Deep at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The GTX 1070 is compared against the published recommended ARC A750 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Still Wakes the Deep need?

Still Wakes the Deep lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Still Wakes the Deep at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists ARC A750 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Radeon RX 6000 series as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 380 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Still Wakes the Deep need?

The store page lists Quad-core Intel or AMD, 2.5 GHz or faster as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-11600 / Ryzen 5 5600X as the recommended one.
