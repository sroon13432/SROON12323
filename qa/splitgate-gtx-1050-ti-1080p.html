Splitgate on a GTX 1050 Ti at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Splitgate, GTX 1050 Ti

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1050 Ti should land around 110-135 FPS in Splitgate, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1050 Ti is well past Splitgate's recommended GTX 660: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the GTX 1050 Ti sits roughly 129% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 15 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Any dual core CPU. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
