Is 6 GB of RAM enough for Black Mesa with a Radeon 780M?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Black Mesa, Radeon 780M

Short answer: 6 GB meets the 6 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Black Mesa asks for 6 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with Radeon 780M the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

RAM at minimum6 GB meets the 6 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. The Radeon 780M is below Black Mesa's recommended 4 GB VRAM card while still clearing the minimum 2 GB VRAM card, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 6 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 20 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 2.6 Dual Core Processor or Greater. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Black Mesa need?

Black Mesa lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 6 GB.

What GPU do I need for Black Mesa at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 4 GB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 2 GB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 170 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Black Mesa need?

The store page lists 2.6 Dual Core Processor or Greater as the minimum processor and 3.2 Quad Core Processor or Greater as the recommended one.
