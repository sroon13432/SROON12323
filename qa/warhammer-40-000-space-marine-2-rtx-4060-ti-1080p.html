How many FPS can an RTX 4060 Ti get in Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2, RTX 4060 Ti

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 once you step one preset down from what the RTX 4060 Ti is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 4060 Ti is below Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2's recommended RTX 3070 while still clearing the minimum RX 580, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 75 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 2600X / Intel Core i5-8600K. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 Ti run Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The RTX 4060 Ti is compared against the published recommended RTX 3070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 need?

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 580 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 620 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 2600X / Intel Core i5-8600K as the minimum processor and AMD Ryzen 7 5800X / Intel Core i7-12700 as the recommended one.
