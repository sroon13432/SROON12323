Is a GTX 1650 enough for Farming Simulator 22 at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Farming Simulator 22, GTX 1650

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1650 should land around 55-70 FPS in Farming Simulator 22, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1650 lands just above Farming Simulator 22's recommended RX 570 - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 35 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-3330 or AMD FX-8320 or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1650 run Farming Simulator 22 at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1650 is compared against the published recommended RX 570 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Farming Simulator 22 need?

Farming Simulator 22 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Farming Simulator 22 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 570 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 215 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Farming Simulator 22 need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-3330 or AMD FX-8320 or better as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-5675C or AMD Ryzen 5 1600 or better as the recommended one.
