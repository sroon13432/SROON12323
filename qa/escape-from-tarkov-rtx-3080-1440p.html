Escape from Tarkov on an RTX 3080 at 1440p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Escape from Tarkov, RTX 3080

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 35-40 FPS at 1440p in Escape from Tarkov once you step one preset down from what the RTX 3080 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 3080 falls short of Escape from Tarkov's recommended RTX 4070 and is around the minimum GTX 1660, so expect to run well below the recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 80 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or similar. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
  5. If 1440p at the recommended preset is short of 60 FPS, render at 1080p and let the monitor scale - the frame time gain is larger than the visual loss at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3080 run Escape from Tarkov at 1440p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1440p on the High preset is 35-40 FPS. The RTX 3080 is compared against the published recommended RTX 4070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Escape from Tarkov need?

Escape from Tarkov lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 64 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Escape from Tarkov at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 4070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 640 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Escape from Tarkov need?

The store page lists AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or similar as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-14700F or better as the recommended one.
