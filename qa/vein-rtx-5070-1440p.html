Can an RTX 5070 run VEIN at 1440p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — VEIN, RTX 5070

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 30-35 FPS at 1440p in VEIN once you step one preset down from what the RTX 5070 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 5070 falls short of VEIN's recommended RTX 3080 and is around the minimum RTX 2060, so expect to run well below the recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 100 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
  5. If 1440p at the recommended preset is short of 60 FPS, render at 1080p and let the monitor scale - the frame time gain is larger than the visual loss at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 5070 run VEIN at 1440p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1440p on the High preset is 30-35 FPS. The RTX 5070 is compared against the published recommended RTX 3080 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does VEIN need?

VEIN lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 24 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for VEIN at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 3080 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RTX 2060 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 730 meet the recommended figure.
