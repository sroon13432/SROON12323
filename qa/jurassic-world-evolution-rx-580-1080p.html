Jurassic World Evolution on an RX 580 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Jurassic World Evolution, RX 580

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 580 should land around 55-65 FPS in Jurassic World Evolution, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe RX 580 sits a little under Jurassic World Evolution's recommended RX 480 but comfortably above its minimum GTX 1050, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 12 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i5-2300/AMD FX-4300. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 580 run Jurassic World Evolution at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-65 FPS. The RX 580 is compared against the published recommended RX 480 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Jurassic World Evolution need?

Jurassic World Evolution lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 12 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Jurassic World Evolution at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 480 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1050 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 230 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Jurassic World Evolution need?

The store page lists Intel i5-2300/AMD FX-4300 as the minimum processor and Intel i7-4770/AMD FX-8350 as the recommended one.
