Can a GTX 1060 run Factorio at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Factorio, GTX 1060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1060 should land around 80-95 FPS in Factorio, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 10 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Quad core 3Ghz+. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  2. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1060 run Factorio at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 80-95 FPS. The GTX 1060 is compared against the published recommended GTX 1050 Ti (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Factorio need?

Factorio lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Factorio at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 1050 Ti as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Intel HD Graphics as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 160 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Factorio need?

The store page lists Quad core 3Ghz+ as the minimum processor and Quad core 4Ghz+ from 2020 or newer as the recommended one.
