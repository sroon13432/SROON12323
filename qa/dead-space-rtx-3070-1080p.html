Dead Space on an RTX 3070 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Dead Space, RTX 3070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RTX 3070 should land around 65-80 FPS in Dead Space, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 50 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Ryzen 5 2600x, Core i5 8600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3070 run Dead Space at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 65-80 FPS. The RTX 3070 is compared against the published recommended RTX 2070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Dead Space need?

Dead Space lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Dead Space at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1070 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 510 meet the recommended figure.
