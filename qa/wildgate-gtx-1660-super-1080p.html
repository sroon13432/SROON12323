Can a GTX 1660 Super run Wildgate at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Wildgate, GTX 1660 Super

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1660 Super should land around 55-70 FPS in Wildgate, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1660 Super lands just above Wildgate's recommended ARC A580 - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 9 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-4440 or AMD Ryzen 5 1600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1660 Super run Wildgate at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1660 Super is compared against the published recommended ARC A580 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Wildgate need?

Wildgate lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Wildgate at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists ARC A580 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1050 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 330 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Wildgate need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-4440 or AMD Ryzen 5 1600 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-9700K or AMD Ryzen 7 3700X as the recommended one.
