How many FPS can an RTX 3060 get in ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree) at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree), RTX 3060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the Maximum preset the RTX 3060 should land around 80-100 FPS in ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree), and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe RTX 3060 is well past ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree)'s recommended RX Vega 56: the store page asks for that card for 1080p / 1440p @ 60 FPS (Maximum), and the RTX 3060 sits roughly 63% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • VRAM has room. 12 GB on the RTX 3060 against a requirement block that mentions 8 GB means high-resolution textures are not what will hold you back.
  • Install it on an SSD. The store page lists 60 GB SSD required. On a mechanical drive this game streams slowly enough that the hitching looks like a GPU problem when it is not.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 3 3300X. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Set 'Effects Quality' to Medium to prevent frame dips during dragon fire and massive spell casts.
  2. Disable Microsoft Device Association Root Enumerator in Device Manager if experiencing periodic micro-stutters.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Can the RTX 3060 run ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree) at 1080p?
