Big Walk on a GTX 1070 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Big Walk, GTX 1070

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1070 should land around 55-70 FPS in Big Walk, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i7-7700K or AMD Ryzen 5 1600X. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1070 run Big Walk at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The GTX 1070 is compared against the published recommended RX 5600 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Big Walk need?

Big Walk lists 12 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Big Walk at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 5600 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 750 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 350 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Big Walk need?

The store page lists Intel Core i7-7700K or AMD Ryzen 5 1600X as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-10600K or Ryzen 7 3700X as the recommended one.
