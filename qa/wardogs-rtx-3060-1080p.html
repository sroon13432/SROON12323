How many FPS can an RTX 3060 get in WARDOGS at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — WARDOGS, RTX 3060

Short answer: playable, but not at the recommended preset. Expect roughly 45-55 FPS at 1080p in WARDOGS once you step one preset down from what the RTX 3060 is nominally asked for.

Playable, tunedThe RTX 3060 is below WARDOGS's recommended RX 6700 XT while still clearing the minimum RX 590, so the game runs but not at the published recommended preset.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 50 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel® Core i5 8600, AMD Ryzen 5 3500. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 3060 run WARDOGS at 1080p?

Partly. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 45-55 FPS. The RTX 3060 is compared against the published recommended RX 6700 XT (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does WARDOGS need?

WARDOGS lists 16 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for WARDOGS at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 6700 XT as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and RX 590 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 540 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does WARDOGS need?

The store page lists Intel® Core i5 8600, AMD Ryzen 5 3500 as the minimum processor and Intel® Core i7 12700k, AMD Ryzen 7 5700x as the recommended one.
