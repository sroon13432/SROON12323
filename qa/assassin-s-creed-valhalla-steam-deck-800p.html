How many FPS can a Steam Deck get in Assassin's Creed Valhalla at 800p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 24 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Assassin's Creed Valhalla, Steam Deck

Short answer: yes. At 800p on the High preset the Steam Deck should land around 125-155 FPS in Assassin's Creed Valhalla, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe Steam Deck is well past Assassin's Creed Valhalla's recommended R9 380: the store page asks for that card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the Steam Deck sits roughly 139% higher on our relative index.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 160 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Ryzen 3 1200 3.1 GHz / Intel Core i5-4460 3.2 GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.

Can the Steam Deck run Assassin's Creed Valhalla at 800p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 800p on the High preset is 125-155 FPS. The Steam Deck is compared against the published recommended R9 380 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Assassin's Creed Valhalla need?
