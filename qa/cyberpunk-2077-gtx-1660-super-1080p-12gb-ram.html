What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 12 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • VRAM is tight. The store page's requirement block mentions 8 GB of video memory and the GTX 1660 Super has 6 GB. Textures at High will start streaming from system memory, which shows up as blurred ground and stutter while you turn; one notch down on texture quality fixes it.
  • Install it on an SSD. The store page lists 70 GB SSD required. On a mechanical drive this game streams slowly enough that the hitching looks like a GPU problem when it is not.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i7-6700 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Crowd Density: Setting this from High to Medium saves 10-15% CPU load in crowded downtown areas.
  2. Volumetric Fog Resolution has minimal visual impact at Medium but boosts FPS by ~8%.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.
