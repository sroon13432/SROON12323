Do I need more than 4 GB RAM to play Ravenfield on a Steam Deck?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Ravenfield, Steam Deck

Short answer: 4 GB meets the 4 GB minimum but falls short of the 8 GB recommended for this game. Ravenfield asks for 4 GB at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings, and with Steam Deck the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 4 GB against a 8 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 1 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 32-bit, 2 GHz or Faster. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Ravenfield need?

Ravenfield lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 4 GB.

What GPU do I need for Ravenfield at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 750 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Intel HD 4000 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 70 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Ravenfield need?

The store page lists 32-bit, 2 GHz or Faster as the minimum processor and 64-bit, 3 GHz or Faster as the recommended one.
