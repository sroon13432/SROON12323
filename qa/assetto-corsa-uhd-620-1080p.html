What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 30 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, AMD Athlon X2 2.8 GHZ, Intel Core 2 Duo 2.4 GHZ. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the UHD 620 run Assetto Corsa at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 75-95 FPS. The UHD 620 is compared against the published recommended Any DirectX-capable GPU (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Assetto Corsa need?

Assetto Corsa lists 2 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 6 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Assetto Corsa at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists Any DirectX-capable GPU as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and Any DirectX-capable GPU as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 20 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Assetto Corsa need?

The store page lists AMD Athlon X2 2.8 GHZ, Intel Core 2 Duo 2.4 GHZ as the minimum processor and AMD Six-Core CPU, Intel Quad-Core CPU as the recommended one.
