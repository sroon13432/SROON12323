Palworld with 16 GB RAM and an RTX 3070: playable or stuttering?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Palworld, RTX 3070

Short answer: 16 GB meets the 16 GB minimum but falls short of the 32 GB recommended for this game. Palworld asks for 16 GB at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings, and with RTX 3070 the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 16 GB against a 32 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • VRAM fits exactly. Palworld is written around 8 GB of video memory and that is what the RTX 3070 carries, so texture settings at the recommended preset are fine but the next preset up is not.
  • Install it on an SSD. The store page lists 40 GB SSD required. On a mechanical drive this game streams slowly enough that the hitching looks like a GPU problem when it is not.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-3570K 3.4 GHz / 4 Core. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • 1440p is the expensive part. 1440p draws 78% more pixels than 1080p, which is roughly one full preset's worth of performance. If you need 60 FPS more than sharpness, 1080p on a 1440p monitor with integer scaling is the cheaper trade.

Settings to start from

  1. Lowering 'View Distance' from Epic to High gives an instant 12-15 FPS boost near large pal bases.
  2. Turn off 'Motion Blur' and set 'Ride Camera Distance' to maximum for improved spatial awareness and smoother visuals.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.
