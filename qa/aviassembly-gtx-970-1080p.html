How many FPS can a GTX 970 get in Aviassembly at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Aviassembly, GTX 970

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 970 should land around 55-65 FPS in Aviassembly, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 300 MB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core I5 4460 3.20 Ghz or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 970 run Aviassembly at 1080p?

Aviassembly lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 4 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Aviassembly at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 970 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 970 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 210 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Aviassembly need?
