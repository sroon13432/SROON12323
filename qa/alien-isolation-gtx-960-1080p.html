Alien: Isolation on a GTX 960 at 1080p: what settings and FPS?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 20 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Alien: Isolation, GTX 960

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 960 should land around 85-105 FPS in Alien: Isolation, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 35 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, 3.16Ghz Intel Core 2 Duo E8500. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If experiencing frame drops, lower Volumetric Lighting and Shadow Quality to Medium.
  2. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 960 run Alien: Isolation at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 85-105 FPS. The GTX 960 is compared against the published recommended 2 GB VRAM card (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Alien: Isolation need?

Alien: Isolation lists 4 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Alien: Isolation at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists 2 GB VRAM card as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and 1 GB VRAM card as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 80 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Alien: Isolation need?

The store page lists 3.16Ghz Intel Core 2 Duo E8500 as the minimum processor and AMD: Phenom II X4 955 - 4 Core, 3.2 GHz or Intel: Core 2 Quad Q9650 - 4 Core, 3.0 GHz as the recommended one.
