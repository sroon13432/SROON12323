Do I need more than 8 GB RAM to play Witch It on an RX 5500 XT?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 26 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Witch It, RX 5500 XT

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 16 GB recommended for this game. Witch It asks for 8 GB at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings, and with RX 5500 XT the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 16 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 7 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i3, 2.4GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
