Is an RX 5700 XT enough for Scrap Mechanic at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Scrap Mechanic, RX 5700 XT

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 5700 XT should land around 55-65 FPS in Scrap Mechanic, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe RX 5700 XT sits a little under Scrap Mechanic's recommended RTX 3060 but comfortably above its minimum Intel Iris Xe, which puts it in the 'drop one preset' bracket.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 30 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-1235U or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.
