How many FPS can an RX 6700 XT get in Blade and Sorcery at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 19 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Blade and Sorcery, RX 6700 XT

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the RX 6700 XT should land around 55-70 FPS in Blade and Sorcery, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Quad Core 3GHz. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RX 6700 XT run Blade and Sorcery at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 55-70 FPS. The RX 6700 XT is compared against the published recommended RTX 2070 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Blade and Sorcery need?

Blade and Sorcery lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 16 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Blade and Sorcery at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RTX 2070 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 1070 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 510 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Blade and Sorcery need?

The store page lists Quad Core 3GHz as the minimum processor and 8-16 Core 4GHz as the recommended one.
