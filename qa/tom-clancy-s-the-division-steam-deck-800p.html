Will a Steam Deck hold 60 FPS in Tom Clancy’s The Division™ at 800p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 18 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Tom Clancy’s The Division™, Steam Deck

Short answer: yes. At 800p on the High preset the Steam Deck should land around 95-115 FPS in Tom Clancy’s The Division™, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2400 | AMD FX-6100, or better. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.
  • Handheld resolution. At 800p the Deck's 1280x800 panel does a lot of the work for it; a dock pushing 1080p to a TV costs roughly 40% of the frame rate for the same settings.
  • No dedicated video memory. This graphics solution borrows from system RAM, so the memory figure above is shared with the operating system and the game has less of it than the number suggests.

Settings to start from

  1. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  2. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  3. Start at Low, then raise one setting at a time - shadows, foliage and volumetric fog cost the most frames per unit of visible difference in this kind of scene.
  4. Turn on upscaling if the game offers it (DLSS on GeForce, FSR or XeSS on anything else). At Quality it usually returns 25-40% of the frame rate for a small sharpness cost at 1080p.
