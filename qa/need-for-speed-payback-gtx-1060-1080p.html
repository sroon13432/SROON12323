Is a GTX 1060 enough for Need for Speed™ Payback at 1080p?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 26 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Need for Speed™ Payback, GTX 1060

Short answer: yes. At 1080p on the High preset the GTX 1060 should land around 60-70 FPS in Need for Speed™ Payback, and the recommended preset is the right place to start.

60 FPS readyThe GTX 1060 lands just above Need for Speed™ Payback's recommended RX 480 - enough for the published 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) target, with little headroom to spare.

What limits you first

  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 30 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel i3 6300 @ 3.8GHz or AMD FX 8150 @ 3.6GHz with 4 hardware threads. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  2. Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

Can the GTX 1060 run Need for Speed™ Payback at 1080p?

Yes. The estimated frame rate at 1080p on the High preset is 60-70 FPS. The GTX 1060 is compared against the published recommended RX 480 (1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings)).

How much RAM does Need for Speed™ Payback need?

Need for Speed™ Payback lists 6 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings.

What GPU do I need for Need for Speed™ Payback at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX 480 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 750 Ti as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 230 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Need for Speed™ Payback need?

The store page lists Intel i3 6300 @ 3.8GHz or AMD FX 8150 @ 3.6GHz with 4 hardware threads as the minimum processor and Intel i5 4690K @ 3.5GHz or AMD FX 8350 @ 4.0GHz with 4 hardware threads as the recommended one.
