Is 8 GB of RAM enough for Sonic Frontiers with a GTX 1650 Super?

Asked by the CanMySpec Spec Desk · 22 Nov 2026 · 1 answer · Answered from the official Steam requirements
Answer from the CanMySpec database — Sonic Frontiers, GTX 1650 Super

Short answer: 8 GB meets the 8 GB minimum but falls short of the 12 GB recommended for this game. Sonic Frontiers asks for 8 GB at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings, and with GTX 1650 Super the memory question is separable from the graphics one.

What limits you first

  • Memory is at the minimum, not the recommendation. With 8 GB against a 12 GB recommendation, background apps (browser tabs, Discord, recording software) are competing with the game. Close them before you judge the frame rate.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 30 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-3470 or AMD Ryzen 5 1400. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Keep your GPU driver on the latest Game Ready / Adrenalin branch before comparing frame rates.
  2. If frame pacing stutters, lower Shadows, Volumetric Lighting and Ambient Occlusion one step each.
  3. Keep the recommended preset and drop shadow quality one notch: it is the single largest cost in most modern renderers and the least visible in motion.
  4. Leave ray tracing off unless you have a card that supports hardware upscaling with it; the reflections are not worth half your frame rate on this tier.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Sonic Frontiers need?

Sonic Frontiers lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 12 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Sonic Frontiers at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists RX Vega 56 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 660 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 270 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Sonic Frontiers need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-3470 or AMD Ryzen 5 1400 as the minimum processor and Intel Core i5-6600 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600 as the recommended one.
