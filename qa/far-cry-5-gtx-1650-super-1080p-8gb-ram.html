What limits you first

  • Memory is not the limit. 8 GB covers the 8 GB this game asks for, so any stutter you see will come from the graphics card, a hard drive, or the CPU rather than from RAM.
  • Storage is a size question, not a speed one. The store page lists 40 GB available space; the publisher does not require an SSD, so free space is the only thing to check.
  • The CPU assumption. Every frame rate on this page assumes a processor at or above the published minimum, Intel Core i5-2400 @ 3.1 GHz or AMD FX-6300 @ 3.5 GHz or equivalent. If yours is older, the GPU figure is the ceiling and the CPU decides how far below it you land.

Settings to start from

  1. Enable Fullscreen mode over Borderless Windowed to reduce input latency.
  2. Keep GPU drivers updated to the latest Game Ready branch for optimal frame stability.
  3. The recommended preset is the starting point, not the ceiling - you have headroom to raise texture and shadow quality until the frame rate drops below your target.
  4. Cap the frame rate just under your refresh rate rather than leaving it uncapped; a steady frame time at 60 feels better than a swinging 70-90.

Frequently asked questions

How much RAM does Far Cry® 5 need?

Far Cry® 5 lists 8 GB as its minimum memory requirement and 8 GB at recommended settings; this question assumes 8 GB.

What GPU do I need for Far Cry® 5 at 60 FPS?

The publisher lists GTX 970 as the recommended card for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and GTX 670 as the minimum. Cards with a relative index at or above 210 meet the recommended figure.

What CPU does Far Cry® 5 need?

The store page lists Intel Core i5-2400 @ 3.1 GHz or AMD FX-6300 @ 3.5 GHz or equivalent as the minimum processor and Intel Core i7-4770 @ 3.4 GHz or AMD Ryzen 5 1600 @ 3.2 GHz or equivalent as the recommended one.
