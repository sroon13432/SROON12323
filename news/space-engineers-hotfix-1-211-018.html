Space Engineers: Hotfix 1.211.018

Space Engineers · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

Fixed scenario scripts and mods failing to convert grids between ship and station, which kept the hangar closed in The First Jump - Mission 3 for example

What the notes say

  • Fixed a rare crash when loading a world with Event Controllers set to Incoming Missile
  • Fixed the new separate Park On/Off and Handbrake On/Off toolbar actions unintentionally working as toggles
  • Fixed NPCs not opening rotated Angled Doors on their path
  • Fixed NPCs being unable to walk through Angled Door B
  • Fixed scenario scripts and mods failing to convert grids between ship and station, which kept the hangar closed in The First Jump - Mission 3 for example
  • Fixed EMP HUD interference and its glitch sound playing while seated in a grid that is immune to EMP

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
