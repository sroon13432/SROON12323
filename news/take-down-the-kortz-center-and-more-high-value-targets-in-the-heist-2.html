Take Down the Kortz Center and More High-Value Targets in The Heist Event

Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

To commemorate The Kortz Center Heist’s inaugural Heist Event, you and your accomplices are guaranteed to find The Outcome of Endeavour — previously only available to those who reached Elitist status in this summer’s Fine Art Collector program. Plus, adorn the walls of your Mansion with a staggering work of mid-century realism: Play GTA Online anytime through November 11 to get The Great Circle Back.

Get in, get out, and make a killing in this week’s GTA Online Heist Event. Assemble a crew of familiar faces or perfect strangers, gather your gear, execute a robbery, and exfiltrate safely to earn doubled first-time completion bonuses for completing all the Original Heists, The Doomsday Heist, and The Diamond Casino Heist.

What the notes say

  • Celebrate GTA Online’s colorful cast of supporting characters from November 12 through December 2.
  • All Contact Missions — including Madrazo’s Dispatch Services, Gerald’s Last Play, Premium Deluxe Repo Work, and Lowriders — are paying out Double Rewards (stacking to 4X for GTA+ Members).
  • Also, from November 12 through December 2, Freemode Challenges are paying out 4X GTA$ and RP, and the Stunt Race Series is rewarding 3X GTA$ and RP for all competitors.
  • Sumo (Remix) and the Community Series also offer Double and Triple Rewards, respectively.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
