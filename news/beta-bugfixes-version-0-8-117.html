Beta bugfixes (Version 0.8.117)

Manor Lords · posted by the game's developer on Steam·24 October 2026·Source

Employed families helping construct their own burgage plot will not perform “cosmetic construction” anymore. That allows prioritizing any other potentially productive tasks.

Honey Hunting Affinity now takes 50% of the Woodland Environment as the chance to succeed at establishing a beehive at a tree.

What the notes say

  • While a crop grows, the other fertility types now gain slightly less.
  • Fallow cycles are generally needed to keep fields sustainable.
  • Rye’s fertility bonus increased from x1.5 to x1.75 and yield increased 7%.
  • Rye becomes the better choice from bread making at the Bakery if Wheat is below 60% fertility.
  • Upgrading or converting a building now properly updates the building menu, and relocating relocates the proper upgraded building.
  • Fixed workers preferring cosmetic construction of close buildings that doesn’t increase progress over productive construction of far buildings.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
