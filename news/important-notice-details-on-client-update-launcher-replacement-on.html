[Important Notice] Details on Client Update & Launcher Replacement on Steam

Wuthering Waves · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

: Steam will support Tiered Client Resource Downloads for the game, allowing you to select and download game resources of your preferred graphics quality and save storage space. Tiered Client Resource Downloads is a feature that allows you to select between different resource tiers. In future versions of Wuthering Waves, the original single resource package will be divided into three tiers (UHD, HD, and SD) based on graphics quality, models, and other assets. You may select and download the tier that best suits your storage space and device specifications.

: When the Official Launcher becomes available on Steam, the platform will also support pre-download for future versions of the game, allowing you to download resources for the upcoming version in advance. This feature will become available once pre-download for Version 4.0 starts.

What the notes say

  • This feature will become available once pre-download for Version 4.0 starts.
  • Due to this client upgrade, the download method for the game on Steam will be replaced with our Official Launcher, which will be used for future game package downloads.
  • To ensure that the new version and the launcher run properly and prevent file conflicts or download errors, please follow the steps below to update the game:
  • After downloading the Official Launcher, you can select and download the resource tier that best suits your storage space and device specifications from the launcher.
  • ※This uninstalling and reinstalling process will only affect your local files for the game client; your account data and in-game progress will not be affected.
  • If you encounter in-game issues such as login failures, payment errors, or missing map assets, please verify whether the old game package has been completely uninstalled.
