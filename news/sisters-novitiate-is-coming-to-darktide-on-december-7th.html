Sisters Novitiate is coming to Darktide on December 7th!

Warhammer 40,000: Darktide · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

The Adepta Sororitas will make their presence known on Tertium, with the Sisters Novitiate class launching into Warhammer 40,000: Darktide with fervour and fury. Wielding a devastating arsenal of weaponry and channelling their unwavering faith through a unique talent system, these zealous warriors will come thundering into the Hive ready to prove their mettle in the Emperor’s name!

The Sisters Novitiate Class will launch alongside a free update introducing the long-anticipated Meltagun to Darktide! The iconic Warhammer 40,000 weapon can be wielded by both the Sisters Novitiate and the Veteran class.

What the notes say

  • The iconic Warhammer 40,000 weapon can be wielded by both the Sisters Novitiate and the Veteran class.
  • Unlike other classes, the Sisters Novitiate are able to choose from six divine and devastating Acts of Faith, as opposed to blitz and combat abilities.
  • Their abilities draw upon reserves of Faith, allowing them to unleash brutal Neural Whip attacks to the inspiring Icon of Purity and protective prayers.
  • Faith can take many forms for the Sisters Novitiate, each channeling the Emperor's divine will and bringing his wrath upon his enemies.
  • Any two Acts of Faith can be equipped at a time, allowing players to choose how their Novitiate will unleash righteous fury on the battlefield.
  • Though still Novitiates of the Adepta Sororitas, these battle eager warriors have come to Tertium ready for war!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
