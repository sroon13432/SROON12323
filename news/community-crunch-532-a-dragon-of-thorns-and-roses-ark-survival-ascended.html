Community Crunch 532: A Dragon of Thorns and Roses

ARK: Survival Ascended · posted by the game's developer on Steam·14 November 2026·Source

Where some of its kin are keen to strike, Silvanor seems to possess a remarkable sense of patience. Those who cross its path may find themselves met with a watchful gaze rather than an immediate attack, almost as though it's giving Survivors a chance to reconsider their next move.

With its sweeping green wings, moss-covered scales, and wildflowers blooming across its body, Silvanor appears as though a piece of the forest itself has come to life. But beneath that mantle of green lies a creature with a temperament as intriguing as its appearance.
