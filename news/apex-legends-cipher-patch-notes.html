Apex Legends™: Cipher Patch Notes

Apex Legends™ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Apex Legends: Cipher is here! It’s bringing new features, new weapon combos, and big changes to Ranked alongside the usual slew of bug fixes and balancing passes. Read on for info about the Akimbo Wingman, Strongholds, new Ranked features and more! Season 31 begins on November 3rd, so get the squad together and get ready to jump into Apex Legends: Cipher!

Starting in Cipher, we’re launching Legend Banning in Diamond+ matches, allowing you to vote for Legends to be banned from selection for each match. Scoring adjustments for wide-skill matches will also be live at season start and will adjust entry costs if your Rank is too far above or below the median lobby rank.

What the notes say

  • Scoring adjustments for wide-skill matches will also be live at season start and will adjust entry costs if your Rank is too far above or below the median lobby rank.
  • Akimbo Wingman joins the weapon roster as a formidable foe at close and mid-range, leaving Legends’ bodies in its wake.
  • Find them in ground loot, or at Heavy Arsenals, and gear up to become the fastest gun-slinger in the Apex Games.
  • In addition to our existing Care Packages and Replicators, these new Structures will airdrop onto the map near the mid game.
  • Strongholds are locked, bunker-style structures that deploy dynamic cover and contain valuable loot.
  • Players must occupy and control the zone surrounding the Stronghold to capture them.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
