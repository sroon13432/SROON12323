BATTLEFIELD 6 GAME UPDATE 2.1.2.0

Battlefield™ 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·9 November 2026·Source

Battlefield 6 Game Update 2.1.2.0, Casino Heist, brings the new seasonal Leaderboards system, expanded M142 MRAS deployment across Conquest maps, additional small modes on Vegas Strip in Battlefield 6, and a broad weapon and attachment balance pass alongside new attachment options across the arsenal. This update also delivers quality-of-life improvements, Portal additions, audio and UI polish, and bug fixes across Multiplayer and REDSEC.

Welcome to the European, a world-class casino and resort, featuring a shopping arcade, food court, and so much more. At the European, there’s something for every squad: Create your own flanks with fully-destructible facades, engage in close-quarters combat on the casino floor, take a sniping post on a balcony, or relax and rush the lazy river trenches.

What the notes say

  • Casino Heist brings the fight on the Strip to the casino floor in high-stakes battles in Conquest and Escalation.
  • Complete contracts to earn DOUGH, a new limited-time currency to redeem for private security and casino-themed cosmetics and rewards.
  • The NSW4 is a highly modular, short-range carbine, extensively customizable for every combat situation.
  • With default subsonic ammo, the NSW4 is the perfect weapon for staying under the radar when firing.
  • Utilize its full potential by mastering both combat and weapon knowledge.
  • The Signal Jammer is a recon’s secret weapon for eliminating the enemy’s reinforcements and intel, including distorting maps, killfeed, objective status, and enemy location.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
