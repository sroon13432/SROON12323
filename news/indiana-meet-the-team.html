Indiana: Meet the Team

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

Today, we would like our #BestCommunityEver to meet the team working on the upcoming Indiana DLC for American Truck Simulator! They have all prepared a short message for you, sharing a little bit about their background and what they are currently working on for the Hoosier State.

If you've missed it, we have previously also shown you a blog with a glimpse from the research trip to Indiana, where a part of the team went on to take in the inspiration for creating this DLC. And now, it's time to meet the rest of them!

What the notes say

  • Now, together with my awesome team, we are officially moving our focus to Indiana!
  • Players can look forward to enjoying not only the beautiful countryside with sweeping views of scenic farmland, but also exploring the heart of the state - its vibrant capital, Indianapolis!
  • I can't wait for everyone to experience what we're creating."
  • I've worked on many projects, such as the Kansas, Illinois, Texas, Missouri, and Road Trip DLCs, as well as many others.
  • I specialize primarily in designing truck stops, small and large gas stations, car washes, truck washes, rest areas, and weigh stations.
  • I'm currently working on Indiana, where I'm in charge of the largest city in the entire DLC.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
