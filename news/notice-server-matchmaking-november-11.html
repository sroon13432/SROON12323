[Notice] Server Matchmaking | November 11

AION 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

The next round of matchmaking is available with a change: Advanced Access (Early Access) and Launch servers can now be paired together for all global regions. We evaluated the matching quality over the last two weeks and have determined that some Launch servers are best positioned to be competitive with Advanced Access servers. Please keep in mind that we will continue to monitor the pairs over the next two weeks and reevaluate matches for all future pairings.

No. We will update server pairings periodically to keep the combat balanced. For this reason, the same faction may now be matched together (Example: Asmodian vs. Asmodian). Today's pairing may be replaced by a new challenger down the road, so make your mark while you can. Good luck Daevas!
