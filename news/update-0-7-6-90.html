Update 0.7.6.90

Enlisted · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

Adjusted the weapons of the 50th Infantry Division: replaced the SVT-40 of 3 riflemen with Mosin M91/30 rifles and replaced the PTRS-41 Scoped of 2 AT gunners with PTRS-41.

Pz.III J has been moved to the 19th Panzer Division and it now costs 600 points (replacing the Pz.III B, which cost 900 previously).

What the notes say

  • Increased the starting reinforcement points for Germany.
  • Increased the reinforcement point rewards for capturing points.
  • Reduced the capture time of the first point.
  • Pz.III B has been moved to the 2nd Panzer Division and it is now free.
  • Pz.III J has been moved to the 19th Panzer Division and it now costs 600 points (replacing the Pz.III B, which cost 900 previously).
  • Reduced the cost of Pz.IV F1 from 1500 to 1000 points.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
