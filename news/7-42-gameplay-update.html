7.42 Gameplay Update

Dota 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

Added unit ability hotkey settings for Lycan's Summon Wolves, Kobold Soldier, Giant Wolf, Radigull, Odobenus Frostmage, Eimermole Foreman, Ancient Croaker, Ancient Marshmage, and Ancient Ogre Seal

Fixed being able to sell an Aghanim's Shard the same frame it is applied when transferring an Aghanim's Shard from a courier to a hero with a full inventory

What the notes say

  • Added functionality to bind hotkeys for individual abilities on Invoker's Invoked Spells, Largo's songs, and Kez's Sai abilities
  • Added unit ability hotkey settings for Lycan's Summon Wolves, Kobold Soldier, Giant Wolf, Radigull, Odobenus Frostmage, Eimermole Foreman, Ancient Croaker, Ancient Marshmage,
  • Added option to show silhouettes for units when they are visually obstructed
  • Added Hand of Midas as its own category in Dota+ Gold Received Breakdowns
  • Improved client performance when spawning illusions
  • Fixed some stat gathering code to be more performant on server, especially around hero deaths

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
