Hotfix 1.15.1 is now LIVE! - Not for Problem Reports!

Victoria 3 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

Today's hotfix brings the first set of bugfixes, balancing and improvements since the release of Update 1.15 and State and Revolution! Some highlights include: Regime Change now allowing the target country to adopt normally unavailable laws as long as the country doing the regime change has those laws enacted, Copper being both consumed less and being more available, more AI improvements and more!

Regime Change now allows the target country to adopt normally unavailable laws such as Council Republic so long as the country imposing the law change has that particular law enacted

What the notes say

  • Regime Change will now establish friendly relations with the target country.
  • Any IGs that were put into power by the regime change will join a lobby in favor of the imposing country, while any IGs forced out of power will join a lobby opposing that country.
  • Revoking Favoritism character interaction can now only be taken once every 5 years.
  • Appoint Favorite character interaction can now be taken once every 6 months.
  • A White victory in the Whites and Reds Journal Entry guarantees Russia cannot decrease the autonomy of Congress Poland or use the Install Favored Ruler Overlord Action for 10 years
  • The AI now correctly counts the wages of new jobs as a cost when picking production methods, and counts only workers who are actually laid off as wage savings

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
