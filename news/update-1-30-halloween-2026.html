Update 1.30 - Halloween 2026

Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

Halloween Showdown 2026 is a three-week event built around community cooperation. Inspired by last year’s celebration, each week brings a new pair of shared goals, presented as a fight between two characters, with separate rewards for each side. Everyone’s contributions count toward the weekly totals, so work together to unlock the rewards - and choose which character you want to back.

Night changes Villedor. The streets grow harder to read, every distant sound feels a little closer, and somewhere in the static, a signal is trying to break through.

What the notes say

  • This Halloween, turn up the noise - and listen to what answers.
  • Let’s dive into what we’ve prepared for you in this patch.
  • Villedor is dressed for the occasion, with Día de los Muertos decorations across the open world and a traditional Halloween look in Tower Raid.
  • Special Halloween-themed enemies appear in both areas throughout the event and drop Halloween Candies, which you can spend at Baka’s Event Shop.
  • Each week, featured enemies also begin appearing in encounters and remain afterward; while they’re the week’s targets, defeating them in the open world earns you Showdown Candies.
  • You can find more Showdown Candies in Halloween Chests - 40 during the day or 80 at night - and spend them at Baka’s to support either side of the weekly matchup.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
