American Truck Simulator: 1.62 Update Release

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

You can now find brand-new Tourist Boards to uncover in Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, Colorado, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Texas, Washington, and Louisiana, with one Tourist Board added to each of these states, as well as Nebraska, which receives two Tourist Boards; a new postcard will be awarded for every one of these completed!

Before we get into the details of the new features, we would like to send a huge thank you to everyone who participated in the Open Beta and helped report any bugs or other issues, enabling us to fine-tune the update. Now, let's take a look at what's in store!

What the notes say

  • If you have been enjoying the new Atlas feature in the recently released Road Trip: Ford DLC, we have something more for you!
  • If you don't own all these map expansions, you can still explore the locations available in the states you own.
  • Completing this Cross-Border Tourist Board will also reward you with a special Route 66 Air Freshener accessory!
  • We are continuing to expand the Improved Material System with this update, bringing it to more truck interiors.
  • We are updating the Goodyear Tires Pack paint jobs to reflect the company's updated branding.
  • The latest Goodyear logo and slightly adjusted colours are now featured across the paint jobs, bringing them in line with the current design.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
