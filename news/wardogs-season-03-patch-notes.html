WARDOGS | Season 03 Patch Notes

WARDOGS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

Sabatulo is WARDOGS' first city map, packed with tight streets, towering high-rises and plenty of destruction, alongside key locations such as a central hospital, swimming pool, power station and the river embankment. Sabatulo focuses on a mix of urban firefights, close-quarters combat and verticality. Fight room to room, street to street, or take the high points for oversight of the city.

Assault rifle torso damage is back to Season 1 values, and LMG torso damage now matches assault rifles. Both deal more torso damage than in the Season 2 update:
