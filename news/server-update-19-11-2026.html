Server Update 19.11.2026

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

For some time the win rate of the “blue” team at top and near top Battle Rating brackets has been higher than the “red” team, while on lower BRs the contrary was the case, where the “red” team had higher win rates due to Yak-130 with guided bombs being too effective. Thus, the following balance changes have been made based on the combined data of these matches:

Pseudo-RCS of stealth munitions like the AGM-154 and small munitions like the GBU-39 has been increased, so the AI-controlled air defense now engages them at a longer range. Short-range air defense systems now also shoot such munitions down not only in self-defense but also to defend nearby objects.

What the notes say

  • AI-controlled Pantsir-S1s now fire missiles at targets up to 12 km away instead of 20 km, the same as the ItO 90M (Crotale NG).
  • From Battle Rating 13.3 and above, the OTOMATIC and 2S38 gun SPAA have been removed from airfield air defenses.
  • Airfields are now defended by two Pantsir-S1, two 2S6, and two HQ11 SAM systems for one team, while the other is protected by four ItO 90M and two SPYDER AIO SAM systems.
  • — Battle Rating in Naval and Ground Battles has been reduced from 9.3 to 9.0 and from 10.3 to 9.0 respectively.
  • Battle Rating in Air Arcade Battles has been reduced from 9.7 to 9.3.
  • The use of KAB-500Kr guided bombs increases the Battle Rating to 10.3 in Naval and Ground Battles as well as Air Realistic Battles and to 10.7 in Air Arcade Battles.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
