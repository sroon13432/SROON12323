100 hours with GTA 6: A sprawling adventure that will be so much better on PC

PC Gamer · industry report·18 November 2026·Source

Grand Theft Auto 6 is not on PC so I can't review it. But somehow I, a member of PC Gamer, got access to GTA 6 before it launched. For the past two weeks I've secretly sunk approximately 100 hours in it (on something called a PlayStation 5) and now, finally, I can talk about it. What a relief.

So this isn't a review, but I can give a verdict of sorts:

More from the report

  • It's (mostly) great. But it will be (mostly) so much greater when it comes to PC.
  • I guess I shouldn't be surprised by just how massive and detailed the world is. It's Rockstar: huge open worlds is what they do, and they've had a decade to do it.
  • I'm constantly impressed by the macro and micro of it all. I can step back and take in the sheer size and scale of Leonida: the archipelago representing the Florida keys to the south,
  • I've lost hours doing nothing but wandering around the world.
