One week from launch, GTA 6's physical map reportedly leaks online thanks to the £350 collector's edition on show in a UK department store

Eurogamer · industry report·13 November 2026·Source

It was reportedly snapped during Selfridge's sizable GTA 6 's takeover, and is seemingly the physical map included in Rockstar's £350 collector's edition.
