Get PCE-842 in the Coastal Defender Event!

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

The PCE-842 class was a type of patrol escort vessel built for the United States Navy during the Second World War. It is the only class that the U.S. Navy officially designated as “patrol craft escort”. These ships were intended to carry out coastal patrol duties, anti-submarine warfare, and even the escorting of convoys in coastal waters. A total of 68 vessels of this class were constructed, and they went on to serve in the navies of several countries around the world. Take part in the new naval event to earn the PCE-842, a U.S. Coast Guard flag, and themed decals!

In the game, the PCE-842 is a rank IV event vessel for the US Coastal fleet. Being quite large and slow, it excels at holding control points; it is armed with a 76 mm bow-mounted gun, six 40 mm Bofors, and four 20 mm Oerlikons. But the most interesting feature of the PCE-842 is its depth charge armament! In addition to standard depth charges that roll off the stern, the ship is equipped with depth charge projectors that launch charges off the sides and ahead of the vessel!
