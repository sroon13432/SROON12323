Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra is Now Available!

Path of Exile · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Upon defeating them, you will get to select a Fate for the boss of the area you're in. Each Fate has a handful of effects, increasing both the rewards and difficulty of the encounter.

Our November expansion introduces a new challenge league, a set of fundamental changes to the endgame alongside improvements to existing endgame systems, new unique items, and more!

What the notes say

  • Mirages have returned, with a chance to spawn randomly in Maps - or as a rollable Map modifier along with other mechanics like Mirrors of Delirium or the Viridian Wildwood.
  • Slaying Beyond Bosses no longer stops monster Beyond portals from spawning. Sometimes when you defeat a Beyond boss, they'll open a portal to their realm.
  • The Sacred Grove no longer appears in Maps, instead being replaced with tangled roots. You can even use currency on them to add modifiers.
  • Gwennen now offers an entirely new service, but her gambling days are far from over. She just gambles on your items now!
  • Atziri has moved into the Temple of Atzoatl, kicking out the Vaal Omnitect, and she has made several adjustments.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
