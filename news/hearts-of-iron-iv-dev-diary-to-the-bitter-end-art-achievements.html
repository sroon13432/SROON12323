What the notes say

  • I myself have played a significant part in the research, being the one closest to the source material.
  • When it came to the making of the art, I have tried including as many references to actual historical events, locations, or figures, without further ado let's delve into the bread.
  • For both the national spirits and the focus icons, I personally scoured museums here in Bucharest, along with online and physical archives, for reference material.
  • Same faces, better graphics, plus a glimpse into their creation process (and yes, Romania is getting scientists too!).
  • Notice how each portrait starts with an initial source photo, paired with a few color and uniform references (while a much larger pile of sources is kept in a separate document).
