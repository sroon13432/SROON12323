Update 0.7.6.73

Enlisted · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

- Adjusted the weapons left out of Making Enlisted a Better Place №75:

What the notes say

  • Adjusted the weapons left out of Making Enlisted a Better Place №75:
  • PPSh-41 (S) (Premium): Shots deviation 73 -> 55.
  • M1 carbine with grenade thrower: Ammo count 15/60 -> 15/105.
  • Captured MP 40 (Premium): Rate of fire 570 -> 630.
  • Added a nickname decorator for 5 years of continuous logins.
  • Fixed the missing hands of Emelyan Barykin.
