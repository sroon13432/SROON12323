Fortress Mode Now Has Audio(!!) + Myth & Magic progress 🎶 Patch 53.17

Dwarf Fortress · posted by the game's developer on Steam·9 November 2026·Source

We wanted to update you on the progress of Myth & Magic. We were hoping to have the update out by the end of the year but unfortunately we won’t be able to reach that goal. Tarn has run into significant issues with the graphics engine and myth generation and we want to take the time to do it right. There is also an update to the graphics engine in progress which will allow us to include all the special magical effects the artists have been working on.

There’s been a good amount of progress to Myth & Magic already! We’ve got new wizard equipment in a variety of different styles. Wizards will come to your fort if your dwarves are able to entice them.

What the notes say

  • One of the main ways to do this is by discovering ancient ruins underground which will allow them to study new magic and give you access to their powers.
  • You will also be able to build magical workshops associated with their magical arts.
  • There will be a variety of different workshops associated with the types of magic that could appear in your world.
  • Take a guess at the different magic types based on these workshops:
  • Each wizard will progress through their path of knowledge with the research web, first in history during world generation and then in your fortress.
  • There's going to be a bunch of spells wizards can use in combat, but beware because your enemies will also have magic on their side as well.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
