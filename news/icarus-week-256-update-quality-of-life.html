Icarus Week 256 Update | Quality of Life

ICARUS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

A long time ago, back in the Week 65 update, we added the "Search" bar functionality for several UI elements, including the Tech Tree. This feature highlighted unlocks that matched your search and while that was initially very helpful, the continued expansion of the tech trees in Icarus have led us to improving this functionality further. Now, when you search in the tech tree, it will pan the tree to the first found match which makes it much easier to locate what you might be looking for.

This week focuses on a range of quality-of-life improvements, including search functionality in the Tech Tree, quick-access buttons in the Field Guide, and an important creature spawning fix.

What the notes say

  • Railings can now snap to all full sized Ramps, not just Stairs
  • Elysium Terrain has had a pass to prevent spawning of creatures on rocks outside the map
  • Structure for the Elysium quest Lost will now cleanup if the server setting is enabled
  • The durability of the Alteration benches have been increased to fully reflect their tier
  • Fixed non-varying damage amounts displaying as a range when it isn't
  • The Lava Hunter and Sandwyrm Queen bestiary points have been reduced to bring them in line with the other bosses

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
