AMD says it 'loves gamers' only weeks after proclaiming it is a datacenter-first company

PC Gamer · industry report·18 November 2026·Source

AMD landed itself in hot water with gamers a couple of weeks back by saying the quiet part out loud: CEO Dr. Lisa Su announced that it was a "datacentre-first company" in a call with investors, who were likely to want to hear that sorta thing. Yet upon reaching gamers' ears, the news wasn't so well received.

Now, AMD Japan has tried to settle things down by proclaiming "AMD loves gamers."

More from the report

  • To be fair, those two statements aren't mutually exclusive. AMD can be datacentre first, and absolutely is, and still claim to care about gaming.
  • What makes matters worse is that AMD Japan also added: "AMD values its interactions with gamers above all else."
  • In Q3, AMD made more than double the cash from its datacentre segment ($8.5 billion) than its client and gaming segment ($3.7 billion)—the latter includes various Ryzen, as well as Radeon,
  • So, from a purely business perspective, AMD is undoubtedly all in on datacentres. I don't like to admit it,
