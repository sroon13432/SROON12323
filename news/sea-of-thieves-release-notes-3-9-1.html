Sea of Thieves Release Notes – 3.9.1

Sea of Thieves: 2026 Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

Custom Seas expands its collection of Legendary Locations with the return of Golden Sands Outpost, restored to its pre-Lost Sands appearance and available as a dedicated location for custom sessions.

The Devil’s Shroud surrounds the island, keeping crews focused on the action. Due to the unique nature of the location, some World Events, creatures and island-specific features will be unavailable.

What the notes say

  • The Devil’s Shroud surrounds the island, keeping crews focused on the action.
  • Due to the unique nature of the location, some World Events, creatures and island-specific features will be unavailable.
  • Within Emergent World Events, Sea Forts can now be disabled from becoming an emergent encounter during a session.
  • While disabled, all Sea Forts will remain accessible but without posing a threat or offering treasure.
  • Within Emergent World Events, Ashen Garrison encounters can now be disabled during a session.
  • While disabled, Ashen Garrisons will remain locked; however, creators can spawn Keys to allow players access.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
