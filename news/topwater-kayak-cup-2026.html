Topwater Kayak Cup 2026!

Fishing Planet · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

The Topwater Kayak Tournament is coming to Fishing Planet, and it's time to put your fishing skills to the test! Catch fish from your kayak using spinning or casting rods with topwater lures. The winner of each Round will be determined by the total weight of 10 fish in their Keepnet.

Remember, everyone who registers for the Tournament will receive Pond Passes to all participating waterways: Falcon Lake, Selenge River, San Joaquin Delta, Saint-Croix Lake, and Maku-Maku Lake, so you can join the competition even if you haven't unlocked these locations yet!

What the notes say

  • Check out the Topwater Kayak Pack, featuring special lures, an exclusive kayak, outfit, and fishing gear to help you prepare for the Tournament!
  • Please review our Competitive Activity Rules before participating.
  • We’re always happy to see families enjoying Fishing Planet together!
  • Just a little reminder: when joining Tournaments or Competitions, each player must have their own device.
  • If you share one, only one family member can take part.
