Announcing the DCS: MiG-23M from Ugra Media

DCS World Steam Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 October 2026·Source

We're excited to announce the DCS: MiG-23M Flogger, now in development by Ugra Media, the team behind the DCS: Syria and DCS: Cold War Germany maps. Plans include a full-fidelity cockpit, the Sapfir-23D radar, and R-23 and R-60 air-to-air missiles. We are really excited to announce this iconic Redfor aircraft and can’t wait to see it in action. Learn more below.

We're also taking a closer look at the real MiG-23M. This variant gave the Flogger a true look-down/shoot-down radar and became the Soviet Air Force's main air superiority fighter of the 1970s, with its first frontline regiments based in East Germany. Learn more below.

What the notes say

  • Command T put the DCS: L-39 Albatros to the test against the real L-39, and to go with his video, the DCS: L-39 Albatros is 40% off on the E-Shop with the code CTL39.
  • After seven years, the 4YA Training Servers will close on November 1st.
  • Following last week's announcement of the DCS: MiG-27K, the Flogger family in DCS is growing.
  • We're excited to announce that Ugra Media, the team behind the DCS: Normandy 2.0, DCS: Syria, and DCS: Cold War Germany maps, is developing the DCS: MiG-23M Flogger.
  • This is Ugra Media's first aircraft module, and taking on an aircraft as iconic as the MiG-23 is no small step.
  • Before work could begin, the team had to meet the standards we set for every full-fidelity DCS module, and they have.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
