Alexander, Warrior Jar Artisan Keycap has arrived!

ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree) · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

Designed as a 1U keycap (20.61x8.757x26.61mm) for R1 placement, Alexander is compatible with MX-style switches. He is made from a high-quality PU resin using a silicon mold and finished with hand-painted details.

. He is the perfect companion for any Tarnished looking to add a little strength, courage, and jar-shaped charm to their setup.

What the notes say

  • High-quality PU resin construction
  • Silicone- molded with hand-painted details
  • Compatible with MX-style switches
  • Exclusive to the Bandai Namco Store

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
