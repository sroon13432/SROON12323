Patch 2.2 – Buttoned-Up and Beautiful Update

BattleBlock Theater® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Months ago we released BattleBlock Theater’s Quality of Death Update, the follow up to the Quality of Life Remaster, which technically was the follow up to… the game. We truly appreciate all the feedback we’ve received from everyone since launch. We have continued to listen, tinker and tweak the theater, and today we’re thrilled to present the Buttoned-Up and Beautiful Update! This one contains some finishing touches on some legacy requests as well as brings one of the oldest feature requests into the spotlight. The theater is buttoned-up and looking gorgeous. Not to mention, there’s a ton of bug fixes, voila!

Are you tired of seeing your 100% progress? Well look no further, performers! With this new handy-dandy reset progress button you never have to worry about that hideous A++ taking up beautiful real estate again! Buckle your pants for this one folks, RESET PROGRESS is now available at a theater near you! (psst, located in the Settings Menu)
