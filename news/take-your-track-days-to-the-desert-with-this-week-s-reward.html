Take Your Track Days to the Desert with this Week's Reward

Forza Horizon 5 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Rally isn’t only in this car’s name; you can also find traces of what this 911 was meant to do all over its body. To start, the Rallye’s suspension has been lifted by 2 inches, and an extra 1.2 inches can be added whenever needed. Look at this Porsche’s shoes, and you’ll find a set of Pirelli Scorpion tires built to be used on any terrain.

Not all tracks are created equally; some of them are filled with dirt and stuff that’s, well, not tarmac. However, you will be pleased to know that your off-roading track day experience won’t be limited to regular roads, as the 2023 Porsche 911 Rallye is now available to unlock until November 19.

What the notes say

  • With the same 3.0-liter flat-six engine that you can find inside a Carrera GTS, the Rallye can reach 100 kph in just over 3 seconds.
  • With a fantastic engine, all-wheel drive, and a complete off-road package, the Rallye is ready to take on any road imaginable and become a champion.
  • Round and round you’ll go to the Oval Track taking over the Stadium once more during all four weeks of Horizon Track Day.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
