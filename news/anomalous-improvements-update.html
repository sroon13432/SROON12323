Anomalous Improvements Update

Black Mesa · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

Experience a major visual upgrade with local, room-accurate reflections. Parallax-corrected cubemaps bring dynamic interior depth and realistic specular reflections to both the Black Mesa Research Facility and Xen like never before!

We fine-tuned the engine to deliver significantly better performance across the entire game! Enjoy higher frame rates, smoother gameplay, and solid stability across every chapter, including a fully optimized Xen.

What the notes say

  • Enjoy higher frame rates, smoother gameplay, and solid stability across every chapter, including a fully optimized Xen.
  • Jump straight into the action with a streamlined, clutter-free UI built from the ground up for speed and responsiveness, and plug & play controller support!
  • We now have full native layout and glyph support for Xbox, PlayStation, and Steam Controllers, alongside refined mouse and keyboard navigation.
  • Full UI modding support is also now live via the Black Mesa Steam Workshop.
  • If you take Black Mesa out into the wild, snap a pic and share it with us!
  • We’d love to see where in the world (or Borderworld) you play!
