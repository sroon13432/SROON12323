October 27, 2026 Hotfix #4

PlanetSide 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

A new client build will be updated 10/27/26. All servers will be down for the scheduled maintenance at 6am pdt. Please see the patch notes below. There will be a 3hr downtime window.

As previously announced, we’re continuing to celebrate our victory over client crashes with a new giveaway! This time, we’ve decided to distribute the rewards through a Directive.

What the notes say

  • To receive the reward, simply log into the game, deploy to an active continent, and take at least one physical step in the game world.
  • If you stepped away from PlanetSide 2 due to client crashes, now is a great time to return!
  • Enjoy Double XP, the ongoing sales, and our latest giveaway as we celebrate a more stable game together.
  • PlanetSide 2’s 14th Anniversary is just around the corner, bringing new content and even more gifts for our community.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
