DevBlog #74

The Isle · posted by the game's developer on Steam·31 October 2026·Source

This month I was mostly looking at oviraptor and fixing issues for the hordetest. Unfortunately not much to talk about for a lot of it. There was the implementation of the oviraptor dodge and then a small update to the speed calculations for it to make it work properly for all growth stages. The tenontosaurus hillslide was one of the rare instances of something that worked in editor, but always broke in builds. It took some time to track down but it is resolved now.

Outside of implementing the last of oviraptor and trying to resolve the remaining few of its bugs, this month was almost entirely focused on fixing issues found in HordeTest. Some standout issues that had to be fixed were zones breaking when toggling to individual or species migrations, icons not showing correctly for migration area types, plants sometimes spawning inside one another and food would on a few occasions respawn when it was not supposed to (which was related to the coconuts not respawning at all).

What the notes say

  • More work was done with the entomb character saving to fix persistence issues with skins and prime growth scales.
  • Adjusted eggs to be only eaten if dino is an egg eater (only oviraptor for now).
  • Also fixed some hatchling interaction issues and female-only mutations being inherited by males.
  • Last is the work done for footstep sounds and VFX transitioning seamlessly based on size and weight is done.
  • Now we are moving that tech to update the slide and hillslides, with the inclusion of snow surface type.
  • I’ve been busy on bugfixes and working on a few new tools for the team.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
