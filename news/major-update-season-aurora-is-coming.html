Major Update: Season Aurora Is Coming!

Delta Force · posted by the game's developer on Steam·7 November 2026·Source

At 01:59 UTC+0 on November 10, the Operations entrance and matchmaking mode will be closed, and the Market and Auction House functions will be disabled.

At 01:59 UTC+0 on November 10, the Operations entrance and matchmaking mode will be closed, and the Market and Auction House functions will be disabled.

What the notes say

  • Martelo deploys the Ariete and charges forward continuously.
  • Damage taken from the front is reduced during the charge, and it ends upon colliding with an enemy or wall, triggering a Heavy Strike that damages and knocks down enemies in range.
  • Martelo can also end the charge early by leaping and slamming the Ariete into the ground ahead, dealing damage and knocking down enemies in range.
  • Fires multiple smoke grenades from a shoulder-mounted launcher around the operator, creating a halo of smoke upon detonation.
  • Hold to enter single-shot mode, then release after selecting a target location to fire a smoke grenade that creates a full smokescreen covering the entire area.
  • The grenade begins a detonation countdown after being thrown and explodes after a few seconds.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
