Arena Breakout: Infinite Halloween Event

Arena Breakout: Infinite · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

It's almost that time of year again when everyone grabs a jack-o'-lantern and heads out into the night... 🎃 (Yes, we're talking about Halloween!) During the Halloween period, the land of Kamona will be joining in on the spooky festivities as well! We hope every Operator has a "spooktacular" night! Mwahahaha!

Start from Oct 29, take the Unripe Festive Pumpkin into the raid, and evolve them to the Miraculous Festive Pumpkin. Unlock your jack-o'-lantern collection!

What the notes say

  • Purchase Unripe Pumpkins for 100 Koen each, with a daily purchase limit of 2.
  • Each pumpkin occupies a 2×2 space in your inventory and cannot be stored in the Secure Case.
  • Bring Unripe Pumpkins into Forbidden Zone and Lockdown Zone raids and successfully extract to earn EXP toward their evolution.
  • EXP is calculated based on the total value of loot extracted.
  • For every 10,000 Koen worth of extracted loot, you will earn 2 EXP.
  • You can also pick up enemy Operators' pumpkins and extract with them.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
