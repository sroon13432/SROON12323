Stellaris Dev Diary #440 - The Inquisitor Shipset and Galactic Halls

Stellaris · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

If you’re curious about how Ideologies will work in the upcoming Willpower expansion, check out our previous few Dev Diaries. Today, we’re shining a spotlight on the work of our artists at Abrakam, with a closer look at the Inquisitor shipset and Galactic Halls.

When we began work on Willpower, we knew we wanted a shipset that captured the idea of “spreading a message throughout the stars.” What does that actually look like, though?

What the notes say

  • It took a while to translate that idea into a physical form that felt at home in Stellaris.
  • You’ll notice recurring themes of light, radiation, verticality, and spectacle.
  • For the Galactic Halls, we also wanted each design to embody the spirit of its ethic.
  • That was perhaps an even harder task… what does Xenophilia look like, for example?
  • One thing that’s harder to convey in a blog post is how the Galactic Halls, well… sound.
  • Their ambience has a haunting resonance, almost like chanting, that adds to their sense of grandeur.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
