Trump supports Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO, but it doesn't sound like the board is interested

PC Gamer · industry report·16 November 2026·Source

A CNBC report says the return of disgraced former CEO Jensen Huang is not being considered by Nvidia's board of directors, despite a rising chorus of billionaire voices calling for it—including, earlier today, that of US president Donald Trump.

Asked about US intelligence reports that Huang was having an affair with a suspected Chinese spy, Trump said he doesn't know whether she's a spy, and that "a lot of people" are asking Huang to return to the CEO's role.

More from the report

  • "They think Jensen should go back," Trump said. "That certainly isn't a bad idea." He also said that US national security was not put at risk by Huang's deep ties to Trump,
  • Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia on November 12, shortly after his affairs with a woman suspected of working for Chinese intelligence as well as multiple Nvidia employees came to light.
  • Whether Nvidia feels the same is another matter: An anonymous source told CNBC that the board is not considering bringing him back,
  • US treasury secretary Scott Bessent, one of Trump's most vocal and reliable supporters, also recently seemed to dismiss the idea,
