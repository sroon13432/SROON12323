Work in Progress: Update 15.11: Round 2

World of Warships · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Because the update is still being tested, the information in this article is tentative and reflects the state of game development at the time of its publication. Some changes and new features may be removed entirely or implemented differently by the time the update goes live.

This December, a 6-week-long holiday season update hits the game, bringing Santa's Gifts galore, Snowflakes, a free Tier IX ship on first login, two distinct Event Passes, and a historical adventure themed around the 1941 raid on Pearl Harbor.

What the notes say

  • Balance changes to the Auxiliary and Torpedo Weaponry consumable and Concentrated AA Fire have been disabled.
  • We have applied the following balance changes to the Horror From the Abyss operations:
  • In Daring Raid (formerly known as Vanished Expedition), Gathering Interdiction (formerly known as Madness Crusade), Hidden Snare (formerly known as Origin of Terror) and Grave Threat,
  • In Daring Raid (formerly known as Vanished Expedition) and Hidden Snare (formerly known as Origin of Terror) the enemy spawn rate has been increased.
  • In Gathering Interdiction (formerly known as Madness Crusade), the number of enemy ships that must be destroyed to complete the main task has been increased: 14 to 18.
  • In Grave Threat, increased Oblivion's shields regeneration rate while monuments are active.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
