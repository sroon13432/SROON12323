Counter-Strike 2 Update

Counter-Strike 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Purchase a Singapore 2026 Viewer Pass to receive an upgradable Singapore 2026 Coin. With an active pass, you can upgrade your coin and earn rewards by playing the Pick'Em Challenge. Additionally, you will show up on the Active Pass Leaderboard where you can compare your Pick'Em Challenge performance to your friends.

The PGL Singapore 2026 Major Hub is now available. Visit the hub to purchase tournament items, play the Pick'Em Challenge, and more.

What the notes say

  • The PGL Singapore 2026 Major Hub is now available. Visit the hub to purchase tournament items, play the Pick'Em Challenge, and more.
  • Purchase a Singapore 2026 Viewer Pass to receive an upgradable Singapore 2026 Coin. With an active pass, you can upgrade your coin and earn rewards by playing the Pick'Em Challenge.
  • Added Cobblestone map to official Casual, Competitive, Wingman, Retakes, and Deathmatch matchmaking.
  • Removed Boulder, Fachwerk, and Shelter from official matchmaking pool.
  • Added 8 new music kits from Monstercat.
  • Fixed head dipping too low at extreme aim angles.
