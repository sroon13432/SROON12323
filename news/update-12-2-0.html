Update 12.2.0

THE FINALS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

This week we’re bringing a refreshed Store, more info about TGM26 and some balance changes. You still have a few more days to redeem any tickets you may have left before the Frights Galore Event closes! We have more coming, so stay tuned.. and watch your head.

The Grand Major 2026 is coming up, and all Contestants are invited! We have seen your passion, online and in person last year, so this time we’re hosting our very own

What the notes say

  • Every Contestant participating in the event will also receive a participation Diploma and Multibucks.
  • We’re making some tweaks today to some of the Medium’s automatic and semi-auto weapons.
  • These are aimed at two problems: Improving the Medium’s chances when dueling versus the now 175 HP Lights, and providing Mediums with more viable tracking aim options in their weapon pool.
  • We feel these buffs to Medium will be similarly handled by the Heavy, but will allow Medium versus Light match-ups to be a little more balanced.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
