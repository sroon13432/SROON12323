Intel hit with class action alleging it 'misled investors' – company's recent downturn due in part to how darn expensive PCs are right now

PC Gamer · industry report·16 November 2026·Source

Hot on the heels of a monumental share price drop for the company, Intel now faces a class action lawsuit from its own shareholders.

This is according to an alert from law firm Robbins LLP, which recently informed all investors. This suit alleges "Intel misled investors about demand for its products and the Company's future growth potential."

More from the report

  • The class action can involve anyone who bought stock between July 23, 2026, and October 29, 2026, known as the "class period".
  • With the memory crisis in full effect, PC sales are down worldwide ( estimated to be 20% down in October ),
  • It also argues Intel's OEM and distribution partners were left with hardware they couldn't sell to consumers due to rising prices,
  • The suit also alleges that Intel's reporting of its operations and ability to capitalise on the AI boom "were materially false and misleading when made,
