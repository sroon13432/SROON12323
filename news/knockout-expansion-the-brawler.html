Knockout Expansion - The Brawler!

Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

The Brawler is brutal, bad-tempered, and absolutely relentless. Sending him to Hoxxes was honestly the best choice for everyone's safety. With rock-punching fists powered by a portable Morkite reactor on his back, the Brawler is ready to knock out any bugs in his way, no matter their size!

Whether you prefer aggressive in-your-face tactics, fast hit-and-run, or oppressive stone-walling, the Brawler has something for you. A true dwarf, ready for a proper walloping, the Brawler is crashing onto the stage. Think you can control him?
