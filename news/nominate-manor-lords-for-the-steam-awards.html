Nominate Manor Lords for the Steam Awards

Manor Lords · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

The Manor Lords development team started strong last December with Major Update #5, which added four new maps to the game. Each map had a distinct theme and gave players a fresh landscape in which to face new challenges and take on new town-building experiments. This update implemented some frequently requested community features, too, including starting location selection, new buildings, jobs, and resources like the quarry and rough stone. It also revamped the castle upgrade system.

But Manor Lords has made some big strides in development this year, and the team has put an immense amount of effort into putting out more frequent and more impactful updates. Your nomination during this year's awards can help spread the word about Manor Lords, and invite new players to experience the game for themselves.

What the notes say

  • Over the course of the last year, Greg and his team have released four major updates and one additional update.
  • In March, Major Update #6 brought another new map (Twin Lakes), a new burgage plot level, a new family-based progression system, and improved battlefield visibility during combat.
  • The new burgage plot level improved mid-game progression for players and helped the game's pacing feel a bit more historically grounded.
  • In October, Major Update #8 brought even more new content and a brand new production chain for Manor Lords.
  • With these additions, players can now create a full-fledged dairy industry in their towns.
  • Cows and calves can be fed with the new Animal Feed commodity, which requires the new Oats field crop to craft.
