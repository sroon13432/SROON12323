DCS 2.9.31.29434 Update

DCS World Steam Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

When in VIS mode, Relative Targeting is enabled and will use the physical target location based on aircraft sensor position, which may differ from the target coordinates displayed in the cockpit when there are differences between the INS navigation solution and the GPS position.

When in PRE mode, Absolute Targeting is enabled and will use pure GPS navigation to fly to the target coordinates uploaded from the aircraft, provided the bomb time-of-flight is sufficient to acquire GPS signals (>24 seconds) to perform a course correction prior to impact.

What the notes say

  • Known Issue: Absolute Targeting is always enabled in PRE mode, even though a sensor in track mode or applying a cursor slew should enable Relative Targeting.
  • hover TDC over any contact (within radar antenna gimbal limitation) and press T5, radar antenna will turn to desired az/el then SPOT
  • if a selected contact is outside radar antenna gimbal limitation, radar wont SPOT until contact goes inside gimbal limitation within 60sec
  • Updated the external 3D model of the pilot in the cockpit (the 3D model of the pilot after bailing out will be updated in a future update)
  • IMPORTANT: In the Mission Editor, set the radio frequency for all La-7 flights in your missions to a frequency within the 3.7–6.06 MHz range

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
