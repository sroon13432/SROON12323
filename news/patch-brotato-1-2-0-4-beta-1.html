Patch Brotato 1.2.0.4.beta-1

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

More balancing brought to weapons in continuity of the previous patch. Also introduce small changes to some items characters.

What the notes say

  • Boomerang: cooldown halved on every tier (30/28/26/24 -> 15/14/13/12)
  • Whip and Flail: they now aim at the farthest enemy within reach instead of the
  • Flail: moved from the Primitive set to the Heavy set.
  • Crowbar: its damage now scales with Engineering instead of Melee Damage, and it
  • Awl: its dart traps now fire 6/9/12/15 darts depending on the tier, and every tier
  • Sticky Grenade: damage 8/13/19/31 -> 10/15/20/35, Ranged Damage scaling
