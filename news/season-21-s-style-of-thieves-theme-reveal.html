Season 21’s Style of Thieves Theme Reveal!

Sea of Thieves: 2026 Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

now has Outfit Saving! Yes, we are going on about it a bit, but it’s relevant in the context of this article and this contest, let us have it. Style of Thieves has always been focused on inspiring our pirates to wade through their wardrobes and consider combinations of cosmetics that they may not naturally find, were it not for us dangling a pair of gloves as the incentive to do so. But now that Outfit Saving is here, you can enjoy the contest’s established attiring fun, but also save the fits you find that you’d want to wear again in the future!

Quality of life aside, nothing else is changing for #StyleOfThieves this time out, so we’ll start with the standard recap of how the contest works, and how you could claim a pair of the Fashionista’s Finery Gloves for yourself. Posts dedicated to this Season’s themes will appear on
