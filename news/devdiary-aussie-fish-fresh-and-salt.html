DevDiary: Aussie Fish, Fresh and Salt

Fishing Planet · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Follow the river downstream to where the tide brings seawater into the estuary. Some fish stay in fresh water, others thrive in the saltier stretches, and a few make their way between the two as they grow. Take barramundi, for example. Some young barra leave the shelter of the estuary’s mangroves and head upriver, then return downstream as adults to spawn. That’s why you can catch the same species both in the river and estuary, but you might find it at different stages of its life.

As we mentioned in our first DevDiary, Cassowary Coast brings together two fishing areas. You’ll unlock the River Area at level 44 and the Estuary Area at level 78.

What the notes say

  • All visuals shown in this DevDiary showcase a work-in-progress version of the waterway and are subject to change before release.
  • You’ll unlock the River Area at level 44 and the Estuary Area at level 78.
  • Now let’s meet the fish you’ll be chasing in Australia’s tropical north, then take a look at a few upcoming features that’ll make your time on the water a little smoother.
  • And before we get down to the fish species, we are excited to share something else many of you have been asking for.
  • We've added several visual variations for most species at this location, with some fish having up to five different skins!
  • See if you can catch them all and add each variation to your collection.
