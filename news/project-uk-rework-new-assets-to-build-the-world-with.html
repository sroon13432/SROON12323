Project UK Rework: New Assets to Build the World With

Euro Truck Simulator 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·14 November 2026·Source

But how do we decide which buildings make it into the game? It all begins with extensive research! Our research department works closely with the map designers to identify the buildings and architectural styles commonly found in the areas we're recreating. As the map layout takes shape, the team carefully selects the structures that best represent each location. One of our senior map designers has even drawn on their own experiences of living in England to help identify those familiar buildings and details that give British towns and cities their unique character.

What makes a town or city feel unmistakably British? Is it the familiar rows of brick houses, the traditional pubs on street corners, or the charming shops lining a busy high street? For our UK Rework project in Euro Truck Simulator 2, capturing these familiar sights is an important part of bringing Britain to life. That's why our talented teams have been busy creating hundreds of new assets, and today, we'd like to share a closer look at some of them!

What the notes say

  • As you travel across the UK, you'll notice how the architecture changes from one region to another, with each town and city reflecting its own unique character and history.
  • To help capture that authentic British atmosphere in our rework project, our teams have been hard at work creating hundreds of new assets, over 370 of them, in fact!
  • Among these new additions, you'll find a wide range of residential homes, including traditional terraced and semi-detached houses, modern housing developments, and Tudor-style architecture.
  • Of course, no British town would be complete without its local pub!
  • Our artists have also been creating high street shops, restaurants, commercial buildings, and plenty of other familiar sights you'll encounter on your travels.
  • However, creating these assets hasn't been without its challenges!
