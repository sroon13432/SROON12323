DEVLOG - NOVEMBER

Far Far West · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

The whole UI of the game has been reworked! We got tired of the old brown buttons, which looked kinda cheap and clashed with how the game has evolved since release. We were stuck between two different art directions, and it was starting to look like a mess.

We’ve decided to switch to a more modern, more dynamic look, while keeping the sharp edges and that “torn” feeling the previous buttons had. We’ve also taken this opportunity to refine the layout of some UIs and make them clearer than before!

What the notes say

  • We’ve also taken this opportunity to refine the layout of some UIs and make them clearer than before!
  • This lets us add detailed information to many UIs while keeping them clean and uncluttered.
  • For instance, Jokers now tell you how many of them you can stack before it’s useless, or what their diminishing returns are!
  • This is only a sneak peek at all the quality-of-life upgrades we’ve made, and they’ll be covered more thoroughly in the update’s patch notes!
