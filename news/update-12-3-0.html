Update 12.3.0

THE FINALS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

TGM26 is right around the corner and the teams are ready to take the action into the Arena. This week brings the much-awaited Esport Bundles, the new TGM26 Event Pass, the return of the Head Hunter LTM and much more, including some balance changes and bug fixes!

Think you know who’s taking home the trophy? Prove it. TGM26 Pick’em is live, and it’s your chance to call The Grand Major before it happens.

What the notes say

  • TGM26 Pick’em is live, and it’s your chance to call The Grand Major before it happens.
  • Only five of the 16 teams will reach The Throne on Day 3.
  • Pick those five in the order they qualify, predict how the crowns get shared out, then take the Hot Seat with Scotty & June for 10 questions on how the event plays out.
  • Complete all three to lock in your picks and earn an exclusive in-game badge based on your accuracy:
  • Results will be revealed from November 27 to 29 as match results are confirmed.
  • Log in with your Embark ID to take part, and choose wisely, because you only get one submission.
