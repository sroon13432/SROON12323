Warframe: Tau Available December 9

Warframe · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 November 2026·Source

After 13 years of exploring the Origin System, it’s time to venture into an entirely new star chart when Warframe: Tau launches on all platforms! Explore the mafia-riddled metropolis of Fornax and the badlands of Perita, take on new Missions, uncover mysteries and much more.

Explore interconnected hubs throughout the city, from the Lastbloom Lounge to Manifold 90-BF, and discover new factions, enemies and activities along the way.

What the notes say

  • Take to the skies through the Tau Highway Network or explore the streets on foot with the Night Walk, gathering resources and uncovering secrets throughout the metropolis.
  • Make new connections with The Fornax Informer, an all-new Syndicate featuring three distinct factions working together.
  • Work with Larynde at the Lastbloom Lounge, Ikkilyst at Manifold 90-BF and a third Faction you’ll discover during the Tau Quest.
  • Take on new Hub Bounties, earn Standing and unlock unique Rewards.
  • Protect the neighborhoods from The Hunra’s criminal operations in Racket, or cause some chaos of your own in Mayhem by destroying vehicles, cash machines and more.
  • Race through the highways of Fornax, try your luck at Portau or explore additional minigames throughout the city.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
