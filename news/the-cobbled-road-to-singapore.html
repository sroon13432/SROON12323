The Cobbled Road to Singapore

Counter-Strike 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

The Singapore 2026 Major is right around the corner, which means it's time for Tournament items! Major Viewer Passes and Team and Player Autograph stickers are now available for purchase in the Major Shop! As before, 50% of the proceeds go to the players, teams and tournament organizer, with revenue shared based on a team's initial seed and final placement.

Cobblestone veterans will feel at home in familiar locations like drop, chicken coop, and stables. But don't get cold feet, the map has undergone changes and features new pathways and locations for players to master. And it's cold now, so watch your crunchy footsteps as you trudge through the snow to the bombsites.

What the notes say

  • Team and Player Autograph stickers are available in Paper, Foil, Holo, and Gold finishes for a fixed price and can be purchased directly.
  • Souvenirs are craftable for a fixed price as well based on the rarity of the weapon you choose.
  • Purchase and activate a Singapore 2026 Viewer Pass to immediately receive a Bronze Singapore Challenge Coin, Steam.tv chat flair, and unlimited team graffiti for the duration of the event.
  • Upgrade your coin by making picks in the Pick'Em Challenge.
  • Every time you upgrade your coin you'll earn 300 tokens that you can spend on the stickers or souvenirs of your choice.
  • Whether it's a classic Monstercat release from Tristam & Braken or an April Fool's joke gone viral from Noisestorm, there's an offering for everyone!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
