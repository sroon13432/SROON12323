Plug in Digital acquires News Tower publisher Twin Sails Interactive, creating new publishing group to "compete in a highly competitive market"

GamesIndustry.biz · industry report·23 November 2026·Source

French publisher and distributor Plug In Digital has acquired Paris-based indie publisher Twin Sails Interactive - formerly the digital division of boardgame publisher Asmodee, and publisher of News Tower - and announced the two will combine to form an indie-focused distribution and publishing group that combines "Plug In Digital’s global scale with Twin Sails Interactive’s hands-on creative collaboration."
