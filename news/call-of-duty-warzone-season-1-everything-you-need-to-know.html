Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1: Everything You Need to Know

Call of Duty® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

, players will get their hands on new Multiplayer maps and modes, a new area, bosses, and other bonus content in DMZ, plus new weapons, attachments, and more. For full details, check out our Season 1 deep dive into Multiplayer and DMZ.

Season 1 fully integrates Modern Warfare 4 into Call of Duty: Warzone, introducing a fresh leveling experience, an influx of new weapons, new gameplay mechanics, and more.

What the notes say

  • Explore the massive Hajin Battle Royale map, bringing Call of Duty: Warzone to South Korea for the first time in a region narratively linked to the Modern Warfare 4 Campaign.
  • Drop into the new Zodiac Resurgence map, a compact combat zone built around the DRC Advanced Systems drone assembly and manufacturing headquarters.
  • At Season 1 launch, drop into Battle Royale and Resurgence, including Casual variants for each, as well as Rally Point, Massive Resurgence, and Loaded Resurgence.
  • In the Mid-Season, modes expand with the introduction of Extreme Resurgence and Rockets and Snipers.
  • See the revised selection of Tactical and Lethal Equipment items that will be available to equip to your Loadouts, as well as new and returning Field Upgrades and Killstreaks.
  • Whether traveling by land, water, or air, there’s a vehicle for you.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
