Account Restrictions (Nov 19, 14:00 PST / 23:00 CET)

AION 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

To keep AION 2 fair for everyone, we continuously monitor in-game activity and the overall service environment. Following a thorough review, we have restricted a number of accounts found to be in violation of our Operation Policy.

Botting and gold farming: use of macros, bots or other unauthorized programs, and organized play patterns consistent with gold farming or real-money trading (RMT).

What the notes say

  • Botting and gold farming: use of macros, bots or other unauthorized programs, and organized play patterns consistent with gold farming or real-money trading (RMT).
  • Economy abuse: Abnormal trading patterns that disrupt the game economy.
  • Restrictions are only applied after review, in line with the Operation Policy.
  • Normal play, including long grinding sessions, is not affected.
  • Separately from the above, a small number of accounts have been temporarily locked as a precaution.
  • This happens when we detect access from a device or connection that poses a potential security risk to the account, such as a possible account compromise.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
