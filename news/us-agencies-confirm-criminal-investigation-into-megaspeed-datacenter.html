US agencies confirm criminal investigation into Megaspeed - datacenter company is in a colossal tangle over Nvidia chips

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

Megaspeed is a company with a lot of eyes on it at the moment, and many eyes makes many, uh, news stories. The US Department of Commerce and Department of Justice have thought it to be a good time to drop some details on its investigations into the Singaporean datacentre company, once led by one Alice Huang.

If you've already forgotten who Alice Huang is, she was the person first suspected of having an affair with Jensen Huang. The ex-Nvidia CEO later admitted to an affair with someone that has remained unnamed.
