1.28 - Careful reopening

People Playground · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Especially the loss of so much valuable user-generated-content is painful to bear. Many of these mods represented hundreds of hours programming and art by passionate players. It is due to my own inadequacy that these measures had to be taken. I am sorry.

The Workshop is currently safe to use, and the most recent version of the game prevents the loading of mods or any C# code, safety measures do apply, despite contraptions being safe.

What the notes say

  • The changes to the modding structure are here for safety, and despite it not being immediate, resorting to third-party loaders or retrograded versions is not a safe mean of playing the game.
  • Instances of people who have gotten infected from playing the game with such methods have been found, and uploads deleted.
  • " files uploaded onto the workshop, please immediately report them to Steam, and do not open, or download them as they contain user's personal data.
  • The game will continue to have regular safety updates until the Lua system has been implemented.
  • There might be some issues, since this has only really been tested on Debian.
  • Feel free to message me on the Discord server or via email.
