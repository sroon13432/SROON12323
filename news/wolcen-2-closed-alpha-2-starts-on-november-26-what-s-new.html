Wolcen 2 Closed Alpha 2 Starts on November 26! - What's New?

Wolcen: Lords of Mayhem · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Closed Alpha 2 brings a series of improvements to the overall combat feel and pacing, the Combat Mastery, and the Twilight Procession providing more clarity on build paths and easier use, as well as a smoother UI and user experience based on your feedback. With these latest improvements your adventure through a small section of Act I will continue in this playtest.
