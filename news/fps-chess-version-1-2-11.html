FPS Chess - Version 1.2.11

FPS Chess · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

This patch brings last patch's experimental black bishop responsiveness change and applies it to all projectiles. This may cause some instabilities so I'll be looking for user reports this week, but the payoff should be a huge improvement in how the game feels when playing as black in general.

October was a great month for the development of FPS Chess 2 and things are really starting to come together! I can finally afford to give more attention to the original game starting with this small but very important patch.

What the notes say

  • Remember to continue giving me your suggestions for fixes and balance in the official discord or on the steam community discussion page!
