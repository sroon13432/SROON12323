Get ready for the PUBG Global Championship!

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 November 2026·Source

With all 12 PUBG Global Series (PGS) tournaments now concluded, only one event remains to bring the 2026 PUBG Esports season to its grand finale:

At PGC 2026, each team's starting stage is determined by its performance throughout the season. The 32 participating teams are divided into

What the notes say

  • eight direct invites to the Grand Finals, eight direct invites to Survival Stage Day 1, and 16 teams starting in the Qualifying Stage
  • Results from each stage determine which teams advance, with 16 teams ultimately competing in the Grand Finals.
  • This year, the Survival Stage combines daily qualification opportunities with a cumulative points race, making every match crucial to securing a place in the Grand Finals.
  • The Qualifying Stage marks the first challenge of PGC 2026, with 16 teams competing for a stronger starting position in the Survival Stage.
  • Although no teams are eliminated during the Qualifying Stage, their results determine when they enter the Survival Stage, making the opening matches an important part of the competition.
  • On each of the first four days, 16 teams compete, with the highest-scoring team of the day immediately securing a place in the Grand Finals.
