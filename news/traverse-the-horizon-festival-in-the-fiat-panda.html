Traverse the Horizon Festival in the Fiat Panda!

Forza Horizon 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

The charming little box featured above is the 1987 Fiat Panda, designed to facilitate motion through rough roads across rural Italy. Its impact, however, is worldwide as this car has stolen the hearts of many across the globe.

The Edo Circuit Road Race is filled with very technical corners and narrow roads. Take a small car that excels in handling to complete a lap around this new track!

What the notes say

  • Take a small car that excels in handling to complete a lap around this new track!
  • Race under the moonlight in the Shuto Charge Street Race.
  • This high-speed track takes you through the heart of Tokyo City.
  • Remember that traffic doesn't care about you taking the lead!
