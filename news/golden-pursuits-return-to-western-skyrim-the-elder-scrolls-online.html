Golden Pursuits—Return to Western Skyrim

The Elder Scrolls® Online · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

The Return to Western Skyrim Golden Pursuits campaign is now live from November 9 until December 2, 2026 at 10AM ET. During this new campaign, you can earn special rewards as you explore, dig up relics, and complete activities within Western Skyrim and Blackreach, including a new pet and mount!

The homeland of the Nords calls upon its beloved champions to battle the darkness at its heart. It is time to rediscover the city of Solitude and the depths of Blackreach during a new Golden Pursuits campaign!

What the notes say

  • Complete five activities—Antiquities revealer and leads bundle
  • Complete 10 activities—10,000 Gold
  • Complete 15 activities—Antiquities booster, leads, and crate bundle
  • This includes a Sovngarde Crown Crate
  • Complete 20 activities—Sea of Ghosts Coral Crab pet
  • Complete 25 activities—Antiquities mythic leads and crate bundle

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
