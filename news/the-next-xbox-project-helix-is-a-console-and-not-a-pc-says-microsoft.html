The next Xbox, Project Helix, is a console and "not a PC" says Microsoft, as it clarifies that computational power isn't sole focus this time

Eurogamer · industry report·16 November 2026·Source

Microsoft has stressed that its next-generation Xbox console, Project Helix, "is not a PC that can also play console games", but rather "a console that is also capable of playing games from your PC". The company has also underlined the importance of using gen-AI-adjacent "neural rendering" technology going forwards, and a desire to remove "all the barriers of play that have historically existed" - notably in making the past generations of games playable.
