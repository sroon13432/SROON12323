Creator Spotlight: FroggyJolson

Albion Online · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

My Albion Online journey actually started back in 2017. I bought the game when it was still B2P, but ended up quitting after just three weeks. Fast forward to September 2024, when I decided to give Albion another shot. People on Twitch were mostly playing Mists and, as a competitive player who loves PvP, I knew I had to give it a try.

I mainly create content on Twitch, but I also upload at least one video per week to YouTube, along with some shorts and clips. I mostly focus on solo PvP in the Mists and Outlands.

What the notes say

  • Since then, I've been enjoying the game and playing almost every day!
  • Entertainment and good vibes are what you'll find on my streams!
  • Whether you're a completely new player who wants to learn more about Albion or an experienced player looking for some exciting PvP action, everybody is welcome.
  • I'm always happy to answer questions, share knowledge, discuss builds, and help newer players get into the game.
  • And don't worry, more experienced players definitely won't be disappointed by the PvP they'll see!
  • As we always say on stream: First, you come for the DROPS...
