The Porsche 911 R is Your New Ally for Endurance Racing

Forza Horizon 5 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Optimization did not stop at the engine. Porsche also modified the suspension to improve the driving experience and tire duration. Body modifications improve airflow and downforce. All that’s missing is a driver daring enough to take this car to the limit. If you're ready, you can unlock it for 20 PTS until November 26.

A car with this type of heritage and design philosophy can't fall short in the power department. The 911 R passes the test with flying colors, thanks to its flat-six, 4.2-liter engine that produces 565 bhp.
