Enjoy a Superpowered Track Day with an Elite 911

Forza Horizon 5 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Engineers behind this project brought ideas from F1 and applied them to this car, which led to improvements such as the 88 lbs of downforce, as well as the aerodynamic improvements in its body kit and adjustable wing.

The GT3 RS is everything you love about a 911, but kicked up a notch (or maybe two). Packed with a 4-liter six-cylinder engine, it can produce 518 bhp and 342 lb-ft, flying to 60 mph in almost 3 seconds.

What the notes say

  • Packed with a 4-liter six-cylinder engine, it can produce 518 bhp and 342 lb-ft, flying to 60 mph in almost 3 seconds.
  • Made from lightweight materials from front to back, this Porsche was designed to obliterate tracks, with you at the wheel.
  • Race across the Forza: Warthog Rally event in an M12S Warthog CST to unlock the Halo Theme Part 2 Car Horn.
  • Then tune in to Horizon XS to catch Halo Theme Gungnir Mix (feat.
  • Steve Vai) or Radio Eterna to listen to Installation 04 and The Trials, all straight out of Halo’s OST!
