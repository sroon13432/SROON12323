Ask Shadow Anything——Season Aurora Edition

Delta Force · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

There are two main reasons. The first is how many players are available when you queue, which depends on your region, the time of day, your server node, and the map you've selected. The second is that, as we've added more maps and modes to give you more ways to play, players have become spread across more queues. That means fewer people in each queue, making matches harder to fill.

Hey everyone, Shadow here. Welcome back to Ask Shadow Anything. Today, let's talk about matchmaking, optimization, and a few features and stories you keep asking about as season Aurora started earlier this week.

What the notes say

  • Reducing queue times has been a priority for us, and we're trying different approaches to help you get into matches faster.
  • We know that sometimes you just want to head into a map on your own and play at your own pace.
  • We'll consider bringing solo queues back at selected times, but we also need to make sure you can find a match and have a good experience once you're in.
  • If the waits are too long or the matches aren't good enough, we'd rather hold off.
  • Having to clear space before you can even play shouldn't become a bigger hurdle with every update.
  • Over the past few versions, we've worked on reducing the game's size and separating resource downloads so you have more choice over what you install.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
