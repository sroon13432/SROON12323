What the notes say

  • While the main focus of Engines of War was on expanding the game with new content, we wanted to continue improving quality of life in this update.
  • One area of feedback we wanted to address was reducing the amount of repetitive actions required for logistics gameplay.
  • There were also some improvements to intel for the map screen that we’ve been wanting to make for some time.
  • The goal is to continue improving quality of life in future updates, which is of utmost importance to players, especially those involved in Facilities and logistics.
