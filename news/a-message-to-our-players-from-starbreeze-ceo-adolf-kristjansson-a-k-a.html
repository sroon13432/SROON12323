A message to our players from Starbreeze CEO Adolf Kristjansson (a.k.a. AK)

PAYDAY 3 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

So we spent the last twelve months earning it back. We listened more than we talked, or at least we tried to. Then we got to work. We reworked PAYDAY 3, using your feedback as the main brief, plus the direction from our team for what it should be. We upgraded the game to Unreal Engine 5 and revisited the heists. Every one of them is now part of the base game, they look better than they ever have, and there are more ways to play them.

Thank you to the veterans who carried a crew of rookies through their first heist (you were kind, supportive and you welcomed the new recruits into our community). Thank you to the rookies who tripped every alarm in the building, but still returned for more! Thank you to everyone who recommended the game to a friend, and convinced them that it was worth their time.

What the notes say

  • Last weekend was the first time we got to see what happens when hundreds of thousands of people load into the game we have been rebuilding.

  • settled another 2.8 million arguments, and 441,000 problems were solved with a throwing knife, which is just showing off.
  • To everyone who has been with us since the beginning: you kept this game alive when it would have been easier to walk away, and what comes next is built on what you told us.
  • Regular updates will keep coming, we will keep showing our work and we will keep listening, because that is what got us here.
