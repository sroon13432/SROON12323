CELEBRATE 10 YEARS OF KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS

DEATH STRANDING DIRECTOR'S CUT · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 October 2026·Source

For DEATH STRANDING fans, highlights include footage from the earliest prototype of DEATH STRANDING, original design artwork by Yoji Shinkawa, and development materials from DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH.

KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS is celebrating its 10th anniversary with a special exhibition, BEYOND THE STRAND: The Odyssey of Creation and Endeavor, opening October 31 at Azabudai Hills Gallery in Tokyo!

What the notes say

  • The exhibition takes a look back at 10 years of creation and challenge, featuring never-before-seen production materials, immersive exhibits, and exclusive video installations.
  • Visitors can also experience a 360° DEATH STRANDING video installation projected onto a six-meter-diameter screen, along with a life-size 10th Anniversary Ludens statue.
  • The exhibition will also feature a full-scale recreation of the iconic red door from OD - KNOCK, original merchandise, and special collaborations with shops at Azabudai Hills.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
