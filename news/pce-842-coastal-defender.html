PCE-842: Coastal Defender

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

The PCE-842 is a fairly large ship by coastal fleet standards, built for holding capture points. Her top speed is rather modest, but she makes up for it with good survivability and powerful firepower: a 76 mm gun on the bow, along with six 40 mm Bofors autocannons and four 20 mm Oerlikon cannons.

Meet the PCE-842, a true tank of the coastal fleet. She’s a slow ship with excellent firepower, depth charges and the Hedgehog anti-submarine mortar!

What the notes say

  • She’s a slow ship with excellent firepower, depth charges and the Hedgehog anti-submarine mortar!
  • Her stern depth charge racks and side-mounted depth charge launchers let her cover a wide area astern.
  • The Hedgehog anti-submarine mortar rounds out the depth charge armament, offering a convenient way to hit a target dead ahead before following up with a heavier barrage from the stern.
  • The PCE-842’s standout feature is her extensive depth charge armament.
  • Fully upgraded, she carries standard depth charges on racks at the stern, a pair of depth charge launchers on each side, and the forward-firing Hedgehog anti-submarine mortar.
  • Ships of the PCE-842 family were built in large numbers and saw action in several wars around the world.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
