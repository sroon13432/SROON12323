Update 1.5.1 Hotfix – November 19, 2026

Sid Meier's Civilization VII · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

As we prepare for a fourth Age, we have been re-evaluating many of the per-Age costs in the game. Firaxis Feature Workshop participants will see many of these adjustments in the Workshop, but we felt this particular change would be an improvement to the current iteration of the game as well.

An additional hotfix with these changes and other Switch-specific fixes will be coming at a later date; thanks for your patience while we get this through additional Nintendo submission requirements. In the meantime, multiplayer and crossplay remains compatible with other platforms.

What the notes say

  • Specialist costs have been adjusted to scale slower per Age (2/3/4 instead of 2/4/6).
  • This change was implemented in Update 1.5.1, but was missing in those patch notes.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
