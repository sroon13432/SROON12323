Jump Into Gray Zone Warfare at Its Lowest Price Yet: 35% Off

Gray Zone Warfare · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

Each edition includes additional gear, weapons, and resources, giving PMCs a stronger starting point while offering extra value to those supporting the game’s continued development. Supporter Edition owners also receive exclusive morale patches.

To celebrate the launch of Rogue Ops, we’re offering the lowest discount yet. The base game and all editions of Gray Zone Warfare are available at a 35% discount from October 22,  10 AM PDT / 1 PM EDT, to November 5, 10 AM PST / 1 PM EDT.

What the notes say

  • With Update 0.5, we are avoiding a full wipe and proceeding with a partial wipe instead.
  • This will allow us to rebalance the in-game economy for the new update while letting players retain their progression and avoid having to grind from scratch.
  • Players will retain their progression from Update 0.4, including completed tasks, rank, unlocked apparel, vendor levels, and statistics, while their locker and money will be wiped.
  • When first entering Update 0.5, players will need to choose which mode they want to transfer their existing character progression to.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
