Immortal Wraith Gauntlets | Player-Designed & FREE for All

Throne and Liberty · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

During the launch of Nix, FirstSpark Games held a Community Gauntlet Design Contest, inviting players to create an original gauntlet design for a chance to have the winning design added to the game as an official cosmetic. There were a lot of incredible submissions, and everyone in the office had their own favorite!

From November 18 (PT) to December 18 (PT), the Immortal Wraith Gauntlets Skin will be delivered via in-game mail to all new and existing accounts. Players must claim the skin by December 18 (PT) before it is removed.

What the notes say

  • Players must claim the skin by December 18 (PT) before it is removed.
  • With so many ways to experiment with new classes and characters right now, grab the Immortal Wraith Gauntlets and give them a spin.
  • Congratulations, Calypso, and thank you to everyone who submitted a design!
  • We look forward to holding more community contests in the future.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
