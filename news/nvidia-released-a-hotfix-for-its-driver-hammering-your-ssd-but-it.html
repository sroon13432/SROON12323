Nvidia released a hotfix for its driver hammering your SSD, but it doesn't actually work

PC Gamer · industry report·22 November 2026·Source

Over the last couple of weeks, some Nvidia users noticed their SSDs were taking a hammering, reporting their driver's log file was being overwritten constantly, sometimes to the tune of 30 GB every hour. Which obviously isn't great for the lifespan of the valuable SSD you've got it installed on.

Given the problem was caused by recent driver updates, rolling back to version 621.01 was the best solution until Nvidia could do something about it. Which they did, releasing a hotfix as version 621.35. It's not available in the GeForce app yet, but you can download it directly from Nvidia's site. Not that you should.

More from the report

  • As users on the Nvidia subreddit are reporting, the problem persists. While the hotfix reduces the peak write rate and prevents it for a short while after reboot,
  • So yeah, the best solution remains to roll back to version 621.01 and, as always with Nvidia drivers, to hold off on updating to the latest driver until it's been confirmed safe.
