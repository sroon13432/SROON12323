Slaughter Mode Update 5

DOOM: The Dark Ages · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

Update 5 for DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations introduces Slaughter Mode; an endless horde survival experience that challenges players to battle through escalating demon encounters across multiple arenas. Complete Rounds, earn Skulls and push deeper into each run as you fight to set a new Personal Best.

: Each round features either a randomized or curated encounter. Clear the arena to be transported to the next battleground, where another challenge awaits

What the notes say

  • : Each round features either a randomized or curated encounter.
  • Clear the arena to be transported to the next battleground, where another challenge awaits
  • : Complete as many Rounds as possible and earn Skulls throughout each run.
  • Skulls advance your Profile Level, unlocking cosmetic rewards and new Badge Ranks
  • : Reach a Round Checkpoint to Secure your current Skulls against death and unlock that Round as a starting point for your next run.
  • Start from any passed Checkpoint Rounds to reach later encounters faster or start over from the beginning
