Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition – Update 188329

Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Fixed an issue where the game would be unresponsive for a few seconds after fully loading the map and just before starting the game (more obvious with larger maps and more players).

This is just a small taste of what is coming in the latest update. For a more complete list, please visit our site and check out our blog. Happy Gaming!

What the notes say

  • Fixed some crashes and out of sync issues.
  • Fixed an issue with garrisoned units getting killed when building is destroyed.
  • Fixed some exploits and gameplay related bugs.
  • Fixed an issue with large group of units slowing down around obstructions.
  • Fixed an out of sync issue related to box formation.
  • Fixed an out of sync issue occurring when watching replays with Reveal Map set to “Explored” or “All Visible”.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
