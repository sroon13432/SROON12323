CROSSPLAY HAS ARRIVED TO PEAK

PEAK · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

It's finally here. PEAK is now out on consoles, which means CROSSPLAY is available for PEAK Steam players. The game has launched to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2, so you can play with any of your fellow scouts on these platforms!

There is no save progress transfer for PEAK to other platforms, you'll just have to get those badges again, or act as a guide for your friends that are new to the game!
