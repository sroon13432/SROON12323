Darkest Dungeon - Ode to Mods Update - 28385 - Retail/Coming_in_Hot

Darkest Dungeon® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

When we returned to do work for The Fire’s Edge we had a tough job ensuring that we didn’t cause any real unintended changes to the existing modding community. During this process, our team reached out to prominent modders to assist with testing their mods on the new build, as well as getting them early access to all the new systems. It was awesome to have them participate in this return and we are very grateful for their time and bug reports!

While testing our initial release, the modders asked if it would be okay to compile a list of long standing requests that they had built up over the years. The dream was that if we had some spare cycles we could add some of these requests post release as we completed our work with consoles and other hotfixes.

What the notes say

  • The dream was that if we had some spare cycles we could add some of these requests post release as we completed our work with consoles and other hotfixes.
  • They showed up with a massive, extensively organized and vetted list of requests.
  • We decided to extend development an additional month to support this work directly, and get their help with testing these new additions.
  • Thanks to them, we’ve added an amazing amount of new and improved features that will hopefully bring in yet another amazing era of Darkest Dungeon mods.
  • It was really their testing and support that allowed us to move through this work so swiftly.
  • We also want to thank our co-development team of Flying Rat.
