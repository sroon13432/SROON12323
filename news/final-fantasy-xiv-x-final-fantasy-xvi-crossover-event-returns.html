FINAL FANTASY XIV x FINAL FANTASY XVI Crossover Event Returns!

FINAL FANTASY XIV Online · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

Participate in the event to obtain several event items, including the Torgal mount, the Metian Attire inspired by protagonist Clive Rosfield, a minion, orchestrion rolls, and more. Whether you missed the previous event or simply wish to experience the adventure again, don't miss out!

What the notes say

  • Players must be level 50 and above to start the quest.
  • Players must have completed the main scenario quest “The Ultimate Weapon.”
  • This event is identical to previous iterations.
  • Event quest can be replayed by utilizing the Seasonal Event Replay feature.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
