Meta Hotline | Hot Feedback and Optimization Progress Vol. 12

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

After the v2.3.7 update, we added the Feedback Hot Charts section to the Once Human Feedback Platform, listing recent popular requests from the community. After logging in to the Feedback Platform, you can like the feedback you support and dislike any feedback you disagree with. The Feedback Hot Charts will display likes and dislikes in real time, and our development team will use these results to help determine development priorities. (Feedback Platform link:

The Feedback Hot Charts will also be updated with each version update. After each update, we'll share our plans and progress for addressing the player requests featured in the previous list. We value your feedback and hope the Feedback Hot Charts will give us more opportunities to communicate with you and share our development plans and progress.

What the notes say

  • Every piece of feedback, every like, and every dislike helps the dev team better understand which needs matter most from the players' perspective.
  • Below are the follow-up details for this cycle's top suggestions.
  • The relevant adjustments went live with the October 27(PT) version update.
  • Lift-Off Energy Devices and Floating Aerogels can now be dismantled, giving Metas more flexibility in managing these items and freeing up backpack and depot space.
  • The relevant feature is currently in the planning and development stages.
  • We recognize that the current availability of General Mods could be improved.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
