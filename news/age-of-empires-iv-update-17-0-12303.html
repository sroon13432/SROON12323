Age of Empires IV – Update 17.0.12303

Age of Empires IV: Anniversary Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

The team takes cheating seriously and continues to work on fixes for exploits and other unfair advantages. Usually we don’t share details about upcoming fixes in advance, so cheaters can’t exploit them further or prepare workarounds.

Alongside these fixes, we'll keep reviewing player reports and taking action against accounts that cheat, up to and including permanent bans.

What the notes say

  • Co-op Crucible matches now reliably start when either player has the Block community mods option enabled.
  • Fixed an issue which caused the Crucible Conqueror mode to be unavailable.
  • Fixed an exploit which allowed players to generate resources infinitely in multiplayer matches.
  • Fixed an exploit which allowed Crucible units to be spawned into non-crucible modes.
  • Added additional guards to prevent some methods of cheating.
  • Fixed an issue where Jeanne d'Arc's Divine Arrow could skip cooldown if a movement command is issued right away.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
