Iceland DLC Release

Euro Truck Simulator 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

We are excited to welcome you to Iceland! From the lively streets of Reykjavík to the remote corners of this fascinating island, there is plenty to see and experience along the way. Whether you're travelling along the famous Ring Road, passing towering waterfalls, or navigating winding roads through volcanic landscapes, every journey offers something new to discover.

The Iceland DLC for Euro Truck Simulator 2 is finally here, inviting you to discover a land shaped by fire, ice, and some of the most extraordinary natural landscapes in the world! With so much waiting to be explored, it's time to hit the road and experience the unique beauty of Iceland from behind the wheel!

What the notes say

  • Discover spectacular natural landmarks such as Jökulsárlón, Goðafoss, Gullfoss, Seljalandsfoss, Víkurfjara, Geysir, Thingvellir, and many more.
  • Travel along the iconic Ring Road, connecting vibrant cities, quiet settlements, dramatic coastlines, and remote corners of the island.
  • Navigate narrow, single-lane bridges where drivers must give way to oncoming traffic, bringing an extra layer of realism to your journeys.
  • Take in a stunning variety of landscapes, from vast plains and volcanic fields to majestic mountains, rugged cliffs, and cascading waterfalls.
  • From the dramatic coastline to the breathtaking scenery of Iceland's interior, this expansion offers a journey unlike any other in Euro Truck Simulator 2.
  • Take your time, enjoy the views, and discover the many details our teams have brought to life across this remarkable island.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
