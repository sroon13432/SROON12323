Icarus Week 257 Update | Spelunking Mission Revamp

ICARUS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

This week we're digging back into the Olympus Operation Update with a major rework for the Spelunking Operation. The rework reduces the heavy mining requirements in favor of varied challenges, makes Olympus feel more alive, grants better rewards for your time, and resolves several soft-locks to quest completion.

This week we're taking a fresh look at one of the earliest missions in the Olympus Region, giving it a major rework. We're revisiting the mission's content and overall experience, bringing it up to a better standard while retaining its place in the early Olympus progression.

What the notes say

  • Spelunking mission rework
  • Spelunking v2 reintroducing next iteration's quest frame
  • Spelunking creation of caveworm and prospector corpse for scene setting
  • Spelunking moved cave quest node so it's not completed automatically, removed higher tier ore mining requirement, fixed pod trying to fly back into the sky after finding hut
  • Spelunking - reitemized the hut and changed layout for stylistic integrity
  • new loot subquest section for the miner

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
