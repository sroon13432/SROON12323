Twisted Minds crowned as PGS 12 Champions

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Twisted Minds was one of the most complete teams in the history of PUBG Esports. Living up to its reputation as a roster of world-class players, the team proved its abilities through results. Twisted Minds reached its peak between 2024 and 2025. In 2024, the team claimed three consecutive PGS titles. In 2025, it conquered the Esports World Cup (EWC), followed that victory with another championship at PGS 9, and finished as runners-up at PGS 10. Twisted Minds had established itself as a truly dominant force.

The four players brought their individual strengths together and operated as one. BatulinS read the flow of every battlefield, xmpl initiated engagements, Perfect1ks kept pace with the evolving meta, and Lu stood as the team's unwavering last line of defense. Rather than relying on any single player's performance, all four understood their responsibilities and fulfilled their roles according to the situation. This was Twisted Minds' greatest weapon and the driving force behind its success.
