Revolutionary Blueprints and... New Weapons?

Trailmakers · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

On December 17 we will be gathering all the blueprints that have won Build Challenges in 2026. Then a group of judges will score them individually and the highest scoring blueprint in the Engineer and Imagineer categories will win Builder of the Year and Pioneer of the Year respectively. This comes with cool gifts and the legendary champions mug.

The final Build Challenge of 2026 is over and we turn our heads toward the final community stream of the year: Builder of the Year 2026. This will be the fourth edition of the award which started back in 2023 where Metalburger won Builder of the Year with his Chirpozod.

What the notes say

  • XiaoKungLao won for their Microscope, a revolutionary tool in science - while AC YT won for a guillotine contraption chopping a tricolor car in half.
  • Above is the schedule for when we will reveal the new stuff.
  • And of course we are ending with a brutal showdown between the devs and Trailmakers content creators.
