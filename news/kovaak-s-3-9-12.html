KovaaK's 3.9.12

KovaaK's · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

Redesigned, stacked notifications show Finding, Checking for Updates and Downloading. Players are also notified when scenarios have changed.

Fixed "crouchbug": Vertical knockback required to lift targets is capped at its 60 FPS value, preventing frame-rate-dependent behavior.

What the notes say

  • Failed Steam requests retry automatically.
  • Failed refreshes preserve the existing index.
  • Private, unlisted and deleted items are handled correctly.
  • Refreshing pauses during gameplay to prevent hitches.
  • Refreshes can be cancelled while retaining discovered scenarios.
  • Reverted the 3.9.11 change suspected of causing frame-rate dips.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
