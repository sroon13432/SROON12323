Operation "Bridge of Marigolds" in World of Tanks Blitz

World of Tanks Blitz · posted by the game's developer on Steam·1 November 2026·Source

According to Mexican tradition, in November, the spirits of deceased loved ones temporarily return to the world of the living to enjoy a joyful celebration with their families. Everything is ready for El Día de los Muertos: candles flicker everywhere, flowers decorate the streets, and favorite treats wait on the ofrenda altars. All that's left is to welcome the dear guests—the bridge of marigolds is already leading them home!

The Operation has two progression paths, each offering 60 levels with rewards. The first path is available to all regular Pass holders—so, everyone! The second path offers more rewards and is exclusively available to Premium Pass holders. Complete the Operation missions to boost your Pass Level and earn rewards.
