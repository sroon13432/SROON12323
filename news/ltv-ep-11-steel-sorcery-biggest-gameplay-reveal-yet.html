LTV Ep. 11 ‘Steel & Sorcery’ - Biggest Gameplay Reveal Yet!

Lords of the Fallen · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Titled 'Steel & Sorcery', Episode 11 showcases a major overhaul of melee, magic, and ranged combat, introducing new abilities, expanded weapon variety, and greater freedom to experiment with different playstyles.

community, these changes aim to make every encounter more fluid, aggressive, and rewarding, giving you more ways than ever to unleash devastation on your enemies.

What the notes say

  • Combat takes a significant leap forward with almost 30 fully distinct weapon movesets, nearly double the number featured in
  • Each weapon class brings its own unique maneuvers, from repositioning strikes and endless combo chains to sweeping area-of-effect attacks.
  • One-handed and two-handed fighting styles also offer greater distinction, while new counterstrikes and boss weapons with signature attacks add further depth to your arsenal.
  • One of the biggest additions is seamless mid-combo weapon switching.
  • Players can now swap between two different melee loadouts without breaking the flow of combat, opening up entirely new combinations.
  • Start with a flurry of dual dagger strikes, then transition straight into a devastating scythe swing, all within a single combo.
