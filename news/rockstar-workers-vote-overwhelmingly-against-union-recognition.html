Rockstar workers vote overwhelmingly against union recognition

PC Gamer · industry report·26 November 2026·Source

After two weeks of voting that bracketed the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, Rockstar Games employees have voted overwhelmingly against a recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union.

25% of Rockstar employees voted in favor of the recognition agreement, the union said, while 75% voted against it. 1,544 employees took part in the vote, representing a turnout of just over 93%.

More from the report

  • Rockstar workers actually founded a union earlier this year—the Rockstar Game Workers Union, a subsidiary of the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain—and it will continue to operate…
  • Despite the vote, the union said it will continue with organizing efforts at Rockstar.
  • "For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society—this isn’t a sign to retreat, it’s a sign to double down on what we stand for.
  • "Whilst we haven’t emerged with a recognition deal, this ballot has put our union on the radar of every worker, and opened the door for new kinds of conversation to start happening.
