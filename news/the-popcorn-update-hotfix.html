The Popcorn Update Hotfix

Crab Champions · posted by the game's developer on Steam·14 November 2026·Source

Bullet Damage Falloff no longer scales debuff stacks (fire, ice, lightning, poison, arcane). This means you can still make long range elemental builds without worrying about falloff. This also matches how AOE damage falloff works (no debuff scaling based on how far from the center you are)

Popcorn Shotgun exploding projectile gravity increased by 33% so there is noticeably less downtime before the projectiles land making them much more reliable at hitting moving targets. The goal here is to make the weapon feel less clunky to use and just more fun overall

What the notes say

  • The goal here is to make the weapon feel less clunky to use and just more fun overall
  • A common piece of feedback for a long time now was that enemies felt frustratingly accurate with projectile attacks.
  • I have added a 20% penalty to their prediction accuracy to make good movement more rewarding
  • Reduced the per wave enemy damage increase in Horde islands by 90% as it was leading to people feeling like they got randomly one shot
  • This means more balanced islands with small and large enemies.
  • Looping should still feel just as chaotic but in a more fun way

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
