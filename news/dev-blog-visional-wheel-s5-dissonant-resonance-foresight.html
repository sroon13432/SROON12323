Dev Blog | Visional Wheel S5 "Dissonant Resonance" Foresight

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

The Visional Wheel spins once more. This time, the clue to confronting the Vision lies hidden within scattered Notes. Defeat the Deviants modified by the power of notes, rearrange and purify the broken Score, and turn the enemy's power into your own melody. As the Gulped in the sky casts its gaze, brave Metas will respond to this "performance" from the unknown in their own way.

Deep in the Nova, a colossal face gazes down upon the land. An unfamiliar frequency pierces through the ruins, weaving joy, enrage, sorrow, and fear into a single score—not a blessing for this world, but the final movement of destruction that the new Great One, "The Echo," seeks to perform.

What the notes say

  • Manibus (excluding Novice Scenario), Evolution's Call, Prismverse's Clash, The Way of Winter, Endless Dream, Deviation: Survive, Capture, Preserve, and Isles of Abyss.
  • Metas can switch to the "Vision" world via the Worldstone to experience it.
  • Where The Echo's "Gospel" spreads, the will of fallen beings clings desperately to this land, transforming into a colossal calamity that yearns to speak—the Dissonant Husk.
  • This Gulped suspended in the sky is composed of structures resembling copper pipes, its human-like yet inhuman face sending chills down the spine.
  • Its massive form looms over the earth like a false god, and occasionally it notices the ant-like Metas below.
  • "The Echo" was originally an ancient relic slumbering in the ruins.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
