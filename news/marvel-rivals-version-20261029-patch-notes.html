Marvel Rivals Version 20261029 Patch Notes

Marvel Rivals · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

A mysterious man, fixated on an unknown witch, mistook the number "4" on her person for her actual name. Driven by this obsession, he attempts to sacrifice four "monsters" to complete a summoning ritual. A small animal, an ancient beast, and an old friend successively fall victim to the sacrifice. But Elsa Bloodstone, driven by her monster hunter's instinct, has stepped into this bizarre house and is beginning to unravel the horrifying truth behind this Night of Illusions...

Explore 5 mysterious rooms, solve a multitude of puzzles, and claim limited-time rewards including rare Gallery Cards, Units, Sprays, and more!

What the notes say

  • Gorr's Out-of-Bounds Butchering: Fixed a bug where Gorr could occasionally fly entirely out of the map if he cast Shadow Harvest immediately after generating a Black Berserker.
  • Rogue's Wardrobe Malfunction: Fixed a model clipping issue with Rogue's X-Men '97 Season 2 costume where her left arm would clip through her cape while walking backwards.
  • Black Cat's Missing Glitch: Fixed an issue where the overhead icon for Black Cat's Fatal Weaver costume would disappear when utilizing her Team-Up ability with Spider-Man, Binding Ties.
  • Black Cat's Ultimate Keybind: Fixed a bug where players could still use the 'Q' key to activate the second phase of Black Cat's Ultimate Ability,
  • Invisible Woman's Transparent Trouble: Fixed an issue where casting other abilities wouldn't always break her stealth if she had entered it using Veiled Step.
  • Times Square One-Way Trip: Addressed a bug where players who entered a game from Times Square and clicked "One More Round" on the post-match summary screen were unable to return to Times…
