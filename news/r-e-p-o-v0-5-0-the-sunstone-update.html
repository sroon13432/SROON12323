R.E.P.O. v0.5.0 - The Sunstone Update

R.E.P.O. · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Grab Lead - Added adjustable grab lead for mouse and gamepad, allowing held objects and melee swings to move ahead of your aim. Separate settings for grabbing and melee.

Valuable Soft Lock - If there aren't enough valuables left in the level to complete extraction, new smaller valuables will now spawn a short distance away from players.

What the notes say

  • A whole new map, Sunstone Valley Resort!
  • Localization Added - 21 languages!
  • Cosmetics - Added 10 new cosmetics!
  • Main Menu - Added new buttons in the lower right to view the latest news and visit our Discord or YouTube channel!
  • Headman Manor - 5 new rooms!
  • Swiftbroom Academy - 4 new rooms!
