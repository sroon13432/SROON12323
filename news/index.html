Where these articles come from

Every article on this page is a summary of an announcement the game's own developer or publisher posted on its Steam hub. The headline, the quoted lines and the link back to the original are theirs; nothing is rewritten into a rumour and no article is written for a game that has not posted anything.

What CanMySpec adds is the hardware side: the announcement is matched to the game's published minimum and recommended requirements, so you can see whether an update matters for your card. For the requirements themselves, open the game's spec page or the hardware filter.
