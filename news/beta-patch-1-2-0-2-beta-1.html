Beta Patch 1.2.0.2.beta-1

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Mutation no longer copies effects that do nothing when duplicated or are already capped: Survivor's extra starting weapon choice, Time Shifter's mixed-up waves, Caveman's weapon burning, material attraction above 100%, Treasure Map above 100%, Snail once enemy speed is at its minimum

The -1 Evolution of a mutation now goes on the Tumor, which shows the number of mutations as its first line. An item only becomes mutant when it loses a malus (existing Tumors start counting from now)

What the notes say

  • The -1 Evolution of a mutation now goes on the Tumor, which shows the number of mutations as its first line.
  • Mutation no longer copies effects that do nothing when duplicated or are already capped: Survivor's extra starting weapon choice, Time Shifter's mixed-up waves, Caveman's weapon burning,
  • Copies of the same effect on the Tumor now always merge into one line
  • Copied explosion chance is rounded up to a whole percent, and explosion chance no longer shows decimals
  • Mutagen: base drop chance increased from 3% to 4%, then reduced by 1% every 10 waves from wave 30 (minimum 1%)
  • Weapon damage bonus reduced: +3% per meca power point instead of +5% (+108% at 8 mechanizations instead of +180%)

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
