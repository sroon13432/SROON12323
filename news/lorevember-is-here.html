Lorevember Is Here!

FOR HONOR™ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Ah November! Spooky season is over, the weather is cold and grey, and the titans of Ocarina of Time and GTA6 emerge to take over the internet. With what can a poor little Heathmoorian keep themselves warm at night? Why a lore event, no, a lore extravaganza of course!

We would like to invite the For Honor community to submit their lore stories this November using #lorevember on social media or in the megathread on r/ForHonor and tell us all a tale from the Myre.

What the notes say

  • All mediums will be accepted, purely words, drawn comics, poems and more!
  • As long as you share with us your creativity and handcrafted fan stories.
  • We will finish off the event with an AMA with the narrative team hosted on Reddit r/forhonor at the end of November.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
