Two New Campaigns Incoming!

DCS World Steam Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

Coming in the next update, DCS: Mosquito FB VI Always Ready Campaign by Reflected Simulations puts you in the cockpit with No. 613 Squadron in the months around D-Day, across 10 historical missions recreated from original operations record books and combat reports. Learn more below.

Also coming in the next update, DCS: A-10 Fulda Gap '85 Campaign by Flying Cyking takes you into the first seven days of a fictional Warsaw Pact invasion of NATO, flying the A-10 over the DCS: Cold War Germany map across ten fully scripted missions. Learn more below.

What the notes say

  • The legendary de Havilland Mosquito was arguably the most versatile aircraft of the Second World War.
  • It served in an extraordinary range of roles, by day and by night, from Europe to the Far East, flying daring sorties that helped turn the tide of history.
  • Coming in the next update, this no-nonsense historical campaign zooms in on a single unit, recreating 10 missions based on original operations record books and combat reports.
  • 613 Squadron in the months leading up to D-Day and the tense weeks that followed.
  • As F/L Edward 'Ted' Harrington, alongside your seasoned Aussie navigator, F/O Nigel 'Cal' Callaghan, you'll fly these sorties exactly as they unfolded more than 80 years ago.
  • Soviet armoured divisions are pouring through the Fulda Gap, and NATO needs every hour it can buy.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
