Nominate Among Us for Labor of Love! ❤

Among Us · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

Whether it's sabotage fixes to new roles, we've been working endlessly to get you the best task and ejection experience possible. We're eternally grateful for all of the support we've had since we first released Impostors into the world! Which now that we think about it, was 8 years ago?!

We're not done either! There's still a little over a month and we have some fun announcements planned for that. (Plus this list doesn't include the fact our animated series released this year. Whoa.)

What the notes say

  • Go to the Steam Awards 2026 page, scroll to "Labor of Love" and select Among Us (then vote for other games in the categories!)
  • All that to say: whether you're a Crewmate or Impostor, we hope you'll give us a vote!
  • It's just like an Emergency Meeting, except this time we actually want to be picked.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
