M.E.G. Archives: Community Level Dossiers - Level 993.1 & The Window Theatre

Escape the Backrooms · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

We're kicking off a new initiative to bring some of these ideas into the spotlight, with classified M.E.G. Field Dossiers dedicated to the creativity of our community.

Our research teams have been digging through community submissions, uncovering new level concepts and mapping out sectors imagined by Explorers like you.

What the notes say

  • Today, we're opening the files on two community-designed levels, complete with key intel to help you if you ever found yourself there.
  • An abandoned 1990s multiplex haunted by anomalous Window entities.
  • To escape, explorers must restore power to the server farm, swap out the movie reel, and step through the projection screen.
  • A surreal room complex opening into endless skies of toxic, expanding clouds.
  • One wrong step could lead to instant suffocation or a fatal fall into the void.
