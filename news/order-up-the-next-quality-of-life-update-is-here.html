Order Up! The Next Quality of Life Update is here!

The Sims™ 4 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

(that’s “tasty” in Simlish)! This month’s update is going to be all about Food, Dining, and Drinks! We’ve got more than 150 bug fixes, and a significant portion of this update focuses directly on improving your culinary and dining experiences - including 25+ fixes for Dine Out!  We honestly can’t thank you enough for all the incredible direction you’ve given us along the way (via Discord,  EA Forums, and various subreddits).

. Read on to learn more about the delicious new content and gameplay improvements coming with this patch, and then hop down to the bug fix section for the full, detailed breakdown of all the fixes. Bon appétit!

What the notes say

  • Update your game to discover a treasure trove of new goodies, including new Create a Sim assets perfect for a sophisticated new look.
  • Dress your Elders in elegant new hairstyles, cozy cardigans, and timeless dresses.
  • Then, redecorate their space with new Build Mode items, from charmingly vintage furniture to delightful decor that will make any house feel like a home.
  • For a touch of inspiration, you'll find a new Styled Room in the Build Mode catalog, perfectly arranged with the latest homey items.
  • We've also put together four new Elder Styled Looks in Create a Sim to highlight the new apparel.
  • And if you've fallen in love with the Sims modeling these new looks, you're in luck!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
