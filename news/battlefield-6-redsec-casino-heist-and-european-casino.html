Battlefield 6 | REDSEC: Casino Heist and European Casino

Battlefield™ 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

The Contracts cover a wide range of Battlefield playstyles. You may be asked to capture objectives, earn eliminations, support teammates, complete REDSEC activities, or deal damage across the battlefield. Some Contracts will be completed without much extra effort. Others may push you to change tactics, switch classes, or approach a match from a different angle.

The key is learning where objectives overlap. A successful push can contribute to several Contracts at once, turning a single engagement into meaningful progress toward your next reward.

What the notes say

  • Every completed Contract rewards DOUGH, Casino Heist's limited-time currency, which can be exchanged for a collection of event rewards inspired by the world of Casino Heist.
  • The reward track includes Weapon Stickers, Patches, Vehicle Decals, Player Card customization items, Vehicle Skins, a Weapon Package, and new Heist-themed Soldier Skin variants.
  • As your earnings grow, more rewards become available, giving you new unlocks throughout the event.
  • Available in Battlefield 6, European Casino distills the spectacle of Las Vegas into a fast-paced infantry experience.
  • We tried to offer it all while making it a believable space.
  • At the center of the map stands the tower, visible from nearly every corner of European Casino.
