The Weekly Grind - Nov 3, 2026

skate. · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

The Curse of Morg event has ended. The Isle of Grom has returned to its pre-Morg condition. For the rest of the season, access to the Isle of Grom will require a 24-hour Water Taxi Day Pass for 500 Rip Chips (or unlimited access via the skate.Premium Pass). You may notice the Pump House has been cleaned out - don’t worry! We’re bringing back the Pump House Park when Season 5 ends (but with less Morg).

With the Proximity Chat test on Isle of Morg completed, we saw a lot of conversation on expanding it to other areas. Based on this, we are shifting our plans to get this into your hands earlier than originally planned with the Prox Chat soft launch. Starting Nov 9, Prox Chat will be available (Opt In) for all of San Van - Downtown, The Stadium, S.U.M.R. Camp, and The Isle of Grom.

What the notes say

  • We couldn’t settle on just 10, and 13 is such a great number to say goodbye to Morg, so in no particular order, here’s who will be receiving a Year 6 skate.
  • Grab these shirts from Bad Religion, Sonic Youth, and Motörhead from the in-game Extravert Shop before Nov 10, 2026.
  • We wanted to share a few pieces of content that caught our eye.
  • Remember: if you want to be featured in The Weekly Grind, make sure to share your screenshots, videos, clips via
  • Part of The Weekly Grind will be dedicated to sharing the current Known Issues the team is tracking, any active deployments/fixes, and next steps.
  • Skater spawns off-board and camera tracking freezes when resetting the Session Marker set at a ledge in drop-in position.
