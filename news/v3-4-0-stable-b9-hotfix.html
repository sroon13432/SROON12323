V3.4.0 Stable -b9 hotfix

7 Days to Die · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 October 2026·Source

Digging through a packed backpack in the middle of a horde is about to get much easier. V3.4.0 adds a search bar and filters to your backpack and containers, so you can find what you need in seconds. Stay alert, spend less time searching, and more time surviving!

We have now deployed a hotfix on all platforms to address the issue causing crashes in multiplayer games. This hotfix also addresses the locked inventory slots resetting after closing inventory.

What the notes say

  • Joining a dedicated server or p2p game on 3.4.0 b6 or later may transform some resource items from a client’s 3.3 inventory into different items.
  • 4, Steroids, Chicken Nuggets, and the completed Fireman’s Almanac set have been updated to work with the new backpack inventory capacity changes.
  • Missing block backgrounds in the scrollable backpack have been restored (legacy block texture previews in soft-clipped NGUI panels)

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
