Valve-sanctioned Half-Life remake Black Mesa gets a mondo update and a Steam Deck verified badge

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

Here's a terrifying reminder that the cold hand of Death is on my shoulder: I'm pretty sure I first played Black Mesa back in 2012, at the tender age of 19. In the intervening 14 years, the fan-made Half-Life 1 remake has walked quite the road, including getting Valve's official blessing to sell itself—for money!—on Steam in 2015.

And they're still going, the mad lads. Just yesterday, Black Mesa got a hefty major update "featuring visual upgrades, engine optimizations, and a complete interface overhaul". It's called the Anomalous Improvements Update and it's a real humdinger.

More from the report

  • The biggest improvement—if you are me, a man who tries to play basically everything on his Steam Deck these days—is the Steam Deck verification and much-improved controller support.
  • Black Mesa's also been optimised "to deliver significantly better performance across the entire game". By way of evidence, the devs show a side-by-side of Gordon Freeman hanging out in Xen.
  • There's other goodies too, like an overhauled UI (all the better to gamepad with), a sumptuous lighting rework, and better mod support.
