The Road to Madlu

Blade and Sorcery · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

We've traveled from the warrior Empire of Rakta, across the seafaring shores of Sentara, through the scorching deserts of Khar-Tib, and now our journey ends with perhaps the most mysterious and unusual civilization in all of Byeth.

Madlu was a big challenge for the team because of how unique this faction is, particularly when it comes to its incredibly specific lore, Dalgarian cultural influences, technology, and complicated relationship with the crystal.

What the notes say

  • And because I am a complete stickler when it comes to these things, I think it's fair to say I became something of a lore terrorist for the Warpfrog team during development, lol.
  • Both visually and mechanically, there were a lot of unusual ideas to tackle, but I can say with authority, the Warpfrog team
  • Even with every tiny little niggle I threw at the guys for this very nuanced faction, I couldn't be more proud of how they came out the other side with an A-tier representation of Madlu.
  • Long ago, the Madene inhabited a lush, tropical homeland where their mastery of sorcery enabled incredible feats.
  • However, their unchecked use of the crystal would ultimately lead to catastrophe, corrupting the land and transforming it into a hostile and dangerous place.
  • Yet, curiously, they have not abandoned the crystal itself.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
