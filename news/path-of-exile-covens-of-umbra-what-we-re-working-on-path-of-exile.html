Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra - What We’re Working On

Path of Exile · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

While we’re still monitoring feedback, we’d like to outline some of the things we’re currently working on and are planning to release as individual hotfixes as soon as they are ready:

Over the weekend, we released Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra. The launch went well, and we’re glad to see so many Exiles joining us for the newest expansion.

What the notes say

  • Looking into particular Thread mods and Fate empowerments that are individually too dangerous and out of line, with changes already on the way for some outliers.
  • Looking into smoothing out the early experience of Threads and Shaped Regions so they are less spiky and dangerous when first entering maps.
  • We will be doing a pass on existing Thread mods to generally make ones which add difficulty more worthwhile, either in making that difficulty less punitive or the rewards more enticing,
  • We will be adjusting the behaviour of Wisps released by Harvest encounters, such that they do not spread to Unique or Rare monsters by default,
  • We will adjust Thread expenditure to limit the number that can be spent early on, so that players are less likely to attempt extremely high thread counts very early,
  • Additionally, we will be doing a pass on baseline Thread stats, to lower the amount of toughness they add at lower Thread counts.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
