The Weekly Grind - Nov 10, 2026

skate. · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

In the meantime, we’ve got new Weekly Bounties. Our Baker Realistic Video Contest at Parry Point Park has started, and the search for Slappy continues with Week 5 of “Have You Seen Him?”.

We’re three weeks away from S.K.A.T.E. coming to skate., and we’ve got more stuff to share in the coming weeks, so stay tuned to official skate. channels for all the info.

What the notes say

  • Starting Monday, Nov 9, Prox Chat became available for all areas of skate.
  • - San Van Downtown, The Isle of Grom, The Stadium, and S.U.M.R.
  • For the soft launch, Prox Chat is turned off by Default.
  • You’ll need to opt in by going to Settings - Social - Proximity Voice Chat Toggle (Enable).
  • From now until Monday, Nov 16, 11:59 PM PT, show us your realistic skills in Parry Point Park and submit those clips to skate.reel.
  • 3P is a solid spot for some sick, realistic footage, so let’s see it!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
