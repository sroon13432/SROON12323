Make your season reservation and claim rewards!

Delta Force · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Details: The Halloween celebrations are almost over, but there's still time for one last supply run! Play Detonation mode during the event and complete missions such as finishing matches, planting or defusing bombs as a team, defeating enemies, and winning matches. Reach milestones to earn the final batch of Candy Tokens and grab all the Halloween-exclusive rewards!

We'll be rolling out the Halloween Update on 11/5 with no downtime. Gameplay and matchmaking will not be affected. If you encounter any issues after the update, please restart the game to complete the update. If your game client is out of date, you'll need to update to the latest version to log in.

What the notes say

  • Details: During this event, you'll score double points in both Hazard Operation Ranked matches and Warfare Grade matches.
  • Event Rewards: Play matches to get Weapon Advanced EXP Card, Weapon Intermediate EXP Card, Warfare EXP Multiplier Card (1-time).
  • Rewards: Spend Tekniq Alloy to purchase Quantum Keys, Logistics Vouchers and Advanced Secure Container 7-Day Access Card.
  • During the event, you can head to the designated entry to participate in a non-replacement draw.
  • The first draw costs only 10 Delta Coins/Delta Tickets.
  • After obtaining all rewards in a single round, the next round draw qualification will automatically unlock.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
