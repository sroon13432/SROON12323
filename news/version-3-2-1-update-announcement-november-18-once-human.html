Version 3.2.1 Update Announcement (November 18)

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

The update size is approximately 3.21 GB on PC launchers, 214 MB on the PC client (NOTE: If the update is delayed on Steam, it may be downloaded more than once), 600 MB on Android, and 615 MB on iOS (actual size may differ). We recommend downloading the update over Wi-Fi.

We will perform server maintenance and an update on November 18, 2026, 3:00 PM (PT). Due to data center relocation, the maintenance is expected to last 3.5 hours for South America regions, and 2 hours for other regions. Please manage your time wisely.

What the notes say

  • Content is under development. Final version may differ.
  • MVP Exclusive Settlement Animation: the Dungeon MVP will play a personal close-up, putting their highlight moment in the spotlight!
  • Special "Like Combat" and "Like Character" buttons: two separate buttons that let you recognize good plays or just compliment someone's look.
  • Quick Skip: Metas can click Next during animation playback to go directly to the usual data panel and quickly start the next match.
  • Content is under development. Final version may differ.
  • The Softplume Couch interaction has a special rest effect.
