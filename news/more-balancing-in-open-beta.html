More Balancing in Open Beta

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

, go to your Library, right click on Brotato and go to "Properties" of your game, then select "Betas". In the “beta participation”, select “beta” in the drop down menu.

When playing the beta branch, please deactivate and unsubscribe to all mods in the workshop. Outdated mods might cause crashes and corrupted save.

What the notes say

  • Damage raised to 10 / 12 / 15 / 18, scaling raised to 0.7 / 0.85 / 1.0 / 1.1.
  • Added to the Engineer's and the Well Rounded's starting weapons.
  • When cursed, it now boosts its elemental burning and its mine damage as well.
  • Base damage doubled on every tier, Speed scaling raised to 75 / 100 / 125 / 150%.
  • Now a starting weapon for the Wildling.
  • Its Tier I has been removed: it is no longer a starting weapon.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
