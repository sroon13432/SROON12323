The Weekly Grind - Oct 27, 2026

skate. · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

We wanted to share a few pieces of content that caught our eye. Remember: if you want to be featured in The Weekly Grind, make sure to share your screenshots, videos, clips via skate.reel, in the appropriate channel of the official Discord, or in the community-run subreddit.

On Nov 3, the skies of San Van will clear, the island will be restored as the Isle of Grom, and the Pump House park will revert - but have no fear! With Update 0.33.0, we’re bringing back the Pump House park, but without the Morg influence.

What the notes say

  • With Update 0.33.0, we’re bringing back the Pump House park, but without the Morg influence.
  • Part of The Weekly Grind will be dedicated to sharing the current Known Issues the team is tracking, any active deployments/fixes, and next steps.
  • Skater spawns off-board, and camera tracking freezes when resetting the Session Marker set at a ledge in the drop-in position.
  • When the field of view is set above 90, LOD causes aggressive texture changes each time the skater switches from onboard to offboard.
  • The camera remains stuck at 0,0,0 for the remainder of the round if the user wipes out at a greater distance from the spawn point during a throwdown.
  • Items are not being granted when a player purchases a cosmetic bundle outside of the game while a mission is actively being tracked before rank 2.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
