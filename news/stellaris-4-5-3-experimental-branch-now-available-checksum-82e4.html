Stellaris 4.5.3 Experimental branch now available [checksum 82e4]

Stellaris · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

We have a change to how the AI deals with resource stockpiles and the market, and a major change to how tooltips and modifiers in tooltips are handled. (This change does not affect production or upkeep tooltips, those are handled by a different system that were out of scope for now.) These were both risky enough that we didn't want to include them in 4.5.3 proper, but would ideally like more eyes on them before considering including them in 4.6.0.

AI empires with a nearly full Trade stockpile now spend the surplus on resources they use, based on their real expenses. (This is not currently as aggressive as it probably should be.)

What the notes say

  • AI empires that exactly meet their pop or naval capacity goal now keep setting new goals, and Nomad AI empires with high fleet usage now apply all of their naval capacity plans.
  • Modifier tooltips no longer repeat the same source several times.
  • This includes happiness, growth, pop assembly, workforce, leader experience, shipyard, megastructure build speed, FTL windup, housing, amenities, stability and crime.
  • Leader skill and job tooltips no longer show their name twice, for example "Governing Home Planet: Governing Home Planet: +5".
  • The political power, empire size, housing and amenities tooltips of a pop group now show the base value for the whole group instead of for one pop.
  • Triggered trait modifiers, for example from Aquatic, are now listed under the Species header, and a trait with several habitability modifiers now shows a single combined line.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
