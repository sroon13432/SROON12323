DayZ Badlands | Post-Release Dev Blog (Week 04) | Dog Behaviour

DayZ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

These packs of dogs are intended to act as guards for specific locations, primarily military zones. Unlike wolves, they will not actively seek you out to attack you, so they can be less of a threat depending on how you approach them. Engaging with them should be completely optional for those who do not want to eliminate them.

Welcome to DayZ Badlands post-release dev blog 2! We will be talking about additional behaviors that we are adding to the dogs with Patch 2 of the 1.30 Update. Patch 2 is slated to arrive on the experimental branches next week. More details on this later. For now, let's talk more about dogs.

What the notes say

  • As we have said before, dogs were conceived to not fit the mold of either predator or prey, but to be more of a territorial animal.
  • Currently, they do not have their final intended behavior, as they straight up aggro on you once you are in their territory.
  • With 1.30 Patch 2, you should see that their behavior follows clear steps of escalation, as opposed to attacking you on sight.
  • But that does not make it any easier, since you will have to observe each stage and act accordingly rather than simply defend against a direct attack.
  • They will perform the “sniffing the air” animation, indicating that they sense someone suspicious has entered their territory.
  • They remain stationary during this stage and will not seek you out.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
