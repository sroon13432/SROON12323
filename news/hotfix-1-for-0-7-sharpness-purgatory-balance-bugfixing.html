Hotfix 1 for 0.7 - Sharpness, Purgatory balance & bugfixing

Half Sword · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

In the future, we will be implementing a public beta branch, so we can better aprehend your needs before launching a big update. That will also help with the enourmous variety of hardware configuration out there and help us prepare launches.

What the notes say

  • Resting from Innkeeper heals your visible wounds. Jester as usual is less of a friend.
  • Fixed NPC navigation, they should stop going straight to their death in the Pit.
  • Weapon are lighter by default. Weapon weight setting affects weapons 50% less
  • Giving up is faster and in arena fights there is an indicator when you give up
  • Revenants are completely blind, they only hear
  • Revenants spawn in Purgatory changed

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
