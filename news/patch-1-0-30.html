Patch 1.0.30

Valheim · posted by the game's developer on Steam·25 November 2026·Source

It's time for a patch! This one features a bunch of fixes and improvements, including some long-awaited tweaks to the Forge of Potential. Please let us know what you think, and stay safe out there in the tenth world!

Please note: This patch will affect crossplay compatibility, so please make sure that everyone in your group has updated their game before you try to connect!

What the notes say

  • Forge of Potential will now return substantially more materials when failing
  • Forge of Potential initial RNG is no longer seeded on character names
  • Idol droprates are rebalanced to drop more evenly and show up more consistently in the biome after their target gear as intended
  • Fixed a number of items having the wrong idols for upgrading at the Forge of Potential
  • Forge of Potential location will now spawn further away from the middle of the map (only affects new worlds)
  • Made snow that had been removed by e.g. walking on terrain in Deep North regenerate over time

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
