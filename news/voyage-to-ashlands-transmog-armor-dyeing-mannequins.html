Voyage to Ashlands: Transmog, Armor Dyeing & Mannequins

Windrose · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

We're back with another look at what's coming in Ashlands, our next major update for Windrose! Over the past few devblogs, we've been talking quite a bit about ships and the ways you'll be able to make them your own. But a fine vessel deserves an equally fine-looking captain, doesn't it?

As you progress through Windrose, you'll come across all sorts of armor, each with its own appearance and characteristics. Naturally, some pieces might suit your preferred playstyle better, while others simply look too good to leave behind.

What the notes say

  • Naturally, some pieces might suit your preferred playstyle better, while others simply look too good to leave behind.
  • With the introduction of transmog, you won't have to choose between the two anymore.
  • For those unfamiliar with the term, transmogrification, or transmog for short, is a system that lets you change your character's appearance without affecting their equipment stats.
  • In other words, you can wear one set of armor for its protection and bonuses while displaying the appearance of another.
  • We're adding a new set of five cosmetic armor slots to the character equipment panel: head, torso, arms, legs, and feet.
  • You'll be able to switch between your regular equipment and cosmetic slots directly in the inventory.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
