Nominate Darkest Dungeon for the Labor of Love Award

Darkest Dungeon® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

We have been been developing a new and improved Mod Manager. This Mod Manager is designed to give you a better way to launch, organize, and adjust the load order of your mods. The tool is currently in our coming_in_hot branch, and will soon be released to our retail branches.

2026 was a big year for us! It marked the 10th anniversary of Darkest Dungeon's wide release. We celebrated that milestone with a surprise new DLC, The Fire's Edge. This DLC was released on August 18th, 2026 and added two new heroes, a new burn mechanic and more!
