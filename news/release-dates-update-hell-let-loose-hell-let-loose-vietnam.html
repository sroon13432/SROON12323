Release Dates Update | Hell Let Loose & Hell Let Loose: Vietnam

Hell Let Loose · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

We’re sorry to say that the release date for Updates 22 has been pushed back as a result of the team17 infrastructure issues. Team17’s technical teams have been working to resolve the problem as quickly as possible so that Expression Games can continue delivering the upcoming content planned for Hell Let Loose.

With the end of 2026 approaching, and major updates for both Hell Let Loose: Vietnam and Hell Let Loose on the horizon, we wanted to share news on the scheduled releases for both games.

What the notes say

  • After assessing their impact, we’ve had to revisit our original content plans for Update 22.
  • The focus is now on delivering our newest map, Lake Balaton, in early December.
  • For those already getting ready for the winter break, Lake Balaton and its frozen landscape will soon be drawing you back into the action.
  • Take a first look at the newest map coming to Hell Let Loose below.[dynamiclink href="https://youtu.be/LdyQKvYFooM"]
  • The content deferred as part of these changes remains in active development as we continue developing and refining these upcoming additions, including new vehicles.
  • We’re currently aiming to bring this content to players in early 2027.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
