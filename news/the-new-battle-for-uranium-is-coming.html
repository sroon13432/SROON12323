The new “Battle for uranium” is coming

Crossout Remastered · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Hello, survivors! The new “Battle for uranium” stage will start on Thursday, November 5. This time, the battles will be held in the “Arena” format, which many of the experienced players are very familiar with. Prepare a suitable vehicle and fight to get the unique valuable rewards!

Attention! The “Battle for uranium” will last from November 5 to November 11! The “Clan confrontation” will be unavailable during the “Battle for uranium”. The mode will be available during even hours (GMT time).

What the notes say

  • Now, if two or more players have the same number of points, the player who earned those points first will be ranked higher.
  • Earning points in “Battle for uranium” now depends more heavily on the player’s contribution to the victory, not only on the battle result.
  • Individual efficiency becomes a more important factor in point calculation.
  • When the stage ends, the best players in the “Legend” league will receive additional rewards according to their placement:
  • The players who place 1–100 will additionally receive a safe with rewards, which will be added to player accounts upon claiming the safe.
  • The higher your place in the leaderboard, the more rewards the safe will contain.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
