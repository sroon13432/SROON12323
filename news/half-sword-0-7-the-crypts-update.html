Half Sword 0.7: The Crypts Update

Half Sword · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

It is the first part of our Dungeon Generator system that we will eventually use to create different types of interiors. We started with Crypt because it seemed thematically appropriate for the season, adventurous and "dungeony", but later we will also have more grounded places built in the same generator.

Currently all the layouts of the rooms are premade, but characters and items are spawned there randomly. We are working on cell generation system to create rooms from scratch to mix premade rooms with generated ones.

What the notes say

  • We are working on cell generation system to create rooms from scratch to mix premade rooms with generated ones.
  • In this update you won't be able to take mercenaries with you in the Crypts, but functionality for that is almost done and will be added with one of the patches.
  • To leave the floor with your weapons, armors, and almost-honestly-earned gold, vanquish a boss and choose to walk up the stairs to return safely to the tavern.
  • Or continue your journey for the most exquisite and advanced loot...
  • You put valuables in Shrine, close the lid, open it and get Bread.
  • Purgatory is a special room inside the Crypt, called "Sepulchral Hall".

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
