Update 4.21.0

Throne and Liberty · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

> The effect has a 20% chance to apply Collision: Prone for 2.5 seconds. This chance increases to 80% against targets that are Bound, Sleeping, or Stunned, or using Static Defense.

Maintenance for Update 4.21.0 will begin on November 17, 2026 at 9:30 PM PT (November 18 at 2:30 PM KST / 6:30 AM CET) and is expected to last approximately 5 hours.

What the notes say

  • Adjusted the effects of several Weapon Skills and Skill Cores and corrected hit detection issues.
  • Updated Archboss Skill Core acquisition requirements and improved chest preview and tooltip clarity.
  • Achievements that grant Archboss Weapon Skill Cores will no longer be completed when you obtain a sealed Archboss Weapon.
  • The following changes apply to characters who previously completed an Achievement and received its Skill Core by obtaining the corresponding sealed Archboss Weapon:
  • Archboss Weapons can once again be traded on the Auction House.
  • Skill Core information has been removed from other characters' Heroic Weapons. Equipped Skill Cores can now be viewed in that character's equipment details.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
