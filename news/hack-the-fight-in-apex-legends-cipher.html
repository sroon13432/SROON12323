Hack the fight in Apex Legends™: Cipher

Apex Legends™ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

Thanks to a new firmware upgrade from his sister Mila, Crypto’s role has switched from passive to aggressive intel-gathering. His Surveillance Drone now runs on batteries instead of a cooldown, and the new Sentry Mode tags enemies in sight for you and your squad. Plus, his EMP Ultimate can now be deployed with the press of a button. You’ll know their next move before they do.

In this ring, the deadliest weapon isn’t a gun, it’s information. Crypto’s new firmware update switches his role to aggressive intel-gathering, the Akimbo Wingman adds deadly precision firepower, and new Strongholds offer high-stakes, high-tier loot.

What the notes say

  • If the deadliest weapon is information, the second deadliest is dual-wielded revolvers.
  • The Akimbo Wingman is a dangerous combo of precision and firepower.
  • Take aim, choose your shots carefully, and be the dead-eyed sharpshooter you were born to be.
  • New Strongholds are the evolution of Care Packages, bringing high-tier, high-risk loot in a very loud package.
  • Plan your heist and take the loot, or let another squad ring the dinner bell for you.
  • Play Revenant more aggressively with a shorter Ultimate cooldown, a quicker Tactical, and new Upgrades that extend your Ultimate with every knock.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
