Update 15.10 | Horror From the Abyss, European Cruisers, and Orzeł!

World of Warships · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Earn Stars to climb a reward track filled with six glowing-rune permanent camouflages, three nationless Commanders, the Shadow Blades enemy destruction effect, flags, and Relics.

Four Operations unlock weekly and return in random rotation with adjusted scenarios during the first 2 weeks of Update 15.11.

What the notes say

  • Four Operations unlock weekly and return in random rotation with adjusted scenarios during the first 2 weeks of Update 15.11.
  • Pick up three lower-tier ships via the Event Pass, with the rest available through a sequential bundle and updated Europe containers.
  • The first line includes 6x Black Friday 2026 containers, Tier IV cruiser
  • Keep going for 6x Black Friday 2026 containers, battleship
  • Her unusual torpedo layout includes two external rotating launchers, so she performs best on the surface.
  • Find her with new Commander
