Preview: The Madlu Update

Blade and Sorcery · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Given this is the last of the Byeth Updates, the team really went all out with the weapons, so a grand total of 5 out of the 8 new weapons have special functionality.

Work on 1.4 is progressing very well, as the team are now feature-complete and working on the final bits of polish and bug fixing.

What the notes say

  • The Madene Claws are beast-inspired handheld weapons that augment the user's fists with a set of extremely deadly blades.
  • If you've ever wanted to live out your fantasy of being a certain angry, claw-wielding superhero, well, this one is for you!
  • This crystal-infused device was originally developed to safely handle and manipulate crystals, given the extreme precautions the Madene take when dealing with crystal technology.
  • However, its versatility quickly became apparent, and the device found applications far beyond its original purpose, including combat.
  • It's a neat example of the Madene philosophy, where technology originally developed for practical purposes can be repurposed into something rather dangerous.
  • And, as you might imagine, a device designed around grasping and manipulating things has some interesting potential in a physics-based game!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
