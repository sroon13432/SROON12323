The New World vs. World Borderlands Map Beta Is Live!

Guild Wars 2® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Inspired by Ascalon before and during the Searing, this new battlefield brings one of Tyria's most iconic locations into World vs. World. For the duration of the beta, all borderlands maps have been temporarily replaced with the new map, giving everyone plenty of opportunities to explore and face off in battle. The beta runs through December 8.

For this first beta, we're particularly interested in how the map feels to play. How does it feel to navigate? Are objectives placed in interesting locations? Where are fights happening, and how does the terrain affect combat?

What the notes say

  • Where are fights happening, and how does the terrain affect combat?
  • The new War Score system, which we've been testing with the community, is officially coming out of beta.
  • The updated scoring will take effect with the WvW reset on Friday, November 13.
  • We'll continue monitoring the system and making adjustments as needed.
  • We're also officially releasing the new skirmish and match reward systems.
  • Skirmish rewards are available starting today, while match rewards will begin with the November 13 reset.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
