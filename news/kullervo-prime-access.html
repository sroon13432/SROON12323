Kullervo Prime Access

Warframe · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 November 2026·Source

Kullervo transcends his perpetual prison to join the ranks of Prime Access on December 9 alongside the release of Warframe: Tau. Embellished with the gladiatorial trappings of a preeminent praetorian, he seeks his redemption armed with potent Prime Weapons and exclusive Prime Accessories.

The golden promise of Kullervo's origin was cruelly undercut by his treacherous fate. Restore his dignity, and guide him to a new, better destiny.

What the notes say

  • The iron of Kullervo Prime’s signature shotgun is transmuted to gleaming gold, boasting massively increased status chance and magazine size.
  • When you require the finest and most powerful armaments, only Primes will do.
  • Exquisitely gilded, every Prime Warframe, Weapon and item exists in its purest form — just as the Orokin intended.
  • Learn more about how you can claim them for yourself with our official video guide.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
