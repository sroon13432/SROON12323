Battlefield 6 Season 5: Casino Heist

Battlefield™ 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Fight on the Strip and the casino floor in high-stakes battles in Conquest and Escalation, complete contracts to earn DOUGH (a new limited time currency) to redeem for private security and casino-themed cosmetics and rewards. Go all-in on Battlefield REDSEC with Battle Royale and Rapid Royale, the fastest and most aggressive BR, set on Mirak Valley and the iconic Las Vegas Strip.

At the European, there’s something for every squad: Create your own flanks with fully-destructible facades, engage in close quarters combat on the casino floor, take a sniping post on a balcony, or relax and rush the lazy river trenches. We hope you enjoy your stay.

What the notes say

  • This MRAS (Mobile Rocket Artillery System) is a lightweight rocket launcher vehicle perfect for unleashing area denial devastation while on the move.
  • The NSW4 is a highly-modular, short range carbine, extensively customizable for every combat situation.
  • With default subsonic ammo, the NSW4 is the perfect weapon for staying under the radar when firing.
  • Utilize its full potential by mastering both combat and weapon knowledge.
  • The Signal Jammer is a recon’s secret weapon for eliminating the enemy’s reinforcements and intel–including distorting maps, killfeed, objective status, and enemy location.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
