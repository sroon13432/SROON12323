Developer Update: Bandits Are Coming in Early 2027

7 Days to Die · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Bandits are one of the most anticipated features for our players, and delivering an experience that meets your expectations is our top priority. Through extensive playtesting, we’ve made great progress, but we believe the experience needs a little more time to reach its full potential.

We want to share more with you about what this process looks like, and so today, we're giving you a first look at how the Bandits update is coming together.

What the notes say

  • For the first time in 7 Days to Die, you won’t be the only survivors roaming Navezgane, and zombies won’t be your primary threat.
  • These bandits answer to the Duke of Navezgane, and they aren’t just zombies with guns.
  • They introduce an entirely new kind of challenge – one that forces you to rethink how you approach combat.
  • Instead of rushing into a kill zone, they’ll flank, regroup, or force you to come to them.
  • They’ll also heal themselves and fight off zombies to stay alive.
  • A small group of bandits can overwhelm a well-geared survivor through teamwork.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
