3.1.1 Bug Fixes (October 29)

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Recently, we have received feedback from many Metas that in the Voyager game mode on the Isles of Abyss, wreckage dropped after a ship is sunk may be looted by other players, and ships may be deliberately sunk before loot crates are picked up, affecting the normal reward obtain experience. To address this issue, we have adjusted the drop rules after a Voyager ship is sunk.

This means that even if you fail to enter the cabin to pick up the loot crate before the ship sinks, you will not directly miss out on the corresponding rewards because the ship was sunk. At the same time, it also reduces disputes over reward ownership caused by wreckage looting, making reward obtain in the Voyager game mode more stable and clear.

What the notes say

  • At the same time, it also reduces disputes over reward ownership caused by wreckage looting, making reward obtain in the Voyager game mode more stable and clear.
  • Modified the descriptions of Isles of Abyss specialty resources in interfaces such as the Extraction Pump and Automatic Mining Platform.
  • The modification now clarifies that automatic Production can only be carried out within the island range of the corresponding calamity zone.
