Early Access Supporter Packs Leaving Soon

Path of Exile 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

Each Early Access Supporter Pack includes microtransactions that can be used in both Path of Exile 1 and Path of Exile 2, along with Path of Exile 2 Early Access keys, points to spend in the store, and exclusive in-game microtransactions available only through these packs. Higher tiers also include physical merchandise, such as T-shirts, hoodies, and a Path of Exile 2 artbook.

It’s been almost two years since we released the Early Access Supporter Packs, and it’s almost time for them to go. They’ll be leaving the store forever in a few weeks, to be replaced by a set of five new packs. So, if you still want to upgrade or purchase one of the current packs, there’s not much time left.

What the notes say

  • In addition, Liberator of Wraeclast supporters have the opportunity to nominate a unique reward for inclusion in the Twilight Order Reliquary in Path of Exile 2.
  • This allows you to reserve a pack for up to three months from the start of your payment plan.
  • Simply email us in advance at support@grindinggear.com, and we'll be happy to help.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
