BLACK FRIDAY Campaign

eFootball™ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Earn rewards in the Campaign Hub by playing PvP or VS AI matches during the designated period and then advancing a certain number of Spaces. (Any progress in Exhibition, Training or Skill Up modes does not apply.)

Earn a ticket and get a reward from the ensuing draw. Each type of ticket provides a different kind of reward. A Platinum Ticket gives you a chance of getting up to 5,000 eFootball™ Coins!

What the notes say

  • In this Event, play matches after devising a Game Plan that will lead your squad to one victory after another until the schedule ends.
  • Get rewards for each match won, but note that Player Cards used in a previous victory cannot be selected in subsequent matches during the Event.
  • In this Event, build a Game Plan that increases Player Bonuses and play matches while aiming to complete Bonus Targets.
  • The Bonus Targets completed, Player Bonuses and match results affect how many points are earned after matches.
