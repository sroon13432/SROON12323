v1.15.25 - The Fixes Update!

Stormworks: Build and Rescue · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

There are also fixes for the hydrogen fuel cell, bullet holes draining compartment water above the waterline, explosives on moving vehicles, the train piston, custom springs, the microprocessor editor after alt tabbing, and more. We have also updated the crash reporter to work with our new support website, which will help us keep finding and fixing crashes going forward.

Islands now stay loaded a tile further out and 200m higher above you before unloading, so aircraft and tall vehicles should see less of the world disappearing beneath them. Cargo packages are no longer deleted mid-delivery, and tiles unloaded by the server no longer sometimes stay loaded for multiplayer clients.

What the notes say

  • Cargo packages are no longer deleted mid-delivery, and tiles unloaded by the server no longer sometimes stay loaded for multiplayer clients.
  • Despite this update being focused on fixes and improvements, there are new features and upgrades planned for many of the upcoming updates, and lots to be excited about.
  • Thanks to all players who have given detailed reports and helped us identify these problems.
  • Rework - crash reports now upload automatically and the crash message only shows the crash summary and where the report was saved
