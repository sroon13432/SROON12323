CAPE VINE!

IdleOn - The Incremental MMO · posted by the game's developer on Steam·1 November 2026·Source

Earn 7 unique research related bonuses as you level up your account's Research LV to 1000! Since Research LV is shared across all characters, you basically just need to get to Lv 91 given you have 11 characters.

You can deposit your capes here to make their bonuses account wide! Because capes no longer give stats when equipped, you can wear whatever cape looks nicest!

What the notes say

  • The Cape Vine and its custodian Catherine have arrived in World 4!
  • You can deposit your capes here to make their bonuses account wide! Because capes no longer give stats when equipped, you can wear whatever cape looks nicest!
  • You can take out as many capes as you want, whenever you want. This means you can equip the same cape on all characters!
