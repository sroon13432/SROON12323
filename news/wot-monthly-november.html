WoT Monthly: November

World of Tanks · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

A satellite silent for months has begun transmitting again—and you're not the only ones listening. From November 12, join task force Switchblade in brand-new 7v7 Tier X battles. Secure Antennas, hold your ground, and get to the secrets first.

Load up your favorite Tier VIII vehicles (and check out the ones on sale) to prepare for huge maps and 30v30 battles! Capture sectors, break through enemy defenses, and destroy key objectives as your team pushes across the battlefield.

What the notes say

  • Capture sectors, break through enemy defenses, and destroy key objectives as your team pushes across the battlefield.
  • Rally your clan for competitive battles in Tier XI vehicles!
  • Choose from eight vehicles with unique abilities, then scavenge supplies and grow stronger as the battle progresses.
  • Fight solo or in a Platoon, and see if you can be the last hunter standing.
  • Tier XI takes center stage in Clan Showdown, sponsored by Opera GX, before the world's best teams battle for the WCI title in Chongqing, China.
  • Watch the video for more of what's coming, then head to the full article for the interactive November calendar, event dates, and details.
