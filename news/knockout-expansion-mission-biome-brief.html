Knockout Expansion - Mission & biome brief

Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Knockout is still cooking, but we are ready to reveal more of the ingredients. Before you meet the newest dwarf on the roster, let's look at your new objectives and the new biome that set the stage for this dwarf vs glyphid grudge match. Without further ado, introducing:

That's where you come in. Your job is to gather as much Kytoplasm as possible, by any means necessary. This non-Newtonian substance is so valuable that management has authorized additional support for dwarves who gather it in great amounts.

What the notes say

  • The source of Kytoplasm is the Broodlings found within the mission.
  • They are cowardly little creatures, so we expect you to chase them down with extreme prejudice.
  • The more supplies we deliver, the angrier the bugs get, and this time they don't cool down between stages, so the threat level will only keep escalating throughout the mission.
  • The fourth stage is a full boss stage, meaning the Dreadnought Empress is there to greet any dwarf entering her hive, ready to throw her brood at you.
  • The Empress may use cowardly, defensive tactics and let her brood do the fighting, but she is a major threat to any dwarf who underestimates her.
  • The newest biome expands the fight to a dry, inhospitable place.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
