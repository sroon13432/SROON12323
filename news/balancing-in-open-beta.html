Balancing in Open Beta

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

First of all, thank you to everyone who took the time to left their impression on the Steam Discussion, Discord, Reddit and everywhere else. We truly appreciate it.

Hello Brotatoes. It's been 4 days since the release of Primal Dread DLC, it's time to try our hands at some balancing after reading your feedback!

What the notes say

  • Variant: replaced "50% chance that a mutating item is replaced by a mutated item of a better tier" with "25% chance for an item to mutate when you equip it" (bought in the shop or taken…
  • Time Shifter: Dodge upgrades no longer appear
  • Arsonist: Engineering upgrades no longer appear
  • Chimera Cells (Uncommon, Mutant): +10% chance for an item to mutate when you equip it. Max 5. Unlocked by winning a run with Variant
  • Wildfire Mutation is no longer Variant's unlock and is now unlocked by default
  • Mutation: copied effects get stronger faster: 70% strength at 40 Evolution (was ~67) and full strength at 100 (was 150). Pets, structures and some effects can be copied from 40 Evolution

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
