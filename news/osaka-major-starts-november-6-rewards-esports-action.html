Osaka Major Starts November 6: Rewards & Esports Action

Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

From November 6 to 15, follow every match as elite teams battle through one of the most important events of the season. Whether you're tuning in for the competition, supporting your favorite organizations, or looking to earn rewards, there are plenty of ways to get involved.

Predictions are now open for the Osaka Major. Submit your picks before matches begin, follow the tournament as it unfolds, and earn rewards for making the right calls.

What the notes say

  • Exclusive weapon skin rewards
  • Submit your picks before matches begin, follow the tournament as it unfolds, and earn rewards for making the right calls.
  • The Osaka Major Bundle lets you bring the spirit of the event into every match with a collection of limited-time cosmetic content created specifically for this year's competition.
  • It's an opportunity to support the teams and players you follow throughout the season.
  • Explore official R6 Share team bundles and represent your favorite organizations in-game.
  • Whether you're backing a long-time favorite or discovering a new team during the event, there has never been a better time to get involved in the Rainbow Six Esports scene.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
