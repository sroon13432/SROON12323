40 New User-Created Camouflages in the Eagle’s Shadow Trophy!

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

The Eagle’s Shadow major update has brought a wealth of new vehicles to War Thunder, and we know you’re eager to deck them out in unique camouflages. The Eagle’s Shadow trophy is here to help, containing a variety of camouflages for these newly added vehicles, as well as the best entries from the autumn camouflage contest on WT Live! In addition to camouflages, the trophy may also contain coupons for rare vehicles and a unique decal.

To unlock the trophy, you’ll need the new “Eagle’s Shadow” key that can be purchased on the Market for GJN. For each key you buy, a portion of the sale is split among the skin makers in the box, so with each purchase you are directly supporting our talented community! Read more about the

What the notes say

  • Camouflage trophies can be earned with a specific probability after any battle with vehicles rank II or higher, with activity of no less than 50% (except for \ PvE mode).
  • PlayStation and Xbox players can purchase the trophy in the Item Shop and open it with Golden Eagles.
  • PC players must purchase a key from the Market to open the trophy, with part of each sale going to the camouflage creators.
  • You can activate a key from the trophy on your account or sell it on the Market for Gaijin Coins —  just like the trophy itself.
  • Additionally, all authors who are included in these trophies will receive a unique title, player icon and decal.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
