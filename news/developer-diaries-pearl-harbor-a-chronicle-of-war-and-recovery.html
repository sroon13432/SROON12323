Developer Diaries: Pearl Harbor: A Chronicle of War and Recovery

World of Warships · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

. To commemorate the occasion, we are dedicating a historical adventure to the events that followed. Told from the American perspective, this adventure honors the heroic deeds of military personnel and civilians alike while exploring the aftermath of the attack.

During the adventure, you'll have opportunities to rent Honolulu and Akagi. You can either rent each ship for one week, or one of them for two weeks.

What the notes say

  • Five consecutive chapters, unlocking in stages throughout the Update.
  • The event will be available from December 7th and will last until the release of Update 16.0.
  • During the adventure, you'll have opportunities to rent Honolulu and Akagi. You can either rent each ship for one week, or one of them for two weeks.
  • In some chapters, you'll be able to choose between two combat mission chains, which offer a differing narrative and gameplay choices to play through.
  • A seasoned Commander paired with your chosen ship: American Isaac C. Kidd with Honolulu or Japanese Chūichi Nagumo with Akagi
  • Remember Pearl Harbor flag

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
