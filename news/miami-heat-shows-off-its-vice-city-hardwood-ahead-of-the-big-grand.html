Miami Heat shows off its 'Vice City hardwood' ahead of the big Grand Theft Auto 6 basketball game

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

Grand Theft Auto 6 launches at midnight on November 19 in your local time zone, meaning that people in New Zealand will be playing it while I'm still stuck at work on November 18. Unless, that is, you do your gaming on a PC, in which case GTA 6 won't be launching at all—at least, not until some indeterminate span of time has passed and Take-Two Interactive deigns to bestow it upon us.

But that doesn't mean we can't indulge in all the pre-release hype, just like everyone else, and so here we are with a look at the "Vice City hardwood," upon which NBA team the Miami Heat—in the guise of the Vice City's hometown team—will square off against the Milwaukee Bucks.

More from the report

  • It's an extensive overhaul, with a huge Vice City logo at center court, the VI logos in the paint, and the "I Heart Vice City" slogans at either end.
  • Renting an entire NBA team as a promotional tool really puts a finger on just how big Grand Theft Auto 6 is.
  • And it's not just the dollar signs that impress: GTA 6 is also being recognized as a mainstream cultural phenomenon in ways that previous games in the series never were, or could be.
  • The Vice City-ified Miami Heat will face the Milwaukee Bucks on November 18, one day before the release of Grand Theft Auto 6.
