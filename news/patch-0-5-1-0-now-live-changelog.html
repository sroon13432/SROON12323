Patch 0.5.1.0 Now Live - Changelog

Gray Zone Warfare · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Enemies should no longer magically know your exact position after hearing a gunshot. Instead, sound only gives them an approximate location, and the farther away you are, the less accurate their guess becomes. AI enemies are no longer telepathic either! Rather than instantly sharing your exact position, they'll rely on information they can actually gather, such as gunfire or the screams of their comrades.

We've made significant changes to how AI enemies detect and track players, with one clear goal: AI should be dangerous because it's smart, without creating the impression that it's cheating.

What the notes say

  • We've also made stealth more consistent, so moving slowly should make you harder to detect.
  • Enemies now react more naturally to light and use flashlights when appropriate.
  • We've also improved how enemies behave in combat, from weapon handling and cover usage to movement and positioning.
  • Trained soldiers should fight more precisely and make better use of cover, while untrained enemies will behave more recklessly.
  • Enemies should also reposition more intelligently, return fire while moving, and navigate buildings more naturally, making firefights more dynamic and varied.
  • On top of that, we've addressed several frustrating issues with hitboxes, grenades, and enemy movement, including enemies getting stuck behind doors.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
