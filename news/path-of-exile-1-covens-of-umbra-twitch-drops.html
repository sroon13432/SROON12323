Path of Exile 1: Covens of Umbra Twitch Drops

Path of Exile · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

To show our gratitude to our amazing community of creators, we’ll also be offering the Umbral Portal Effect for purchasing or gifting 2 Twitch Subscriptions of any tier during the twitch drop promotion.

To celebrate the launch of Path of Exile 1: Covens of Umbra, we’ll be running a week of Twitch Drops starting from November 13th 11:00AM PST!

What the notes say

  • Log in to your Twitch account.
  • Visit any Twitch channel in Path of Exile 1 category.
  • While the channel is live streaming Path of Exile 1, purchase a cumulative of two subscriptions of any tier to earn the Umbral Portal Effect. (Prime Subs are excluded).
  • New monthly subs (any tier)
  • Multi-month subs (3-month, 6-month)
  • Gift subs (any tier for any number of months)
