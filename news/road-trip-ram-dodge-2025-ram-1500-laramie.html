Road Trip: RAM & Dodge - 2025 RAM 1500 Laramie

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·9 November 2026·Source

You'll be able to choose from five versions of the Laramie: Standard, Night Edition, Sport Appearance, Southwest Edition, and Chrome Appearance Package. Each has its own look, so there's room to find a style that suits you. The Standard and Chrome packages keep things classic with chrome details, while the Sport Appearance package gives the pickup a sportier look. If you prefer darker details, the Night Edition is another option to consider.

The 2025 RAM 1500 Laramie is a full-size American pickup with a spacious cabin, bold styling, and plenty of power under the hood. Its wide body, tall profile, and clean lines give it a strong presence on the road. Measuring over 20 feet long and nearly 7 feet wide, it's a sizeable pickup that's hard to miss.
