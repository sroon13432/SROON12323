Welcome to the peaceful Plumbob Palms. It’s not your average, retirement home, no, no, here at Plumbob Palms every Elder has a little something to tickle their fancy, from recreation areas, dips in the pools (or skinny dipping - I don’t judge 😉) classes, knitting and so much more, there is truly something for everyone, and I mean
