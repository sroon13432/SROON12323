Community Update - October

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

It’s time for our monthly Community Update! We’re keeping things a little lighter this time around, as the team is fully focused on cooking up new content for Patch 16 and beyond. Don’t worry, we’ll have plenty to show you soon!

Recently, we hosted a Q&A on Reddit, and got many entries. Game Director Dmitry Grigorenko answered a bunch of questions from you. The entire retranscription is here!

What the notes say

  • Recently, we hosted a Q&A on Reddit, and got many entries.

  • : Added brief invulnerability after knockback, fixed item spawns out of bounds, and resolved cloth/robe customization color bugs.
