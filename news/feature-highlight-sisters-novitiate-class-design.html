Feature Highlight - Sisters Novitiate Class Design

Warhammer 40,000: Darktide · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

As the eighth class for the game, we have so much to look forward to, so we sat down with Victor, Gunnar and Teodor to chat. We wanted to know how the class fits into the Warband, what new mechanics it brings, what new weapons it will wield and everything in between!

Heresy Shall Be Met With Fire and Righteous Wrath as the Sisters Novitiate Arrive in Warhammer 40,000: Darktide on December 7th!

What the notes say

  • “I am Victor, Design Director on Darktide, and I can’t come up with a good quote right now… I’m sure you will add that in post”
  • “I am Gunnar, Lead Game Designer on Darktide, and I claimed to make a better tiramisú than Matteo, but then we had a cook-off and he won with eight votes to my seven…”
  • “I am Teodor, Class Designer on Darktide, and like Victor I am too excited to talk about the Sister Novitiate to come up with a fun intro on the spot”
  • The Sisters Novitiate are young recruits to the Adepta Sororitas, the fighting force of the Adeptus Ministorum.
  • Warriors in training that are yet to earn the powerful Power Armour and Bolter weapons of the God-Emperor.
  • But they are no pushovers as they have the training and specialised equipment to combat the ever growing threats on Atoma.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
