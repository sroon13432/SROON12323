Brotato Primal Dread DLC - Out Now!

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Although we are releasing, making Brotato is a never ending process. That's why we plan to make some changes based on your feedback now that the DLC is out. So please, tell us what you think on Discord, Reddit and the Steam Discussions!

Abyssal Terrors was all about diving into the depths of an alien planet. So, we had to think “what’s better than giant evil fish?” And then someone said the word "dinosaur", and the rest is history.

What the notes say

  • Abyssal Terrors was all about diving into the depths of an alien planet.
  • So, we had to think “what’s better than giant evil fish?” And then someone said the word "dinosaur", and the rest is history.
  • In Primal Dread, you will venture into the Jungle and face the deadly dinosaurs that roam this alien forest!
  • With a very different look from Crash Zone and Abyss, the Jungle is the home of many new enemies, and a couple of other new things as well...
  • But potatoes aren't supposed to have 6 arms either so just go along with it.
  • As we're introducing a new environmental hazard, the dreaded holes, flying dinosaurs were an absolute must.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
