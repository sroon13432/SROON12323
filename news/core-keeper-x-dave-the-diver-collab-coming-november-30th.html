Core Keeper x Dave the Diver Collab Coming November 30th!

Core Keeper · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

We’re adding new snacks to the game (Whole-Roasted Shark Head, anyone?), new sushi-restaurant themed decorations, a new weapon, a new armour set, and a new merchant with this update. And that’s just the content! Naturally, we’ll be fixing bugs, improving graphics and performance, and making some adjustments to modding so that it’s easier to create and add your own pets (and reskin existing ones) in Core Keeper.

We’re very excited to announce our next minor content update will be a Core Keeper x Dave the Diver collaboration 🐟🎣 Coming on November 30th, you can expect a nice chunk of crossover content to appear in the underground. From a brand-new Blue Hole scene in the Sunken Sea biome to TWO adorable new pets – a Momo cat and some kind of baby manatee…oh, wait, that’s just Dave 😅 Our bad.
