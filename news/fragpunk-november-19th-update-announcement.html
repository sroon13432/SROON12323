FragPunk November 19th Update Announcement

FragPunk · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

The "Mirror Clash" event starts on November 19th! The popular "Mirror Clash" mode is available for a limited time. Complete event challenges to earn rewards including Glunite Coins*500, "Shard Voyagers" Sticker Pack*2, Basic Sticker Pack*3, Basic Decoration Pack*1, Ultra Can*1, "Sushi Kiities" Sticker*1, and the Lantern Gargoyle Headdress!

FragPunk is scheduled for a maintenance update at 10:00 on November 19th, with an estimated downtime of about 3 hours. Here's what's coming in this update!

What the notes say

  • Fixed the issue where the character state was not correctly reset after exiting the respawn process in special circumstances.
  • Fixed an issue where container mapping errors were incorrectly reported for backpacks and delivery boxes in vehicle backpack slots.
  • Fixed the issue where the sword energy special effects would go missing when "Odin" released charged sword energy in a row.
  • Fixed an issue where an outline appeared on the character's hands when aiming after using a deployable and applying character effects.
  • Fixed an issue where the upper body would shake after sprinting for a while in the relaxed stance with light bows or long melee weapons equipped.
  • Fixed an issue where the "Focus" attribute caused enemies to stay locked onto their target when only one player was locked on.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
