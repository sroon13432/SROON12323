Thank you, Survivors!

DayZ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

We’re thrilled to announce that more than 325,500 units of DayZ Badlands have been purchased across all platforms, and DayZ reached a peak of more than 435,500 concurrent players during the launch period. It’s been a massive success, not just in terms of sales, but also in the amount of community content, opening up more doors and opportunities for DayZ and its community as a whole. We’re humbled by your support and sincerely appreciate all the love you’ve shown Nasdara. You can find the data below:

The work doesn’t stop here. We released the first patch last week, addressing a number of issues. Some modding and community related issues arose along the way, and we’re making internal changes to help future patches roll out more smoothly and consistently. We have also reached out and working to improve our communication with server owners and modders.

What the notes say

  • We released the first patch last week, addressing a number of issues.
  • Some modding and community related issues arose along the way, and we’re making internal changes to help future patches roll out more smoothly and consistently.
  • We have also reached out and working to improve our communication with server owners and modders.
  • We’re also working on a hotfix focused on crashes and other Badlands-related problems that need more urgent attention.
  • We expect it to enter Experimental as soon as this week Thursday (find the patch notes here), followed by a Stable release next week.
  • After that, a second patch will address more issues and make adjustments to some features in DayZ Badlands.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
