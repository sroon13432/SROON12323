Bungie Community Update – October 2026

Destiny 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

We're also exploring more ways to get you updates on the fly. Marathon side, we’ve started a string of Dev Insights leading into Symbiosis and the March Update. These take a bit of time to film and produce, so we’re also looking into smaller, more casual spaces to have discussions. Podcasts? Livestreams? AMAs? We’ve done many of these in the past, but we need to rebuild some bones to get them up to speed again. I could talk on and on about our comms pillars and how we’ll keep chatting as we move forward into the next chapter, but I know what you’re all here for. Video games. More specifically, Bungie video games.

To be clear, this isn’t going to become a weekly series like a TWID/TWAB. Rather, this will be a general space for us to give updates on our studio. Sometimes we’ll talk about Destiny, other times Marathon, and every so often we may just chat about general Bungie news that needs more space than a social media post. We want to make sure that whenever we speak with you there’s a human tone and a takeaway, so we’ll want to take appropriate amounts of time between entries to get you real, meaningful news.
