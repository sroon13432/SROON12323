Grand Theft Auto 6 earned $3.79 billion on launch day

PC Gamer · industry report·20 November 2026·Source

Some very rough napkin math led to estimates that Take-Two Interactive expected to make as much as $3.5 billion during the Grand Theft Auto 6 launch period. It actually made more than that on the GTA 6 launch day: Rockstar says the game pulled in $3.79 billion in "day one retail sales," making it "the biggest entertainment launch in history."

By way of comparison, Avengers: Endgame, ranked on Wikipedia as the highest grossing movie of all time, earned a little more than $2.9 billion over its entire theatrical run. GTA 6 crushed that by nearly $1 billion in a day.
