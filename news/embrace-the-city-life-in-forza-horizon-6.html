Embrace the City Life in Forza Horizon 6

Forza Horizon 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Added a new filter to the Garage and Autoshow menus that, when enabled, shows any special restrictions used in the active Playlist. These display under a "Festival Playlist" header with a toggle for each restriction and make it easier for you to find which cars can be used before you purchase or drive them.

. We've made this change based on community feedback, with the intent of making new cars easier to earn for all players, including those without access to the game's online content.

What the notes say

  • Fixed an issue with the 1990 Nissan #12 Skyline GT-R (BNR32 Gr.A) JTC where players could not place stickers over the windshield banner.
  • Check out the release notes on our Forza Support site for a full overview of the fixes and other changes that are included with this release.
  • We understand this will be disappointing to players with legitimate PR Stunt scores; however, we’ve chosen this approach so that way everyone has a clean sheet to play with going forward.
  • Regardless of the Leaderboards deletion, any records posted to earn the associated Collection Journal items for the PR Stunts will not be lost and will remain unlocked.
  • As a reminder, you can stay up to date with current and previous Festival Playlist events and challenges in this new hub on Forza.net.
  • Be sure to save this one to your bookmarks so you can easily reference it in the future!
