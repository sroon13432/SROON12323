The team behind Ubisoft's 'first playable AI-based R&D project' has been established as its own subsidiary called Motherbrain

PC Gamer · industry report·21 November 2026·Source

Use of generative AI in game dev remains as contentious as it does ubiquitous— a recent GDC report found that around 40% of devs use AI, but more than half think its influence has been negative. Ubisoft is continuing to double down on the technology, this time with Motherbrain, a new subsidiary fashioned from the team behind its "first playable generative AI experience."

As reported by VGC, Motherbrain's purpose is to develop "AI for gameplay innovation, supporting all game creators in developing groundbreaking experiences … Its current focus is on new player experiences, smart NPCs with memory and AI game masters that manage large scale systemic worlds," according to an earnings report which was released Thursday.
