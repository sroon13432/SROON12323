Warframe: Tau Coming In December

Warframe · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

, the world-weary, noir-inspired detective brought to life with an inner monologue voiced by Matt Mercer. Enforce your own brand of street justice with an indomitable suite of Abilities, trusty Hound Companion and signature armaments like the

Your remarkable journey is about to lead to an era-defining trip to the Tau System, where new worlds ripe for exploration are waiting. Jump back into Warframe today and get ready to experience our most expansive update ever this December.

What the notes say

  • As with a Protoframe, you'll be able to switch between his Warframe and Chimera aspects on the fly for a more Sentient appearance and voice-acted dialogue.
  • Your evolution continues in a new region of space beyond anything you've ever known: the Tau System.
  • While there will remain much to do in the Origin System going forward, you'll also be able to enter Missions and explore new worlds across Tau.
  • December will start you off with the mob-run ring city of Fornax, as well as the badlands of Perita, with many more planned for future updates.
  • With the last unconquered clan facing existential peril, its chieftain’s young son, Catulast, seeks help from its ancient foe, the Tenno.
