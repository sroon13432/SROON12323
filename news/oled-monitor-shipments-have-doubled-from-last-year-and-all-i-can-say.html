OLED monitor shipments have doubled from last year, and all I can say is it's well deserved

PC Gamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

I feel like I say this once a week at this point, but gaming monitors occupy one of the only bright spots in the dark cloud that is PC hardware right now. They are getting better and cheaper by the day, and it seems I'm not alone in thinking this, as global OLED monitor shipments doubled in the last year. Woo, go OLED monitors.

This is according to Trendforce, which notes Asus is the market leader for global OLED monitor sales (at 21.7%), with MSI coming in second (14.5%) and Samsung coming in third (11.3%). LGE take fourth place with 8.6%, and Dell closely follows that with 8.2%. The rest of the industry has the remaining 35.7% to split among themselves.

More from the report

  • As for total sales, Trendforce says global OLED sales got a 13% bump from last quarter to this year, and more impressively,
  • And who can blame people for buying in? OLED monitors are known for having super vibrant colours, true blacks, and they tend to have wicked fast response times and refresh rates.
  • They are a bit more expensive than IPS, and older panels can suffer from burn-in, but I can say swapping from an IPS panel to an OLED one is one of the most monumental changes I've made to…
  • OLEDs have been getting cheaper over time, too—they're far from the ultra-expensive pieces of kit we saw just a couple of years ago.
