Space Engineers: Warfare 3 Revealed - Combat NPCs!

Space Engineers · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

NPCs are now being introduced across all existing encounters, bringing new activity and personality to the places you discover. From passing Cargo Ships to dangerous Factorum encounters, you'll find new signs of life throughout the universe. Hostile NPCs will patrol and defend their territory, making some encounters even more challenging, while friendly NPCs will go about their business, adding a new layer of life and atmosphere to the world around you.

Not every encounter is the same, these NPCs offer additional context, personality, and can even reveal the story behind the locations they spawn at. These additions will make encounters feel more like active scenes with a purpose, rather than simple structures waiting to be discovered.

What the notes say

  • These additions will make encounters feel more like active scenes with a purpose, rather than simple structures waiting to be discovered.
  • We're particularly excited to see how NPCs change the experience of exploring and taking on Factorum Encounters, adding another layer of challenge to these dangerous locations.
  • This is another exciting step toward making the Space Engineers more dynamic, and we can't wait to see how the universe’s new inhabitants change the way the game plays out for you!
