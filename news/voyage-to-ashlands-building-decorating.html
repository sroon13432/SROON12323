Voyage to Ashlands: Building & Decorating

Windrose · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

We've seen the settlements you've shared with us, from modest hideouts to elaborate estates, and we're constantly impressed by the creativity of our community. Some of your builds have left us wondering whether there's any limit to what you can accomplish with the tools already available. We're inclined to believe there isn't, but we'd still like to give you a little more to work with.

Our voyage to Ashlands continues! We've already explored some of the changes coming to ship customization, taken a closer look at interface improvements, and introduced armor transmogrification and dyeing. But there's still plenty more to discover.

What the notes say

  • We've talked a lot about making your ship feel like your own and giving your pirate a distinctive look.
  • After all, even the most adventurous captain needs somewhere to return to after a long voyage.
  • So, whether you're planning a cozy retreat, a bustling pirate outpost, or something considerably more ambitious, here's a look at what's coming.
  • Some of the feedback we've received concerns those little details that make all the difference when putting a structure together.
  • Perhaps you have a particular roof shape in mind, but can't quite close off a corner.
  • Or you want to finish a staircase, but none of the available railings fit the look you're going for.
