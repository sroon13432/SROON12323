Game Update 1.3.2

007 First Light · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

As some of you may have noticed, that the outfit “The Grey Standard”, which was redeemable after linking your game to an IOI Account didn’t really match the outfit you may have expected. With this update we’ve aimed to set things right: the outfit we’ve deployed has now its own name, Heritage Tailoring, and is yours to keep. The real “The Grey Standard” is also finally coming to the game and will be unlockable by every player with a little investment of Intel given you have reached Clearance Level 15.

came out, many of you have been playing New Game+, trying out Photo Mode and racing through the Valhalla Protocol. The reception of the update is amazing to see and looking at all the creative, funny and impressive photos across all social channels has become one of most favourite pastimes for a lot of team members here at IOI.

What the notes say

  • brings more stability and polish improvements and gives the suit formerly known as “Grey Standard Suit” its own name and description.
  • Additionally, it also includes a fix for the “Positively Shocking” achievement/trophy, which was reported by quite a few community members directly – Thank you for that!
  • The real “The Grey Standard” is now in the game and no longer requires an IOI Account.
  • This is the outfit that was previously unlocked as “The Grey Standard”.
  • If you already redeemed it, it will stay in your inventory.
  • Fixed a rare crash that could occur during the escape drive cinematic in Chapter 04: THE PAST NEVER DIES when Path Tracing was enabled.
