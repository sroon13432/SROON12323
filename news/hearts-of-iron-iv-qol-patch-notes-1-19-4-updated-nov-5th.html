Hearts of Iron IV | QoL Patch Notes 1.19.4 (Updated Nov. 5th)

Hearts of Iron IV · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

A lot of you have indirectly contributed to this update by sharing your thoughts with us over time and having conversations with each other on what you would want to see to make the game better for all of you. There is always room for improvement and I look forward to your feedback as always, I am sure we will want to iterate more on our QOL features after extensive community usage.

This has been an effort to address some of the outstanding requests we have been seeing over time from our community and we hope you will enjoy them. While there are quite a few things we’ve addressed, we want to do more of these updates that improve the overall user experience to feel more modern than the game actually is (TEN years is a long time…).

What the notes say

  • Fixed a bug where tags were added multiple times to track career profile data in co-op multiplayer games, which could lead to a crash
  • Fixed a bug where spamming chat could lead to you getting kicked
  • Fixed a bug where timing out events could potentially get you kicked from the multiplayer session
  • Fixed a bug where the focus tree minimap would not display correctly when UI scaling was used
  • Fixed a crash in the unit leader trait window
  • Fixed focuses and decisions wrongly blocked or unlocked by annexed countries

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
