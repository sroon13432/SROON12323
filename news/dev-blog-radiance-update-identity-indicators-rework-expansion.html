Dev Blog | Radiance Update, Identity Indicators Rework & Expansion

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Since the radiance system launched, it has been the identity mark of every Meta in this wasteland—it records your pursuit of beauty across Nalcott and preserves each Meta's unique accumulation. We have continued to notice that, because past cosmetics and radiance-related content were released in batches, the radiance presentation of some special cosmetics does not match the current latest standards.

Therefore, we plan a comprehensive update to the Radiance Benefits experience: unify radiance values, rework identity indicators, add new Radiance Shop content, and extend nickname color changes to more scenes, so that identity indicators truly follow you everywhere.

What the notes say

  • This update will go live after the November 18 (PT) version update.
  • If you have any thoughts or suggestions, Metas, please feel free to share them with us via the Feedback Platform (Once Human Feedback Platform).
  • "Team Name Decor" and "Personalized Badges" are the iconic representations of different radiance levels.
  • This update will apply a unified update to all "Team Name Decor" and "Personalized Badges" effects, improving both recognizability and presentation:
  • Previously, nickname color changes only took effect in certain scenes.
  • With this update, the Radiance Shop will be synchronized with new redeemable content, giving Metas more ways to showcase their accumulated radiance.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
