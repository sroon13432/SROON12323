It's RimWorld's birthday!

RimWorld · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 October 2026·Source

To participate, share your RimWorld creations with us! It can be RimWorld fanart, in-game screenshots, gameplay videos (vanilla/non-modded content only), writing, and other creative media. Share it with us by replying to the X @LudeonStudios birthday post or share a link on the Reddit birthday megathread. All submissions must be human-made works, no AI-generated content permitted.

To celebrate, we’re running another community event. Send us all your RimWorld creations to participate: fanart, stories of your favorite colonists and colonies, videos of edits and gameplay, screenshots of the bases you’re most proud of (and the ones that didn’t quite make it), and whatever else you can come up with.

What the notes say

  • To participate, share your RimWorld creations with us! It can be RimWorld fanart, in-game screenshots, gameplay videos (vanilla/non-modded content only), writing, and other creative media.
  • Participation closes on Nov 6, 11:59PM Pacific. Giveaway recipients will be announced the following week on the X birthday post and Reddit thread by @LudeonStudios.
  • Those selected for the giveaway will be contacted on X/Reddit by the LudeonStudios account (and no one else, be careful if someone other than the official account reaches out).
  • You must respond before November 30, or someone else will receive the giveaway.
  • Birthday themes: Cakes, decorations, presents, celebrations.
  • Screenshots: Your colonies, gravships, throne rooms, containment facilities, ideoligions, etc.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
