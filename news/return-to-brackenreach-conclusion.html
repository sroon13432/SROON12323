Return to Brackenreach Conclusion

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

The eerie mists have lifted, the candles have burned low, and the portals to Brackenreach have closed once again! Our Return to Brackenreach World of Trucks event has officially come to an end, and what a spooktacular adventure it has been!

We'd like to thank every single driver who took part in this year's Halloween celebration and helped uncover the mysteries hidden within the marshes and moonlit roads of Brackenreach.

What the notes say

  • Your dedication and teamwork once again proved that our #BestCommunityEver can rise to any challenge, even those from beyond the veil!
  • Of course, no Halloween adventure would be complete without a few treats!
  • Drivers who completed their personal goal of 15 or more deliveries earned the mystical Wisp Lantern cabin item.
  • Thanks to your combined efforts in completing the community goal, those who achieved their personal goal also unlocked the fabled Key to Brackenreach hanging cabin item!
  • Those brave enough to discover this hidden location unlocked the Ember Grimoire cabin item, along with last year's Spooky Horn vehicle accessory, both awarded as Steam inventory items.
  • We've truly enjoyed seeing your fantastic screenshots and videos shared under #ReturnToBrackenreach.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
