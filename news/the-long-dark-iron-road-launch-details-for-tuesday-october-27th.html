THE LONG DARK: IRON ROAD -- Launch Details for Tuesday, October 27th

The Long Dark · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 October 2026·Source

When you buy IRON ROAD, you’re paying for access to the features and content available at launch, but also everything that will be included in the updates we’ll release along the way. For the full roadmap, outlining a range of planned features and content, visit the official website for THE LONG DARK.

We’re excited for you all to dive into IRON ROAD, the new Early Access Expansion DLC for THE LONG DARK. IRON ROAD adds a whole new island, a train, and a deep mystery that will unfold over ~18-24 months of Early Access.

What the notes say

  • To find out what time that is for you locally, please refer to the following global release timings map, or consult one of the many time zone calculators available online.
  • IRON ROAD will launch for $24.99 USD, with a 10% launch discount.
  • At around the midway point through the planned Roadmap, we will increase the price by $5 to $29.99 USD.
  • And then when IRON ROAD hits 1.0 status, we’ll increase by $5 again, to $34.99 USD.
  • IRON ROAD adds new and significant gameplay systems to THE LONG DARK’s core survival sandbox.
  • The addition of the train is a game-changer in many ways.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
