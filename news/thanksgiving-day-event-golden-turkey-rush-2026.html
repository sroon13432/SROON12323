Thanksgiving Day Event: Golden Turkey Rush 2026

Fishing Planet · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Start by harvesting Golden Corn at event locations, then put it on your hook and see what bites. Fish of all sizes will go for this special bait! Bring your catches to the Thanksgiving Cauldrons and work through a series of missions to cook up fish dishes for the feast.

There’s more to bring home than just dinner. Take on the event missions for a chance to earn the exclusive Indian Canoe, in-game currency, and unique fishing items. Complete the Catcher in the Corn Achievement to claim your exclusive Pilgrim Hat.

What the notes say

  • Take on the event missions for a chance to earn the exclusive Indian Canoe, in-game currency, and unique fishing items.
  • Complete the Catcher in the Corn Achievement to claim your exclusive Pilgrim Hat.
  • Most of the event takes place at mid-level waterways, with missions that anglers of all skill levels can enjoy.
  • Thanksgiving Day embodies the spirit of harvest, unity, and, of course, Fishing Planet!
  • Our journey begins at Lone Star Lake, where your first task is to discover the Horn of Plenty, or Cornucopia.
  • Once you find it, you'll unlock a series of missions that will guide you in preparing all the delicious holiday dinner dishes and setting up the perfect Thanksgiving feast at Lone Star Lake.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
