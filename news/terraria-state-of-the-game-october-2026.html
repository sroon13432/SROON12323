Terraria State of the Game - October 2026

Terraria · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

It's spooky season once again - one of our favorite times of the year! We didn't quite get anything together in regards to a Halloween Contest this year, but we have a few contest ideas that might be coming your way soon - courtesy of our brand new Community Manager! More on that in a bit. On the development front, we have been putting the finishing touches on Crossplay Phase One. The Crossplay era of Terraria is drawing very close, and we will share some details on progress and timing in the development news section below.

We put all of the finishing touches on Crossplay Phase One earlier this month and then dropped it into extensive testing. Given the scope of this update in regards to being a new feature that cuts across multiple platforms, we expect Terraria 1.4.6 will get tighter scrutiny during the submissions process. We had hoped to have the update in submission across all platforms earlier this week, but our latest round of tests uncovered a few things that will need to be addressed before we can do so.

What the notes say

  • First Place: Snowi - with Cupcake the Pit Bull. LV: 38 | PL: 957
  • Second Place: Mirelude - with Stephen the Green Slime. LV: 39 | PL: 952
  • Third Place: wynaut - with Diva Slime the Shimmer Slime. LV: 37 | PL: 933
  • Once in submission, we have no way to fully predict when it will be signed off by all of our first party partners.
  • Once we have full signoff, we will push the big launch button and Terraria 1.4.6 - Crossplay Phase One will be in your hands at last!
  • The Terraria Community is legendary amongst video game communities.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
