Eagle’s Shadow: New Rendering! Part 3

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Previously, adjusting the game world’s draw distance was only possible with a single slider that controlled both buildings and vegetation. The maximum draw distance could be set to 350%, but in practice the engine only allowed up to 300%. In the Eagle’s Shadow update, a new slider has been added for adjusting the draw distance of buildings and objects. Both sliders for objects and trees can now reach an honest 500%. “Tree range” controls trees, bushes and other vegetation, including the distance at which their detailed models switch to more efficient low-detail versions.

In Ground Battles, shadows from static objects, such as houses, walls and rocks, are now drawn using software ray tracing. You don’t even need a graphics card with hardware ray tracing support! Previously, the old shadow system would redraw entire areas when spawning in a tank or ship, when the position of the sun changed abruptly, or when replay playback speed changed, which increased CPU load and caused bright artifacts and shadow flicker near the camera.

What the notes say

  • Software ray tracing has also improved shadows in Air Battles.
  • Regular shadow maps covered only a few hundred meters around the aircraft.
  • Everything further had no object shadows, meaning towns, airfields and other objects below looked flat.
  • Software ray tracing fixes this issue by casting a ray toward the sun against a compact copy of large scene objects built on the CPU for each pixel.
  • This gives towns and airfields their shadows at any distance.
  • The blend zone between cascaded and ray traced shadows, previously noticeable at about 300 meters from the camera, is gone.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
