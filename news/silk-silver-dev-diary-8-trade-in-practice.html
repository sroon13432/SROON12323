"Silk & Silver" Dev Diary #8 - Trade in Practice

Crusader Kings III · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Every Market belongs to a de jure duchy, and every de jure duchy with at least one county gets a Market. On the current map that comes to 922. Each Market sits in its Market Capital and at game start takes in every barony of every de jure county in the duchy as a constituent title. The Market Capital is normally the capital barony of the duchy's capital county, but you can name a different barony for it to occupy in history. We do this for 54 duchies, mostly to put their Market on the coast. For example, Aquitaine trades from La Rochelle.

Today we’ll pop the hood and show you all the various machinery that forms the engine of our new CK3 economy. As always, all screenshots are work-in-progress and everything is subject to change. That is especially true for numbers: nearly every value you’ll see in this dev diary is a define or script value that is still being balanced. We want to give you the shape of the system, but you’ll have to wait for the release to experience the final product.

  • The Market's name and its Prosperity follow the duchy, but its buildings don't move.
  • (If you load a save from before Silk & Silver, the Markets get created on load.)
