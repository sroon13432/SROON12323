[Notice] Patch Notes | Nov. 11

AION 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

The following items will be added to the reward group: Wrathful Mind (Bound), Wrathful Will (Bound), and Wrathful Ego (Bound).
