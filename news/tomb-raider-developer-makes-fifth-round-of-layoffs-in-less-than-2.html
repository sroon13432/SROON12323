Tomb Raider developer makes fifth round of layoffs in less than 2 years

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

Crystal Dynamics has confirmed that eight people have been let go in the latest round of layoffs at the beleaguered Tomb Raider studio.

"Crystal Dynamics made a difficult, but necessary business decision to reduce our current studio staff by eight people," the studio said of the layoffs, first reported by Vertical Slice, in a statement provided to PC Gamer.

More from the report

  • "Whenever we phase roles off a project, we make every effort to find new spots for our great colleagues first.
  • The studio added that no ongoing projects are impacted by the cuts.
  • In terms of raw numbers, eight people is not a huge cut, especially when compared to layoffs at other studios—Firaxis reportedly cut roughly 60 employees earlier today, for instance.
