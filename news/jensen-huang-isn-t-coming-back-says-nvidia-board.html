Jensen Huang isn't coming back, says Nvidia board

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned last week after details about multiple extramarital affairs came to light, will not be allowed to un-resign, says the company.

"On November 12th, our board of directors announced that after disclosure of personal behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, standards, and cultural norms, Jensen Huang voluntarily resigned his positions as president, CEO, and board member," said Nvidia chairman Harvey C. Jones during a regularly scheduled investors call today. "The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO."
