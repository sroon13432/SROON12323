Communinet Signal #23

Dune: Awakening · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

In our first in-game event since CHOAM Salvage Rights, we asked you to uncover the truth behind the strange affliction that was spreading across Arrakis. We’re excited to bring new events to Dune: Awakening. The team had a lot of fun working on the event’s content and rewards, adding details across the game to make it more immersive and spooky. Perhaps it was a bit too spooky since even the NPC didn’t want to show up at first.

We hope you’ve recovered from the horrors of The Scourge, because the temperatures are plummeting and Arrakis is about to get a whole lot less hospitable. As we prepare for the biting winds of the Polar Sink, we’ve been working in overdrive to get the Blood and Ice expansion ready for December 1.

What the notes say

  • We’re thrilled with the engagement and feedback surrounding the event.
  • Collectively, you extracted over 100 million liters of blood, collected 2 million eyes, and over 1 million brains.
  • What are we supposed to do now with over 107,365 metric tons of biological mass and fluid?
  • The next in-game event, the Water Crisis, will slide into action several days after the Blood and Ice expansion.
  • There, you’ll fight for scarce water supplies, and work to end the shortage.
  • We are traveling to the Polar Cap, where heat is your ally, and you’ll need to keep your cool to survive the harsh, freezing environment.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
