What the notes say

  • Right click Deep Rock Galactic in your Steam library and select ‘Properties’
  • Under ‘Properties,’ click the ‘Game Versions & Betas’ tab
  • Select ‘experimental’ within that tab
  • You can set it back to the regular version the same way.
  • Please keep in mind that you may encounter bugs/wonky stuff, and that Experimental progress
  • The Company can confirm a recent spate of localized gravitational anomalies in and around Hoxxes IV.
