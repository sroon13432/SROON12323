Armory: Halloween

Enlisted · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

By tradition, at the end of October, we carve jack-o'-lanterns from pumpkins and decorate our beloved weapons with themed motifs and scenes. You will have to find your own pumpkins, but the weapons are already waiting for you as rewards of this event!

During the event you can get 8 rewards total: two unique Colt M1911A1 (Flame) pistols and 6 event weapon orders that you can use to buy unique sniper rifles, namely the Sniper SVT-40 (Transformation) and the Sniper Gewehr 43 (Gothic), up to 3 of each.

What the notes say

  • The tasks can be completed in the Squads mode.
  • The rewards can be claimed during the event without any time restrictions.
  • From November 10th (13:00 UTC) to November 12th (13:00 UTC), after the end of the event, you will still be able to purchase any missing rewards for Gold.
  • From November 10th (13:00 UTC) to November 12th (13:00 UTC), after the end of the event, you will still be able to purchase any missing rewards for Gold.
