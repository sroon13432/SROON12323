Introducing..No Man's Sky: FORSAKEN

No Man's Sky · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

Last month’s COSMOS update saw a huge number of Travellers old and new journeying to the No Man’s Sky universe to celebrate our ten year anniversary. Space has become more alive and varied than ever, and it's been so nice to see players creating Star Bases, owning Space Stations and creating alliances. Our next update FORSAKEN fittingly for Halloween allows players to explore the darker side of the universe! Full patch notes here.

In FORSAKEN players will begin searching for rare haunted tech or biological horrors. Finding these means visiting the spookiest parts of No Man’s Sky, like derelict hulks, abandoned freighters, dig sites on planets or infested outposts.

What the notes say

  • Finding these means visiting the spookiest parts of No Man’s Sky, like derelict hulks, abandoned freighters, dig sites on planets or infested outposts.
  • Any Traveller brave enough to venture into these dangerous wrecks may salvage mementos and trade them with collectors for new biomechanical corvette modules.
  • Allowing players to make beautiful and strange corvette exteriors, and lean fully into that creepy and organic side of sleek sci-fi ships.
  • Not only can you trade for new ship types, but also new weapon and space suit styles.
  • Determined scavengers will be rewarded with the grotesque new larva tank helmet, and even a wriggling grub cannon multi-tool (for the right quantity of preserved grubs).
  • FORSAKEN is particularly crammed with new features and gameplay though.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
