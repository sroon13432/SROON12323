As Rockstar's alleged "union busting" tribunal hearing comes to a close amid GTA 6 launch, judge pledges to deliver verdict "as quickly as I possibly can"

Eurogamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

Closing statements in Rockstar and IWGB's employment tribunal case - revolving around the GTA 6 developer's alleged 'union busting' and the firing of 34 employees - have now been read, bringing the hearing to an end. There's currently no timeline for a verdict, however, with one commentator suggesting a judgement could take weeks or months to arrive.
