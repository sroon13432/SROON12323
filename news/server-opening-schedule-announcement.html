Server Opening Schedule Announcement

Once Human · posted by the game's developer on Steam·14 November 2026·Source

After the version update and maintenance on November 18, 2026 （PT）, the Visional Wheel will rotate once more, changing to Dissonant Resonance. To help Metas prepare, here is the related server opening schedule.

Existing Isles of Abyss servers will add an appropriate number of Vision Worlds based on activity levels, so Metas can experience the Dissonant Resonance game mode right away without transferring servers.

What the notes say

  • After the version update on November 18, 2026 PT, newly opened Isles of Abyss servers will come with 8 Vision Worlds and 4 Basic Worlds.
  • To ensure a quality gameplay experience for Metas and maintain healthy server ecosystems, the server opening schedule has also been adjusted as follows:
  • To meet everyone's long-term need to play on the Isles of Abyss, each region will open 1 Permanent Server on November 18, 2026 （PT）: Isles of Abyss (Permanent Server).
  • Metas from the Isles of Abyss server will eventually all gather on this server.
  • Isles of Abyss (Permanent Server) will open a sufficient number of Basic Worlds and Vision Worlds to ensure a smooth experience for Metas.
  • Vision Worlds will synchronize with the Visional Wheel "Dissonant Resonance" game mode.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
