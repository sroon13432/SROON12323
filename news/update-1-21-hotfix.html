Update 1.21 Hotfix

Crusader Kings III · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

Hello everyone! We're pushing a small hotfix today to tackle some high priority issues reported to us since the release of Silk & Silver. We're continuing to work on additional issues that have been reported, and those fixes will be deployed at a later date.

Starting armies and special troops for many 867 and 1066 characters have been reduced. Among them are Ivar and Halfdan, Haesteinn, Rurik, William of Normandy, Alp Arslan, Harald Hardrada and the Aghlabid emir.

What the notes say

  • Norse and Varangian adventurer invasions now arrive with smaller armies.
  • Bonus troops from Varangian Adventurer conquests are capped at 1,000, down from 2,000.
  • the Eastern Roman Legacy, Frugal Armorers, Horse Breeders, Saharan Nomads and Lords of the Elephant traditions, and the Coking innovation;
  • the Iranian Intermezzo Stabilization phase, the Crusade phase for main powers, and the Expansion movement of the Dynastic Cycle.
  • Republics can now rescind their own monopolies in markets they do not control, so leftover monopolies no longer block trade for others.
  • The Disgraced Patricians modifier and renouncing an election now lower each house member's Prestige gain.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
