Dune: Part Three in WoT Blitz!

World of Tanks Blitz · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

Complete missions in Tier IV–X vehicles and earn Imperial Flags. Each regular combat mission will earn you 100 Imperial Flags, while each special mission will earn you 500 Imperial Flags. Every 1000 Imperial Flags unlock one event level.

First-of-their-kind customization items: Death Commando Voiceover for your tanks, and permanent Dune: Part Three Garage Theme for you to keep.

What the notes say

  • Avatars and profile backgrounds featuring the iconic characters: Paul Atreides, Alia of the Knife, and Stilgar.
  • First-of-their-kind customization items: Death Commando Voiceover for your tanks, and permanent Dune: Part Three Garage Theme for you to keep.
  • New Relic D3 tank with the Manta Devastator legendary camo, and the Guild Mauler legendary camo for Rinoceronte.
  • Manta Ornithopter Attachment and more Dune-inspired rewards!
