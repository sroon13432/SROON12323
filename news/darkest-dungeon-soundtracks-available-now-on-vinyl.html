Darkest Dungeon Soundtracks Available Now on Vinyl

Darkest Dungeon® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

The first 500 orders of each soundtrack will receive a limited edition coloured vinyl. A bloody red transparent vinyl for Darkest Dungeon, and blue/ black smoke for Darkest Dungeon II.

Red Hook Studios and Darkest Dungeon composer Stuart Chatwood have partnered with Lost in Cult to bring you the Darkest Dungeon and Darkest Dungeon II soundtracks on vinyl!

What the notes say

  • For the first time ever, hear The Color of Madness and The Butcher's Circus DLCs pressed to glorious vinyl with the rest of Darkest Dungeon atmospheric orchestrations.
  • The first 500 orders will receive a limited edition bloody red transparent vinyl record.
  • This album is also available in a classic black that will not be unit limited.
  • Get the official Darkest Dungeon II Soundtrack on vinyl for the first time!
  • Experience composer Stuart Chatwood's expansive score across 3LPs.
  • The first 500 orders of the Darkest Dungeon II Soundtrack will receive a special edition Inkfire blue/black smoke coloured vinyl.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
