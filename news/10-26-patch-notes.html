10/26 Patch Notes

DAVE THE DIVER · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 October 2026·Source

With this update, we're happy to share that the "In the Jungle" DLC steam achievements have been added and will be tracked separately from the base game on the Steam Client Beta. Please check the patch note down below.

Due to changes to Steam's achievement system, the newly introduced Achievement Sets track main game and DLC achievements separately, so you can see the completion rate of each set of achievements at a glance.

What the notes say

  • DLC-exclusive Steam achievements that do not affect the completion rate of the existing main game achievements have been added.
  • Achievements you have already completed in the in-game smartphone Achievements app will sync to Steam achievements when you play with the corresponding save file.
  • If you experience any issues after the patch, please reach out to us through the official DAVE THE DIVER Discord server.
  • Please compress the files from the path below and attach them to the ticket.
