'We need to come together': On the eve of GTA 6, 1,600 Rockstar workers are voting on unionization

PC Gamer · industry report·18 November 2026·Source

As I write this, reviews for Grand Theft Auto 6 have gone live. Gamers in New Zealand have been able to play it for almost six hours, and millions of people farther west are eagerly waiting for clocks to hit midnight in their own timezones. In preorder sales alone, GTA 6 has already generated over $1.6 billion in revenue, and will undoubtedly earn millions more over its launch weekend.

As Rockstar is poised to set historic records with its latest release, the workers who made it could be making history of its own: Over 1,600 Rockstar employees in the UK are currently voting on whether to form a union. If it wins recognition when the ballot closes on November 26, the Rockstar Games Workers Union would be the largest in the UK games sector, organizing workers at what's arguably the games industry's highest-profile studio—a major milestone in the push for unionization in game development.
