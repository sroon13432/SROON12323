Hotfix: Mirror Dungeon Achievement Adjustment & Text Clarification

Limbus Company · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

After the Nov. 5th, 2026 Scheduled Update, we have identified an issue where a certain achievement is overly reliant on luck, while another has an unclear description that may be confusing and open to interpretation.

It was determined that the above achievement was far too reliant on luck and may be difficult to clear even on multiple attempts.

What the notes say

  • Before: Clear Mirror Dungeon after triggering the Rebate Token E.G.O Gift effect 3 times in a row during a single run
  • After: Clear Mirror Dungeon after triggering the Rebate Token E.G.O Gift effect for a total of 3 times during a single run
  • Before: Clear Floor 10 or above in the Mirror Dungeon without using the Refresh function
  • After: Clear Floor 10 or above in the Mirror Dungeon using neither the Refresh nor the Keyword Refresh functions in the shop
  • Nov. 5th (Thu) 19:30 (KST)
  • There may be a delay of approximately 10 minutes.
