The Popcorn Update (Early Access Update 12)

Crab Champions · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Added new melee weapon: Baseball Bat: Sends enemies flying! Starts with level 3 Knockback Claws and enemies are always guaranteed to get knocked upwards when hit

Added new weapon mod: Tornado Shot: Chance to fire damaging tornados along with your shots that lift enemies into the air

What the notes say

  • Added new melee mod: Crystal Claws: Enemies drop more crystals if eliminated within 3 seconds of them taking melee damage
  • Added new melee mod: Hard Landing: Chance for enemies to explode when taking knockback damage.
  • Added new melee mod: Potent Claws: Melee damage doubles all debuff stacks on enemies (9 second cooldown per enemy, cooldown is reduced per level)
  • Added new perk: Giant Slayer: Damage increased after clearing elite or boss islands.
  • Added new perk: Charger: Chance for 1-3 charge zones to spawn (stand inside to charge: loot spawns for every player with Charger when fully charged)
  • Added new perk: Quality Shipments: Chests have a chance to be upgraded to Big (2x loot choices) or Gigantic (4x loot choices) variants

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
