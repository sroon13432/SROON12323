It’s been a big year for Rainbow Siege, most recently with the introduction of Operation Split Fire and Noor, its new Operator, the introduction of the Legend Division, a 3v3 Arcade Mode, and more. Now, as Year 11 heads towards its final season, the Siege devs shared their insights on these updates and give a sneak peak at what’s to come in the most recent Rainbow Six Siege Community Checkpoint. In case you missed it, here’s what to know:

What the notes say

  • The new Legend Division is a permanent solo queue playlist that launched with the mid-season update.
  • It’s designed to provide an elite competitive environment for Champion ranked players, and you must meet the following requirements to participate:
  • Starting next season, there will no longer be seasonal Ranked map pool rotations.
  • While the goal of that feature was to keep the Ranked experience feeling fresh, most players didn’t see map rotations as meaningful new content.
  • More often than not, a rotation would foment frustration in the community, especially when a favorite map was replaced with one the players enjoyed less.
  • Moving forward, the Siege dev team is shifting back to a “best of” approach, which will keep the strongest maps in the Ranked pool to provide a more consistent competitive experience.
