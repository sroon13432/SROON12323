Movember 2026

Warframe · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Shave the date, Tenno — from now until November 30, Digital Extremes is proudly supporting the Canadian Movember Foundation with new and returning in-game goodies, Merch, a Captura contest and more!

A good idea is right under your nose — or, in this case, available via the in-game Market for only 1 Credit until November 30! Get the Movember-themed items below from previous years while you can.

What the notes say

  • Movember Sirius and Orion Community Display created by community artist VEROZ
  • Movember Shopkeep Moustache Display Frame
  • Movember Tusker Moustache Display Frame
  • Movember Master Moustache Display Frame
  • Movember Community Glyph 2023 by community artist RetroAlchemist
  • Movember Baruuk Community Glyph - Dark/Bright by community artist UpsidedownSmore
