HOI IV Dev Diary | Romania: Military, Country Mechanic & More Alternate History

Hearts of Iron IV · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

Interacting with countries is done in two ways, the tier can be invested in with political power to grant predetermined bonuses - each country is like an industrial concern that is cheap but requires some initial political power investment and then a smaller amount to maintain its benefits. The tier also affects some decisions attached to the mechanic.

Here you can see all the countries that are available in the mechanic, Romanian relations with Hungary and Bulgaria were not the best so interacting with them is blocked until you either do certain focuses or end up in a faction with them.

What the notes say

  • In the dev corner’s, I added a little sneak peak of the core of the military branch, let’s go through that, and the other parts, in a little more detail.
  • One of the spirits Romania starts with represents this issue, but the idea will upgrade as you do military focuses, until it grants exclusively positive modifiers.
  • Since ships of that size are not in the game, Romania instead receives a nice river terrain bonus.
  • And finally to the right, you will find choices regarding the army’s doctrine and organization.
  • Convincing the Romanian people to turn to communism is complicated by their bad experiences when dealing with the Russian and then Soviet empire.
  • When turning communist, there are two primary factions within the communist party to choose between.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
