City Corner #14 - Shaping the Environment

Cities: Skylines II · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

, it is time to turn our attention to something that can make quite a difference to your cities, even if you have not yet built a single road: the environment. From the landscapes beyond your city’s limits to the ground beneath your citizens’ feet, there is a lot more going on than meets the eye. So, who better to tell us about all of it than the developers working on these changes!

At this point, we had already taken a look at the code. We knew that Cities: Skylines II had climate settings and the beginning of a biome system, so there was a foundation to start building on. The goal was to make better use of these systems and give different environments a more distinct look.

What the notes say

  • At this point, we had already taken a look at the code.
  • We knew that Cities: Skylines II had climate settings and the beginning of a biome system, so there was a foundation to start building on.
  • The goal was to make better use of these systems and give different environments a more distinct look.
  • A biome shouldn’t be just a label for a particular map that shows up at the beginning of a new game.
  • It should also influence the vegetation and other elements found there, so that the landscape feels consistent and realistic.
  • This meant developing four new biomes to support the new maps coming in Park Adventures: arid, tropical, alpine, and Mediterranean, each with its own characteristics and vegetation.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
