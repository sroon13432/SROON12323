Patch 0.208 - Fall's Faire: Hearty Homecoming - Available Now

Palia · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Crisp leaves, warm kitchens, and the smell of something delicious is drifting through Kilima Village... Fall's Faire: Hearty Homecoming has arrived! Gather your closest friends for a new group quest with Sifuu, Hassian, Taylin, and Ulfe, then pull up a chair at the Bulletin Board and help Jina whip up A Feast for Thought. Press play on a brand-new retro Lunar Path: Path of the Classics, welcome the Corgi to your Ranch, and cozy-up your home with fresh Simple Sundries Decor, Gardening Inspired Tints, and Plushes.

Once you've completed Friendship Level 5 with all four Villagers, a new group quest, Beasts Unburdened, will lead to a reunion that's been a long time coming. Expect a few surprises along the way, including a poem from Hassian (yes, really). Finish the quest to receive a unique reward to remember the occasion.

What the notes say

  • Expect a few surprises along the way, including a poem from Hassian (yes, really).
  • Finish the quest to receive a unique reward to remember the occasion.
  • Head to the Bulletin Board to pick up A Feast for Thought and help Jina gather everything she needs for a proper Harvest Festival!
  • Along the way you'll have a few choices to make, and yes, your decisions matter.
  • Contribute the requested items through the Bulletin Board's sub-bundles to earn rewards, including four brand-new Food Profile Icons.
  • The newest Lunar Path, the Path of the Classics, is a love letter to pixel art and virtual pets, with rewards pulled directly from 80’s and 90’s nostalgia.
