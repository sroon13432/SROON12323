I reviewed every strip club in GTA 6 (because you sickos keep googling it)

PC Gamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

One of the first things GTA 6 players searched for was strip club locations—genuinely, data doesn't lie. So, as a responsible guide writer, I loaded up the game and proceeded to spend my initial hours beelining around Leonida's hotspots. There are a few different strip clubs in Leonida, each with their own price range and different activities to try (that sounds sordid, but I'm also talking about, like, pool tables).

There are three strip clubs in the game I've found, plus a load of other night spots, like the Rusty Anchor close to where Jason starts, that have drinking and other activities, too.

More from the report

  • Activities: Drinking, club dancing, private dances
  • This fancy strip club in downtown Vice City is the classiest option you have available, but also the priciest.
  • If you want a private dance, same as with previous GTA games, you just need to greet the dancer in question and then use the interaction icon to pay for it.
  • I appreciated NINE1NINE's lively club atmosphere, but I also ran out of cash very fast. Maybe one to try later in the game when you're a lot richer.
