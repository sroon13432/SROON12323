Slay the Spire 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

Since August (aside from our 1 week studio break), we've been busy working on some big pieces of content that we've alluded to in past Neowsletters. All of these features were included in our Early Access roadmap at launch, but it wasn't until the past couple of months that we've had the time to concentrate all of our focus on creating them. As you can imagine, these are big things that require a lot of design, UX work, art, animation, balancing, etc before they're even ready for the experimental beta branch.

Much like last month, this Neowsletter is coming in a little later than usual. But this time it's because we wanted to wait until we were able to offer some more solid info on when you can expect to see the next update.
