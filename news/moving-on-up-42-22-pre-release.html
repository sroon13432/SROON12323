Moving on Up: 42.22 Pre-Release

Project Zomboid · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

We have news on the upcoming 42.22 Update, available for testing now on our new Pre-Release branch, which replaces the Unstable branch for early testing. Yes, we changed the branch name, as it was causing some confusion among players, especially given the (long) history of Unstable in Build 42.

What does this mean to you? For the average player, absolutely nothing. We recommend you keep on enjoying Project Zomboid on the default Stable branch, currently 42.21, while pre-release goes through final vetting.

What the notes say

  • type who is willing to test and break things before they go live to the broader public, then you can opt-in to the Pre-Release.
  • Going forward, we’ll be releasing builds into the Pre-Release branch before they go forward to the Stable branch (barring urgent hotfixes that may need to go directly to Stable).
  • The goal here is to identify any issues that need to be addressed, while also allowing server admins and modders a chance to look under the hood for upcoming changes.
  • We cannot guarantee that the save will work and/or you will be able to use it in the final release, which will come after Pre-Release testing is complete.
  • In 42.22 you will be able to interact with placed ladders on the map, using the (E) key.
  • So you can be a little less fearful around empty pools now, and finally take in the view from the top floor of the Rosewood Fire Station!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
