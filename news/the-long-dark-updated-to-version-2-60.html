The Long Dark Updated to version 2.60

The Long Dark · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Although food at more than 20% Condition can be added to the Fulmar Boat's Supplies exchange bin, only food at greater than 50% Condition will count towards the calorie total.

The team is currently aware of the following issues in the game. If you encounter one of these issues, there is no need to report it to the Support Portal.

What the notes say

  • The team is currently aware of the following issues in the game.
  • If you encounter one of these issues, there is no need to report it to the Support Portal.
  • There are no clear indicators on the Prototype Train controls for how to select the reverse gear.
  • We are aware of this confusion and will be addressing it soon.
  • In the meantime, players using mouse and keyboard can right-click, and players on controllers can use the left-trigger to move the shifter into the reverse position.
  • The Whale Oil Heater only displays fire/spark effects the first time it is lit after a transition, and does not provide distinct visual feedback for each of the three burn rate settings.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
