DCS: F-15C Eagle New Info

DCS World Steam Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Big news for every Eagle driver! We're now developing two versions of the DCS: F-15C Eagle: an Early MSIP II, representing 1991 and the era of Operation Desert Storm, and a Late MSIP II, representing 2005 with JHMCS, the AIM-9X, and the MIDS datalink. A big thank you to everyone in the community who made the case for an earlier Eagle. You were heard! We're also sharing our current release and pricing plans, and we'd love to hear what you think.

Running a DCS multiplayer server takes far more than launching a mission. In this week's Community Spotlight, we look at DCSServerBot, the open-source platform from Special K that helps more than 200 DCS communities manage well over 600 servers worldwide. Learn more below.
