Balancing Patch

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Since the release of Primal Dread, we’ve seen a lot of feedback around the balance of the game. We wanted to take some time before rushing into it so we tried our hand at balancing in beta first. And now it’s out for everyone!

Balancing is an ongoing process. We are making changes that can change themselves. Try it in game and let us know what you think!

What the notes say

  • Ban: gain 1 ban token every 4 waves, up to wave 20
  • Endless: Piggy Bank and Crown no longer appear in the shop after wave 20 again
  • Fixed random zone selection when some DLCs are disabled
  • Fixed challenge rewards not being unlocked on some saves, and rewards being locked again on "unlock all" saves
  • Codex: fixed the 100% completion count per DLC
  • Weapon popup: controller navigation no longer gets stuck when some buttons are hidden

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
