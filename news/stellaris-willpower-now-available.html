Stellaris: Willpower now available!

Stellaris · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

Ideologies and Gestalt Directives: Ideologies emerge from Fanatic support for an Ethic, spread across borders, and grant bonuses to every empire that adopts them. Each is led by an Ideological Authority who chooses its Tenets from dozens of Ethic-specific options. Gestalt empires can adopt and spread Ideologies too.

Willpower is built around one idea: your beliefs can be a strategic weapon. You can win over the galaxy by shaping minds as well as by fleets.

What the notes say

  • Willpower also unlocks the Shared Burdens and Oppressive Autocracy Civics, so a player with Willpower but without MegaCorp or Galactic Paragons still has a Fanatic Civic for every Fanatic…
  • Willpower is built around one idea: your beliefs can be a strategic weapon.
  • You can win over the galaxy by shaping minds as well as by fleets.
  • Ambition: Force of Will: Convert the galaxy to your way of thinking without firing a shot (or maybe just a few).
  • Send the new Cultural Envoy civilian ship to foreign colonies to convert them, and win the game if most of the galaxy adopts your Ideology.
  • Golden Generation: Raise a generation of leaders at an elite academy, and shape what kind of leaders they become as they develop.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
