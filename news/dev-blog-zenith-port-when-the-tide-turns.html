Dev Blog: Zenith Port — When the Tide Turns

Delta Force · posted by the game's developer on Steam·14 November 2026·Source

For the attacking side, the assault begins at sea. The docks lie ahead, with the city's towers rising behind them. Before you even reach the shore, we want you to feel just how much city there is to fight through.

Welcome to Zenith Port. We're excited to bring UE5 features and Riptide to Warfare: a new look for the battlefield, and a dramatic change to how you fight. Let's show you around.

What the notes say

  • We're excited to bring UE5 features and Riptide to Warfare: a new look for the battlefield, and a dramatic change to how you fight.
  • We chose a port because it brings fighting on land, at sea, and in the air into one place.
  • It also gives us a familiar landscape to transform: once you've learned its streets, watching the sea sweep through them means something.
  • UE5 features, including Lumen and Nanite, gave us new tools to build it.
  • Lumen handles dynamic lighting and reflections; Nanite lets us work with highly detailed geometry.
  • Together, they give us more room to develop the lighting and detail in the city around you.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
