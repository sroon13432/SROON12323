ExileCon 2026 Schedule and Information for Attendees

Path of Exile 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

The schedule for ExileCon 2026, starting in just a week, is now available! Check out the convention website to find out more about the full programme for next weekend. For our online viewers, we will be broadcasting the keynote, gameplay demonstrations, and developer panels throughout the entire weekend. We’ll also post all the talks and panels sometime afterwards, so don’t worry if you can’t catch everything live.

The first and most important reminder is that New Zealand has a system where you must apply for an "NZeTA" online before arriving in the country. As far as we can see, almost all ExileCon visitors from overseas must apply for one. We recommend doing this as soon as possible. Thankfully, the process does not look difficult. Full details and the application form are on this page.

What the notes say

  • Friday 6th November: 2pm - 7pm
  • Saturday 7th November: 7am - 5pm
  • Sunday 8th November: 9am - 5pm
  • For attendees with a VIP or Ultra VIP pass, the ExileCon weekend kicks off with a VIP dinner on Friday evening at Saint Alice Eatery alongside Dr Rudi's Rooftop Brewing.
  • The 215 player VIPs will be joined by 100 Grinding Gear Games staff members, intermingled at the same tables.
  • We have a registration area inside Aotea Centre (where the event is taking place), with signage in Aotea Square to direct you.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
