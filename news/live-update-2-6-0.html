Live Update 2.6.0

ARC Raiders · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

Fixed an issue where being latched by a Tick while depositing a Fuel Cell could break animations and prevent interactions such as breaching or using weapons.

As the days get colder and snow settles in, Cold Snap is back! We’re also putting the finishing touches on a new project, arriving next week.

What the notes say

  • As the days get colder and snow settles in, Cold Snap is back!
  • We’re also putting the finishing touches on a new project, arriving next week.
  • Fixed an issue where the Road Warrior skill could not recycle Weapon Amplification Modules or Candleberries during raids.
  • Fixed an issue where vaulting also drained jump-stamina, and the “Vaults on Vaults on Vaults” skill no longer drains any stamina.
  • Fixed an issue where starting a knockout on a downed Raider could move you and push you through walls.
  • Fixed a bug where enemies could knockout a player immediately after they were revived with a defibrillator; being revived now properly prevents knockouts.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
