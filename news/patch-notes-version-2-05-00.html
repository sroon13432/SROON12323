Patch Notes Version 2.05.00

Crimson Desert Enhanced · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

Fixed an issue where saving and loading the game on an account without access to certain items such as Pre-order Exclusive Bonus or Deluxe Edition Bonus items would reset their enhancement levels.

Charting the Unknown is now available on Apple Mac. It can be purchased and downloaded from the “Buy DLC” menu on the game’s title screen.

What the notes say

  • It can be purchased and downloaded from the “Buy DLC” menu on the game’s title screen.
  • Fixed an issue where players could not talk to Torstein after completing the "Return to your comrades" mission, preventing progression in the "Check the situation in Pailune" mission.
  • Fixed an issue where viewing 'Details' on the Map during certain quest progressions caused related NPCs to disappear, blocking further progress.
  • Fixed an issue at the Spire of the Sun where interacting with the final Crystal right before the completion cutscene played prevented the puzzle from completing.
  • Fixed an issue where furniture previously placed in other houses could disappear when relocating the Greymane camp to Pailune.
  • Fixed an issue in Free Construction mode where a room could not be removed if a large number of items were placed inside it.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
