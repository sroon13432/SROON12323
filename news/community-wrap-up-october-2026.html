Community Wrap-Up - October 2026

Core Keeper · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

We were lucky enough to be invited into the Nintendo subreddit for an AMA where Fredrik, Sven, Herman, and Alex answered your community questions about things like a possible hard-mode for the game, potential crossovers, and our plans for the future of Core Keeper. Check it out here!

Oops, did we accidentally go a little late with this one, Explorers? Have a community update for October... in November! We hope you've been enjoying our creepy Samhain-themed content. Here's what we've been up to in October.

What the notes say

  • We had the delight of offering our first Free Weekend event since we left Early Access this month, and saw hundreds of new players start their adventures in the underground.
  • As always, we have the best and most responsive community and we're very excited to welcome a whole new roster of Explorers to the wonderful world of Core Keeper!
  • We've been getting into the spirit of Halloween and building creepy bases in Creative Mode!
  • Looking for a way to move an ore boulder without breaking it down?
  • This mod makes it so that sledge hammers instantly destroy ore boulders, turning them into an item that can be added to your inventory.
  • This mod is compatible with all sledge hammers and does not mine boulders for resources, so you can place them anywhere in the world as a decorative item.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
