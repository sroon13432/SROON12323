Squad Hotfix 10.6.3 (Client Build 2) Release Notes

Squad · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

There remain a variety of other issues which are continuing to be investigated: from the notorious “0/0 queue” and broken or missing sensitivity settings, to turret stabilization, map and terrain issues, and ongoing problems that popped up as a result migrating the UI over to its new back-end. These will take time to fix, and we will continue working with you to collate information and work towards those solutions together:

As a note, the hotfix number is not incrementing because it's a client-only build. This means servers will not need to be updated or restarted, and that you can continue playing on either the old or new build until you’re ready to update at your earliest convenience.
