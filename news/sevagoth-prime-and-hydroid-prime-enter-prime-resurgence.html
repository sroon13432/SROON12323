Sevagoth Prime and Hydroid Prime Enter Prime Resurgence

Warframe · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 November 2026·Source

Break your backs and crack your oars should you wish to prevail once the maritime marauder stirs. Hydroid Prime takes control of any battlefield through torrential downpours that rip foes asunder — rendering them more susceptible to Corrosive damage. Then, when the time is right, he can summon creatures from the depths to temporarily suspend enemies with his Tentacle Swarm Ability. Those adamant about crowd control would be wise to give Hydroid Prime a chance.

The Void's perilous tempests require a formidable helmsman to navigate — a role that Sevagoth Prime ceaselessly endures. Command the shadows, suffocating any wayward soul foolish enough to stand before this specter of death. If you're all about dealing swaths of damage over time while simultaneously lingering on thanks to high survivability amidst chaotic firefights, then Sevagoth Prime is the ideal Warframe for you.
