Ex-Call of Duty live service manager joins EA as the new GM of Battlefield

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

A few things come to mind when I think about what the greatest fear of a Battlefield player is. It could be a glint atop the mountains on Liberation Peak, reliving the launch of Battlefield 2042, or maybe it's the randomised recoil from the first Battlefield. All of these are good bets, but I think the real shared nightmare of most Battlefield players is that someday it'll morph into Call of Duty.

To be fair, this fear isn't entirely unfounded. Bryan Beede was previously appointed as Battlefield's general manager after spending 19 years working on Call of Duty and has now been promoted to group general manager at EA. Taking his place is another Call of Duty veteran, Sam Cooper, who worked as Call of Duty's global product management and head of live service.

More from the report

  • "I'm excited to share that I've joined Electronic Arts as general manager of Battlefield," Cooper says in a LinkedIn post.
  • "Battlefield is an iconic franchise that has something no other shooter has: all-out warfare, destruction at massive scale, squads play,
  • Now this in and of itself isn't enough to sound any alarm bells for players concerned whether or not Battlefield can stay true to form,
  • A big talking point during Battlefield 6's launch was players begging the devs to ignore COD's trend of gimmicky skins and instead stay true to its in-game realism for cosmetics.
