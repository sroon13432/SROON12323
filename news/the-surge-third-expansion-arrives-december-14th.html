The Surge: Third Expansion Arrives December 14th

Frostpunk 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

After a long journey through the frozen remnants of the USA, your people finally reach a mysterious valley. Rumours speak of a marvel left behind by history’s greatest mind – a relic with the potential to reshape the world itself. Driven by hope and the promise of a better future, they venture into the unknown, searching for answers buried deep beneath the ice.

A revolutionary new resource could elevate your city to unprecedented levels of prosperity, opening the door to an entirely new era of automation. But progress comes at a price. Factions clash over how this power should be used, while the secrets hidden within the valley prove far more difficult to uncover than anyone expected.

What the notes say

  • Factions clash over how this power should be used, while the secrets hidden within the valley prove far more difficult to uncover than anyone expected.
  • Experience a highly story-driven campaign set in an entirely new region, far beyond the borders explored in Frostpunk 2 so far.
  • Discover its history, face its challenges, and make decisions that will shape humanity's future.
  • Discover a new resource capable of taking city automation to unprecedented levels.
  • Put it to use and transform the way your city operates – but remember that not everyone will agree on how this power should be wielded.
  • The Great Storm has always been one of humanity's greatest threats.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
