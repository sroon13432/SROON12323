Caves of Qud's first DLC is forcing its devs to ask important questions: 'How does a crab get high?'

PC Gamer · industry report·17 November 2026·Source

In a GDC interview with Freehold Games in 2025, I asked the developers of magnificently mutated, indulgently arcane science-fantasy roguelike Caves of Qud the same thing anyone would in my situation: What's the ideal number of limbs for a being to aspire to? Their answer was none, because "a perfect sphere is the ideal form" and "we started in perfection and only moved farther from God."

As you can imagine, I was eager to speak with co-creators Brian Bucklew and Jason Grinblat earlier this month about Diaspora, the roguelike's upcoming first expansion. Diaspora aims to add an "influx of riotous life" to Qud, expanding its body plan-agnostic mutation system with playable animal and plant characters.

More from the report

  • "We have text on this in-game," Grinblat said, citing Aphorisms About Birds —an in-game book which notes that our fairy tales pay plenty of attention to the man who's turned into a bird,
  • "It hits too close to home, right? We're having trouble in our world dealing with things transforming into the facsimile of man," Bucklew said.
  • Conversely, Grinblat noted, "becoming a tree sounds really nice." To that, Bucklew agreed, wondering whether "there's some way we can get into that business," possibly through marketing…
  • With an air of existential horror comfortably established, we moved into a more mechanical discussion of Diaspora's new plant and animal playstyles.
