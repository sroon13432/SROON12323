Update 10.7 / Oskil River & World Partition Open Playtest (Nov 6th - Nov 9th)

Squad · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

Set in a war-torn Eastern European landscape, Oskil River features a variety of environments shaped by ongoing conflict. From heavily damaged settlements and urban areas to open terrain and trenches, the map offers opportunities for engaging infantry and armored gameplay. We've also introduced more detailed environmental set dressing to help bring the setting to life and create a more immersive experience.

through their paces. Performance has been a frequent topic of discussion within the community, and we want to use this opportunity to test some of the improvements we've been working on and gather valuable feedback from players across different hardware configurations. Oskil currently runs on World Partition but we'll be asking our hosts to run the following additional World Partition layers.

What the notes say

  • Changes to the friend-or-foe identification system, aimed at refining how players distinguish friendly forces from enemies.
  • But servers will open and close to accommodate the population needs!
  • The general public will not be given access to the Server Hosting Branch for testing.
  • We'd love to hear your thoughts on Oskil River, your experience with the World Partition performance improvements, and the additional gameplay changes included in this playtest.
  • In particular, performance feedback and reports from different hardware configurations will be incredibly helpful.
  • Please keep in mind that these features and improvements are still subject to change and may not reflect their final implementation in a future update.
