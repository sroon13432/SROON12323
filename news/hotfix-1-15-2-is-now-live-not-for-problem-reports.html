Hotfix 1.15.2 is now LIVE! - Not for Problem Reports!

Victoria 3 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Hotfix 1.15.2 has now been released! Making further improvements and bugfixes, notably: Automatically queuing replacements for sunken ships, fixing an issue that prevented AI from forming companies, council republics now release nations as council republics and much more!

Regime Change, Release as Subject and Liberate Country now ignore tech requirements for imposed laws, but Regime Change can no longer be done against countries with Traditionalism so it cannot be used as a rapid modernization tool.

What the notes say

  • The Spectre Haunting the World now spawns a Communist or Anarchist Agitator, depending on researched technologies at the Journal Entry's start
  • Fixed a bug where the AI would leave naval invading generals on defensive orders, resulting in the invasion not progressing
  • Fixed a bug where the AI did not properly recognize a splinter army en route to an HQ as going to that HQ, resulting in it repeatedly sending splinters back and forth.
  • Increased the contribution of high-Activism political movements to Consolidation in the State and Revolution Journal Entry
  • Adjusted Early Troopships (now Troopships) to use Grain instead of Groceries and need less Artillery and Engines.
  • Their default Ship Template also no longer needs Copper or Coal

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
