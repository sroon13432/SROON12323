'Game dev isn't always straightforward': Overwatch's director responds to players upset about season 6's hero being a DPS

PC Gamer · industry report·16 November 2026·Source

Last week we found out a few new things about the next hero coming to Overwatch in season 6. He's an omnic detective, his name's Hudson, he has a fun accent, and he will be a DPS. Hudson is the last hero to be released from the batch teased earlier this year during Overwatch's Spotlight, and after all this time I'm very excited to see what he has to offer. But not everyone shares this sentiment.

There's a good chunk of players who are upset about Hudson, before they've even tried him out, simply because he's not a support hero, which was what Team 4 initially promised him to be. I've seen this discontent online and honestly ignored it, thinking it was either bots or people farming for attention because I cannot fathom why anyone is honestly upset about this.

More from the report

  • I've been maining support in Overwatch for almost 10 years now, ever since open queue grinded me down and I accepted that no matter how much I liked DPS,
  • But Team 4 is taking the criticism seriously, as Keller has addressed it directly. Aside from trying to convince players that shoehorning a hero into a role just because that was the plan…
  • I completely agree. I'd much rather have a hero that was crafted for a role holistically than one forced into it to fill a quota.
  • This is such a non-issue, especially considering how strong the support roster currently is. Look at our poor tank players for example, forced to switch between Orisa and Mauga every game.
