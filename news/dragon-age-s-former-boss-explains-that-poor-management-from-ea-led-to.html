Dragon Age's former boss explains that poor management from EA led to BioWare shutting down because 'the options on the table were all terrible'

PC Gamer · industry report·23 November 2026·Source

Last month we got the devastating news that BioWare, the legendary studio that brought us Mass Effect, Dragon Age, and classics like Neverwinter Nights, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, and the OG Baldur's Gate was being shut down.

The decision was made by EA, just a year after it was acquired by a consortium made up of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund and investment firms Silver Lake and Affinity Partners (owned by the son-in-law of US president Donald Trump, Jared Kushner).

  • It's a bad string of events for sure, but former Dragon Age boss Mark Darrah doesn't believe that it's all down to BioWare's poor management.
  • Decisions like using the Frostbite Engine, "making Dragon Age 2", and "shipping Anthem" were all products of BioWare needing to hit financial targets set out by EA.
