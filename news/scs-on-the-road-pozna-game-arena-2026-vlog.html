SCS On The Road: Poznań Game Arena 2026 Vlog

Euro Truck Simulator 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·15 November 2026·Source

Our booth was buzzing throughout the event, with visitors getting behind the wheel to try out the latest content for both Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator. From exploring the landscapes of the Iceland DLC and British Columbia DLC to experiencing the upcoming Coaches DLC, there was plenty for everyone to enjoy. Our 4D motion simulators also proved to be a popular attraction, bringing an extra level of immersion to the driving experience!

What an amazing few days we had at Poznań Game Arena 2026!  After a busy and exciting weekend in Poznań, we're happy to look back at another fantastic gaming event filled with great experiences, memorable moments, and, most importantly, the opportunity to meet so many members of our #BestCommunityEver in person.
