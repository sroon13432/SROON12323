Bethesda confirms A Wasteland Full of Cats is 'a Fallout-inspired adventure' being made in partnership with a not-for-profit animal rescue

PC Gamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

I'm not quite sure what to make of this so I'm going to just put it out there: A new Fallout game might soon be headed our way, and it's a hidden object game.

This game is a hidden object game and the objects you need to find are cats. You play as one of the cats in the Vault. One day there is an accident in the Vault and all of the cats who live there escape out into the Wasteland. It is up to you with the help of some NPCs you meet along the way to find them and return them to the Vault.
