This Month in Sea of Thieves: November 2026

Sea of Thieves: 2026 Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

After October’s whirlwind of Live Events, it’s hard to believe that we’re already in November, but the calendar never lies – and thus, we’re here with another monthly round-up. Keep reading to find out what you can expect from November and how to make the most of it. Or don’t, and be surprised! We’re not the boss of you.

Sailing into the month, the first Event you’ll encounter is a blistering fight to the death in the Sea of the Damned! Not the most relaxed of beginnings, but ideal for any crews hoping to increase their favour with the Factions – and earn some exclusive cosmetics and Doubloons along the way.

What the notes say

  • To get involved, simply set sail on a Sloop as either a solo or duo crew and use your Hourglass of Fate to pick a Faction.
  • From there, you can dive beneath the waves and emerge into a six-ship free-for-all at your leisure.
  • Turning our attention to the weekly recurring Events, Emissaries of Fortune will once again be active each Wednesday of the month.
  • During this 24-hour rush (10am-10am UTC), crews who boldly raise an Emissary Flag for their chosen Trading Company will enjoy increased rewards for their efforts!
  • But with so many daring Emissaries around, pirates who align with The Reaper’s Bones are sure to be out in force too…
  • Our second weekly Event is slightly less regular than its companion, in that Fight Nights takes a week off whenever Last Ship Standing is active.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
