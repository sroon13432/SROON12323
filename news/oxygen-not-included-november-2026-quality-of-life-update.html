Oxygen Not Included: November 2026 Quality of Life Update

Oxygen Not Included · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

This free update brings the Duplicant swimming skill to all versions of ONI! It also contains new recreational buildings, support buildings, UI/UX upgrades, and some key performance improvements we've been working on for a while. Plus, The Aquatic Planet Pack gets a splashy new critter morph. And everyone gets free access to the Hatch-themed blueprints from our new paid Classic Critter Cosmetics Pack!

The Swimming skill was originally designed for The Aquatic Planet Pack, but our goal when we first started creating these DLCs was to ensure that significant systems changes would be added to all versions of ONI. So we're glad we can now include it for the base game and Spaced Out! as well. We also added the Aquatic Grooming Station to all versions, to help restore balance to Pacu farming.

What the notes say

  • There's a new aquatic critter morph in the colony: the Merpip!
  • They love long refreshing swims, planting aquatic seeds in liquid pools, and feasting on Caviar and molted exoskeletons...delicious!
  • We've added a new recreational roller coaster building to all versions: the Space Racer!
  • It's a callback to one of our favorite OG buildings that was removed from the game many moons ago.
  • The Space Racer ride has a Turbo Mode option that ramps up the Morale boost Duplicants receive during their ride.
  • It just might make them a little queasy in the process.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
