Update 12.1.0

THE FINALS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

With the release of PRIME TIME, our new moderation tools have been hard at work, going through over 10M messages and 500k+ display names. We’re still working on the never-ending tweaking of these new systems as they are new, and we’re still sifting older display names through them. If you believe your username was flagged unnecessarily, please fill out this form, so the name can be reviewed. You can also report chat words and phrases that you believe are being flagged without necessity.

A mysterious fog has taken over the Arena, and with that, a bucket worth of tricks and cosmetics are now available! We’re also bringing some bug fixes to smoothen things out. Don’t forget, you have one more week to collect tickets during the Frights Galore Event!

What the notes say

  • It is the season of pumpkins, skeletons and spiders in your hair after all, and we’ve brought everything you need to celebrate these spooky times.
  • As a result, we’ll continue to make some changes as the season goes on, but for now this is one more immediately actionable change.
  • Between these two changes we think we’re in a lot better place now.
  • Early data from the Season 12 shows that we’ve perhaps slightly over-tuned the power of the CB-01, so this change aims to nudge that down to a more reasonable spot.
  • The Pike jumped significantly in usage, damage output and kill rates following the 12.0 changes, making it one of the more dominant weapons in the game.
  • We still believe it needed that help but we clearly went too far with the changes, so this adjustment to headshots aims to bring that back down slightly.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
