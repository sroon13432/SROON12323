Brotato 1.2.0.3

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

Snowball: only items bought in the shop or found in crates now give Elemental Damage. Tumor mutations no longer give extra Elemental Damage on every mutation.

Fixed a crash that could happen when a wave ended while the Brachiosaurus's foot was active, either on death or when winning the run.

What the notes say

  • Fixed no button being selected after buying an item with a gamepad, when the mouse cursor was left on another shop item.
  • Caveman: Boomerang is no longer offered as a starting weapon.
  • Snowball: only items bought in the shop or found in crates now give Elemental Damage. Tumor mutations no longer give extra Elemental Damage on every mutation.
  • Ammosaurus: now attacks while moving, so it no longer goes a whole run without attacking as Soldier.
  • Brachiosaurus: weapons can now target and hit its foot.
  • Fixed a crash that could happen when a wave ended while the Brachiosaurus's foot was active, either on death or when winning the run.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
