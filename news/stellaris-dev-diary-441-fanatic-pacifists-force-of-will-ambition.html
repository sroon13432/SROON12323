Stellaris Dev Diary #441 - Fanatic Pacifists, Force of Will Ambition

Stellaris · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Social Concord is a new Fanatic Pacifist Civic that lets you become the arbiters of galactic peace. To help you keep the galaxy’s warmongers in check, it grants access to a new Diplomatic Action:

In our final Dev Diary before the release of Willpower, we’re taking a closer look at one of the last major features we have yet to cover: the Force of Will Ambition.

What the notes say

  • Before we explore the Ambition itself, let’s look at Pacifism, one of the Ethics that can make use of this Ascension Perk if they have an appropriate Ideology.
  • Sow Peace trades Influence and Opinion for War Exhaustion and incentivization to end all wars of aggression.
  • If you have enough Influence to spare, you can stack the effect up to five times on the same empire.
  • It's important to note that this new diplomatic option is not limited to Social Concord.
  • Willpower also adds this to the Finisher effects of the Diplomacy tree.
  • Social Concord empires have an extra reason to put it to use: a unique and powerful modifier that remains active for as long as every member of the Galactic Community is at peace.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
