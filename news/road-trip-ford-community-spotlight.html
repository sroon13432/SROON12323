Road Trip: Ford Community Spotlight

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Since the release of the Road Trip: Ford DLC for American Truck Simulator, it has been fantastic to see so many of you swapping big rigs for four wheels and setting out to explore the USA in a whole new way! From scenic drives and sightseeing stops to screenshots, videos and stories from the road, we’ve really enjoyed following your adventures behind the wheel of these iconic Ford vehicles.

For those of you who haven’t yet started your own journey, we’ve picked out a few YouTube videos from creators who have been exploring the Road Trip: Ford DLC. Whether they’re taking a relaxed drive across the open road, collecting postcards using our new Atlas feature, or taking on some courier jobs, we're sure there is something everyone can enjoy watching below.

What the notes say

  • Be sure to check out their channels, leave them a like and subscribe for more content.
  • We also reached out to several content creators who have been checking out Road Trip: Ford DLC and asked them to share their thoughts.
  • Take a look at what they had to say below, and be sure to visit their channels for even more great Truck Simulator content!
  • From when it was announced, many of us were optimistic, and some were confused.
  • Having now tried the first iteration of it, I can confidently say that it is a great addition.
  • The ability to step away from your truck, have a casual cruise, and explore roads from a different angle, is an awesome experience.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
