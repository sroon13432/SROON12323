The PTS has officially reopened!

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Space Marine 2’s Public Test Server works as a separate version of the game featuring WIP content and features. Share your feedback to help us refine the patch before its official release. Your input provides an important perspective that will be reviewed by the developer team and have an impact on further game updates.

The Public Test Server opens once more today! Jump in until Nov 18 and test the new content from the upcoming Patch 16. Check out this quick FAQ to help you get started and ready before launch.

What the notes say

  • Since this is a test environment, expect the unfinished content and possible bugs.
  • As you will access it through a different game version, your progression will not be tracked.
  • It is not accessible on consoles (PlayStation/Xbox) or through the Epic Games Store.
  • (Optional) Right-click on your Library entry and go to Properties.
  • In the “General” tab, set the game’s default Language to “English”.
  • Once you’ve clicked on this entry, install the Public Test Server content.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
