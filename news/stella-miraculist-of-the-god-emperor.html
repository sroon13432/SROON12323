Stella, Miraculist of the God-Emperor

Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

Stella was still a child when Confessor Bernart of the Saint Cognatius Order of the Ecclesiarchy took her into his care, recognising her gift as a sign of the God-Emperor’s favour. Becoming as much her mentor as her warden, he personally oversaw her upbringing as a Miraculist — a living weapon of the Ecclesiarchy. Blessed with the gift of divination and the power to unleash purifying flames, she spent years accompanying the Confessor in his pilgrimages across the Koronus Expanse.

Her blazing aura would lend weight to Bernart’s sermons, her powers exposing heresy in all its forms. To the judgemental and uncompromising Bernart, she became an invaluable instrument for demonstrating the might of the Emperor and guiding the faith of His subjects. Ever grateful to the man who had raised her, Stella served with unwavering zeal, believing this to be her sacred calling and sole means of serving the God-Emperor.
