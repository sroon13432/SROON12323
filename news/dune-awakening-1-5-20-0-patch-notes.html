Dune: Awakening – 1.5.20.0 Patch Notes

Dune: Awakening · posted by the game's developer on Steam·9 November 2026·Source

Patch 1.5.20.0 is now live on all platforms, bringing the much requested offline mode, and a myriad of fixes and improvements addressing player feedback received since the console release. As usual, we’re eager to hear your feedback in our social channels. Thank you for being part of this journey!

It’s now possible to play the game in single-player mode without requiring an internet connection at any point, including creating and deleting single-player characters. A Go Online button allows players to get back online.

What the notes say

  • The Sardaukar Killer and The Great Convention achievements now unlock as intended.
  • Players who should already have them will receive them retroactively.
  • Single player custom server settings are now saved to the cloud, so they carry over when you continue the same save on another device.
  • Tweaked the placement boxes for Rug of the Desert Flyers and Sardaukar Carpet to make them easier to place close to other objects such as walls.
  • Fixed water generation in single player where it was not progressing correctly while the player was offline or on another map.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
