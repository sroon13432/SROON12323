Driver's Diary of October

SnowRunner · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Winter has hit this picturesque Swiss canton hard! Repair an old viaduct, protect roads from avalanches, and help a hot-air balloon festival take off. Your main objective is to restore a space observatory and deliver its delicate optical instruments!

October brought SnowRunner’s Season 19 and its snowy Swiss Alps, while November is just around the corner with RoadCraft’s Foundations Expansion, launching on November 3!

What the notes say

  • The accompanying update also brought improvements to the two latest Mercedes-Benz trucks, crash fixes for Nintendo Switch 2, and more.
  • Season 19 is included in the Year 5 Pass and is available on PC, PlayStation 4 and 5, Xbox One and Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2.
  • And if you want to get your hands on a Season 19 key, join our Discord server and keep an eye out for our regular giveaways!
  • With Season 19 freshly released, we're now turning our attention to SnowRunner's next major update and fourth expansion: Season 20!
  • We're currently looking at a release between the last few days of winter and the early days of spring, so stay tuned for more teasers and reveals as we get closer!
  • We can confirm that Expansion 3: Foundations will release on November 3rd.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
