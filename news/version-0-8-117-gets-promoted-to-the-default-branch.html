Version 0.8.117 gets promoted to the default branch

Manor Lords · posted by the game's developer on Steam·31 October 2026·Source

Unharvested Crops in October will no longer force-rotate, but instead will begin to slowly die and lose their yield, giving a window of time to salvage the field.

Farmhouse workers can boost a Fallow Field’s Maximum Fertility by applying Fertilizer during Summer. Fertilizer lasts for just over 2 years.

What the notes say

  • Fallow fertility replenishment builds up over time with most regeneration happening towards the end of a year cycle.
  • Similarly, crops now get most of their growth in the final months.
  • Upgrade Effect: The Forester’s Hut is repurposed to tend beehives in nearby woodland during Spring and Summer, harvesting Honey and Wax in Autumn.
  • Increases Shoal of Fish capacity by 15%, lowers reproduction by 50% but allows reproduction during all Spring, Summer and Autumn months.
  • Livestock assigned to a building can only be led by assigned workers from that building, and require assigned workers to be able to execute their tasks.
  • Families prioritize consumption of Food types that their Burgage Plots currently request from the Market Supply.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
