Patch v1.2.0.1

Brotato · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

First patchnote after DLC release! We are focusing on bug fixes for now (talking about you Survivor) we intend to address balancing in the coming days after taking the time to read your feedback. Thank you again for your reception on the DLC, we're only getting started!

Buying the Hourglass now correctly updates the shop: the Endless button and boss icon no longer show when the next wave isn't wave 20, and the upcoming wave icons match the actual next wave.

What the notes say

  • Primal Dread: mutagens (green little flask dropped by mutant enemies) drop more often (base chance 2% -> 3%).
  • Mutations can no longer copy the Survivor's "No weapon in the shop" effect.
  • Capped baby Trex speed to 95% of target player max speed.
  • Fixed a crash in co-op when several players chose a reward from a crate at the same time while playing the Survivor.
  • Buying the Hourglass now correctly updates the shop: the Endless button and boss icon no longer show when the next wave isn't wave 20, and the upcoming wave icons match the actual next wave.
  • Shop (wave 19): the Go button no longer gets cut off at the bottom of the screen. The boss icon is now shown inside the Endless and Go buttons.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
