Sea of Thieves Release Notes – 3.9.2

Sea of Thieves: 2026 Edition · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

The free customisation toolset introduced in Season 20 expands with new options and additions! For more info on everything else taking place this Season, head over to our

now has a significantly smaller install package across all supported platforms, and will deliver smaller downloads for each subsequent game update in future.

What the notes say

  • On the release of this update, players will find they are prompted to perform a single full redownload at the newly reduced package size.
  • The Devil’s Shroud surrounds the island, keeping crews focused on the action.
  • Due to the unique nature of the location, some World Events, creatures and island-specific features will be unavailable.
  • The Free Camera lock-on has now been improved, providing smoother movement when following a target.
  • While locked on, the camera will also smoothly shift when hitting obstacles and then return to the desired distance once clear.
  • The Free Camera can now be taken below deck and inside the Captain’s Cabin across all ship types, plus Skeleton Ships and the Burning Blade.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
