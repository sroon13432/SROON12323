Our 2027 Plans - Fixes and Stability

RuneScape: Dragonwilds · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Therefore, after Luminance, we will be slowing down on monthly content. This means quests, activities, and new features. We’re not stopping development of such content but slowing down on shipping it. Instead, our priority will be to fix the game over just releasing more stuff because that’s clearly what is more important right now.

We’ve built out a robust collection of feedback topics from you, the community, about countless things across Dragonwilds that have given us plenty of food for thought, ranging from minor frustrations to game-wide improvements, and everything in between.

What the notes say

  • It means smaller things like “enemy arrows feel too difficult to dodge”, but also bigger things like adding a Quest Log, broader diversity in set bonuses or greater variety inside vaults.
  • It’s setting the foundations for the years to come, to ensure that all of the exciting content we have planned for Dragonwilds’ future is built on solid ground.
  • We have opened a PTR (Player Test Realm), to work together with the community on ironing out issues before things get shipped.
  • Luminance is about to go into submission with platform partners, from that point on, the entire team's donning bug hunting gear.
  • Though off the back of this there will not be a wave of scary hotfixes and patches, we will be gathering improvements for a consolidated, stable release early next year.
  • If we are still staring down a Kalphite den of bugs by the end of January, we will choose to fix those over shipping quests, activities or other new features.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
