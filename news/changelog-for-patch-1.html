Changelog for Patch #1

Enshrouded · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Bosses now stay engaged in combat longer after having been attacked and even when players are far away. Previously, ranged fights against bosses such as the Fell Monstrosity could be frustrating when the boss suddenly started to recover health even while being shot at.

Fixed an issue with the Dragon Youngling that could cause a state where it was defeated but did not trigger the correct dead state. What is dead may never die something something give me my loot!

What the notes say

  • What is dead may never die something something give me my loot!
  • Reduced the size of the combat collider for the Fell mini critters.
  • Previously, they could block arrows when trying to shoot enemies behind them.
  • Added a few food options to the path before and in the first cave to avoid starvation before reaching the first wider area.
  • Props that are part of a Sparkworks setup can no longer be destroyed by attacks from either players or enemies.
  • Added a link to the news section on enshrouded.com to the main menu.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
