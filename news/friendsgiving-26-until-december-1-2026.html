Friendsgiving ’26 – Until December 1, 2026!

Trove · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

But this year, Chefsly has received some unusual requests from his guests, and he needs your help gathering the ingredients for their favorite meals! To make matters worse, the Qubeslys have gone missing just as the feast is about to begin. It's up to you to prepare the perfect Friendsgiving dinner and bring the family back together!

Meet Chefsly in the Hub, follow the event questline, and craft Trovian Bouillon at the Friendsgiving Oven to start preparing delicious dishes for your guests!

What the notes say

  • Explore the Geode Caves for Day Drops, fish for Neanderthal Plum Pike in Candoria, mine Primordial Flame in Dragonfire Peaks, and collect Bleached Bones to cook each guest's favorite meal!
  • Earn Friendsgiving Coins, open Plumed Chests, and unlock event collectibles!
  • Don't forget to complete the Qubeslys' daily quests and explore the Turkeytopia Table for more rewards!
  • Can you prepare every dish, find the missing family, and bring everyone together for a feast to remember?
