NARAKA: BLADEPOINT Update – November 5th, 2026

NARAKA: BLADEPOINT · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

The servers of NARAKA： BLADEPOINT will be suspended for maintenance from Nov 4th, 2026 23：00 (UTC) to Nov 5th, 2026 04：00 (UTC). We suggest you log out in advance and enter the game again after the maintenance.

Ever since merging his soul with Taotie, Kael, once an unremarkable disciple of the Herald, has wielded extraordinary power he can barely control.

What the notes say

  • He began calling himself The One Who Eats Void and threw himself wholeheartedly into the grand cause of saving the world.
  • But his soul and Taotie's are still not fully in sync, leaving him caught between ravenous bouts of gluttony and the constant struggle to keep his own desires in check!
  • Legend has it that this weapon was forged by the 'merfolk', criminals exiled to the Deep Ocean by the Herald.
  • An unusual weapon designed for underwater combat, it inherits the Herald's Prowess.
  • A spiral mechanism between its twin prongs stirs up an undercurrent and conjures swirling curtains of water with each swing and thrust, briefly trapping foes and subduing them unseen.
  • It requires water flow to launch an attack, so it's only usable in the water area.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
