The Gnashbash "Birthday" Celebration Returns!

Guild Wars 2® · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

Step inside the vaults and chat with the Black Lion “Birthday” Manager to learn more about this year’s event. He will mention that some Black Lion goods have been stolen and give you the locations to check to recover them. You’ll earn Black Lion Seals of Approval for your assistance. Check out the Gnashbash “Birthday” Celebration bonus event in your achievements panel for more ways to grab those seals!

The Gnashbash “Birthday” Celebration is back again! From now until December 1, experience a special Black Lion Trading Company event as Captain Evon Gnashblade invites you to a unique “birthday” celebration in Lion’s Arch. You don’t give Evon gifts on his “birthday”—instead, he provides deep discounts and spectacular offers! It’s an opportunity you can’t afford to miss!

What the notes say

  • Players logging in during the event will receive their “birthday” invitation in the mail, encouraging them to visit the Vaults in Lion’s Arch.
  • The message includes a Single-Use Portal to the Vaults Scroll to help you get there.
  • The Black Lion “Birthday” Manager will help you spend your hard-earned seals on a new Party Crown Skin for 250 Orichalcum Ingots and 20 Black Lion Seals of Approval!
  • Tomorrow’s weather forecast is calling for snow in your homestead!
  • The Snowy Garden Homestead Package includes everything you need to transform your homestead into a winter wonderland for 800 gems.
  • Until December 1, Transmutation Charges, Total Makeover Kits, Self-Style Hair Kits, and Name Change Contracts are available at a 20% discount.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
