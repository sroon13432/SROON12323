Coming in early February: Mid-Season Refresh

Last Epoch · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

We are excited to share some news about what’s next for Season 5: Rage of the Frostborn. We are launching a Mid-Season Refresh in early February that includes a limited-time event, a numerical balance pass, and a ladder reset! If you haven’t heard: Mike, our Game Director, has made one thing clear : we want you to hear about what’s coming from us, and we want to share things earlier than we have before. With that said, let’s get into some details about what’s included in the Mid-Season Refresh coming in early February.

When the Mid-Season Refresh arrives, it will introduce a limited-time event, Weaver’s Ascension. This is not a seasonal mechanic. Unlike our other content releases, Weaver’s Ascension will not carry over into Last Epoch: Orobyss. Once the event ends, it’s gone, so be sure to jump in while you can!

What the notes say

  • We have done a numerical-only balance pass that touches every skill in the game.
  • The balance changes will be carried over into Last Epoch: Orobyss.
  • We’ve heard your feedback and know that you’ve been asking for this.
  • We’re excited to see what builds players come up with after all the changes.
  • The Mid-Season Refresh will also include a ladder reset.
  • If you like starting fresh and racing up the rankings, this is your opportunity.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
