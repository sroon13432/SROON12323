Lucky Mystic Portal Event

Throne and Liberty · posted by the game's developer on Steam·9 November 2026·Source

During the event, interacting with a Mystic Globe is guaranteed to open a Lucky Mystic Portal. Standard Mystic Portals will not open.

※ Expiration: Lucky Mystic Keys expire on December 9, 2026. Mystic Keys are valid for 7 days, the same as standard Mystic Keys.

What the notes say

  • November 11, 2026 ~ December 9, 2026
  • All items provided through this event are non-tradable.
  • The event schedule and some rewards are subject to change.
  • ※ Expiration: Lucky Mystic Keys expire on December 9, 2026.
  • Mystic Keys are valid for 7 days, the same as standard Mystic Keys.
  • Don't forget to purchase Mystic Keys and Lucky Mystic Keys from the Shop during the event!
