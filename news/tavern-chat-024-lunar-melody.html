Tavern Chat #024: Lunar Melody

Gunfire Reborn · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Lang Yao's combat has a rhythm all its own. Snap your fingers to send note projectiles piercing through enemy lines; clap your hands and note bubbles will surge out and flood the battlefield. Even better, he can merge his melody with charged weapons, making every shot burst into a unique tune.

In this issue of Tavern Chat, we're bringing you more in-game reveals of the new heroes and new weapons from the "Lunar Melody" DLC. Hope you enjoy them!

What the notes say

  • Besides that, we'll be rolling out the Season 9 gameplay along with a brand-new map and BOSS for free when the DLC launches.
  • On the battlefield, Loong's presence is like moonlight on a dark night—hand seals flow, and clouds gather in an instant to lift her skyward.
  • Toss out her ear pendant, and the falling moon swells in the wind, showering the battlefield with radiant light.
  • When her heart calls, lunar essence gathers into the blade in her palm, unleashing swathes of moonlight with every swing.
  • And with a wave of her slender hand, meteors fall, and the galaxy itself ripples in their wake.
  • Firefly channels a charged laser, gathering energy as it strikes enemies.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
