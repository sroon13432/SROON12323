Premium Car, Premium Interior: Welcome the Lagonda to Your Garage

Forza Horizon 5 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

The Lagonda is just as famous for its inside as it is for its outside. While piloting this car, you’ll find yourself surrounded by premium leather and the latest in 90s technology, including digital screens and displays.

Just like some of your favorite gaming consoles of the era, Aston Martin released their second four-door saloon and went with the polygon look for the 1990 Aston Martin Lagonda.

What the notes say

  • High-quality materials alone don’t make a thrilling ride.
  • That part’s brought to you by the Lagonda’s hefty 5.3-liter V8 that produces 285hp and 320lb-ft of torque.
  • Unlock the polygonal, luxury tank that is the Lagonda for 20 PTS on the Festival Playlist from October 22 until October 29.
  • Time to take revenge for radio stars in this week's collectible challenge featuring our air-filled collectible: the inflatable VHS Collectible.
  • Complete this challenge and claim the Tape Rewind Car Horn.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
