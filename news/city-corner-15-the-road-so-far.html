City Corner #15 - The Road So Far

Cities: Skylines II · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

On today’s City Corner, we are sitting down and gathering around the fire to talk about the overall development of Cities: Skylines II. Since we took over, we have been working on improving the nooks and crannies of every corner of the game. Some of these changes have been easier to spot (looking at you, environmental improvements), while others have happened behind the scenes, quietly making sure everything runs a little better.

One of the areas we want to start increasingly working on is the simulation, and how we can keep all the little systems working together. Citizens need to get around, companies need to find places to operate, services need to do their jobs, and all of this needs to happen without bringing your carefully planned city into a full stop.

What the notes say

  • Our focus so far has been making these systems work together more consistently.
  • We have spent time looking at how citizens make decisions, how they move around in your cities, and how different simulation systems interact with one another.
  • We have also continued working on the behaviour of companies, households, and services, with particular attention to the challenges that come to light as your city grows larger.
  • A lot of this work is not necessarily something you will notice straight away, but it can make a difference when you are looking at the bigger picture.
  • The goal is to have a simulation that behaves more reasonably, puts less unnecessary strain on performance, and gives you a more reliable foundation to build on.
  • And, speaking of performance, this has remained an important part of our work.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
