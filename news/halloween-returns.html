Halloween Returns

V Rising · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 October 2026·Source

This Halloween-themed cosmetic DLC offers a wide range of seasonal-themed decorations for your castle. Though it will only be available to be claimed for a limited time, once you’ve claimed it, you will have access to everything in the pack forever onward! After unlocking the wicked possibilities of a more ghastly abode, you’ll wonder how you ever unlived without them.

It’s the time of year when zombies, ghouls, ghosts, and most importantly of all, Vampires rise to dominate the night! Let all those vile living creatures cower in the light while darkness creeps over the land. A time of celebration for us all, where we may flex our eternal power.

What the notes say

  • Claim the DLC in the storefront to get access to a spooky collection of decorations.
  • Take advantage of it while you can, and be sure to let all of your clanmates know to claim it before the time passes.
  • True evil is not done by those creatures that lurk in the darkest corners of Vardoran, but in the weight of judgment by your elders.
  • On October 26th, in partnership with Makeship, another V Blood ascends from Vardoran into our humble, unready earth.
  • See you all on October 26th when the Haunted Nights Castle Pack becomes available to claim!
  • Be sure to check back and bear witness to the return of everyone’s most feared foe, and one of our most demanded plushies yet!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
