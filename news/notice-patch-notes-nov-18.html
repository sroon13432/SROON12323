[Notice] Patch Notes | Nov. 18

AION 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

Players who have already obtained the Abyssal Forge: Weapon Selection Chest (Bound) using a Gear Change Voucher \ (Bound) will retroactively receive 1 Gear Change Voucher: Ludra \ (Bound).

Abyssal Forge: Ludra (Easy): Has the same HP as Normal mode, with mechanics that are easier to handle. Stats are adjusted so critical hits land more easily.

What the notes say

  • After scheduled maintenance on November 17 - Before scheduled maintenance on December 16, 2026 (PST)
  • After scheduled maintenance on November 18 – Before scheduled maintenance on December 17, 2026 (CET)
  • During the event period, Abyss Points earned from the following content will be tripled.
  • A new Substance Morph recipe will be added, allowing you to convert higher-grade gathering materials into two lower-grade gathering materials.
  • An issue where the chat automatically scrolls to the bottom when a new message is received while scrolling through chat history will be fixed.
  • Players who used Ludra Weapon Exchange Tickets before the substance morph recipe was adjusted will be compensated for the difference.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
