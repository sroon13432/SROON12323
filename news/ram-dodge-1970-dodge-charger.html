RAM & Dodge: 1970 Dodge Charger

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

The Dodge Charger 500 was introduced in 1969 as a performance-focused version of the second-generation Charger, developed with improved aerodynamics in mind for NASCAR competition. Its most recognizable features included a flush-mounted rear window that replaced the standard Charger's recessed glass and a modified front end designed to improve airflow. For 1970, the Charger 500 returned with updated styling for the new model year, including a redesigned grille and front bumper.

The 1970 model marked the final year of the second-generation Charger, building on the iconic design that had defined the nameplate during the muscle car era. The Charger 500 featured a distinctive front end with a wraparound chrome bumper, a clean grille, and the signature hidden headlights. Its long hood, sweeping roofline, sculpted bodywork, and revised rear styling gave the Charger 500 a character that remains instantly recognizable.

What the notes say

  • The Charger 500 featured a distinctive front end with a wraparound chrome bumper, a clean grille, and the signature hidden headlights.
  • Its long hood, sweeping roofline, sculpted bodywork, and revised rear styling gave the Charger 500 a character that remains instantly recognizable.
  • In American Truck Simulator, the 1970 Dodge Charger 500 will offer plenty of options to make this classic muscle car your own.
  • The vehicle comes in two trim levels: Standard and SE, with the SE featuring a more luxurious interior and a special exterior badge.
  • Players can also choose from different vinyl roof covers, a variety of exterior and interior colours, and two different steering wheels designs.
  • It will also feature its iconic sliding headlight covers, adding to its distinctive front-end design.
