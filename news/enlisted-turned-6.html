Enlisted turned 6!

Enlisted · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

You'll get rewards during the whole event, and the further you progress through it, the more valuable they'll become. You can easily skip a few tasks and still get the main rewards!

From November 10th (13:00 UTC) to December 10th (13:00 UTC), get battle score in battles and move up the rewards ladder! You have two days to complete each task!

What the notes say

  • Tasks are updated every two days at 13:00 UTC, and the scoring starts again. Be sure to collect your reward before then,
  • The task can be completed in the Squads mode.
  • From December 10th (13:00 UTC) to December 12th (13:00 UTC), after the end of the event, you will have the opportunity to purchase any missing rewards for Gold.
  • An improved version of the Browning Automatic Rifle, manufactured by Colt in 1942 for the Netherlands.
  • It is chambered for the .30-06 Springfield cartridge and comes with detachable 20-round magazines.
  • The squad can be filled with 6 soldiers: it comes with 4 machine gunners and an engineer armed with Colt R75A machine guns.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
