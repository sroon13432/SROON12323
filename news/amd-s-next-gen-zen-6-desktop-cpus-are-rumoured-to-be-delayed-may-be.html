AMD's next-gen Zen 6 desktop CPUs are rumoured to be delayed, may be pushed back to second half of 2027

PC Gamer · industry report·20 November 2026·Source

Zhang Zhonghao makes mention of Computex in their post, which some are speculating means a launch date after the show in June of next year.
