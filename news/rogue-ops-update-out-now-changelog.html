Rogue Ops Update Out Now | Changelog

Gray Zone Warfare · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

You can now create multiple playable characters and explore plenty of new content, including new weapons and enormous new locations. The update also brings a wide range of community-requested improvements, a streamlined health system, enhanced visuals, and environmental improvements that make Lamang feel more alive.

Update 0.5 sets you on the path to Ground Zero, introducing a new gameplay loop centered around crafting and building your Hideout, along with new contraband operations that allow you to build your reputation as a rogue PMC.

What the notes say

  • If you'd like to further support the development of Gray Zone Warfare, you can upgrade to one of the game’s higher editions.
  • Each edition includes gear, weapons, and resources, giving PMCs a stronger starting point and offering extra value to those backing the game’s continued development.
  • Supporter Edition owners also get early access to major updates through the Supporter Preview, several days before the full release.
  • With Update 0.5, we are avoiding a full wipe and proceeding with a partial wipe instead.
  • This will allow us to rebalance the in-game economy for the new update while letting players retain their progression and avoid having to grind from scratch.
  • Players will retain their progression from Update 0.4, including completed tasks, rank, unlocked apparel, vendor levels, and statistics, while their locker and money will be wiped.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
