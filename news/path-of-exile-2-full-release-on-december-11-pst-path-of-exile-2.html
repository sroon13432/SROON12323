Path of Exile 2 Full Release on December 11 PST

Path of Exile 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Experience the complete six-Act Campaign, two new Classes and weapon types, sweeping balance changes, a new Challenge League, and much more as Path of Exile 2 enters Full Release.

Explore 24 distinct new areas and face 18 new unique deadly bosses. In addition, discover over 20 new Champion Rare encounters, each with its own mechanics and surprises.

What the notes say

  • The Shadow introduces Stealth, an entirely new mechanic in Path of Exile 2 unlike any stealth mechanic you've seen before.
  • While in Stealth, monsters can't see you until you get within a short distance of them.
  • Being in the shadows also empowers your dagger skills, granting them a range of additional bonuses.
  • In addition to daggers, the Shadow wields another new weapon type: Traps.
  • Prepare your traps while in stealth, then trigger them automatically as you strike. Traps explode in the order they were thrown, allowing you to set up the perfect combo!
  • On release, the Shadow has two Ascendancy classes: the Assassin and the Infiltrator.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
