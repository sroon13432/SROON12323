The Scourge - A Spooky Event Comes to Dune: Awakening

Dune: Awakening · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 October 2026·Source

It begins as a rumor. Crazed attackers sighted in the deep desert. Sun-touched madmen who seem to spring from nowhere, only to disappear again into the darkness. Later, their bodies are found twisted and broken beneath the Arrakeen sun.

A mysterious disease has begun spreading across Hagga Basin, leaving madness and death in its wake. Venture into the desert, confront the infected, and uncover the truth behind the strange affliction.

What the notes say

  • Venture into the desert, confront the infected, and uncover the truth behind the strange affliction.
  • Speak to Frida Maltros at a trade station to begin the investigation.
  • Then search Hagga Basin for the afflicted and collect Infected blood from your encounters.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
