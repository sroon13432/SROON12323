Rally DLC and Update 1.9 Out Now!

Arma Reforger · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

brings a new single-player racing experience to the islands. Choose from three rally cars, take on six tracks, and put your driving skills to the test against the clock. With a major vehicle simulation overhaul arriving in the same update, there is plenty changing beneath the bonnet, too.

Customizable race conditions give you more ways to test yourself. Learn the route, get a feel for the grip, and find the balance between speed and making it through the next corner. A little confidence can save you time. Too much... and you might meet a tree.

What the notes say

  • Customizable race conditions give you more ways to test yourself.
  • Learn the route, get a feel for the grip, and find the balance between speed and making it through the next corner.
  • Three rally cars bring different strengths and handling characteristics to the starting line.
  • Each comes with a choice of racing liveries, but what is underneath will matter more for your finishing time.
  • Vehicle access follows the approach used in Arma 3; where owners can drive DLC vehicles, while players who do not own the DLC can still ride in them as passengers.
  • An upgraded 130-horsepower engine, a five-speed gearbox, and a wider body kit turn the familiar Šárka into a much more ambitious machine.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
