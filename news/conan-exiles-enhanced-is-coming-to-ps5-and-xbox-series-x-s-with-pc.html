Conan Exiles Enhanced Is Coming to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with PC Cross-Play!

Conan Exiles Enhanced · posted by the game's developer on Steam·27 October 2026·Source

Following its successful launch as a free update for Steam owners in May 2026, Conan Exiles Enhanced is making the leap to consoles following strong demand from the community. Existing owners will receive a free upgrade from PlayStation®4 to PlayStation®5 and from Xbox One to Xbox Series X|S.

“We’ve heard loud and clear from our console community that they want to experience the Enhanced version of Conan Exiles. We’re very happy to finally confirm that it’s on the way, and we can’t wait for players on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S to experience the Exiled Lands at their best,”

What the notes say
