Extra Large Map Update, New Achievements

Kingdoms and Castles · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

To make this possible we spent a lot of time optimizing. We took maps that many of you modded to be larger and analyzed them. We needed to find out exactly where and why we were losing frame time or getting lag spikes on these huge maps. We reworked the code to alleviate the issues we found as best we could:
