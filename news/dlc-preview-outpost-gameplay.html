DLC Preview: Outpost gameplay

Barotrauma · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Once you have an outpost, you can expand, decorate, and manage it through the Outpost Management UI. Add new modules like bedrooms, nightclubs and farms, decorate both your outpost and your submarine, and assign Dwellers to do Research and other tasks that benefit you.

Any outpost in the game can be conquered and made your own, and additionally you can buy an outpost from an NPC you will encounter. You can even own multiple outposts.

What the notes say

  • Just as you conquered your outpost, you will need to protect your outpost against attackers.
  • Research defensive weaponry and train your Dwellers to keep the outpost safe.
  • Your outpost can trade with neighboring outposts, and you can see and interact with these supply ships on your travels.
  • With enough resources, you can even send colonists ahead to populate later biomes.
  • Travelling to and from your outpost is facilitated by a shortcut network that allows you to return to your home base without backtracking through the map.
  • For a longer, more detailed sneak peek at all the outpost systems, head over to our blog, and stay tuned for more content previews soon!
