Lost & Found Update

Escape the Backrooms · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Fixed an issue where kicking a player could leave their character visible in the game and cause the elevator control panel to disappear.

Fixed an issue where launching the game offline and subsequently reconnecting to the internet would not restore online functionality.

What the notes say

  • Level 94: Fixed an issue where a player disconnecting inside the puzzle room could prevent the door from closing and the clown chase from starting.
  • Darkened Suburbs (Level 9): Fixed an issue where the Wretch could become trapped in the initial corridors of the MEG bunker.
  • Fixed an issue where a player's character could become invisible during the Deathmoth carry-to-nest animation when viewed in spectator mode.
  • Fixed an issue where rebinding the left mouse button to the right mouse button could prevent interaction with the puzzle before The Hub.
  • Fixed an issue where returning to the game after Alt-Tabbing could cause the interface to lose focus, preventing further interaction.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
