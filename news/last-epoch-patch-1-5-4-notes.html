Last Epoch Patch 1.5.4 Notes

Last Epoch · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Fixed a bug where the Displacement node would cause shields to be consumed without effect when there are no valid targets. The shields will now only be consumed if Shield Throw was successfully cast.

Fixed a bug where the "Glancing Blow chance per 5 Dexterity" threshold bonus of the Careful Footing passive node would cause Glancing Blow chance to increase indefinitely over time.

What the notes say

  • Fixed an issue where Runic Invocation's Trinity of Elements did not stay attached to the player after leaping with Stride to Safety or evading.
  • Fixed an issue with the SFX of Power Rush (Shield Bash at the end of Lunge and Shield Rush).
  • Previously, you could not hear the Shield Bash audio when triggered after Lunge or Shield Bash.
  • Fixed a bug where corrupting a unique with variant modifiers and granting it an affix would display the variant modifier in the item’s sealed corrupted slot, instead of the added affix.
  • Gauntlet of Strife: Fixed a bug where legendary items with variant modifiers could not be rerolled with an item whose Legendary Potential matched the item's legendary affix count.
  • Lightless Arbor: Fixed an issue where the light level granted by the dungeon ability would not properly change based on its amber amount.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
