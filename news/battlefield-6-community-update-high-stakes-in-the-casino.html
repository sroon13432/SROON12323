Battlefield 6 - Community Update - High Stakes in the Casino

Battlefield™ 6 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Our newest map in Battlefield 6 drops your squad into a nighttime, infantry-only combat zone set inside a large, European-style resort. Engagements move quickly from open courtyards into packed gaming floors and narrow hallways where destruction plays a central role.

Blow through breakable walls to carve out flank paths or just play in the lazy river. But watch your head, because the massive center tower can come crashing down to permanently change the flow of the map.

What the notes say

  • But watch your head, because the massive center tower can come crashing down to permanently change the flow of the map.
  • Players who love the tight, back-and-forth infantry combat of maps like Operation Metro will find a similar flow here, updated with the modern tactical destruction of Battlefield 6.
  • Along with the start of Casino Heist, the limited-time event goes live across Battlefield 6 and REDSEC.
  • Defeated enemies will drop Contracts that give you additional objectives to complete during your matches, from capturing points and reviving teammates to exploring the European Casino map.*
  • Claiming every standard item on the reward track will also unlock one final challenge for a bonus completion reward.
  • Keep an eye out for our dedicated Event Guide for the full breakdown.
