Peter Moore, who famously announced GTA 4 with a tattoo, posts fake GTA 6 tattoo pic and says he hopes it will give the industry 'a shot in the arm'

PC Gamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

videogame executive Peter Moore, known for his time at Sega, Xbox, and Electronic Arts as well as a couple of famous tattoos, has expressed hope that the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 will provide a much-needed "shot in the arm" for the videogame industry.

Moore began with a faked photo of himself rolling up his sleeve to reveal a GTA 6 tattoo—although the photo is based on his real-world tattoo-based reveal of Grand Theft Auto 4 on Xbox 360 at E3 2006.

More from the report

  • "In all seriousness, the videogame industry could really use a moment like this," Moore wrote on X after posting the image. "We've been through a challenging period.
  • "That's why the launch of GTA 6 feels so significant. It's more than the release of a hugely anticipated game.
  • Moore's career in the game industry began at Sega, where he oversaw the launch of the Dreamcast and quickly rose to become president and chief operating officer of Sega of America.
  • And he did indeed announce GTA 4 with a tattoo—apologies for the resolution, it was 20 years ago.
