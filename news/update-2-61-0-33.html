Update 2.61.0.33

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

From the Battle Rating of 13.3 and above the SAMs used in convoys have been changed. The Pantsir-S1 has been replaced with the 2S6 Tunguska and the ItO 90M has been replaced with FlaRakRad. This change doesn’t affect the very first convoys at the beginning of the battle which are formed from the strongpoints’ garrison; their SAM composition stays the same: Pantsir-S1 for the “red” team and ItO 90M for the “blue”.

A bug that occurred when using the Wayland windowing system on Linux, where switching to another window caused focus to return to the game window immediately upon hovering the cursor over a visible part of the window, rather than only after interacting with it has been fixed.

What the notes say

  • Patch notes reflect only key changes, meaning they may not include a complete list of all improvements made.
  • Additionally, War Thunder is constantly being updated and some changes may not require an update.
  • Changes reflected in patch notes are formed by taking reactions and requests of the community from the bug reporting service, forums and other official platforms into account.
  • Bug fixes and changes are implemented in order of importance, for example a game-breaking bug will be worked on and implemented sooner.
