Editions for Gray Zone Warfare: Rogue Ops

Gray Zone Warfare · posted by the game's developer on Steam·22 October 2026·Source

Now is the perfect time to jump into Gray Zone Warfare. The Rogue Ops update for Gray Zone Warfare sets you on the path to Ground Zero, introducing a new gameplay loop centered around Hideout and crafting, new opportunities, and more of Lamang’s secrets to uncover.

Gray Zone Warfare editions offer more than just in-game perks. They’re also a way to support the game’s ongoing development. To help you choose the edition that’s right for you, we’ve put together a complete overview of what each one includes.

What the notes say

  • All items included in the editions can be obtained in-game without purchasing them.
  • The editions simply offer a more convenient starting point while supporting the game’s development.
  • If you’ve purchased one of the higher editions, the upgrade will be automatically applied to your account.
  • You can pick the Tactical, Elite, or Supporter Bundles, or purchase the Standard Edition first and upgrade to Tactical, Elite, or Supporter at any time.
  • If you already own the Tactical and Elite Editions, you will only need to cover the remaining difference to complete the bundle.
  • As per Steam’s terms, all DLC purchases are non-refundable.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
