Luxion's Challenge – Now Live!

Trove · posted by the game's developer on Steam·17 November 2026·Source

is here, bringing an epic boss battle, new auras to collect, a fresh way to customize your Club World, and plenty of improvements to make your adventures even better.

Gather your friends, sharpen your weapons, and prepare to face a powerful new foe—the Trials of Luxion have a brand-new final challenge!

What the notes say

  • Watch out for his powerful tail whip, dodge his ground-shaking wing pounds, and find your way past his wing shield to claim victory.
  • You'll even have a friendly guide following you around the Hub to help you get started.
  • Tiny Quests are getting a collection of improvements, including better Synergy and reward balancing across different world difficulties.
  • Tiny Quest consumables now contribute more toward Synergies and rewards, and Gold rewards are now achievable at every difficulty!
  • We've also polished up reward tooltips, pet previews, and other little details to keep your Tiny Quest adventures running smoothly.
