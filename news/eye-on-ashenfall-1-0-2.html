Eye On Ashenfall | 1.0.2

RuneScape: Dragonwilds · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

At level 14 Magic, you'll be able to equip Earth Runes for Staves and Wands. Earth attacks deal Blunt Damage, staggering enemies and interrupting attacks.

We're back! Another month, another update. Let's talk about everything coming your way. A new quest, new activities, Earth Magic and... Penguins?

What the notes say

  • Earth Magic joins Air, Fire and Water, and brings a new, earthy flavour to Magic.
  • Earth Magic specialises in AOE Damage, launching waves of earth at the target.
  • The final hit of the Staff Combo hits the area around the target too, catching nearby enemies.
  • The Staff's Special Attack creates an earthquake, with multiple aftershocks.
  • Once cast, you can leave it to do its thing while you reposition, to make terrain difficult for your foes.
  • There's also a defensive benefit to this - Armour of the Earth.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
