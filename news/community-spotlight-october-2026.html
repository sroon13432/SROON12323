Community Spotlight 🧐 October 2026

Space Engineers · posted by the game's developer on Steam·2 November 2026·Source

Welcome to Pelagia. This world is a volcanic one, a matriarch to dozens of islands born anew from the hearth within. Its oceans cover nearly the entire surface, broken up only by a handful of island chains and archipelagic landmasses - hence the name, Pelagia.

We can't wait every month to spotlight mods and blueprints that catch our eye and have the potential to make your time in Space Engineers more interesting and unique.

What the notes say

  • A gigantic Earth-like planet with a 500 km diameter and 10 Gs of gravity.
  • Do you have the skills and patience needed to escape its clutches?
  • Oh, the aerodynamics mod and Cryo respawn mod are there to add some extra challenge too!
  • Vanilla Star System world for use with the "Real Orbits" mod, with planets adjusted to prevent overlapping gravity wells.
  • Other planets are in default locations (to my knowledge, correct me if I am wrong) and have been replaced with the terraforming-compatible variant.
  • Welcome to the Salvage Guild planet Osara (pronounced O-sara).

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
