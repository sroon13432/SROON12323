PRINTED AND POLISHED

Rust · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

The overarching theme for this overhaul is shifting the difficulty. The previous blueprint fragment system caused a lot of friction in the early game progression, especially for players with less time on their hands. Due to the spawn locations of basic fragments, transitioning to a Workbench level 2 could be a difficult task. If you couldn’t compete, you weren’t just slowed down, you were locked out. The lowest tier blueprint can now be found in common loot sources such as barrels and boxes, but most importantly can be upgraded into the next tier of blueprint.

Workbenches no longer require blueprint fragments to craft, and scrap isn’t used on the tech trees. Instead, we’re introducing three tiers of blueprints; blueprint fragments, blueprint pages and blueprint books. These are the new currencies spent to progress tech trees. One for each tech tree level.

What the notes say

  • Items can still be researched with scrap on the research table.
  • There is a much greater emphasis on finding, trading for, or making allies to get items you need.
  • Workbench upgrades which affected scrap used on the tech tree have also been reworked.
  • Instead of reducing the amount of scrap used, the salvage workbench upgrade now has a 20% chance to not consume a blueprint.
  • The prototype workbench upgrade will now be a flat 25% success rate, with the cost doubling removed.
  • We don’t know exactly how this will play out, and we’d be kidding ourselves to think we’ve got it perfect on the first iteration.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
