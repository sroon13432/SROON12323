Patch Notes - WS v1.3.7 / BL v1.5.7

Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

Owning a fleet has always demanded substantial progress - ships of your own, and crews that man them. We wanted to keep that intact while opening up more ways to access naval content earlier.

Join us in watching the video below as we walk through the key highlights included in this update. Prefer reading instead? You can find the full text breakdown further below.

What the notes say

  • The first is the ability to begin your Sandbox with a small trading ship through the new Advanced Starting Options.
  • You can now travel between certain coastal villages without owning a single ship.
  • You'll need to be in a coastal village, out of an army, with less than fifty troops, and carrying the fare, which scales with your party size and the hostility of both ends of the trip.
  • And for when you're just starting out, we've made the waters a little more approachable as well.
  • Smaller pirate parties now spawn to give new captains encounters they can successfully take on.
  • Then, as you move on to building up a fleet, you have more flexibility in deciding which ships to take with you.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
