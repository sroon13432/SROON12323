WARDOGS | Minor Balance Patch 0.31 - Pricing Change

WARDOGS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

We’re aware of the ongoing conversation around in-game items, weapons and vehicles, their balance, and their unlock tracks. Our Design Team is currently examining how best to implement changes that will improve the situation, but that work will take considerably more development time.

A few weapon archetypes sit in an awkward spot right now, and we’re looking at how to improve them in a future update, including more incentives like Suppression Assists. That’s a wider piece of work. For now, LMGs are getting a small price reduction to reduce some friction.

What the notes say

  • For now, LMGs are getting a small price reduction to reduce some friction.
  • The Verba has seen a lot of use this season, but it wasn’t creating a financial incentive to bring one.
  • Lowering the price means a miss hurts less, and makes it more worthwhile taking into a match.
  • Last season tanks were on the battlefield from day one and we didn’t want a repeat, which meant setting their unlock levels later than we would have liked.
  • Now the season is underway and the XP modifier has started to kick in, we’re comfortable pulling those unlock levels back down slightly.
  • Small changes to infantry loadout costs, to reduce the cash sink Bandages can become and to bring the Molotov in line with where the Frag Grenade currently sits.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
