Ghostly Halloween Update 1.03

TCG Card Shop Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·28 October 2026·Source

Bulk Boxes are now consolidated into generic versions to support all current and future card expansions. Along with this change, the new OG Bulk Box (Omega Grade) will be created when the total bulk card value exceeds $5,000. The Tetramon and Destiny Bulk Boxes will remain in game but will no longer appear in the price app or be available for creation.

The default branch now uses the new rendering method with the GPU issue fixed and optimizations implemented to help the game runs better. Materials and lighting have also been adjusted to bring the visuals as close as possible to the classic look.

What the notes say

  • Spooky season is here at last and what better way to celebrate than to rip some pack and hunt down six new Ghost cards!
  • Ascension Card Box and Playmat (Halloween) are added in game, along with a new poster decoration featuring artwork by Bad Moon Art Studio.
  • Removed Tetramon and Destiny bulk boxes (it will remain in game, but won't be shown in the price app and cannot be created anymore)

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
