Beat Saber Update v1.46.0 with Original Soundtrack Vol. 9

Beat Saber · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

This update also brings a long list of improvements and fixes. There's news for mappers, with new Level Editor features like walls on all 5 layers, easings for light color events and a Preview Object Colors toggle, plus a big batch of editor fixes. Hit sounds are no longer cut out on dense maps, chain links are fixed, and we cleaned up visual bugs in the Ghostbusters, Coldplay and Spooky environments, plus the flickering lasers in "Witchcraft" on AMD graphics cards. You'll find everything in the full release notes below.

Seven artists, seven very different tracks. OST 9 goes from alternative rock and indie pop to hip-hop, electro house, symphonic metal and hardcore. Every song was made together with our mapping team, so each one is built for Beat Saber while still sounding exactly the way the artist intended. And it all plays out in the new Prism environment.

What the notes say

  • Star Slinger – “Nostalgia is a Curse”
  • pineappleCITI – “One of Us”
  • Added: Original Soundtrack Vol. 9, a free pack with 7 new tracks
  • Added: Prism environment
  • Fixed: Chain links had small gaps between their hitboxes
  • Fixed: Hit sounds cut out on dense maps and on maps with changing note speed
