Planned balance changes in the upcoming update

Crossout Remastered · posted by the game's developer on Steam·18 November 2026·Source

Comment: unpopular autocannons. The reduction in energy drain will allow for the use of additional necessary modules in a build. The damage changes help maintain the weapon’s balance in light of the reduced energy drain and the introduction of additional modules.

We remind you that all the new features described in this news are not final and may be changed before they are introduced into the game or may not make it into the game at all. You can see all the planned changes in more detail on the special testing server.

What the notes say

  • You can see all the planned changes in more detail on the special testing server.
  • Comment: the cabin has high efficiency in ranked game modes.
  • The changes build on previous adjustments, and the shield will now be used much less frequently.
  • The changes increase the efficiency of the armoured car’s turn via the perk but make its use less common.
  • The changes also increase the efficiency of the wheel when used in combination with other movement parts.
  • Comment: armoured cars equipped with “MVP Flagship” currently have inflated survivability and maneuverability parameters.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
