Hotfix 762172

Don't Starve Together · posted by the game's developer on Steam·6 November 2026·Source

Fixed bug causing Skittersquids to not spawn during Dusk, and a crash when Skittersquids are set to “Little” in world settings.

What the notes say

  • Added map screen sounds.
  • Fixed bug causing Depth Worm Waves to never come even if enabled.
  • Fixed bug causing Moonblind Crows and Misshapen Birds to behave incorrectly during a Moonstorm event.
  • Fixed bug causing Skittersquids to not spawn during Dusk, and a crash when Skittersquids are set to “Little” in world settings.
  • Fixed bug causing Werepigs to always be stunlocked when attacked.
  • Fixed bug causing additional Mermummies to spawn on every reload.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
