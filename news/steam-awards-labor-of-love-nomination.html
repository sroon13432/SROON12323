Steam Awards Labor of Love Nomination

War Thunder · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

We’ve put a stronger focus on quality of life this year, we’ve been developing new gamemodes, adding and improving new features for all parts of the game, refining the graphics and detailing bomber cockpits too. We still have one final update for you this year as well, we really hope you’ll like it.

This year we celebrated War Thunder’s 14th anniversary. It’s been a very long road, many of you have been with us since the very start, but wherever you joined us along the way we’re just glad that you’re here.

What the notes say

  • Today Steam is organizing the Steam Awards, where you can vote for your favorite games in several categories.
  • We ask that you consider nominating us for the "Labor of Love" category.
  • Through all the ups and downs War Thunder has an incredibly devoted team behind it.
  • Many have been here since the beginning, and it’s become a large part of our lives in the same way that it’s become a large part of yours.
  • Spending as much time as you do with us is humbling, and while we don’t always get things right, you’re still here, and that means more than we can say.
  • Not really, what matters to us is that you’d consider voting anyway.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
