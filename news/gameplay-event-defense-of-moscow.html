Gameplay event: Defense of Moscow

Enlisted · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

Infantry against tanks. In this event, German armored vehicles can also capture points, while the Soviets must hold the line, relying on smart positioning and their anti-tank rifles. Time to find out who will prove stronger in this important battle!

Autumn 1941. The Eastern Front, 30 kilometers from Moscow. As the enemy advances toward the capital, every meter of frozen ground becomes a scene of fierce clashes between infantry and tanks.

What the notes say

  • The tasks can be completed in the special Events mode.
  • The rewards can be claimed during the event without any time restrictions.
  • From November 29th (13:00 UTC) to December 1st (13:00 UTC), after the end of the event, you will have the opportunity to purchase any missing rewards for Gold.
  • Both during the event and the purchase phase, you can purchase the RPG-41 VK separately in the Shop for Gold too.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
