Stellaris Dev Diary #438 - Warmongers, Golden Generation, and Absolute Ethics

Stellaris · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

The previous Willpower Dev Diaries have been somewhat focused on the more 'friendly' approaches to spreading Ideologies, but is that all there is?

To demonstrate the more aggressive aspects of Ideologies, let's play with an empire using the new Fanatic Militarist Civic: Warmongers

What the notes say

  • While it may look complicated at first, the core idea behind Warmongers is quite simple: Fight big enemy, get big strong.
  • Stay at peace, or pick a fight with an empire weaker than your own, and you'll suffer a penalty.
  • Additionally, you'll gain access to a new type of living standard: Defect Filtering, and a unique set of military-themed Strata.
  • These special Pop Strata come with unique effects tied to mandatory military service, making even your idle population part of the war machine.
  • There really isn't room for anything in this Fanatic Militarist society that does not involve conflict, but while we're at it let's adopt a Militarist Ideology and pick a Tenet.
  • To be clear, this is a general Militarist Tenet and available to anyone who adopts a Militarist Ideology.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
