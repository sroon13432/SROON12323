Post 1.0 Dev Blog

Fields of Mistria · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Now that we are in a post-1.0 world, we wanted to share what the team is working on and what you can expect from Fields of Mistria in the months ahead! Below you will find a summary of our current goals, which in the short-term are primarily focused on making Fields of Mistria accessible to more players worldwide.

This has been an incredible experience, and we're so grateful to everyone who's joined us on this journey, whether you've been here since Early Access or visited Mistria for the first time with 1.0.

What the notes say

  • One of our highest priorities is finishing the localizations for all of Fields of Mistria’s supported languages.
  • A large localization update will be released before the end of the year!
  • We'll continue releasing patches to fix bugs, improve stability, and make performance improvements.
  • We want to ensure every player has the best possible experience with Fields of Mistria.
  • Your feedback and bug reporting have been incredibly helpful for tracking down and fixing bugs!
  • We're excited to share that we are exploring opportunities to bring Fields of Mistria to other platforms!

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
