EA removes some live service fluff from Skate and ends seasonal structure "in favour of regular, agile content updates"

Eurogamer · industry report·26 November 2026·Source

EA is ditching seasonal updates and shaving some of the live service fluff off Skate (for some reason stylised as skate.) going forward. Normally, such news would be cause for concern.

Here, the publisher assured players the transition will lead to more regular updates since devs can now push out new features without waiting for the next season to roll around.
