"Silk & Silver" Dev Diary #10 - 2D Art & Achievements

Crusader Kings III · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

moves the focus away from the throne room and down to the harbor, the market square and the counting house. Most of our illustration library was built around kings and their courts, so a lot of the work this time was about giving merchants and patricians a lot of visual content.

We started where we always do, with a round of quick sketches to find the mood of the expansion. We knew from the start that we wanted to focus the story on a merchant visiting a busy trade port somewhere far from home.

What the notes say

  • As a Merchant, increase your Company Size to Trade Empire
  • As the Central Germanic head of a Republic, reach Kingdom Tier. May be locked for custom characters; meaning that you will not be able to make a custom ruler that already fulfills all these…
  • Eat a Central Germanic Burgher (A Patrician in a Republic of Central Germanic culture)
  • Upgrade your Trade Company or Consortery to a Palace and every external Building to its final tier
  • Take the Found the Hanseatic League Decision
  • Max out your Alcohol Trade Expertise and hold a Monopoly in that Goods Category in your home Market
