Developer Diaries: New Map: Raider's Bay

World of Warships · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

The two flanks are significantly different from one another. The northern side features several fairly large islands and offers more or less familiar gameplay. The southern side is more unusual: it's rich with small islands, making it challenging for battleships and submarines to navigate. The flanks are separated by large islands in the center of the map, which surround the Key Area. Contesting it will be both risky and rewarding if successful.
