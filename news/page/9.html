Where these articles come from

Game Updates are the announcements a game's own developer or publisher posted on its Steam hub. The headline, the quoted lines and the link back to the original are theirs; nothing is rewritten into a rumour and no article is written for a game that has not posted anything.

Industry stories come from the public news feeds of GamesIndustry.biz, Video Games Chronicle, Gematsu, Eurogamer and PC Gamer. We keep the headline, quote the published sentences and link to the full story at the source.

Stocks & Market is a daily record of the quotes Yahoo Finance returns for the listed chip makers, platform holders and game publishers behind the games here, with the exact time of every quote. It is published data, not investment advice.

What CanMySpec adds is the hardware side: a patch note is matched to the game's published minimum and recommended requirements, so you can see whether an update matters for your card. For those figures, open the game's spec page or the hardware filter.
