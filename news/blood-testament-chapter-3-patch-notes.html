Blood Testament Chapter 3 Patch Notes

Hunt: Showdown 1896 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·3 November 2026·Source

A four barreled, medium ammo pistol with a rotating firing pin which allows for a high fire rate. Favored by Hunters on safari as a self-defense weapon due to its superior reliability and stopping power. Opening the break-action will always eject all rounds.

A variant of the Lancaster with an under-barrel blade, changing its melee attacks to be similar to a Heavy Knife in both swing pattern and damage.

What the notes say

  • All Bileweaver Infested Compounds (new and Old) will have a chance to spawn in Chapter 3.
  • Players get matchmade into a separate queue if their ping is above a threshold.
  • The goal is to reduce exposure to high-latency gameplay.
  • Should they not be able to find a match, they rejoin the regular matchmaking queue.
  • “Reap the Harvest” is a new Community Event that is planned to start later in Chapter 3.
  • The Community Event will consist of 5 Stages, each offering a reward to the whole of the community.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
