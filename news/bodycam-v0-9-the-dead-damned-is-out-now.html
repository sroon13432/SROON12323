Bodycam v0.9 The Dead & Damned is OUT NOW

Bodycam · posted by the game's developer on Steam·13 November 2026·Source

The zombies are back, with a brand new mode on Trenches and a full pass on Village and Asylum. Multiplayer gets its share too: Easy Anti-Cheat, a reworked Public Pool, a new drone and RC car room in the lobby, and two new weapons in your loadout.

Before getting into the details, we want to take a moment to explain why this update looks the way it does, and where the studio is heading.

What the notes say

  • It was a demanding update, and right after it, we went heads-down on Locked & Loaded.
  • So we made the call to take Zombies offline in early September, when Locked & Loaded launched.
  • We know there is a strong Zombies community, and that many of you love Bodycam for it.
  • Bringing it back around Halloween, like last year, was important to us.
  • At the same time, it takes a lot of the dev team's time to work on two games at once.
  • We want to take Zombies much, much further: we know there is a strong community around it that wants more, and this game mode deserves real dedication.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
