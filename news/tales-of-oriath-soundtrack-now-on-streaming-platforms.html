Tales of Oriath Soundtrack Now on Streaming Platforms!

Path of Exile 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

This release was made possible thanks to the incredible work of our composer, Kamil Orman-Janowski, alongside a solo cello performance by Martin Roberts.

Introducing Tales of Oriath, a collection of tracks from the Path of Exile 2 soundtrack! The full soundtrack will follow at a later date.

What the notes say

  • Thrax, Cyclops of Trarthus
  • Whether you're exploring Wraeclast, taking a break between maps, or simply want to enjoy the music outside of the game, you can now listen to these tracks wherever you go.
  • A huge thank you to everyone who has supported and enjoyed the music of Path of Exile over the years.
  • We hope you'll enjoy listening to these tracks as much as we do!
