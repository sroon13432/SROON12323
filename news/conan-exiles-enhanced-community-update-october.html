Conan Exiles Enhanced Community Update - October

Conan Exiles Enhanced · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

It’s been over a month since our last community update but we promise the wait was worth it as this week we’ve had some major announcements coming to the Exiles Lands.

We know a big part of the community has been asking and hoping for Enhanced to come to consoles and that is what will happen next year.

What the notes say

  • Thank you to everyone in the console community for being so patient and understanding while we worked on the logistics of bringing the upgrade to PS and XBOX.
  • Read all about Conan Exiles Enhanced coming to consoles here: Conan Exiles Enhanced Is Coming to PlayStation®5 and Xbox Series X|S as Free Upgrade - Conan Exiles
  • This time Akhamet will be taking on a bigger role in the quest and maybe a bigger part of your experience going forward.
  • More details about Part Three, and what awaits players in the next chapter of the story, will be revealed closer to release.
  • If you are in need of a good laugh, check out this hilarious thread on the forums where some of the people in the community share all the silliness they came across in the Exiled Lands.
  • What’s something that you still haven’t done yet in the game?
