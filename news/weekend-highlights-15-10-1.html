Weekend Highlights | 15.10 #1

World of Warships · posted by the game's developer on Steam·7 November 2026·Source

Cultists and airships and Relics, oh my! Update 15.10 comes plunging into World of Warships with a new horror-themed PvE event front-and-center. That's just the tip of the iceberg, though, because we also have an all-new line of European cruisers entering the game in Early Access, as well as the return of Black Friday, UI improvements, and more! Check it all out in our dedicated article:

World of Warships' grandest tournament kicks off this weekend and will conclude in spectacular fashion later this month. Watch the top teams from each region face off for their share of the 50,000 USD cash pool, and enjoy some in-game rewards yourself by collecting Twitch Drops and completing special missions. What the notes say

  • The Recruiting Station's winter campaign continues, with a second shot at a head start for the grand finale coming in Update 15.11.
  • Recruit new players, complete Division missions, and grab some fresh Recruiting Supplies from the Recruiting Station Store.
  • But most importantly—revel in the joy of naval combat with your friends!
