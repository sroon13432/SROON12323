Gabe Newell stuns superyacht enthusiasts by treating crew like human beings: 'That is something we've never really seen before in our industry'

PC Gamer · industry report·16 November 2026·Source

The Leviathan, the 111-meter superyacht owned by Valve co-founder Gabe Newell, has a number of unconventional features. It has a pool for SCUBA testing. It has an NBA-regulation basketball court. An Aston-Martin can be found parked on its helipad. It forgoes the typical teakwood deck in favor of easier-to-clean synthetic flooring.

"The most surprising thing is what happens when we go through these doors," says Superyacht Times editor-in-chief Francesca Webster as she enters into an immaculate communal dining space on the Leviathan's main deck. "Rather than having the traditional saloon which we normally see on a superyacht—welcoming guests and being the first point of contact to the interior—Gabe actually opted to utilize this incredible space for the crew mess."

More from the report

  • Instead of banishing his crew to whatever dank closets and canned rat-gruel might be typical for superyacht staff, Newell enjoys his meals alongside the crew each day.
  • "It's all about shared experience on board his boats. And that is something that we've really never seen before in our industry," Webster says.
