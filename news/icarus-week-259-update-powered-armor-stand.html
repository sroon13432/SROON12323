Icarus Week 259 Update | Powered Armor Stand

ICARUS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

In a few weeks we will be celebrating 5 years of Icarus where we will be looking back at the last 260 weekly patches as well as some exciting news, more details will follow next week.

This week, we're taking a look at the brand-new Armor Stand. When powered, it will slowly repair any armor placed on it, giving you an easy way to keep your gear in good condition.

What the notes say

  • Fixing skinning state and corpse materials on many of the workshop animals as it was not applying their correct cosmetic variation in some cases
  • Adjusting Advanced Armor Stand item, item template and talent to Core feature level
  • Fixing legacy creatures with variations (cat/dog/horse) prior to phenotypes where their corpses where not displaying the correct materials during various skin states
  • Fixing horse corpses (missed in last commit)
  • Added Bear Mount Talent icon.
  • Added the remaining bear saddle SKs to the project and to the DT. Tweaked the arctic survival saddle texture to accommodate LODs

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
