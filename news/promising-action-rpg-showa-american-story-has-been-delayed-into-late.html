Promising action-RPG, Showa American Story, has been delayed into late 2027 in order to feel "cohesive, responsive and complete" at launch

Eurogamer · industry report·25 November 2026·Source

Publisher 4Divinity and developer Nekcom Entertainment have announced that upcoming action-RPG Showa American Story will now launch on 28th October, 2027. This comes after various sites noticed a Steam page update that listed the game as launching in 2027, slipping from the previous, vague window that suggested the game would come out this year.

The announcement was made on the developer's Twitter account.
