SWTOR DirectX 12 Technical Test coming soon!

STAR WARS™: The Old Republic™ · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

The SWTOR team is excited to announce that the DirectX 12 Technical Test launches later this week! This is a major milestone for the long-term modernization of the game, and we’re looking forward to working directly with you for this critical first round of testing.

As we’ve shared in detail over the past year, the journey to a modernized SWTOR client began by reducing rendering features down to an absolute minimum in order to establish a reliable and performant baseline to build upon.

What the notes say

  • There are many important details about what you can expect at this stage, how to participate, and how to share feedback as we continue on this modernization journey.
  • The DirectX 12 client has come a long way from the earliest versions, and visual fidelity will evolve as development continues.
  • This initial test is primarily focused on client stability and performance across a variety of PC hardware and gameplay scenarios.
  • : You will notice that many environmental assets and visual effects are low-fidelity or missing, and graphics options have been limited.
  • This first phase is strictly about testing the engine’s core functionality, not the artwork.
  • We wanted to make participating in this test as simple as possible.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
