What the notes say

  • These four new Weapon Skins can be applied to any gun and are therefore usable by all Vault Hunters, but each is themed around the vibes of a base game Vault Hunters in
  • : "Troubled Upbringing" is modeled after Vex, "Fresh Spray" after Rafa, "Tally Them All" after Amon, and "Merch Line" after Harlowe.
  • If you missed the rewards from our previous Community Challenges, you can still grab the SHiFT code for prior Vault Hunter Skin rewards for the paid DLC Vault Hunters
  • Requires a copy of Borderlands 4, SHiFT Account, and Internet connection.
  • *Story Pack 1 DLC has two parts: new story content in Mad Ellie and the Vault of the Damned, and a playable Vault Hunter with C4SH the Rogue.
  • Included in the Borderlands 4 Super Deluxe Edition, Vault Hunter Pack, and available for separate purchase.
