Space Engineers: Warfare 3 Update & Pack Live Now!

Space Engineers · posted by the game's developer on Steam·16 November 2026·Source

Warfare 3 makes combat more dynamic for both PvE and PvP players. Guided weapons, countermeasures, EMP technology, new targeting tools, expanded automation and configurable world settings create new decisions before, during and after every engagement - and give Engineers more ways to design ships, defenses and weapons around the battles they expect to face.

is here, bringing one of the biggest combat overhauls Space Engineers has ever seen. From Combat NPCs, guided missiles and torpedoes to countermeasure flares, EMP weapons, Jump Detection, new automation options, and much more, this update changes how battles are fought, defended, and engineered.

What the notes say

  • Anti-Personnel Turret Ammo Box
  • The PRO-1 Rocket Launcher can now lock onto grids, display a lead indicator and fire Homing Missiles at locked targets.
  • A new world setting controls whether players can use Relative Dampeners to match the movement of hostile grids.
  • Heavy grids performing long-distance jumps can generate temporary Jump Signature GPS markers, creating new opportunities for pursuit and interception.
  • Event Controllers can detect incoming guided missiles and torpedoes, allowing automated alarms, defensive systems or countermeasure launches.
  • Target lock now uses the center of a grid rather than its Pivot Point, while the Target Info LCD App provides more useful subsystem information.
