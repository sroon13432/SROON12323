V Rising · posted by the game's developer on Steam·19 November 2026·Source

As for work, I’ve spent the last few years doing social media, marketing, and community stuff in games. I’ve worn a lot of hats: making posts and funny pictures, running the socials, working with creators, organizing community events, hosting streams, and listening to what players have to say (a lot of listening, actually). Along the way, I got to work with some really great communities, and that’s probably my favorite part of the job. Now I get to wear a cape and some cool fangs while I do it.
