Newzoo analyst expects Grand Theft Auto to turn around a rough year for console gaming: 'without GTA 6 the console market would likely be down year-over-year in 2026'

PC Gamer · industry report·18 November 2026·Source

Consoles cost more than ever, and even if you can afford to buy one you don't have as much left over to buy games for it as you used to. That's made 2026 an underwhelming year for console gaming sales, but according to the latest forecast from videogame market analysts Newzoo, the imminent launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 will turn that around.

"As we explored in the Global Games Market Report 2026," said Newzoo's director of market intelligence, Emmanuel Rosier, "without GTA VI the console market would likely be down year-over-year in 2026. Instead, the game's launch is expected to generate billions of dollars in revenue and give console spending a significant boost."

More from the report

  • Rosier is the analyst who, back in August, was skeptical about the idea of BioWare surviving long enough to make another Mass Effect game.
  • "Western console has been losing ground," he went on to say. "Its share of global games revenue fell from 21.5% in 2015 to 17.1% in 2025, and its revenue has been broadly flat since 2021.
  • Newzoo's prediction is that GTA 6 "will lift console full-game spending by 17.5% this year". But that's just the start of the story.
