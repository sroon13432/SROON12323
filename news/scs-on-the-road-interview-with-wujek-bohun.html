SCS On The Road: Interview with Wujek Bohun

Euro Truck Simulator 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·7 November 2026·Source

We're back with another episode of our SCS On The Road series! This time, we're joined by Polish YouTuber and content creator Wujek Bohun, who we recently had the pleasure of welcoming to our offices for a tour and a chat! You may have already seen his own video from the visit on his YouTube channel, but today we're excited to share our interview with him in his own studio!

In this episode, we sat down with Wujek to learn more about his journey as a content creator, how he first got started on YouTube, and the experiences that shaped his career along the way. We also took a trip down memory lane as he shared his earliest memories of video games and how his passion for them has developed over the years.

What the notes say

  • Of course, we couldn't miss the opportunity to talk about Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator!
  • From his favourite DLCs to some of the features and improvements he'd love to see in the future, we covered plenty of interesting topics.
  • A big thank you to Wujek Bohun for taking the time to visit us and sharing his story!
  • We hope you enjoy this latest episode of SCS On The Road, and don't forget to drop a like and subscribe if you liked it.
