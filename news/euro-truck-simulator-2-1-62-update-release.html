Euro Truck Simulator 2: 1.62 Update Release

Euro Truck Simulator 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Before we head to the news, we would like to thank everyone who took part in the Open Beta and reported any issues or provided general feedback on our forum. This makes it much easier for our team to fine-tune everything and helps ensure a smooth transition to the full update release.

We are releasing three new editions of the DAF XG+! The Emerald and Sapphire Editions bring distinctive green and blue paint jobs, exclusive exterior accessories, and specially designed interiors, offering drivers a stylish new way to personalise their trucks.

What the notes say

  • We are introducing new truck garage models across the entire base map and all map DLCs, so you’ll be able to enjoy these refreshed garages wherever you go.
  • The new garages feature updated visuals both in the interior and exterior, with greater attention to detail and a more modern, immersive feel.
  • We hope this will make upgrading your garage feel even more rewarding.
  • Of course, performance was also an important consideration.
  • We are continuing to expand the Improved Material System with this update, bringing it to more truck interiors.
  • The latest Goodyear logo and slightly adjusted colours are now featured across the paint jobs, bringing them in line with the current design.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
