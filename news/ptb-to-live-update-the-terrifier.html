PTB-to-Live Update | The Terrifier

Dead by Daylight · posted by the game's developer on Steam·20 November 2026·Source

Coming away from the 10.3.0 PTB, we saw how much of a blast you had testing out (and coming face-to-face with) The Terrifier's Bag of Toys! With the release of 10.3.0 right around the corner, we wanted to give you a sneak peek at the Killer Power and addon changes you can expect to see.

The Acid Bottle was a little too effective while looping, so we toned down how quickly it could be thrown, while increasing the duration of Acid Pools to keep the focus on more thoughtful and strategic ricochets and setups.

What the notes say

  • We’ve increased movement speed while aiming the Handgun, making aiming feel slightly more forgiving, while still limiting its effectiveness in loops.
  • This gives the Killer more time to plan out their plays and keeps Toy progression consistent, so it's not harder to gain Deadliness when using maxed Toys.
  • We've shifted the focus towards improving the Acid Pool’s hit potential to make the Bottle feel more rewarding to use.
  • These have both received small number nerfs as we found their PTB states to have potential to be too oppressive, especially as we make it a little easier to reach max Toy Deadliness.
  • As always, we want to thank you all for providing feedback during the PTB, and we hope you’re looking forward to release on November 24th!
  • Keep an eye out for the Patch Notes for all the details.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
