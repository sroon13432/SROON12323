Halloween Greetings and Updates 🎃

MiSide · posted by the game's developer on Steam·30 October 2026·Source

The game is tentatively scheduled to arrive on mobile in early 2027! We'll be sharing more news on our official social media accounts. Stay tuned!

Happy Halloween! This year, alongside a 20% discount matching our historical low, we've also prepared a special Halloween illustration for you!

What the notes say

  • We're also considering the possibility of bringing Mita to consoles, but we don't have anything to officially announce just yet.
  • To everyone who previously purchased merchandise through Kickstarter or other platforms and is still waiting to receive all their items: we're sorry to have kept you waiting so long.
  • The collectible figure brand we're working with has encountered unexpected production issues, delaying delivery of some items.
  • We've been keeping track of production and shipping progress, and we'll continue working to move things forward so that your merchandise reaches you as soon as possible.
  • Peaceful Mode is still in active development, and we've been working hard on it!
  • We did underestimate how much development time this content would require, and we're sorry to have kept you waiting so long.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
