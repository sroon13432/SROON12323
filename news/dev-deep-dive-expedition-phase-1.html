Dev Deep Dive: Expedition - Phase 1

Marathon · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

Expedition is a fully PvE cooperative roguelite mode with a dedicated queue across Perimeter, Night Marsh, Outpost, and Cryo Archive. Whether you’re rolling out solo, duo, or with a full trio, you’ll work together with multiple Runner crews to confront the ever-evolving horrors that make Tau Ceti a dangerous, unforgiving frontier. And worry not, you’ll be able to complete Priority Contracts while in Expedition mode, so you’ll be able to uncover more mysteries at your own pace.

CyberAcme and other interested parties have aligned to establish a Runner non-aggression pact to combat the increased UESC security measures across Tau Ceti IV. No matter if you can beat them: it's better if you join them.

What the notes say

  • With the update just around the corner, we’d like to give some insight on Phase 1 of our new permanent cooperative PvE game mode: Expedition.
  • Featuring a whole new curated experience with bespoke rulesets, Expedition includes a new in-run upgrade system we're calling Flash Boosts.
  • All in all, our goal is to make buildcrafting more interesting and make every run series unique, and an opportunity to test a new build out.
  • Each session will consist of three runs of escalating difficulty called a Run Series.
  • Build up your shell, complete your series, push your limits and profits that would even make Traxus proud.
  • We’ll reveal more about Expedition’s Phase 2 in March, then keep improving the mode with your feedback as we shape Marathon’s future.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
