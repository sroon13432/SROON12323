GTA 6 looks gorgeous, and seeing it run at a stable 30 fps on ageing PS5 Pro hardware makes me hopeful for PC performance

PC Gamer · industry report·18 November 2026·Source

From what we're already seeing of Grand Theft Auto 6 on console, it's boding very well for a future PC release. It might only be targeting 30 fps on console, but with how stable that is and with the graphics looking so stunning on older PS5/PS5 Pro hardware, I've got high hopes for how it might run on our gaming PCs.

Digital Foundry (DF) has taken one for the team here by doing some performance testing on an inferior platform (cheap shot, I'm sorry). And judging from the screenshots, videos, and frame rate analysis so far, GTA 6 looks very much next-gen in terms of scale, scope, and fidelity.

More from the report

  • We're talking fine detail on the local scale combined with open, bustling sight lines to the horizon, depending on your vantage point.
  • It's hard to describe how fantastic GTA looks. It does seem like a new era for open world games.
  • What particularly impresses me, judging from DF's gameplay and graphics shots, is how seamlessly fine detail combines with great lighting and breaks into long distance views.
  • One minute you're inside a bustling store, with incredibly realistic ambient lighting and reflections thanks to the ray tracing implementation (plus ray traced shadows on the PS5 Pro),
