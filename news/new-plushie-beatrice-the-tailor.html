NEW PLUSHIE: Beatrice the Tailor

V Rising · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 October 2026·Source

As with the previous plushies, this is an entirely opt-in campaign! If there’s enough interest and enough people pre-order, Makeship will produce as many as are desired and send them out to everyone who ordered one. These are going to be a limited run, so if you want it, make sure you hop on the opportunity as soon as you can!

Vardoran’s roundest and most beloved V Blood has arrived, and rest assured, she is everything we could have ever imagined and more. With her scissors, craft bag, and little arm yarn full of push pins, she’s everything you loved about the matron of Dunley, all wrapped up in a small, soft, huggable package.
