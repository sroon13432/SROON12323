Two Weeks Until ExileCon 2026!

Path of Exile · posted by the game's developer on Steam·25 October 2026·Source

The keynote officially starts at 1:00 PM on November 6th PST. We’ll go live at 12:30 PM with a pre-show discussion featuring community streamers ZiggyD, Mathil, RaizQT and DarthMicrotransaction.

The main presentation showcasing the latest Path of Exile 1 and Path of Exile 2 developments is around 2 hours long, and will be followed by other content throughout the two days.

What the notes say

  • Various talks from our developers, live gameplay showcases, interviews, and, of course, race finales for Path of Exile 1 and Path of Exile 2!
  • The keynote will be available to watch on our Twitch and YouTube channels shortly after it airs live.
  • The developers’ talks and panels will be posted sometime after ExileCon.
  • We will eventually publish all of them so you can rewatch them at any time.
  • We treat ExileCon as our regular livestream, so all relevant trailers and announcement pages will be available as soon as the content is announced during the keynote.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
