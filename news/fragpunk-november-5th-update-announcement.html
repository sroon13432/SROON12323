FragPunk November 5th Update Announcement

FragPunk · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Farlands is still in active development. Please note that all test data will be wiped when the test ends and will not carry over to future versions. This includes character levels, mission progress, resources stored in your Warehouse and base, vehicle and equipment progression, and other data accumulated during the test. More playable characters and weapon blueprints will become available as development continues. Keep an eye on official update announcements for details.

Our new large-scale gameplay experience, Farlands, launches in Early Access on November 5! Venture beyond Shard Clash into the vast borderlands: take orders, haul cargo, and extract. Between cooperation and betrayal, bring your loot home.

What the notes say

  • Venture beyond Shard Clash into the vast borderlands: take orders, haul cargo, and extract.
  • Between cooperation and betrayal, bring your loot home.
  • The "Wasteland Rhapsody" Battle Pass is still live (October 22 - December 31).
  • Meanwhile, the "Farlands Early Access Pass," made specially for Farlands, launches with Early Access on November 5 and is free to claim!
  • Level up the Battle Pass to earn Materials, Supply Chests, and other rewards, plus courier cosmetics like "GoldDash" and "GreenDash."
  • The FarLands Challenge goes live on November 5 and runs through November 19.
