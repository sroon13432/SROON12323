Warhammer Survivors update buffs XP and weapons, nerfs those damn Beasts of Nurgle

PC Gamer · industry report·25 November 2026·Source

If you've played over a hundred hours of Vampire Survivors you've probably forgotten how slow its early progression feels. It certainly seemed that way based on the reaction to Warhammer Survivors from Vampire Survivors veterans, who complained about its grindiness compared to their beloved bullet heaven originator, despite the arc of both games being quite similar. Developers Auroch Digital made Warhammer Survivors in partnership with Vampire Survivors creator Poncle, so of course the two games have a lot in common.

Other complaints were more valid. The Beasts of Nurgle did have too many hit points, and their ability to regenerate only exacerbated that. The treasure chest-opening animation did take a bit too long. And some of the weapons were undertuned. Fortunately, those issues and more have been addressed in hotfix v2.18.
