Icarus Week 258 Update | Workshop Lantern

ICARUS · posted by the game's developer on Steam·12 November 2026·Source

This week, we're introducing the Inaris Lantern, offering prospectors another lighting option alongside the recently introduced Inaris Flashlight. Like the Flashlight, the Lantern is battery-powered, but its standout feature is its impressive 360-degree lighting coverage. Rather than concentrating light in a single direction, the Lantern illuminates the surrounding area, providing excellent visibility across your entire workspace. This makes it an ideal choice for prospectors working through the night, setting up camp or carrying out tasks where a wider spread of light is needed.

We're also offering a sneak peek at what's coming next week: the Powered Armour Stand! This handy new addition will automatically repair your armour while it's placed, making it a useful addition to any workshop or base.

What the notes say

  • Improved accuracy on Raptor collision assets to provide more consistent/predictable hits
  • Increased distance at which damage hit popups render when hitting boss creatures
  • Additional Spelunking Mission Clean-up so objects don't get left behind post mission completion
  • Strange Troop Operation (Styx Great Hunt) now accepts legendary flamethrower to complete the flamethrower task
  • Unlocked workshop battery lantern
  • Raptor physics asset cleanup, fixed head location, rotation, and size to be more accurate

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
