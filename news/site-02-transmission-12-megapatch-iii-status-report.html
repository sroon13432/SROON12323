Site-02 Transmission #12 | Megapatch III Status Report

SCP: Secret Laboratory · posted by the game's developer on Steam·25 October 2026·Source

Before we dive in, we have released a developer log on our YouTube channel that discusses what has happened between now and February involving the Surface Zone (SZ) rework, SCP 953, and other features in development.

In this post, we will be revealing the progress we have made on 15.0 and what we have left before we can launch the much-anticipated beta and full release later this year.

What the notes say

  • What is left before the beta
  • The SCP item limit for humans has been reduced from three SCPs to two.
  • Human slow walk speed has been increased to 2.3 m/s from 1.6 m/s (for comparison, walking is 3.9 m/s & sprinting is 5.4 m/s).
  • The Chaos Insurgency footstep volume and range has been altered to match that of the MTF’s footsteps. Note that the sound effects remain the same,
  • A new security office is being added to the side room of PT-00 in LCZ, locked behind a Containment Tier 2 door.
  • Scientists will now spawn exclusively in checkpoints. In addition to this, a Facility Guard spawn has been replaced with a Scientist spawn.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
