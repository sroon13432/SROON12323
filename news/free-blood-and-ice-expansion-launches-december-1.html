Free Blood and Ice Expansion Launches December 1

Dune: Awakening · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Since then, we've rebuilt the endgame, released three DLCs, and brought the game to consoles alongside single-player and the conclusion of Book One. More than one million new players have joined us since the console launch, and we're grateful for the response.

We're also introducing new in-game events, a cosmetics store, and announcing the work on some of the most-requested features from the community, including offline single-player, character transfers, and full cross-play.

What the notes say

  • The expansion introduces the Polar Sink, an entirely new survival map where everything you've learned about surviving in the desert will be put to the test.
  • Instead of protecting yourself from extreme heat, you'll need to find ways to stay warm.
  • The technology you've developed to survive the desert won't be enough here, and you'll need to upgrade your equipment, gather new resources, and adapt your strategies to survive the cold.
  • Your journey begins in the tundra, where wealthy water shipper families have established lavish strongholds.
  • While conditions here are more manageable than on the ice cap itself, these families won't welcome anyone interfering with their operations.
  • Beyond the tundra lies the heavily guarded ice cap, the most prized territory of the water shipper families.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
