Halloween Update - v1.31

Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

Still, we couldn’t let Halloween pass without dressing up. A touch of it has found its way into Avalon: two new transmog sets are waiting for you, along with a small patch bringing fixes and improvements. All the details below. And for now, let's celebrate with a Halloween trailer!

A witch’s outfit is waiting for you somewhere in the swamps. Where exactly? Let’s just say it’s a spot that suits the grave mood of Halloween perfectly. We’re not spoiling more. Good luck!

What the notes say

  • A witch’s outfit is waiting for you somewhere in the swamps.
  • Let’s just say it’s a spot that suits the grave mood of Halloween perfectly.
  • Disclaimer for those who finally find it: yes, it has basically no combat stats.
  • For everyone who wants to walk into battle looking like an easy target.
  • Unseen makes your armor invisible: you keep all the protection, your enemies see none of it.
  • Some skeletons in Horns of the South have a chance to drop pieces of armor upon death.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
