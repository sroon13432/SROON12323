7.42a Gameplay Patch

Dota 2 · posted by the game's developer on Steam·24 November 2026·Source

Fixed removing Aghanim's Scepter from Morphling while replicated Gush projectiles are flying, leaving a permanent projectile on the map

Fixed Beastmaster's Thunderhide not dying when the Summon Thunderhide ability is lost (Rubick's Spell Steal or Morphling's Replicate)

What the notes say

  • Fixed a server crash related to the Tormentor dying
  • Fixed a crash that sometimes occurred when building Ristul's Devotion
  • Fixed a server crash that could happen when Kez's Grappling Claw is reflected
  • Fixed a crash related to Techies' Sticky Bomb
  • Fixed pseudo-queued orders not getting the correct priority (e.g. Naga Siren's Mirror Image interrupting Power Treads toggling)
  • Fixed some subtle "stop" command behavior

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
