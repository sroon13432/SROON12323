Accompanying the new Focus Pack is a small update that mainly focuses on re-balancing some aspects of the Eastern Front, where Romania notoriously fought during the war. You of course can make your own decisions and avoid fighting a costly eastern war on the behest of others and carve out your own future.

SOV: Builds more divisions before the war when world tension is high, and focuses on military factories during the Great Patriotic War

What the notes say

  • FIN: Sissi and National Armor Focus now also give a 20% mastery bonus for 180 days
  • FRA: France starts with Stronghold Network unlocked in 1936
  • SOV: Desperate Measures now provides +50% mobilization speed instead of 25% and -10% minimum training level instead of -5%
  • Added Land Forts in a few key places representing the stalin line.
  • Re-worked some of Romania's MIOs, also for those without TTBE.
  • Added 1 General and 1 Admiral, also for those without TTBE.
