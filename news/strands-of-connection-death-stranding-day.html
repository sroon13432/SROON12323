STRANDS OF CONNECTION - DEATH STRANDING DAY

DEATH STRANDING DIRECTOR'S CUT · posted by the game's developer on Steam·8 November 2026·Source

The community has helped me do so many things I never thought I'd do, and come out of so many shells. It helped me make so many friends and form so many connections. It has also helped me find all the skills I had tucked away thinking I could never use, and it is all thanks to this amazing community that I managed to really embrace and enjoy them.
