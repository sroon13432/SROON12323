DevDiary: Where Rivers Meet Mangroves

Fishing Planet · posted by the game's developer on Steam·5 November 2026·Source

Choosing a name for a waterway is part of discovering a place. We always spend time exploring its geography, nature, and culture, looking for something that captures its character. Sometimes, a real name already tells the story, as with Dnipro River in Ukraine or Emerald Lake in the USA. Other times, we create a name that doesn’t exist, but feels like native to the region.

We wanted to give this corner of the world the attention it deserves. That’s why our Australian journey will take us to two different locations, and they’re just the beginning of something bigger.  We’ve never done anything quite like this before, so let’s dive into our first DevDiary and discover what awaits at Cassowary Coast, Queensland, Australia!

What the notes say

  • All visuals shown in this DevDiary showcase a work-in-progress version of the waterway and are subject to change before release.
  • We wanted to give this corner of the world the attention it deserves.

  • We’ve never done anything quite like this before, so let’s dive into our first DevDiary and discover what awaits at Cassowary Coast, Queensland, Australia!
  • Australia gave us a different challenge: finding a name for a place where freshwater flows toward the sea, connecting two fishing worlds.
  • And there it was: Cassowary Coast, already on the map, right in the region that inspired our new waterway.
