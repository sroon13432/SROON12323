BODYCAM V0.9 THE DEAD & DAMNED - RELEASE DATE & WHAT'S COMING

Bodycam · posted by the game's developer on Steam·29 October 2026·Source

0.9 is a smaller, in-between update. A big part of the team is still focused on the larger, long-term projects coming to Bodycam, and in the meantime we wanted to bring you some fresh content. Zombies get the spotlight this time, but multiplayer isn't left out, with a reworked Public Pool and the arrival of the anti-cheat.

Let's start with the one you probably didn't see coming. Zombie Trenches is a brand new game mode built on the Trenches map, and we can't wait for you to try it. More on that one soon.

What the notes say

  • We went back over them to fix a lot of what you reported, especially around spawns and how zombies behave, and gave them a proper polish pass.
  • Weapon boxes now drop weapons with random attachments, using the same attachment system as your loadout, so what you pull from a box changes a lot more from one run to the next.
  • Public Pool has been the least played map since Locked & Loaded, and we wanted to change that.
  • We gave it a big visual rework to bring it up to the quality we're aiming for across Bodycam, and we made some improvements to its layout along the way.
  • This is a big one for multiplayer and it should help a lot.
  • We'll keep a close eye on it after release and keep improving it.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
