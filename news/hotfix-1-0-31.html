Hotfix 1.0.31

Valheim · posted by the game's developer on Steam·26 November 2026·Source

Unfortunately yesterday's patch made two achievements unable to be unlocked, so we have a hotfix for you today to get that sorted. We also just wanted to let you know that we're not done with the adjustments to the Forge of Potential yet, and that we're reading your feedback!

Please note: This patch is network compatible with 1.0.30, so while console platforms might take some additional time to update to 1.0.31, you should still be able to access crossplay.

What the notes say

  • Made The Artisan achievable again after 1.0.30
  • Made The Master Chef achievable again after 1.0.30
  • Fixed a memory leak related to the Build UI
  • Fixed choppier / regular frame drops on PS5

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
