Operation Vital Signal Revealed

Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege · posted by the game's developer on Steam·15 November 2026·Source

Operation Vital Signal delivers meaningful improvements across Siege. Discover the Doc remaster, Solo/Duo and Flex Ranked playlists, the new MBZ-19, a Bank update, modernized maps, and more arriving on December 1.
