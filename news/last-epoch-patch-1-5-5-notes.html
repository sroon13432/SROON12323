Last Epoch Patch 1.5.5 Notes

Last Epoch · posted by the game's developer on Steam·23 November 2026·Source

Fixed an issue where Maelstrom's Arctic Chill node could only apply one stack of Chill per enemy per Maelstrom instance. This also affected Slow when converted through Cyclone.

Fixed the Pestilence Hound minion cosmetic's head and mouth not animating and its feet floating above the ground. Zombie Hound enemies received the same animation fix.

What the notes say

  • Fixed controller aiming speed for channeled abilities varying with frame rate.
  • The cursor now moves at the same speed regardless of FPS.
  • The Crystal Cave: Fixed an issue where the VFX for the rock falling on top of the bridge was active before the rock fell.
  • Outcast Camp: Fixed an issue where players leaving the game in this town would respawn far from amenities.
  • Fixed an issue where scene transition loading screens would occasionally display an erroneous brief dimming of the display.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
