Route 66 Paint Jobs Pack Release

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·11 November 2026·Source

Since the release of our Illinois DLC this May, we have also recreated the full length of Route 66 in American Truck Simulator, giving players the chance to experience this historic highway in-game. Earlier this year, our work was recognised as an Official Route 66 Centennial Project. This designation, approved by the United States government and Congress, places American Truck Simulator among the initiatives honouring the legacy of Route 66 as it celebrates its 100th anniversary.

During its heyday, Route 66 stretched 2,448 miles from Chicago to Los Angeles, becoming one of the most iconic highways in the world. On November 11, we mark the 100th anniversary of the day Route 66 was officially established as part of the U.S. Highway System. To celebrate this milestone, the Route 66 Centennial Commission has brought together a range of official projects that help preserve, showcase, and share the story of this legendary road with the world.

What the notes say

  • On November 11, we mark the 100th anniversary of the day Route 66 was officially established as part of the U.S.
  • To further support the Mother Road and its legacy beyond the virtual world, we are introducing the Route 66 Paint Jobs Pack.
  • Proceeds from the pack will go towards the American Truck Simulator Grant Program, run by the Route 66 Road Ahead Partnership, established by the United States Congress.
  • The program will provide funding to eligible businesses and events along Route 66, helping them restore, maintain, and preserve this historic highway.
  • We would also like to extend a huge thank you to William from the Route 66 Centennial Commission for working with us to make this possible.
  • The Route 66 Paint Jobs Pack features five unique designs inspired by this legendary highway, along with a range of themed accessories.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
