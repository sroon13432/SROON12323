The first images of the Lenovo Legion Go Beam have seemingly leaked, and it's got a love-it-or-hate-it colour palette

PC Gamer · industry report·19 November 2026·Source

With the pricing of memory right now, it's hard to get too excited for gaming handhelds, but Lenovo's reportedly more budget-friendly Legion Go Beam does intrigue me. And, with a recent leak, I'm now looking forward to hearing about the full details in the new year.

This leak comes courtesy of Windows Latest, which managed to get its hands on three images, plus the name, and a suggestion that it will be less expensive than the Legion Go 2. Admittedly, the Legion Go 2 and its OLED screen are climbing up to $2,000 right now, so undercutting that isn't super tough.

More from the report

  • The Go Beam has a white shell with lime green accents at the bottom, on the face buttons, around the thumbsticks, and on the start and menu buttons.
  • There is, unfortunately, a lot we don't know about this handheld. We don't know the screen's panel technology; we don't know what size and weight it is;
  • Windows Latest is guessing it will be cheaper than the Legion Go based on its feature set, which is not a bad call.
  • You get most of the controls you expect from a modern handheld, with triggers and bumpers, as well as two paddles on the back.
