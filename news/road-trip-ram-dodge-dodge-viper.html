Road Trip: RAM & Dodge - Dodge Viper

American Truck Simulator · posted by the game's developer on Steam·21 November 2026·Source

The Viper made its debut as a concept car at the 1989 North American International Auto Show in Detroit. After an enthusiastic public response and numerous requests for a production version, it followed soon after. The Viper eventually went on sale in 1992, quickly capturing the attention of automotive enthusiasts and the press as well.

Today, we would like to show you another detailed preview from the upcoming Road Trip: RAM & Dodge DLC for American Truck Simulator, as we will be showcasing the legendary Dodge Viper!

What the notes say

  • In 1996, four years after the debut of the production of the Dodge Viper, a coupe version, the Viper GTS, was introduced.
  • At the heart of both versions lies the Viper's mighty 8.0-litre V10 engine, delivering 450 horsepower in the GTS and 460 horsepower in the ACR, paired with a six-speed manual transmission.
  • This is a true beast under the bonnet, and one that calls for a confident driver who knows how to handle its power.
  • While both versions share the same unmistakable Viper character, they offer different ways to make the car your own.
  • The ACR boasts plenty of details that reflect its track-inspired nature.
  • You'll find lighter rims, racing seat belts, distinctive ACR badges, and a choice of Black or Cognac leather interiors.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
