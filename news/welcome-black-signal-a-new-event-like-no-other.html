Welcome Black Signal: A New Event Like No Other

World of Tanks · posted by the game's developer on Steam·10 November 2026·Source

Join the fight in Black Signal, a new 7v7 event for Tier X vehicles continuing the story of the Sector 3 campaign! Capture antennas, earn points, and keep the pressure on with unlimited respawns. Complete daily missions and advance through 12 Data Blocks to unlock valuable rewards, including a unique commander, a 2D style, five 3D attachments, Battle Pass Points, and more!

Capture and hold antennas to earn Signal Points for your team. Control more antennas to score faster and be the first to reach 2,500 points. If time runs out, the team with the highest score wins.

What the notes say

  • Capturing an antenna restores 25% of your vehicle's HP and heals allied vehicles within the capture zone.
  • Use this bonus to recover, reinforce your position, and prepare for the next push.
  • After a short delay, choose a respawn point, switch to another eligible vehicle, and adapt your approach to the changing battle.
  • Shells you use in Black Signal battles are returned after the battle, so you can focus on the fight without worrying about their cost.
  • Capture neutral positions, hold allied antennas, and take enemy-controlled ones to shift the score in your favor.
  • Secure by entering the circle around neutral antennas to start earning Signal Points, then defend allied positions to keep them flowing.

Quoted from the announcement itself, shortened where a line ran long.
