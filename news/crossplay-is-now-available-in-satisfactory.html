Crossplay is now available in Satisfactory!

Satisfactory · posted by the game's developer on Steam·4 November 2026·Source

Today we have a significant update for us as this marks yet another milestone for Satisfactory, It is exactly one year since the original Console release today and to celebrate we are now supporting Crossplay between PC and Consoles!

Now Steam, Epic, PlayStation 5® and Xbox Series S|X Pioneers can Construct, Automate, Explore & Exploit in multiplayer together even if they are not on the same platform!

What the notes say

  • If you would like to read more about Crossplay in specific and learn how to get started, You can continue reading the patch notes or go here:
  • If you play the latest update and encounter any issues that are hindering your experience, please let us know over at our QA Site
  • Fixed two instances where opening and closing a UI would result UObject limit crash, commonly seen on the Blueprint Designer and for the Vehicle Inventory UI
  • Fixed items dismantled incorrectly merging into the same crate even if it was further than 100 meters away, making it harder to find them after dismantling
  • R for Reload) can now be properly rebound and will also now update other inputs that are connected to match the same newly selected key if a conflict is found.
  • Fixed Truck Stations being incorrectly allowed to be Nudged while snapping to a Vehicle path which could lead to many other issues after placed
