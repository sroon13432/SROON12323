Where these prices come from

The discount, the normal price and the discounted price are the USD values Steam's own store search and app-details endpoints returned when this page was built, and each card carries the moment it was read. Steam does not publish an end time for a store-wide sale in those endpoints, so none is shown: a price can change or end at any moment, and the store page is the only authority on what you pay.

“Record low” is this site's own measurement, not Steam's: a price tracker reads every game in the database once a day and keeps the days a price moved. Tracking started on 27 Nov 2026, so on the first day of a title's tracking its only reading is also its lowest — the badge becomes a real comparison as daily readings accumulate, and every game page states how many readings stand behind its number.

What CanMySpec adds is the hardware side. Every deal is matched to the game's published minimum and recommended requirements, so a card can say “runs well”, “runs at minimum” or “upgrade needed” for the PC you saved. A title this site does not track yet is listed with its price and store link only.
