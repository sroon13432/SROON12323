Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · This policy covers canmyspec.com and every page under it.

CanMySpec is a reference site: you read pages and leave. There is no account, no newsletter, no comment system and no payment on this site, so most of what a modern website collects, we do not collect at all. What follows is the complete list of what is collected, by whom, and how to stop it.

1. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, postal address, password, or any payment detail. There is no registration and no login. Nothing on this site asks for a government identifier, and the site does not build a profile of you that it keeps after your visit.

2. What is collected, and why

a. Server logs (GitHub Pages)

The site is served as static files from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it processes the request needed to deliver a page, which includes your IP address, browser user-agent, the page requested and the time. GitHub uses that data to deliver and secure the service under its own privacy statement; CanMySpec does not receive or keep a copy of per-visitor logs.

b. Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-P1F06LY5ZX) to understand which pages are used and how the site is found. It records page views, the referring site, an approximate location derived from your IP address (city level, not your address), device type, browser and language, and interactions such as filter and language-switcher use. Google does not record your full IP address in Analytics. The data is aggregated in reports and is not used to identify you. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google privacy policy.

c. Your language choice

If you switch the site between English, Korean and Japanese, that choice is stored in your browser's local storage so the next page opens in the same language. It never leaves your device, and clearing site data removes it.

d. The "Detect My PC" feature

The hardware checker reads your graphics card and memory from your browser (through WebGL and browser APIs) and runs the comparison in your browser. The result is not transmitted to us. CanMySpec does not learn which GPU you have, and the detection does not install anything or set a cookie.

3. Cookies and advertising

CanMySpec displays advertising served by Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-3774179792647771) and, through Google, by third-party vendors.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

4. Affiliate links and other sites

Some pages link to shops or to our own tools, and a purchase made after following such a link can earn a commission. Those destinations are separate services with their own privacy policies; once you leave CanMySpec, this policy no longer applies. Links to Steam, the App Store, GOG, Epic and publisher sites are provided for reference and are not endorsements.

5. How long data is kept

CanMySpec keeps no visitor data of its own to delete. Aggregated analytics is retained in the Google Analytics property according to its retention setting (currently the standard 14-month window for user-level data), after which only aggregate reports remain. Advertising data retention is controlled by Google as described in its policy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to be informed about the processing described above, to access the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the right to know what is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — CanMySpec does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the Google advertising described above, which you can switch off through Google's settings.

To make a request, write to contact@canmyspec.com and say what you are asking for. Because CanMySpec holds no account records, most requests will be answered by explaining exactly which third party holds the relevant data and how to reach it.

7. Children

The site is written for a general audience interested in PC hardware and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has provided information to a third party through this site, contact us and we will help you address it.

8. Security

Pages are served over HTTPS from static hosting, which means there is no application database of visitors to breach. Emails sent to the contact address are read and kept only as long as needed to resolve the matter raised.

9. International transfers

Analytics and advertising data are processed by Google, which may process it outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If the site adds a feature that changes what is collected, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Substantial changes will be described on this page rather than made silently.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the site does with data: contact@canmyspec.com. Related documents: editorial methodology, terms of use, about CanMySpec.
