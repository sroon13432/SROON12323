How these guides are written

Every requirement figure quoted here comes from the publisher's own Steam store page and is reproduced unchanged. Where a guide gives GPU counts, they are generated from the same database that powers our hardware filter each time the site is rebuilt, so a number cannot drift from the data behind it.

Guides state what a piece of hardware will do at a specific resolution and preset, because those two variables change the answer more than any part number. Where a conclusion depends on the game, the resolution or the display, the guide says so instead of averaging it away.

Requirements are a publisher's target rather than a measurement: our minimum vs recommended guide explains what each column is actually written against.
