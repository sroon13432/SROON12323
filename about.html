Our Mission

CanMySpec.com was built with one goal: to provide PC gamers with lightning-fast, zero-bloat access to accurate hardware requirements and real-world 60 FPS graphics optimization guides. Whether you are running an entry-level gaming laptop or an enthusiast RTX 40-series rig, we help you determine instantly if your PC can run the latest Steam releases.

Data Sources and Methodologies

Our database compiles official developer specifications, Steam community review scores, and engine architectures. Every game profile features side-by-side Minimum vs. Recommended specifications, GPU tier recommendations, and actionable graphics setting tweaks to maximize frame stability.
