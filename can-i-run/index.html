How the verdict is calculated

“Runs well” means your card reaches the game's recommended GPU tier and your memory covers its recommended RAM. “Runs at minimum” means it clears the minimum tier but not the recommended one. Anything else is listed as needing an upgrade — and the game page then names the card and the memory figure that would clear it.

Every number here is published: the requirements are the ones the store page lists, and the tiers are the same GPU index the home-page filter and the per-game checker use. A title whose store page leaves a requirement blank is never counted as passing on that line.

The Steam deals page puts the same verdict on today's discounts, and the news desk carries every deal as an article.
