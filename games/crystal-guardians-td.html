Hardware & Performance Analysis

Crystal Guardians TD asks for "GTX 670" at minimum and "GTX 960" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 56% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 604 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
