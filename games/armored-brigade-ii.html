Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Armored Brigade II?

Armored Brigade II lists GeForce GTX 750 (2GB VRAM, OpenGL 4 compatible, 1024x768 resolution) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 750 (2GB VRAM, OpenGL 4 compatible, 1024x768 resolution) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Armored Brigade II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 750 (2GB VRAM, OpenGL 4 compatible, 1024x768 resolution). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Armored Brigade II need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Armored Brigade II need?

2 GB available space.
