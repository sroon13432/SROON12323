Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Monster Hunter Stories 2: Wings of Ruin?

Monster Hunter Stories 2: Wings of Ruin lists NVIDIA®GeForce® GTX 960 / 2GB VRAM or AMD Radeon RX 560 / 2GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA®GeForce® GTX 960 / 2GB VRAM or AMD Radeon RX 560 / 2GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Monster Hunter Stories 2: Wings of Ruin at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA®GeForce® GTX 960 / 2GB VRAM or AMD Radeon RX 560 / 2GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Monster Hunter Stories 2: Wings of Ruin need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Monster Hunter Stories 2: Wings of Ruin need?

28 GB available space.
