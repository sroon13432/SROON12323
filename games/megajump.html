Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for MEGAJUMP runs from "GTX1060" in the minimum column to "GTX1080 ti" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, 100% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).
