Hardware & Performance Analysis

Alisa's Grimoire asks for "DirectX 11/OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU" at minimum and "OpenGL ES 2.0 hardware driver support required for WebGL acceleration. (AMD Catalyst 10.9…)" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
