Hardware & Performance Analysis

Arknights asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 194% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at Publisher minimum requirements.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Unity carries more of its cost on the CPU, which is why the processor row is worth reading beside the card.
