Hardware & Performance Analysis

BattleCore Arena asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 (4GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050TI (4GB)" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.

What GPU do I need for BattleCore Arena at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050TI (4GB) or AMD RX 5500XT (4GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
