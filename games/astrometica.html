Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Astrometica?

Astrometica lists Nvidia GTX 1660 Ti and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX 2070 Ti with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Astrometica at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX 2070 Ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Astrometica need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
