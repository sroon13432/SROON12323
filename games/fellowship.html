Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Fellowship moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 2060". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, 100% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
