Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Explorer of Yggdrasil?

Explorer of Yggdrasil lists DirectX 9/OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and OpenGL ES 2.0 hardware driver support required for WebGL acceleration. (AMD Catalyst 10.9, nVidia 358.50) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Explorer of Yggdrasil at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is OpenGL ES 2.0 hardware driver support required for WebGL acceleration. (AMD Catalyst 10.9, nVidia 358.50). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
