Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Solasta: Crown of the Magister?

Solasta: Crown of the Magister lists Radeon R9 285 / GeForce GTX 950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Radeon RX 590 / GeForce GTX 1060 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Solasta: Crown of the Magister at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Radeon RX 590 / GeForce GTX 1060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Solasta: Crown of the Magister need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
