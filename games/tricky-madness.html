Can my PC run Tricky Madness?

Tricky Madness lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 Ti and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950 Ti with 3 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tricky Madness at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950 Ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 3 GB at recommended settings.
