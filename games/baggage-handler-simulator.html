Hardware & Performance Analysis

Baggage Handler Simulator asks for "NVIDIA GTX 960" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 1650" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 92% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
