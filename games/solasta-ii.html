Hardware & Performance Analysis

Solasta II asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce 2070 10 GB" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Solasta II lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB / AMD Radeon 5700 8 GB and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce 2070 10 GB / AMD Radeon 5700 16 GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Solasta II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce 2070 10 GB / AMD Radeon 5700 16 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
