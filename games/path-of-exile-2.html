Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Path of Exile 2 moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960 (3GB), Intel® Arc™ A380," to "NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2060, Intel® Arc™ A770,". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, roughly 150% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 100 GB install wants its own room on a fast drive, with space left over for patches. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Path of Exile 2?

Path of Exile 2 lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960 (3GB), Intel® Arc™ A380, or ATI Radeon™ RX 470 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2060, Intel® Arc™ A770, or ATI Radeon™ RX 5600XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Path of Exile 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2060, Intel® Arc™ A770, or ATI Radeon™ RX 5600XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
