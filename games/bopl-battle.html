Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bopl Battle?

Bopl Battle lists Intel UHD Graphics 630 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 1650 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 1650. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Bopl Battle need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
