Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run V-Rally 4?

V-Rally 4 lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 560 or AMD Radeon HD 6870 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 780 or AMD R9 290 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for V-Rally 4 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 780 or AMD R9 290. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does V-Rally 4 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does V-Rally 4 need?

19 GB available space.
