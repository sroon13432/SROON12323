Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Cylne moves from "NVIDIA 8800 (SM3-compatible video card)" to "NVIDIA GTX 560". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cylne lists NVIDIA 8800 (SM3-compatible video card) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 560 or higher with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cylne at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 560 or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
