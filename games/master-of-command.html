Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Master of Command?

Master of Command lists GeForce GTX 1050 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 2060 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Master of Command at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 2060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Master of Command need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

9 GB available space.
