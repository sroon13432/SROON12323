Hardware & Performance Analysis

skate. asks for "GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (4GB)" at minimum and "GeForce RTX 3060 (12GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 4.4 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
