Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers DAVE THE DIVER moves from "NVIDIA Geforce GTS 450" to "NVIDIA GTX 1060 3GB". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).
