Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Pro Cycling Manager 2020?

Pro Cycling Manager 2020 lists 512 Mo, ATI Radeon HD 5570/NVIDIA GeForce GT 240/Intel HD 4600 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1 GB, AMD Radeon HD 7850/NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Pro Cycling Manager 2020 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1 GB, AMD Radeon HD 7850/NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Pro Cycling Manager 2020 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
