Hardware & Performance Analysis

Highly Likely lists "nVidia GeForce 8800 GT" at minimum and "NVidia GeForce 9600GT" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Highly Likely?

Highly Likely lists nVidia GeForce 8800 GT or AMD Radeon HD2900 XT (512MB VRAM) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GeForce 9600GT or higher, AMD Radeon HD3850 or higher (512MB VRAM with Shader Model 4.0) with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Highly Likely at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GeForce 9600GT or higher, AMD Radeon HD3850 or higher (512MB VRAM with Shader Model 4.0). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Highly Likely need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Highly Likely need?

3 GB available space.
