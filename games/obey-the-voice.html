Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Obey the Voice™ moves from "Nvidia GTX 1650 4GB" to "Nvidia RTX 2060 6GB". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 86% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Obey the Voice™?

Obey the Voice™ lists Nvidia GTX 1650 4GB or AMD Radeon RX 570 4GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX 2060 6GB or AMD Radeon RX 5700 8GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Obey the Voice™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX 2060 6GB or AMD Radeon RX 5700 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Obey the Voice™ need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Obey the Voice™ need?

5 GB available space.
