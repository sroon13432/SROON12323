Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for MarZ: Tactical Base Defense are built around one card, "DirectX 10 compliant graphics card with 2GB dedicated memory", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
