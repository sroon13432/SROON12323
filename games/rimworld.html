Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for RimWorld list "Intel HD Graphics 4000": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
