Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Cyberline Racing runs from "GTX 650Ti, Radeon HD 5870 (May work on lower specifications)" in the minimum column to "GTX 750, Radeon HD 6950 (May work on lower specifications)" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
