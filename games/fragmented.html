Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Fragmented runs from "DX10 GPU with 1GB VRAM: NVidia GTX 460/ AMD Radeon 5850" in the minimum column to "DX11 GPU with 3GB+ VRAM: NVidia GTX 970/ AMD Radeon 390" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 7.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
