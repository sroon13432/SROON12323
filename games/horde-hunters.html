Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Horde Hunters are built around one card, "GeForce GTX 750", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 92 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
