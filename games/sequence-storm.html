Hardware & Performance Analysis

SEQUENCE STORM asks for "Intel HD graphics 520" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 780" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, a jump of 5.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
