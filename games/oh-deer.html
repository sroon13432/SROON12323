Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Oh Deer moves from "NVIDIA GTX 1060" to "NVIDIA RTX 2080". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, a jump of about 167%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Oh Deer?

Oh Deer lists NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD RX 580 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 2080 or AMD 6700-XT with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Oh Deer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 2080 or AMD 6700-XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Oh Deer need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Oh Deer need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
