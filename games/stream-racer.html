Hardware & Performance Analysis

Stream Racer asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 108%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stream Racer?

Stream Racer lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 560, with 4 GB graphics memory and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT, or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stream Racer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT, or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Stream Racer need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
