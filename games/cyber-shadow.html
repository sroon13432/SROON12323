Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cyber Shadow names the same GPU, "ATI Radeon X1300", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 246 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cyber Shadow?

Cyber Shadow lists ATI Radeon X1300 / Nvidia GeForce 6600 GT / Intel HD 3000 or better and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and ATI Radeon X1300 / Nvidia GeForce 6600 GT / Intel HD 3000 or better with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cyber Shadow at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is ATI Radeon X1300 / Nvidia GeForce 6600 GT / Intel HD 3000 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cyber Shadow need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
