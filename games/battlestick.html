Hardware & Performance Analysis

BattleStick asks for "On-Board with WebGL support, else the game will use Canvas mode with medium-quality preset" at minimum and "512MB VRAM with WebGL support" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 246 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run BattleStick?

BattleStick lists On-Board with WebGL support, else the game will use Canvas mode with medium-quality preset and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and 512MB VRAM with WebGL support with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for BattleStick at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 512MB VRAM with WebGL support. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does BattleStick need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does BattleStick need?

100 MB available space.
