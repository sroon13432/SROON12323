Hardware & Performance Analysis

We're Tethered Together asks for "Nvidia GTX 1060" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1660" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 696 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
