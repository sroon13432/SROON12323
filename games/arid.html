Hardware & Performance Analysis

Arid asks for "Nvidia GTX1050 Ti" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, something like 50% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Arid?

Arid lists Nvidia GTX1050 Ti / AMD Radeon 570, 4GB VRAM and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX1060 / AMD RX 590 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Arid at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX1060 / AMD RX 590. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Arid need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
