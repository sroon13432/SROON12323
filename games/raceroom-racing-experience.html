Hardware & Performance Analysis

RaceRoom Racing Experience asks for "VRAM 512MB" at minimum and "VRAM 8GB" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 90 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2013, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
