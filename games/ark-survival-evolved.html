Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for ARK: Survival Evolved list "NVIDIA GTX 670 2GB/AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
