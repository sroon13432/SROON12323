Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Silent dream list "Integrated graphics": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
