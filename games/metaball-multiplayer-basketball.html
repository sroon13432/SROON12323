Hardware & Performance Analysis

MetaBall - Multiplayer Basketball asks for "GeForce® GTX 560" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, 4.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
