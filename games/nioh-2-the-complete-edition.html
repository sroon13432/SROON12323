Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Nioh 2 – The Complete Edition?

Nioh 2 – The Complete Edition lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or over, VRAM 4GB or over and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 SUPER or over, VRAM 6GB or over with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Nioh 2 – The Complete Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 SUPER or over, VRAM 6GB or over. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Nioh 2 – The Complete Edition need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

85 GB available space.
