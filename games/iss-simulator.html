Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for ISS Simulator runs from "GeForce GTX 1070" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 3090" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and High-end, a jump of about 114%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is high-end territory (RTX 3090 bracket), where the requirement says more about the game's ceiling than about the card you need to buy. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
