Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run South Park™: The Fractured But Whole™?

South Park™: The Fractured But Whole™ lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560Ti / GTX 650 / GTX 750 / GTX 950 / GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon HD 7850 / R9 270 / R9 370 / RX… and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 / GTX 750ti / GTX 960 / GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon R9 280X / R9 380 / RX 470 with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for South Park™: The Fractured But Whole™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 / GTX 750ti / GTX 960 / GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon R9 280X / R9 380 / RX 470. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does South Park™: The Fractured But Whole™ need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
