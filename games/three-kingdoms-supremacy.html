Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Three Kingdoms: Supremacy?

Three Kingdoms: Supremacy lists Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 660 (2GB) | AMD® Radeon™ R7 370 (2GB) or Intel® Iris® Xe Graphics or AMD® Radeon™ Vega 8 and 8 MB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 (6GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB) | Intel® Arc™ A580 (8GB) with 16 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Three Kingdoms: Supremacy at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 (6GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB) | Intel® Arc™ A580 (8GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Three Kingdoms: Supremacy need?

8 MB of memory at minimum and 16 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Three Kingdoms: Supremacy need?

10 GB available space.
