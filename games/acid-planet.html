Hardware & Performance Analysis

Acid Planet asks for "Almost any graphics card" at minimum and "Slightly better graphics card" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 246 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

    Use Fullscreen mode instead of Borderless Windowed to cut input latency and gain a few FPS.
