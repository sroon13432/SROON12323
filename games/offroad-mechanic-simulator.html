Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Offroad Mechanic Simulator?

Offroad Mechanic Simulator lists GTX 1060 6GB and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and GTX 1660Ti with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Offroad Mechanic Simulator at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GTX 1660Ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Offroad Mechanic Simulator need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Offroad Mechanic Simulator need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
