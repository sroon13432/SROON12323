Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Chaos Express: Delivery Simulator moves from "nVidia GeForce GTX 1060" to "nVidia GeForce RTX 2060". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 72% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
