Hardware & Performance Analysis

Twilight Town: A Cyberpunk Day In Life names the same GPU, "Integrated Graphics Card", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
