Hardware & Performance Analysis

King's League II names the same GPU, "1GB VRAM", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run King's League II?

King's League II lists 1GB VRAM or OpenGL 2.1+ support and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1GB VRAM or OpenGL 2.1+ support with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for King's League II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1GB VRAM or OpenGL 2.1+ support. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
