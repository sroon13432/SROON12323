Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers The Pub Life Simulator moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Ti". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 56% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
