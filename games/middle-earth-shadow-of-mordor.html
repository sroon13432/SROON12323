Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Middle-earth™: Shadow of Mordor™?

Middle-earth™: Shadow of Mordor™ lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 | AMD Radeon HD 5850 and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 | AMD Radeon HD 7950 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Middle-earth™: Shadow of Mordor™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 | AMD Radeon HD 7950. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Middle-earth™: Shadow of Mordor™ need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

44 GB available space.
