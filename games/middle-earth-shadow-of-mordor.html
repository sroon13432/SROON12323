Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Middle-earth™: Shadow of Mordor™ runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is roughly 133% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 57 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
