Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Hello Neighbor runs from "GTX 770" in the minimum column to "GTX 1060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 127% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
