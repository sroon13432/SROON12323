Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Candellum run from "GeForce MX 150 (2048 MB); Radeon R7 260X (2048 MB)" to "GeForce GTX 1050 (2048 MB); Radeon RX 580 (8192 MB)". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Candellum?

Candellum lists GeForce MX 150 (2048 MB); Radeon R7 260X (2048 MB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1050 (2048 MB); Radeon RX 580 (8192 MB) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Candellum at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1050 (2048 MB); Radeon RX 580 (8192 MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Candellum need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Candellum need?

5 GB available space.
