Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era?

Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1650 (4 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ RX 5500 XT (4 GB) / Intel® Arc™ A580 (8 GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 (6 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 OEM (6 GB) / Intel® Arc™ A750 (8 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 (6 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 OEM (6 GB) / Intel® Arc™ A750 (8 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
