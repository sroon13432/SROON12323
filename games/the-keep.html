Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Keep asks for "3D graphics card supporting DirectX with 256 MB of memory" at minimum and "3D graphics card supporting DirectX with 512 GB of memory" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 481 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Keep?

The Keep lists 3D graphics card supporting DirectX with 256 MB of memory and 1024 MB RAM as its minimum, and 3D graphics card supporting DirectX with 512 GB of memory with 2048 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Keep at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 3D graphics card supporting DirectX with 512 GB of memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Keep need?

1024 MB of memory at minimum and 2048 MB at recommended settings.
