Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War II - Anniversary Edition (Classic)?

Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War II - Anniversary Edition (Classic) lists A 128MB Video Card (Shader Model 3) - Nvidia GeForce 6600 GT / ATI X1600, or equivalent and 1 GB (XP), 1.5 GB (Vista) RAM as its minimum, and A 256MB Video Card (Shader Model 3) - Nvidia GeForce 7800 GT / ATI X1900, or equivalent with 2 GB (XP and Vista) RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War II - Anniversary Edition (Classic) at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is A 256MB Video Card (Shader Model 3) - Nvidia GeForce 7800 GT / ATI X1900, or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War II - Anniversary Edition (Classic) need?

1 GB (XP), 1.5 GB (Vista) of memory at minimum and 2 GB (XP and Vista) at recommended settings.
