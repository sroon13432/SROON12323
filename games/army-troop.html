Hardware & Performance Analysis

Army Troop names the same GPU, "DirectX 11 capable GPU, NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 @ 2 GB", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Army Troop?

Army Troop lists DirectX 11 capable GPU, NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 @ 2 GB / GTX 1050 or ATI® Radeon™ HD 7850 @ 2GB / AMD RX 550 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 capable GPU, NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 @ 2 GB / GTX 1050 or ATI® Radeon™ HD 7850 @ 2GB / AMD RX 550 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Army Troop at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 capable GPU, NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 @ 2 GB / GTX 1050 or ATI® Radeon™ HD 7850 @ 2GB / AMD RX 550. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Army Troop need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
