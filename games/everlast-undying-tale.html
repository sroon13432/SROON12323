Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Everlast: Undying Tale?

Everlast: Undying Tale lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (req. 6GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon RX 580 (req. 6GB VRAM) / Intel ARC A380 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (req. 6GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon RX 580 (req. 6GB VRAM) / Intel ARC A380 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Everlast: Undying Tale at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (req. 6GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon RX 580 (req. 6GB VRAM) / Intel ARC A380. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Everlast: Undying Tale need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

20 GB available space.
