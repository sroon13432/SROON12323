Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Mugen Souls run from "DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics card with 512Mb RAM" to "DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics card with 1GB RAM". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Mugen Souls?

Mugen Souls lists DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics card with 512Mb RAM and support for v3 shaders and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics card with 1GB RAM and support for v3 shaders with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Mugen Souls at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics card with 1GB RAM and support for v3 shaders. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Mugen Souls need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Mugen Souls need?

4 GB available space.
