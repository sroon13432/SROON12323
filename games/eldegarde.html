Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Eldegarde runs from "GTX 1660 Super" in the minimum column to "RTX 3070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 82% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
