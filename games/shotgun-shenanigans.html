Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Shotgun Shenanigans list "Geforce GTX 500 series": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
