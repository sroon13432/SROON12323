Between its two tiers Aviators VR moves from "8 GB VRAM, RTX 3060" to "8 GB VRAM, RTX 3070". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, about 41% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
