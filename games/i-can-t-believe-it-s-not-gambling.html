Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for I Can't Believe It's Not Gambling runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GT 730" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 TI" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 3.9 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
