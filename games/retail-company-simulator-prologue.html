Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Retail Company Simulator: Prologue runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is about 56% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.27 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
