Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hyperdimension Neptunia U: Action Unleashed?

Hyperdimension Neptunia U: Action Unleashed lists DirectX 10.x or OpenGL 3.3 or better graphics card with 1 GB RAM and support for v4 shaders and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 10.x or OpenGL 3.3 or better graphics card with 2 GB RAM and support for v4 shaders with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hyperdimension Neptunia U: Action Unleashed at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 10.x or OpenGL 3.3 or better graphics card with 2 GB RAM and support for v4 shaders. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Hyperdimension Neptunia U: Action Unleashed need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
