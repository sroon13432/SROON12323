Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Super Blue Fighter are built around one card, "Intel HD 3000", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
