Hardware & Performance Analysis

LEGO® Batman™: Legacy of the Dark Knight asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, 4 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 SUPER, 8 GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of 3.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is mid-range by our index (RX 7600 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
