Hardware & Performance Analysis

Thronefall names the same GPU, "Nvidia GeForce GTX970", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
