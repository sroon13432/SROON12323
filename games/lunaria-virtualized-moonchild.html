Hardware & Performance Analysis

LUNARiA -Virtualized Moonchild- asks for "512MB Video Memory" at minimum and "1GB Video Memory" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
