Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run ISLANDERS?

ISLANDERS lists Nvidia GeForce GTX950 or higher and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX970 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for ISLANDERS at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX970. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does ISLANDERS need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
