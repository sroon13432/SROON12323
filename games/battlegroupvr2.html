Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run BattleGroupVR2?

BattleGroupVR2 lists NVIDIA GTX 970 or equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 4080 or equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for BattleGroupVR2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 4080 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does BattleGroupVR2 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does BattleGroupVR2 need?

8 GB available space.
