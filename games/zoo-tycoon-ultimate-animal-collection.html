Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Zoo Tycoon: Ultimate Animal Collection moves from "Nvidia GT GTX 650 || AMD R7 260" to "Nvidia GTX 660 Ti || AMD R7 370". Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a rise of roughly 78% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
