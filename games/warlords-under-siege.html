Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Warlords Under Siege?

Warlords Under Siege lists Nvidia GeForce 710M or higher and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX970 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warlords Under Siege at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX970. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warlords Under Siege need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Warlords Under Siege need?

4 GB available space.
