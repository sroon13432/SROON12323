Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Nidhogg list "DirectX 8-compatible graphics card with at least 32MB of video memory": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Nidhogg?

Nidhogg lists DirectX 8-compatible graphics card with at least 32MB of video memory and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 8-compatible graphics card with at least 32MB of video memory with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Nidhogg at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 8-compatible graphics card with at least 32MB of video memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Nidhogg need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 512 MB at recommended settings.
