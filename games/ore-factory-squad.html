Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ore Factory Squad ⛏️ asks for "NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA RTX 2070" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 104%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
