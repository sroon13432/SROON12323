Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run ROB RUN?

ROB RUN lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 580 or Intel Arc A380 and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 or AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT or Intel Arc B570 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for ROB RUN at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 or AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT or Intel Arc B570. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does ROB RUN need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
