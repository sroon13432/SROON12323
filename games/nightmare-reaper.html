Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Nightmare Reaper moves from "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 670" to "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 4.1 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.
