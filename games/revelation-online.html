Can my PC run Revelation Online?

Revelation Online lists NVIDIA GeForce GT610/Intel HD 4000 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 TI, 2 GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Revelation Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 TI, 2 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
