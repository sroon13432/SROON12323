Hardware & Performance Analysis

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 105% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
