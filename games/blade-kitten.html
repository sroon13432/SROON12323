Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Blade Kitten?

Blade Kitten lists ATI Radeon X1800 or NVIDIA GeForce 7800 or better and 1GB RAM as its minimum, and ATI Radeon HD 3850 or NVIDIA GeForce 8800 or better with 2GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Blade Kitten at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is ATI Radeon HD 3850 or NVIDIA GeForce 8800 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Blade Kitten need?

1GB of memory at minimum and 2GB at recommended settings.
