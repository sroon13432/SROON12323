Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Guild Wars 2®?

Guild Wars 2® lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 680 / AMD Radeon HD 7970 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 680 / AMD Radeon HD 7970 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Guild Wars 2® at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 680 / AMD Radeon HD 7970. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Guild Wars 2® need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Guild Wars 2® need?

70 GB available space.
