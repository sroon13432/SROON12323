Hardware & Performance Analysis

Rebel Inc: Escalation asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 630 (2 GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 (2 GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 3.9 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.
