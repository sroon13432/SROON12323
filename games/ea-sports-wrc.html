Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for EA SPORTS™ WRC runs from "Nvidia GTX 1060 Radeon RX Vega 56" in the minimum column to "Nvidia RTX 2070 Radeon RX 5700 XT" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, a jump of about 104%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 110 GB install wants its own room on a fast drive, with space left over for patches. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
