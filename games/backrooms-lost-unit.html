Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Backrooms Lost Unit moves from "AMD RADEON RX 5300/ NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650" to "NVIDIA RTX 3070". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Upper mid-range, a jump of 10.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
