Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Storage Hustle runs from "NVidia GeForce GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "NVidia GeForce GTX 2060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 86% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
