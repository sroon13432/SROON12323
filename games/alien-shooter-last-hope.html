Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Alien Shooter - Last Hope list "512 MB video card": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
