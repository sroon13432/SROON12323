Slice, Dice & Rice

★ Mixed (64% of 600 reviews) 💾 Size: see official requirements

Slice Dice &amp; Rice is an indie fighting game with no health bars. Inspired by Bushido Blade, it revolves around the idea of finding an opening in opponents defense and ending the round with a single, clean hit.

🚀 Recommended GPU: Vidia Geforce GTX660 with 1 GB RAM (DirectX 11)
Target: 60 FPS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Slice, Dice & Rice?

Slice, Dice & Rice lists GeForce 9800 GT with 512 MB RAM (DirectX 10) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Vidia Geforce GTX660 with 1 GB RAM (DirectX 11) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Slice, Dice & Rice at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Vidia Geforce GTX660 with 1 GB RAM (DirectX 11). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Slice, Dice & Rice need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Slice, Dice & Rice need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
