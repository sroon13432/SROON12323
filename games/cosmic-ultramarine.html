Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cosmic Ultramarine?

Cosmic Ultramarine lists Integrated Graphics and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Integrated Graphics with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cosmic Ultramarine at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Integrated Graphics. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cosmic Ultramarine need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
