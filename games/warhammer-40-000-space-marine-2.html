Hardware & Performance Analysis

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 asks for "6 GB VRAM, AMD Radeon RX 580" at minimum and "8 GB VRAM, AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, roughly 176% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 75 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
