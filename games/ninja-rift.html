Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Ninja Rift run from "DirectX 10 compatible GPU with 1GB VRAM (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650)" to "DirectX 11 compatible GPU with 2GB VRAM (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050)". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ninja Rift?

Ninja Rift lists DirectX 10 compatible GPU with 1GB VRAM (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7750 or integrated… and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 compatible GPU with 2GB VRAM (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX 560) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ninja Rift at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 compatible GPU with 2GB VRAM (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX 560). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
