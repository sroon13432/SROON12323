Hardware & Performance Analysis

PlanetX asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.4 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run PlanetX?

PlanetX lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX-460 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for PlanetX at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does PlanetX need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does PlanetX need?

7 GB available space.
