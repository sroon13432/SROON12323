Hardware & Performance Analysis

MisBits asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 660" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 780/GTX 960" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a rise of roughly 86% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
