Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run MisBits?

MisBits lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 660 / Radeon R9 270 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 780/GTX 960 or Radeon R9 290/RX 570 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for MisBits at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 780/GTX 960 or Radeon R9 290/RX 570. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does MisBits need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
