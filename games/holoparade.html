Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for HoloParade are built around one card, "GPU 4GB VRAM", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

HoloParade lists GPU 4GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GPU 4GB VRAM with 4 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is GPU 4GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 MB at recommended settings.
