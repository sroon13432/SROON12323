Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Strategos moves from "GeForce GTX 1060" to "GeForce RTX 2060". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 72% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 22 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Strategos?

Strategos lists GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 2060 or AMD equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Strategos at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 2060 or AMD equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Strategos need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
