Hardware & Performance Analysis

Fire Commander asks for "GTX 780" at minimum and "GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 92%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 12 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
