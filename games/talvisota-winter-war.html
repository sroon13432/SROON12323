Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Talvisota - Winter War moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
