Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Car Detailing Simulator: Prologue moves from "NVidia GeForce GTX 780" to "GTX 1060 6GB". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 92% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
