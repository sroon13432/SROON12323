Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for TheDawn runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 660 2GB" in the minimum column to "Video NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 970 4GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 186% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
