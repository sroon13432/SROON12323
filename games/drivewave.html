Hardware & Performance Analysis

DriveWave asks for "NVidia GeForce GTX 670" at minimum and "NVidia GeForce GTX 1060 4GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 178%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
