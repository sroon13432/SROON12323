Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Fear The Wolves moves from "1 GB, GeForce GTX 650 Ti/Radeon R7 260" to "4 GB, GeForce GTX 970/Radeon R9 390". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.6 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
