Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Damned 2 moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 4GB" to "NVIDIA GTX 1070ti". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1070ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
