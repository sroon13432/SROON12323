Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Buttons Up! are built around one card, "1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable)", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

What GPU do I need for Buttons Up! at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
