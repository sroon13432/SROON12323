Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run House Party?

House Party lists Direct X 11 GPU w/ 2GB Available Video Memory and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB, AMD RX570 or above with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for House Party at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB, AMD RX570 or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does House Party need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does House Party need?

8 GB available space.
