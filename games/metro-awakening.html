Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Metro Awakening are built around one card, "RTX-2070 8GB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Metro Awakening?

Metro Awakening lists RTX-2070 8GB and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX-2070 8GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Metro Awakening at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX-2070 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Metro Awakening need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Metro Awakening need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
