Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Gloomhaven runs from "GeForce GTX 760, Radeon R9 280," in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1070, Radeon RX Vega 64," in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 14 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RX Vega 64 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
