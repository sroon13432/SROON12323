Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Gloomhaven?

Gloomhaven lists GeForce GTX 760, Radeon R9 280, or above and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1070, Radeon RX Vega 64, or above with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gloomhaven at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1070, Radeon RX Vega 64, or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Gloomhaven need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Gloomhaven need?

14 GB available space.
