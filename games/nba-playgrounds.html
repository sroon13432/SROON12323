Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run NBA Playgrounds?

NBA Playgrounds lists GeForce GT 630 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 670, similar or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for NBA Playgrounds at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 670, similar or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does NBA Playgrounds need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

7 GB available space.
