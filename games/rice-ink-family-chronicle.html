Rice & Ink: Family Chronicle

★ Very Positive (81% of 55 reviews) 💾 2 GB available space

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Rice & Ink: Family Chronicle moves from "DirectX 11 GPU — Intel Iris Xe" to "AMD Radeon 780M integrated". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, roughly 189% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
