Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Phoenix Point runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 950" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 119% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
