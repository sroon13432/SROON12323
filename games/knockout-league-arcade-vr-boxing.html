Hardware & Performance Analysis

Knockout League - Arcade VR Boxing lists "Nvidia GeForce GTX970," at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX980," as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
