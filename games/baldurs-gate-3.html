Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Act 3 (Baldur's Gate Lower City) is heavily CPU-limited; modern high-core CPUs prevent FPS drops in the city square.
  • 💡
    DirectX 11 runs noticeably smoother than Vulkan on NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPUs.
  • 💡
    Turn 'Slow HDD Mode' OFF if installed on an NVMe SSD to unlock faster companion texture streaming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Baldur's Gate 3?

Baldur's Gate 3 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 / AMD Radeon RX 480 (4GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 SUPER / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Baldur's Gate 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 SUPER / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p / 1440p @ 60 FPS (Ultra Settings).

How much RAM does Baldur's Gate 3 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Baldur's Gate 3 need?

150 GB available space (SSD required).
