Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dungeon Warfare 2 asks for "Graphic Card with at least 256MB of VRAM" at minimum and "Graphic Card with at least 512MB of VRAM" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dungeon Warfare 2?

Dungeon Warfare 2 lists Graphic Card with at least 256MB of VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Graphic Card with at least 512MB of VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dungeon Warfare 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Graphic Card with at least 512MB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dungeon Warfare 2 need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
