Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for PBA Pro Bowling 2023 runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 660" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
