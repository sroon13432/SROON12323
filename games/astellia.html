Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Astellia?

Astellia lists NVIDIA : GTS450, GTX650 / AMD : HD 6770 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA : GTX770, GTX960 / AMD : R9 280X with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Astellia at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA : GTX770, GTX960 / AMD : R9 280X. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Astellia need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Astellia need?

60 GB available space.
