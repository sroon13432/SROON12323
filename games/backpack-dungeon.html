Can my PC run Backpack Dungeon?

Backpack Dungeon lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti or equivalent and RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti or equivalent with RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
