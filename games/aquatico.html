Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Aquatico moves from "GeForce GTX 1050 3 GB" to "GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a rise of roughly 85% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 10 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Aquatico?

Aquatico lists GeForce GTX 1050 3 GB or AMD Radeon™ RX 560 4 GB and 10 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB or AMD Radeon™ RX 590 6 GB with 10 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Aquatico at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB or AMD Radeon™ RX 590 6 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Aquatico need?

10 GB of memory at minimum and 10 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Aquatico need?

10 GB available space.
