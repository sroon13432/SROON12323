Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Space Prison runs from "GeForce GTX 650" in the minimum column to "Radeon RX 560/ Nvidia GeForce GTX 950" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a jump of about 133%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
