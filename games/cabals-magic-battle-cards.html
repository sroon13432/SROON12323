Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cabals: Magic & Battle Cards names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB)", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
