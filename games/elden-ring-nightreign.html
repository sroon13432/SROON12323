Hardware & Performance Analysis

ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 8GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 64% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
