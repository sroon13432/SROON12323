Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Stardew Valley run from "256 MB video memory, shader model 3.0+" to "Integrated Intel UHD Graphics". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stardew Valley?

Stardew Valley lists 256 MB video memory, shader model 3.0+ and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Integrated Intel UHD Graphics or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stardew Valley at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Integrated Intel UHD Graphics or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 4K @ 144 FPS.

How much RAM does Stardew Valley need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Stardew Valley need?

500 MB available space.
