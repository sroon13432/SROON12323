Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for The First Templar - Steam Special Edition are built around one card, "Direct 9.0c compatible graphics card with 256 MB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2011, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
