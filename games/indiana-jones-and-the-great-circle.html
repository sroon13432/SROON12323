Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Indiana Jones and the Great Circle moves from "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 SUPER 8 GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080Ti 12 GB". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 94%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 120 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 4070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
