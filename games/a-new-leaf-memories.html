Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers A New Leaf: Memories moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX960+" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX1060+". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, about 79% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 6 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
