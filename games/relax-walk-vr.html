Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Relax Walk VR runs from "GeForce GTX 1070" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1080 Ti" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 35% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1.46 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (GTX 1080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
