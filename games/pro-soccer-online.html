Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Pro Soccer Online run from "Any DirectX 11" to "Nvidia RTX 3080". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
