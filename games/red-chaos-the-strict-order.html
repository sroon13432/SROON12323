Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Red Chaos - The Strict Order?

Red Chaos - The Strict Order lists GeForce GTX 660, Radeon R7 370 or equivalent with 2 GB or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce 970 GPU or AMD Radeon RX 570 GPU with 4GB of VRAM with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Red Chaos - The Strict Order at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce 970 GPU or AMD Radeon RX 570 GPU with 4GB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Red Chaos - The Strict Order need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

20 GB available space.
