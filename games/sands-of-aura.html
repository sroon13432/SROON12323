Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sands of Aura?

Sands of Aura lists Nvidia GTX 960 / AMD R9 285 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB / AMD RX 580 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sands of Aura at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB / AMD RX 580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sands of Aura need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
