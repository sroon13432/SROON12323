Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Links moves from "Intel(R) HD Graphics 4000" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, 19.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
