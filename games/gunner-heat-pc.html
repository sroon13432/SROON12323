Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gunner, HEAT, PC! asks for "Dedicated GPU" at minimum and "Dedicated desktop GPU, GTX 1070" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
