Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Nioh: Complete Edition runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 780 VRAM 3GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 VRAM 6GB" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 80 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 780 Ti bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
