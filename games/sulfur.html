Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers SULFUR moves from "GTX 970" to "GTX 1080". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, roughly 100% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2026 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
