Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Dream Of Mirror Online run from "GeForce2 MX/Radeon 7500" to "GeForce FX/AMD Radeon 9600". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dream Of Mirror Online?

Dream Of Mirror Online lists GeForce2 MX/Radeon 7500 or equivalent 64mb VGA Card and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce FX/AMD Radeon 9600 or higher with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dream Of Mirror Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce FX/AMD Radeon 9600 or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dream Of Mirror Online need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dream Of Mirror Online need?

2 GB available space.
