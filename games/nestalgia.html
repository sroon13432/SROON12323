Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run NEStalgia?

NEStalgia lists Integrated graphics and 500 MB RAM as its minimum, and Integrated graphics with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for NEStalgia at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Integrated graphics. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does NEStalgia need?

500 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does NEStalgia need?

40 MB available space.
