Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Expanse: A Telltale Series runs from "Radeon HD 7790, 2 GB" in the minimum column to "Radeon RX 590, 8 GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 182%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
