Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Little Nightmares III?

Little Nightmares III lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce RTX 2080, 8 GB or AMD RX 6800 with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Little Nightmares III at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce RTX 2080, 8 GB or AMD RX 6800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Little Nightmares III need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Little Nightmares III need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
