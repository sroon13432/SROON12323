Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Hamidashi Creative run from "NVIDIA GeForce 8000 及以上" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
