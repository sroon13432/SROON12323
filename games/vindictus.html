Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Vindictus?

Vindictus lists GeForce GTS 250 / Radeon HD 4850 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 950 / Radeon R9 270 with 16GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Vindictus at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 950 / Radeon R9 270. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Vindictus need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 16GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Vindictus need?

At Least 15 GB of Free Space.
