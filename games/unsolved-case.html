Hardware & Performance Analysis

Unsolved Case asks for "Intel Hd graphics 520" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GT 740, AMD Radeon R7 250" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of about 120%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
