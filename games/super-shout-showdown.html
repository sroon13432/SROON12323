Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Super Shout Showdown?

Super Shout Showdown lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT or equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 2060 / RX 6600 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Super Shout Showdown at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 2060 / RX 6600 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Super Shout Showdown need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Super Shout Showdown need?

10 GB available space.
