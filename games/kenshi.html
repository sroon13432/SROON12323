Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Kenshi?

Kenshi lists Pixel shader 5.0 capable card and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and Pixel shader 5.0 capable card with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Kenshi at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Pixel shader 5.0 capable card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

14GB HD space.
