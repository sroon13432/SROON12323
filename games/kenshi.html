Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Kenshi list "Pixel shader 5.0 capable card": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 14 GB install also names an SSD, and that part is not optional: texture streaming on a spinning drive is where this title falls apart. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
