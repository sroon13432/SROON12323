Hardware & Performance Analysis

Only the minimum is published for Fall Guys: "NVIDIA GTX 660" for Publisher minimum requirements. With no recommended card to compare against, the listed GPU is a floor and a 60 FPS target cannot be read from the spec sheet.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Unity carries more of its cost on the CPU, which is why the processor row is worth reading beside the card. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fall Guys?

Fall Guys lists NVIDIA GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum. The publisher does not list a recommended GPU, so 60 FPS cannot be guaranteed from the requirements alone.

What GPU do I need for Fall Guys at 60 FPS?

The store page does not state a recommended GPU. The published minimum is NVIDIA GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7950, which targets Publisher minimum requirements.

How much RAM does Fall Guys need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Fall Guys need?

4 GB.
