Hardware & Performance Analysis

Platypus II names the same GPU, "32MB 3D Video Card", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Platypus II?

Platypus II lists 32MB 3D Video Card and 128 MB RAM as its minimum, and 32MB 3D Video Card with 128 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Platypus II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 32MB 3D Video Card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Platypus II need?

128 MB of memory at minimum and 128 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Platypus II need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
