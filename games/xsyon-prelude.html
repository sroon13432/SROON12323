Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Xsyon - Prelude moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT, ATI Radeon HD 4850" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470, ATI Radeon HD 5870". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 6.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Xsyon - Prelude?

Xsyon - Prelude lists NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT, ATI Radeon HD 4850 or Intel HD Graphics 3000 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470, ATI Radeon HD 5870 or better with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Xsyon - Prelude at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470, ATI Radeon HD 5870 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.

4 GB available space.
