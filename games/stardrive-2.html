Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run StarDrive 2?

StarDrive 2 lists 1 GB nVidia Geforce GT460 or equivalent, 500 MB ATI HD4850 or equivalent and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1 GB nVidia Geforce GTX660 or equivalent, 1 GB ATI HD7850 or equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for StarDrive 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1 GB nVidia Geforce GTX660 or equivalent, 1 GB ATI HD7850 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does StarDrive 2 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does StarDrive 2 need?

3 GB available space.
