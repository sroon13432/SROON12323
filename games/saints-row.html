Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Saints Row?

Saints Row lists GeForce GTX 970 / AMD Radeon RX 480 and 8192 MB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 3080TI / AMD Radeon RX 6800XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Saints Row at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 3080TI / AMD Radeon RX 6800XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Saints Row need?

8192 MB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Saints Row need?

50 GB available space.
