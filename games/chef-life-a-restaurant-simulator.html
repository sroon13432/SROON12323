Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Chef Life: A Restaurant Simulator?

What GPU do I need for Chef Life: A Restaurant Simulator at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760, 2 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7870, 2 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
