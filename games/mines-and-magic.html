Hardware & Performance Analysis

Mines and Magic lists "Integrated, HD Graphics 2000" at minimum and "Nvidia GFX 650" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Mines and Magic?

Mines and Magic lists Integrated, HD Graphics 2000 and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GFX 650 or higher, AMD RX 200 or higher with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Mines and Magic at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GFX 650 or higher, AMD RX 200 or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Mines and Magic need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
