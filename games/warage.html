Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Warage are built around one card, "Radeon x1700", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 123 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
