Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run SRX: The Game?

SRX: The Game lists Nvidia GTX 750 Ti or AMD Radeon R7 260X (Requires at least 2GB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660Ti or AMD Radeon RX 590 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for SRX: The Game at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660Ti or AMD Radeon RX 590. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does SRX: The Game need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does SRX: The Game need?

28 GB available space.
