Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Cyber Knights: Flashpoint runs from "GeForce GTX 660, 2 GB" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1070, 8 GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.9 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
