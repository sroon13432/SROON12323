Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Biophobia?

Biophobia lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti / AMD Radeon HD 7850 / Integrated graphics (with sufficient VRAM) and 4 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti / AMD Radeon HD 7850 / Integrated graphics (with sufficient VRAM) with 4 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Biophobia at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti / AMD Radeon HD 7850 / Integrated graphics (with sufficient VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Biophobia need?

4 MB of memory at minimum and 4 MB at recommended settings.

1 GB available space.
