Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Heart of the Machine moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 550 Ti (1 GB)" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760 (2 GB)". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, a jump of 3.7 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Heart of the Machine?

Heart of the Machine lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 550 Ti (1 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ HD 7770 (1 GB) and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760 (2 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ R9 380 (2 GB) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Heart of the Machine at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760 (2 GB) / AMD® Radeon™ R9 380 (2 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Heart of the Machine need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

5 GB available space.
