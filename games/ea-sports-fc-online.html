EA SPORTS FC Online

💾 40 GB available space ⚙️ Frostbite

EA SPORTS FC Online (FC 온라인, formerly FIFA Online 4) is Nexon's free-to-play football game for Korea, installed from the Nexon download page and not sold on Steam. Nexon's spec table asks for a Core i3-2100 or Athlon II X4 620 with 4 GB of RAM and a GeForce GT 730 at minimum, and a Core i5-2550K or FX-6350 with 8 GB and a GTX 460 when recommended, on 40 GB of free disk space and DirectX 11. Nexon also runs an automatic PC check before download.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 / AMD Radeon HD 6870
Target: 60 FPS

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Nexon's own PC check (FCOSystemInfoCheck.exe) on the download page is the fastest way to see which row your machine clears before installing 40 GB.
  • 💡
    The storage line is free space, not install size: the client, patches and replay files all grow inside that 40 GB.
  • 💡
    Frame rate in matches is decided by the GPU and by the DirectX 11 path, so a laptop with integrated graphics should expect the minimum row rather than the recommended one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run EA SPORTS FC Online?

EA SPORTS FC Online lists NVIDIA GeForce GT 730 / AMD Radeon HD 7570 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 / AMD Radeon HD 6870 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for EA SPORTS FC Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 / AMD Radeon HD 6870. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended (Nexon download page).

How much RAM does EA SPORTS FC Online need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does EA SPORTS FC Online need?

40 GB available space.
