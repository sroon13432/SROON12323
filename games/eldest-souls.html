Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Eldest Souls?

Eldest Souls lists GeForce GTX 660 or equivalent with 2 GB of video RAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 960 or equivalent with 4GB of video RAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Eldest Souls at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 960 or equivalent with 4GB of video RAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Eldest Souls need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Eldest Souls need?

2 GB available space.
