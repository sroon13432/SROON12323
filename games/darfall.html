Hardware & Performance Analysis

Darfall lists "GTX 950" at minimum and "GeForce RTX 3060" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Darfall?

Darfall lists GTX 950 / AMD Radeon RX 460 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6700 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Darfall at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6700. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Darfall need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
