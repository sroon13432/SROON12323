Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Despot's Game: Dystopian Battle Simulator list "GeForce GTX 670": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
