Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Far Cry® Primal moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 (1GB VRAM)" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a jump of 4.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
