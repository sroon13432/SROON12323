Hardware & Performance Analysis

DAISY THE SWIMMER asks for "GeForce GTX 1060 3GB, AMD Radeon RX 570 4GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 6GB, AMD Radeon RX5600XT 6GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a rise of roughly 63% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
