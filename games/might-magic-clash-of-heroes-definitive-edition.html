Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition runs from "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT, 512 MB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650, 2 GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, 4.5 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition?

Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition lists NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT, 512 MB or AMD Radeon HD 6570, 1 GB or Intel HD Graphics 4400 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650, 2 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7770, 2 GB with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650, 2 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7770, 2 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Might & Magic: Clash of Heroes - Definitive Edition need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
