Hardware & Performance Analysis

Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080, 8 GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, something like 56% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 12 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes?

Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6GB or AMD Radeon RX 580, 4GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080, 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT, 6 GB with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080, 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT, 6 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
