Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for The Silent Age run from "256 MB NVIDIA GeForce 8600 GTS" to "2048 MB NVIDIA GeForce GT 750M". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
