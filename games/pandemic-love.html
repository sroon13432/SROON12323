Hardware & Performance Analysis

Pandemic Love names the same GPU, "DirectX compatible card", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
