Hardware & Performance Analysis

Periphery Online asks for "Intel HD Graphics 3000" at minimum and "GTX 960" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, a jump of 23.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
