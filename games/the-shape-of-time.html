Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Shape of Time?

The Shape of Time lists GB Direct3D 11 capable video card - GeForce GTX 960 or Radeon R9 380 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and 6 GB Direct3D 11 capable video card - GeForce GTX 1660Ti or Radeon RX 590 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Shape of Time at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 6 GB Direct3D 11 capable video card - GeForce GTX 1660Ti or Radeon RX 590. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Shape of Time need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Shape of Time need?

5 GB available space.
