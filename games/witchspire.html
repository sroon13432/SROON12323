Hardware & Performance Analysis

Witchspire asks for "NVIDIA GTX 1070" at minimum and "NVIDIA RTX 3080TI" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Mid-range to High-end, a jump of about 111%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is high-end territory (RTX 3080 Ti bracket), where the requirement says more about the game's ceiling than about the card you need to buy. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Witchspire?

Witchspire lists NVIDIA GTX 1070 / Radeon RX 6600S or equivalent and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 3080TI / AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT or equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Witchspire at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 3080TI / AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Witchspire need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Witchspire need?

12 GB available space.
