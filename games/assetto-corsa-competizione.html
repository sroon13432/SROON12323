Hardware & Performance Analysis

Assetto Corsa Competizione asks for "GeForce GTX 750 4GB, Radeon RX 460 4GB" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
