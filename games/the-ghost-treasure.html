Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Ghost Treasure asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 4.6 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
