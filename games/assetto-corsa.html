Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Assetto Corsa run from "DirectX 10.1 (e.g. AMD Radeon HD 6450, Nvidia GeForce GT 460)" to "DirectX 11 (e.g. AMD Radeon 290x, Nvidia GeForce GTX 970)". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Assetto Corsa?

Assetto Corsa lists DirectX 10.1 (e.g. AMD Radeon HD 6450, Nvidia GeForce GT 460) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 (e.g. AMD Radeon 290x, Nvidia GeForce GTX 970) with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Assetto Corsa at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 (e.g. AMD Radeon 290x, Nvidia GeForce GTX 970). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Assetto Corsa need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
