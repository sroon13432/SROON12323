Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Crystal Call?

Crystal Call lists NVIDIA GTX 770; AMD Radeon 6950 HD; AMD Vega 8 IGP (Intel IGP not supported); 4GB VRAM or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 980 or better; AMD Radeon 580 or better; 4GB VRAM or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Crystal Call at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 980 or better; AMD Radeon 580 or better; 4GB VRAM or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Crystal Call need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
