Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Crowhille - Detective Case Files VR runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1080ti" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA,RTX 2060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 48% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
