Hardware & Performance Analysis

Liminus: The Silent Guard lists "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Ti" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
