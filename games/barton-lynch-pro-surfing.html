Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Barton Lynch Pro Surfing are built around one card, "Graphics card (NVIDIA): NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 1050", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.
