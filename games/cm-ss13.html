Hardware & Performance Analysis

CM-SS13 asks for "Integrated Graphics. If it can render a spreadsheet, it can render this game" at minimum and "Dedicated GPU (Not for the game, but so you can watch YouTube while you wait for the Round…)" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2026 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
