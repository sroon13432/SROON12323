Hardware & Performance Analysis

Winter Ember asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, about 79% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Winter Ember lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 with 8 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Winter Ember at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
