Hardware & Performance Analysis

Life Not Supported names the same GPU, "GeForce GTX 900 series", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (RX 460 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
