Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Planetary Annihilation: TITANS run from "Shader 3.0" to "Dedicated Graphics Chip (Not Integrated)". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
