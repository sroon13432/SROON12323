Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Truck Racer moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800 series" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® 560 series". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 8.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Truck Racer?

Truck Racer lists NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800 series or ATI™ Radeon™ 4870 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® 560 series or AMD Radeon™ HD 6850 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Truck Racer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® 560 series or AMD Radeon™ HD 6850. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Truck Racer need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Truck Racer need?

5 GB available space.
