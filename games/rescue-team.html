Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Rescue Team run from "Video 64 MB RAM" to "Video 128 MB RAM". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 51 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
