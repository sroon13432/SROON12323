Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Intravenous 2 moves from "512 MB VRAM, Radeon Vega 7/GTX 1030/Intel Iris 550" to "512 MB VRAM, RX 460/GTX 1050/Iris Xe DG1". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, something like 82% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (RX 460 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
