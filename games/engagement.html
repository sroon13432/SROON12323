Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Engagement?

Engagement lists Any GPU with WebGL 2.0 / DirectX 11 support: Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Any discrete GPU or modern integrated: NVIDIA GTX 1050 / AMD RX 560 / Intel Iris Xe or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Engagement at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Any discrete GPU or modern integrated: NVIDIA GTX 1050 / AMD RX 560 / Intel Iris Xe or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Engagement need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

500 MB available space.
