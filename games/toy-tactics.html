Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Toy Tactics runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 1070" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
