Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers XAOC moves from "Nvdia Geforce 8600 GT" to "Nvdia Geforce GTS250". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 5.8 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

XAOC lists Nvdia Geforce 8600 GT / ATI HD 4650 or above and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvdia Geforce GTS250 / ATI HD 4850 or above with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for XAOC at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvdia Geforce GTS250 / ATI HD 4850 or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does XAOC need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
