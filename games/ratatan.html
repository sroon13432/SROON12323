Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ratatan asks for "GeForce GTX 950, Radeon R7 360," at minimum and "GeForce RTX 2060, Radeon RX 5600 XT," at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Mid-range, 22.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
