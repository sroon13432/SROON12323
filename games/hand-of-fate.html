Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hand of Fate?

Hand of Fate lists Nvidia 260 GTS or Radeon HD 4850 - 512 MB of VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia 460 GTX / Radeon 5770 - 1 GB of VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hand of Fate at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia 460 GTX / Radeon 5770 - 1 GB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Hand of Fate need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Hand of Fate need?

5 GB available space.
