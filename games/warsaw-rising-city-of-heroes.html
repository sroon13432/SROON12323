Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for WARSAW RISING: City of Heroes ("1 GB VRAM - NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470" and "2 GB VRAM - NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
