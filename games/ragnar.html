Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Ragnar moves from "GeForce GTX 950, Radeon R7 360," to "GeForce RTX 2060, Radeon RX 5600 XT,". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, a jump of 22.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ragnar?

Ragnar lists GeForce GTX 950, Radeon R7 360, or Intel HD Graphics 630 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 2060, Radeon RX 5600 XT, or Intel Arc A580 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ragnar at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 2060, Radeon RX 5600 XT, or Intel Arc A580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ragnar need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
