Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for HANDS OVER list "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Can my PC run HANDS OVER?

HANDS OVER lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD Radeon R9 280 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD Radeon R9 280 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for HANDS OVER at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD Radeon R9 280. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does HANDS OVER need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does HANDS OVER need?
