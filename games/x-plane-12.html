Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run X-Plane 12?

X-Plane 12 lists A Vulkan 1.3-capable video card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel Arc GPU with at least 2 GB VRAM and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and A DirectX 12-capable video card from NVIDIA or AMD with at least 4 GB VRAM with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for X-Plane 12 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is A DirectX 12-capable video card from NVIDIA or AMD with at least 4 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does X-Plane 12 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does X-Plane 12 need?

23 GB available space.
