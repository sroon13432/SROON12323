Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gothic Clicker names the same GPU, "AMD Radeon HD 7750", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Gothic Clicker?

Gothic Clicker lists AMD Radeon HD 7750 or Nvidia GeForce GTX 650 and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon HD 7750 or Nvidia GeForce GTX 650 with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gothic Clicker at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon HD 7750 or Nvidia GeForce GTX 650. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Gothic Clicker need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 512 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Gothic Clicker need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
