Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Deskrawl: Idle ARPG runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GT 730" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
