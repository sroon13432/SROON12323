Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tiny Tina's Wonderlands?

Tiny Tina's Wonderlands lists AMD Radeon RX470 (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 4GB) and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon™ RX 590 8GB (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tiny Tina's Wonderlands at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon™ RX 590 8GB (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tiny Tina's Wonderlands need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Tiny Tina's Wonderlands need?

75 GB available space.
