Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Tiny Tina's Wonderlands moves from "AMD Radeon RX470 (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 4GB)" to "AMD Radeon™ RX 590 8GB (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB)". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, about 79% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 75 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
