Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dragonheir: Silent Gods asks for "NV GeForce 8600 GT, ATI Radeon HD2600XT" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 66.7 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
