Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Heroes of Science and Fiction runs from "Dedicated: with 4 GB VRAM" in the minimum column to "RTX 2060" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Mid-range, a jump of 5.8 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

What GPU do I need for Heroes of Science and Fiction at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 2060 / RX 5600 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
