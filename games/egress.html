Hardware & Performance Analysis

Egress asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce GTX 750 Ti" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 1050 Ti" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (RX 460 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Egress?

Egress lists NVIDIA® GeForce GTX 750 Ti / ATI Radeon HD 7950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 460 series or higher with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Egress at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 460 series or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Egress need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Egress need?

5 GB available space.
