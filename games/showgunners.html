Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Showgunners?

Showgunners lists GeForce GTX 960 4 GB / Radeon RX 570 4 GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1080 8 GB / Radeon RX 5700 XT 8 GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Showgunners at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1080 8 GB / Radeon RX 5700 XT 8 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Showgunners need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Showgunners need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
