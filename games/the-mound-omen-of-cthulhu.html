Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu asks for "Nvidia GTX 1660 SUPER 6GB" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX 3070 8GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, about 68% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 23 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 5060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
