Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers EA SPORTS FC™ 25 moves from "AMD RX 570" to "AMD RX 5600 XT". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, roughly 100% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 100 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
