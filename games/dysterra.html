Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dysterra?

Dysterra lists GTX 950 or R9 380 and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 2060 or RX 5700 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dysterra at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 2060 or RX 5700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dysterra need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

30 GB available space.
