Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for What the Fog runs from "GPUMark of around 5000. Eg: GTX 950" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1060 (6 GB)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 114%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
