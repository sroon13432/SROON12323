Hardware & Performance Analysis

Etheria: Restart asks for "Nvidia GTX 960" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX2070" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 18 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
