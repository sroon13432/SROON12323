Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Fall of the Dungeon Guardians - Enhanced Edition asks for "Shader Model 3.0 GPU ; NVidia GeForce GTX 260 (2008)" at minimum and "Shader Model 3.0 GPU ; NVidia GeForce GTX 760 (2013)" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Low-end, a jump of 4.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest.
