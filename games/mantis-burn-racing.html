Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Mantis Burn Racing® run from "NVIDIA GeForce 500 series" to "NVIDIA GeForce 700 series plus". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4.1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
