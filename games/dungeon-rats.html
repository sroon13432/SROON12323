Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Dungeon Rats ("Nvidia Geforce GTS 250" and "Nvidia Geforce GTS 450") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.46 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dungeon Rats?

Dungeon Rats lists Nvidia Geforce GTS 250 / Radeon HD 4770 (1Gb) or better and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia Geforce GTS 450 / Radeon HD 4870 (1Gb) or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dungeon Rats at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia Geforce GTS 450 / Radeon HD 4870 (1Gb) or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dungeon Rats need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
