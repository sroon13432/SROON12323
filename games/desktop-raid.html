Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Desktop Raid runs from "GTX1050ti" in the minimum column to "GTX1070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 131% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
