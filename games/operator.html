Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for OPERATOR runs from "GTX 1060 (minimum 4GB VRAM)" in the minimum column to "RTX 3060 TI" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 120%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
