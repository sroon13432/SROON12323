Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stellar Overload?

Stellar Overload lists AMD Radeon HD 7870 (2GB) or NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 (2GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon RX 480 or NVIDIA GTX 970 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stellar Overload at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon RX 480 or NVIDIA GTX 970. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Stellar Overload need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

5 GB available space.
