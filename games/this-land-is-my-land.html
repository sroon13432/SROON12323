Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers This Land Is My Land moves from "Geforce GTX 950" to "Geforce GTX 1080". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 4.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
