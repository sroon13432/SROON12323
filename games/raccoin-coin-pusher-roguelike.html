Hardware & Performance Analysis

RACCOIN: Coin Pusher Roguelike asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 3gb, AMD Radeon R9 Fury" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060，AMD Radeon 5600XT" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 72% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
