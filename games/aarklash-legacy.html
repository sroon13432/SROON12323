Hardware & Performance Analysis

Aarklash: Legacy asks for "Integrated" at minimum and "ATI/Nvidia dedicated" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2013, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
