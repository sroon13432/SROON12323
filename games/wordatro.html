Hardware & Performance Analysis

Wordatro! names the same GPU, "integrated graphics", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
