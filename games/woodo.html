Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Woodo?

Woodo lists AMD Radeon RX 560 (4 GB) / NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (4 GB) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT / NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Woodo at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT / NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Woodo need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Woodo need?

2700 MB available space.
