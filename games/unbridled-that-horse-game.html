Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Unbridled: That Horse Game runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti with 4 GB VRAM" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 with 8 GB VRAM" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of about 131%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
