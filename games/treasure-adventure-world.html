Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Treasure Adventure World are built around one card, "Graphics Card that supports Pixel Shader 2.0", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Can my PC run Treasure Adventure World?

Treasure Adventure World lists Graphics Card that supports Pixel Shader 2.0 and 256MB VRAM and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and Graphics Card that supports Pixel Shader 2.0 and 256MB VRAM with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Treasure Adventure World at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Graphics Card that supports Pixel Shader 2.0 and 256MB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
