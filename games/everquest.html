Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers EverQuest moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 Ti" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, roughly 167% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run EverQuest?

EverQuest lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 Ti / AMD Radeon HD 7970, hardware feature level 11_0 or greater and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 5700, hardware feature level 11_1 or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for EverQuest at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 5700, hardware feature level 11_1 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does EverQuest need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does EverQuest need?

20 GB HD space.
