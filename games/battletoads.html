Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Battletoads runs from "Nvidia GTX 650" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 650Ti" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Battletoads?

Battletoads lists Nvidia GTX 650 or AMD R7 250x and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 650Ti or AMD R7 260 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Battletoads at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 650Ti or AMD R7 260. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
