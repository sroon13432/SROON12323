Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers The Walking Dead: Betrayal moves from "GTX 660 Ti" to "GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 4.6 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
