Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run MicroTown?

MicroTown lists 1280x720+, shader model 4.0+ and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1280x720+, shader model 4.0+ with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for MicroTown at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1280x720+, shader model 4.0+. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does MicroTown need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does MicroTown need?

500 MB available space.
