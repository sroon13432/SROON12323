Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Heroes of Civilizations?

Heroes of Civilizations lists Intel Graphics Media Accelerator 950 or equivalent and 128 MB RAM as its minimum, and Intel Graphics Media Accelerator 4500MHD or equivalent with 256 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Heroes of Civilizations at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel Graphics Media Accelerator 4500MHD or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Heroes of Civilizations need?

128 MB of memory at minimum and 256 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Heroes of Civilizations need?

100 MB available space.
