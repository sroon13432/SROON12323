Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Seafarer: The Ship Simulator ("NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 6 GB" and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 (12 GB)") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 64 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 42 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
