Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bus World?

Bus World lists Geforce GTX 950 or Radeon R9 280 (no support for onboard cards) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX 590 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Bus World at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX 590. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Bus World need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Bus World need?

11 GB available space.
