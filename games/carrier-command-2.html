Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Carrier Command 2 runs from "Intel HD 6000" in the minimum column to "GeForce 870 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, about 73% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
