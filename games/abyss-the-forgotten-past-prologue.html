Hardware & Performance Analysis

Abyss The Forgotten Past: Prologue asks for "Radeon X600 XT" at minimum and "Compatible with DirectX9.0c" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
