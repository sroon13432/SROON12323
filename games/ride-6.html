Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for RIDE 6 runs from "GeForce GTX 1660 Ti" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 2080" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, 122% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

What GPU do I need for RIDE 6 at 60 FPS?
