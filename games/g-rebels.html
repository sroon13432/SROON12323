Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run G-Rebels?

G-Rebels lists 8 GB GPU (GTX 1070 or AMD RX 5700 XT level) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and 12+ GB GPU (RTX 4080 or 7900 XTX level) with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for G-Rebels at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 12+ GB GPU (RTX 4080 or 7900 XTX level). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does G-Rebels need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does G-Rebels need?

30 GB available space.
