Hardware & Performance Analysis

Factory Town 2: Paradise asks for "Geforce GTX 960" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 980 Ti" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 100% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 Ti bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
