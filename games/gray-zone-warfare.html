Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Gray Zone Warfare runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1080 8GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2080 Ti 11GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 60% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 4070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
