Hardware & Performance Analysis

World of Tanks asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1650" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 120 GB install wants its own room on a fast drive, with space left over for patches. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run World of Tanks?

World of Tanks lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon R9 270, 1280x768 screen resolution and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 1650 / AMD Radeon RX 580 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for World of Tanks at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 1650 / AMD Radeon RX 580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
