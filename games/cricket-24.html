Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cricket 24 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 (4 GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti (6 GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.2 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
