Hardware & Performance Analysis

Office Battle asks for "512 MB of VRAM" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GT630" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Office Battle lists 512 MB of VRAM and DX9 support and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GT630 or AMD Radeon HD 4730 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Office Battle at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GT630 or AMD Radeon HD 4730. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
