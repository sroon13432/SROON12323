Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Amnesia: The Bunker?

Amnesia: The Bunker lists OpenGL 4.0, Nvidia GTX 460 / AMD Radeon HD 5750 / Intel HD 630 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and OpenGL 4.3, Nvidia GTX 970 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT / Intel Xe-HPG with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Amnesia: The Bunker at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is OpenGL 4.3, Nvidia GTX 970 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT / Intel Xe-HPG. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Amnesia: The Bunker need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
