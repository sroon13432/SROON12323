Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim asks for "Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with 512 MB of RAM Sound: DirectX compatible sound card" at minimum and "DirectX 9.0c compatible NVIDIA" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2011, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
