Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Total War: WARHAMMER III ("Nvidia GTX 900/AMD RX 400 series" and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Ti/AMD RX 5600-XT/Intel Arc A750") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 120 GB install wants its own room on a fast drive, with space left over for patches. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
