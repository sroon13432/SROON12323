Hardware & Performance Analysis

Revenge of the Killer Octopus names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Revenge of the Killer Octopus?

Revenge of the Killer Octopus lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Revenge of the Killer Octopus at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Revenge of the Killer Octopus need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Revenge of the Killer Octopus need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
