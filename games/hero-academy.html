Can my PC run Hero Academy?

What GPU do I need for Hero Academy at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DX11 & DX10 video cards, minimum resolution 1280 x 800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2GB of memory at minimum and 2GB at recommended settings.
