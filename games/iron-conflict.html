Hardware & Performance Analysis

Iron Conflict asks for "AMD Radeon HD 7850, NVIDIA GeForce 660 series" at minimum and "AMD Radeon RX 470" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a rise of roughly 58% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 470 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Iron Conflict?

Iron Conflict lists AMD Radeon HD 7850, NVIDIA GeForce 660 series and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon RX 470 or NVIDIA GeForce 1050 Ti series and above with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Iron Conflict at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon RX 470 or NVIDIA GeForce 1050 Ti series and above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Iron Conflict need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
