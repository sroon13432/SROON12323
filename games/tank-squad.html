Tank Squad

★ Mostly Positive (70% of 901 reviews) 💾 25 GB available space

🚀 Recommended GPU: 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 4070 / AMD RX 7900 GRE), a GPU that supports DLSS or FSR upscaling filter

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tank Squad?

Tank Squad lists 6 GB VRAM, GeForce GTX 570 / Radeon HD 6970 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 4070 / AMD RX 7900 GRE), a GPU that supports DLSS or FSR upscaling filter with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tank Squad at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 4070 / AMD RX 7900 GRE), a GPU that supports DLSS or FSR upscaling filter. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tank Squad need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Tank Squad need?

25 GB available space.
