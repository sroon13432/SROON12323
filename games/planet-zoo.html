Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Planet Zoo?

Planet Zoo lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 (2GB) / AMD Radeon R9 270X (2GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB) or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Planet Zoo at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB) or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Planet Zoo need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Planet Zoo need?

16 GB available space.
