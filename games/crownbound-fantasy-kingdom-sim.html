Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Crownbound - Fantasy Kingdom Sim runs from "NVidia Geforce GTX 660 2GB" in the minimum column to "NVidia Geforce GTX 970 4GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
