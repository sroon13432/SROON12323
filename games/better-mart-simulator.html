Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Better Mart Simulator moves from "NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti" to "NVIDIA RTX 2060". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of about 169%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
