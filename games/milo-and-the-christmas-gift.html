Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Milo and the Christmas Gift list "Direct X 9.0c compatible video card": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
