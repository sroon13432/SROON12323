Hardware & Performance Analysis

Songs of Conquest asks for "Integrated graphics card" at minimum and "GTX 970, RX 570" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.5 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
