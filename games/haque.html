Hardware & Performance Analysis

Haque asks for "128 MB" at minimum and "512 MB" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
