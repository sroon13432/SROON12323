Hardware & Performance Analysis

Foobar Zero asks for "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 1060" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, something like 36% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
