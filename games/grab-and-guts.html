Hardware & Performance Analysis

Grab and Guts lists "NVIDIA GTX 660" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 650 Radeon HD 3600 (1GB)" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 133 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
