Hardware & Performance Analysis

Final Fantasy XIV: A Realm Reborn asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 67% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at Publisher minimum requirements.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. Storage is 140 GB and the requirement asks for an SSD specifically, so a hard drive is out of the question here. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
