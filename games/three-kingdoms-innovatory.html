Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Three Kingdoms: Innovatory moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060(6GB)". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Three Kingdoms: Innovatory?

Three Kingdoms: Innovatory lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB or over, AMD Radeon R7 370 2GB or over and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060(6GB) or over, AMD Radeon RX580(8GB) or over with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Three Kingdoms: Innovatory at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060(6GB) or over, AMD Radeon RX580(8GB) or over. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Three Kingdoms: Innovatory need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Three Kingdoms: Innovatory need?

10 GB available space.
