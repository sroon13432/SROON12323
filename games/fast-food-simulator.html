Can my PC run Fast Food Simulator?

Fast Food Simulator lists Nvidia GTX 1050 / RX 570 (4GB+ of VRAM) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX 3060 / RX 6700 (8GB+ of VRAM) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fast Food Simulator at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX 3060 / RX 6700 (8GB+ of VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
