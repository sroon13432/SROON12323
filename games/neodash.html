Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Neodash?

Neodash lists GeForce GTX 950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 960 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Neodash at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 960. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Neodash need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Neodash need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
