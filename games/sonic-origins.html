Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Sonic Origins runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750, 2 GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770, 2 GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a rise of roughly 57% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sonic Origins?

Sonic Origins lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750, 2 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7790, 2 GB and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770, 2 GB or AMD Radeon R9 280, 3 GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sonic Origins at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770, 2 GB or AMD Radeon R9 280, 3 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sonic Origins need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Sonic Origins need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
