Hardware & Performance Analysis

Beetleball asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 760, AMD Radeon R7 270X," at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470," at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Beetleball?

Beetleball lists NVIDIA GeForce 760, AMD Radeon R7 270X, or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470, or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Beetleball at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470, or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Beetleball need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
