Hardware & Performance Analysis

House Flipper Remastered Collection lists "RX 6500 8GB" at minimum and "8GBs VRAM" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
