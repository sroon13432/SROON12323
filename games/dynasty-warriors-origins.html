Hardware & Performance Analysis

DYNASTY WARRIORS: ORIGINS asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (VRAM 6GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 (VRAM 8GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 79% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
