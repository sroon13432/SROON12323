Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe names the same GPU, "32 MB", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 0 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
