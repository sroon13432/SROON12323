Genshin Impact

💾 110 GB available space ⚙️ Unity

Genshin Impact is HoYoverse's open-world action RPG, free on PC through the HoYoPlay launcher and not sold on Steam. The official PC requirements ask for Windows 10 64-bit, a 6th-generation Core i5 or Ryzen, 8 GB of RAM and a GeForce GTX 1050 (or Intel Iris Xe) at minimum, and a 7th-generation Core i7 or Ryzen 5000 series with 16 GB and a GTX 1060 6GB when recommended, with 110 GB of storage either way.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB or better
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Genshin Impact runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 163% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at Official minimum; the recommended row is what makes Official recommended realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 110 GB install also names an SSD, and that part is not optional: texture streaming on a spinning drive is where this title falls apart. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    The 110 GB figure is a floor: HoYoverse warns on the same page that storage requirements grow with future updates, so leave headroom on the drive.
  • 💡
    HoYoverse explicitly recommends installing on an SSD for smoother play, which matters more here than GPU tier because of the open-world streaming.
  • 💡
    Render resolution is the biggest lever on this engine: dropping it one step buys more than turning every effect down, especially on the GTX 1050 minimum tier.

Can my PC run Genshin Impact?

Genshin Impact lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / Intel Iris Xe and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Genshin Impact at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.
