Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Voidtrain runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 133% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
