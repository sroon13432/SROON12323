Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Astroflux?

Astroflux lists Intel HD 4000 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Intel HD 4000 with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Astroflux at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel HD 4000. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Astroflux need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Astroflux need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
