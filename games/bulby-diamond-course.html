Hardware & Performance Analysis

Bulby - Diamond Course asks for "Intel HD Graphics" at minimum and "Gtx 550" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 4.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
