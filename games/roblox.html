Roblox

💾 300 MB ⚙️ Roblox Engine

Roblox is a free platform of player-made experiences, installed from roblox.com and not sold on Steam. Roblox publishes a single minimum set rather than separate minimum and recommended tiers: a 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11 install, a processor from 2005 or newer at 1.6 GHz, 1 GB of RAM, DirectX 10 feature level support and 300 MB of storage, with 4-8 Mb/s of bandwidth recommended.

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Roblox are built around one card, "DirectX 10", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between Official minimum (single published tier) and Official guidance (no separate recommended tier published) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Released in 2006, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Roblox's requirement covers the client only; individual experiences inside it can be far heavier, so a machine at the minimum should stay on lighter games.
  • 💡
    For best performance Roblox recommends a PC under five years old with a dedicated card, or a laptop under three years old with integrated graphics.
  • 💡
    The page lists 4-8 Mb/s as the minimum connection; in a household with several players, more bandwidth is needed than the client's own minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Roblox?

Roblox lists DirectX 10 or higher feature level and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 10 or higher feature level with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Roblox at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 10 or higher feature level. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official guidance (no separate recommended tier published).

How much RAM does Roblox need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Roblox need?

300 MB available space.
