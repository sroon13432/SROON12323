Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Hammer & Anvil VR moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1080". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RX Vega 64 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
