Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Project Motor Racing runs from "NVIDIA RTX 2060" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 3070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, 176% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 24 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 75 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Project Motor Racing?

Project Motor Racing lists NVIDIA RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 580 (6 GB VRAM or more) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 3070 / AMD Radeon RX 6800 (8 GB VRAM or more) with 24 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Project Motor Racing at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 3070 / AMD Radeon RX 6800 (8 GB VRAM or more). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Project Motor Racing need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 24 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Project Motor Racing need?

75 GB available space.
