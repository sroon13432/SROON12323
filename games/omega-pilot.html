Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Omega Pilot runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti/AMD Radeon RX 470" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 1060/AMD Radeon RX 480" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a rise of roughly 44% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
