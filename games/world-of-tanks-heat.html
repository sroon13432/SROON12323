Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for World of Tanks: HEAT runs from "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 1050 (2GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 (6 GB), AMD Radeon™ RX 5600XT (6 GB)," in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of about 169%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
