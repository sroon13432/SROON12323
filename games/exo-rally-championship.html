Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Exo Rally Championship?

Exo Rally Championship lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX Vega 56, 8 GB and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12GB) or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Exo Rally Championship at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12GB) or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Exo Rally Championship need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Exo Rally Championship need?

10 GB available space.
