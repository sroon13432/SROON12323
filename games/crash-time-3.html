Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Crash Time 3?

Crash Time 3 lists DirectX 9.0c compatible 256 MB graphics card and 1 GB (2 GB for Vista) RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 9.0c compatible 256 MB graphics card with 1 GB (2 GB for Vista) RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Crash Time 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 9.0c compatible 256 MB graphics card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Crash Time 3 need?

1 GB (2 GB for Vista) of memory at minimum and 1 GB (2 GB for Vista) at recommended settings.
