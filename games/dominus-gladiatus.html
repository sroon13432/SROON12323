Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Dominus Gladiatus are built around one card, "DX 11 Compatible GPU", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
