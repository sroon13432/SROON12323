Hardware & Performance Analysis

WorldBox - God Simulator asks for "128mb Video Memory, capable of Shader Model 2.0+" at minimum and "256mb Video Memory, capable of Shader Model 2.0+" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2.5 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
