Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers SUCCUBUS: Prologue moves from "Radeon R9 280" to "Radeon RX 580". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 22 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
