Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Echoes of Aincrad runs from "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060 [6GB]" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2070 [8GB]" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
