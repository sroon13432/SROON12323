Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run SCP: Nine-Tailed Fox?

SCP: Nine-Tailed Fox lists 3D-capable card with 2GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650Ti or AMD HD 6870 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for SCP: Nine-Tailed Fox at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650Ti or AMD HD 6870. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does SCP: Nine-Tailed Fox need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does SCP: Nine-Tailed Fox need?

600 MB available space.
