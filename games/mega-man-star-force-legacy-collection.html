Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Mega Man Star Force Legacy Collection list "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Mega Man Star Force Legacy Collection?

Mega Man Star Force Legacy Collection lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon™ RX 5500 XT 8G and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon™ RX 5500 XT 8G with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Mega Man Star Force Legacy Collection at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon™ RX 5500 XT 8G. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
