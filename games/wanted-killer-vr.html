Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Wanted Killer VR moves from "NVIDIA GTX 970" to "NVIDIA GTX 980". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Wanted Killer VR?

Wanted Killer VR lists NVIDIA GTX 970 / AMD equivalent or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 980 / AMD equivalent or greater with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Wanted Killer VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 980 / AMD equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Wanted Killer VR need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Wanted Killer VR need?

5 GB available space.
