Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Princess Maker 2 Refine?

Princess Maker 2 Refine lists 32MB or greater graphics card and 256 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GeForce GTX 280 or ATI Radeon HD 6630 or higher with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Princess Maker 2 Refine at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GeForce GTX 280 or ATI Radeon HD 6630 or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Princess Maker 2 Refine need?

256 MB of memory at minimum and 512 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Princess Maker 2 Refine need?

2 GB available space.
