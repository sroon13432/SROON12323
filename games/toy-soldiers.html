Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Toy Soldiers?

Toy Soldiers lists 1 GB Dedicated Graphics Card with Shader Model 3.0 or Higher** and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 2 GB Dedicated Graphics Card with Shader Model 3.0 or Higher** with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Toy Soldiers at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 2 GB Dedicated Graphics Card with Shader Model 3.0 or Higher**. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Toy Soldiers need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Toy Soldiers need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
