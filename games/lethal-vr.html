Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Lethal VR list "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 604 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lethal VR?

Lethal VR lists NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lethal VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Lethal VR need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
