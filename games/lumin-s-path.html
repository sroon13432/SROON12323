Hardware & Performance Analysis

Lumin's Path lists "NVIDIA Geforce GT 210 (512MB +)" at minimum and "NVIDIA Geforce GT 440 (1024MB +)" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
