Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Altheia: The Wrath of Aferi?

Altheia: The Wrath of Aferi lists GeForce GTX 660 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1060 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Altheia: The Wrath of Aferi at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Altheia: The Wrath of Aferi need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Altheia: The Wrath of Aferi need?

12 GB available space.
