Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for 军团战棋Legion War are built around one card, "HD4400", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
