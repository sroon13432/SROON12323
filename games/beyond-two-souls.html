Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Beyond: Two Souls moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 660 with 2GB VRAM" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 with 4GB VRAM". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 186% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 49 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Beyond: Two Souls?

Beyond: Two Souls lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 660 with 2GB VRAM or AMD Radeon HD 7870 with 2GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 with 4GB VRAM or AMD Radeon R9 390 with 4GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Beyond: Two Souls at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 with 4GB VRAM or AMD Radeon R9 390 with 4GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Beyond: Two Souls need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Beyond: Two Souls need?

49 GB available space.
