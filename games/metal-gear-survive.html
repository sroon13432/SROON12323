Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers METAL GEAR SURVIVE moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 (2GB)" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 (DirectX 11 graphic card required)". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.1 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
