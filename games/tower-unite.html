Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tower Unite?

Tower Unite lists DX10 Compatible GPU with 4 GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DX11 Compatible GPU with 8 GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tower Unite at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DX11 Compatible GPU with 8 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tower Unite need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Tower Unite need?

30 GB available space.
