Apocalipsis

★ Mostly Positive (71% of 378 reviews) 💾 2 GB available space

Apocalipsis is a point-and-click adventure game, in the vain of Samorost and Machinarium, where all puzzles have a hidden meaning. With a unique artstyle inspired by 15th century engravings it tells a story about heartbreak, redemption and, quite literally, the end of the world.

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Apocalipsis moves from "512 MB VRAM, NVIDIA GeForce 8600GT/ATI Radeon HD 3850/INTEL HD 4400" to "1 GB VRAM, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 550 Ti/AMD Radeon HD 5850". Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is a jump of 4.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Apocalipsis?

Apocalipsis lists 512 MB VRAM, NVIDIA GeForce 8600GT/ATI Radeon HD 3850/INTEL HD 4400 and 2048 MB RAM as its minimum, and 1 GB VRAM, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 550 Ti/AMD Radeon HD 5850 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Apocalipsis at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1 GB VRAM, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 550 Ti/AMD Radeon HD 5850. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
