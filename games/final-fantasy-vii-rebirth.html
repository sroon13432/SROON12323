Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH runs from "AMD Radeon™ RX 6600 (RX 6600)" in the minimum column to "AMD Radeon™ RX 6700 XT" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 55% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 155 GB install wants its own room on a fast drive, with space left over for patches. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
