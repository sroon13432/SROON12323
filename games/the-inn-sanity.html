Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Inn-Sanity runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce 1080Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, roughly 100% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 10 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 27 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
