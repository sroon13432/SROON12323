Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Medieval Fantasy Survival Simulator 2: Gladiator Edition are built around one card, "1024x768", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 799 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it. Simulation titles like this lean on CPU-side systems, so the processor beside the listed GPU deserves as much attention.
