Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run MH-Zombie?

MH-Zombie lists Intel® UHD Graphics 615 or equivalent and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 2080 or equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for MH-Zombie at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 2080 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does MH-Zombie need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does MH-Zombie need?

2800 MB available space.
