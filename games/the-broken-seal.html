Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Broken Seal?

The Broken Seal lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon RX 480 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 or AMD R9 FURY X with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Broken Seal at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 or AMD R9 FURY X. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Broken Seal need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Broken Seal need?

5 GB available space.
