Hardware & Performance Analysis

Glimvale : My Mini Overworld asks for "128MB, OpenGL 3+" at minimum and "Intel Core i5-4690" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
