Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for SOK list "GTX 1050": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 51 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
