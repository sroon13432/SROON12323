Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cities: Skylines II?

Cities: Skylines II lists Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 970 (4 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 480 (8 GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3080 (10 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 6800 XT (16 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cities: Skylines II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3080 (10 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 6800 XT (16 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cities: Skylines II need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

60 GB available space.
