Hardware & Performance Analysis

DYNASTY WARRIORS 7: Xtreme Legends Definitive Edition asks for "VRAM 128MB" at minimum and "VRAM 256MB" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run DYNASTY WARRIORS 7: Xtreme Legends Definitive Edition?

DYNASTY WARRIORS 7: Xtreme Legends Definitive Edition lists VRAM 128MB or better, 3D accelerator chip required(DirectX 9.0c or better/Pixel Shader 3.0 or better) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and VRAM 256MB or better, 3D accelerator chip required(DirectX 9.0c or better/Pixel Shader 3.0 or better) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for DYNASTY WARRIORS 7: Xtreme Legends Definitive Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is VRAM 256MB or better, 3D accelerator chip required(DirectX 9.0c or better/Pixel Shader 3.0 or better). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
