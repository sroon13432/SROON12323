Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Sweet Transit moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT, 512 MB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 7.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at Publisher minimum requirements; the recommended row is what makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
