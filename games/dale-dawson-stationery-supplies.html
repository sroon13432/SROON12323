Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Dale & Dawson Stationery Supplies moves from "Nvidia Geforce GTX 960" to "Nvidia Geforce GTX 1080Ti". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 3.6 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (GTX 1080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
