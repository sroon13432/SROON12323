Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bee Simulator runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 550 Ti ou ATI Radeon HD 5770" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 ou AMD Radeon HD 7870" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, 169% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.
