Hardware & Performance Analysis

Arma 3 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, 18.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 80 GB install also names an SSD, and that part is not optional: texture streaming on a spinning drive is where this title falls apart. DirectX 10 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580, with 6 GB VRAM, or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
