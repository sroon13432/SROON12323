Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 6 GB VRAM". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 142%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 100 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
