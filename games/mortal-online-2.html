Hardware & Performance Analysis

Mortal Online 2 asks for "GeForce GTX 1060 6GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB" at minimum and "GeForce RTX 2060. Radeon RX 5700 XT" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, 122% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 80 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
