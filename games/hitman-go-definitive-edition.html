Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Hitman GO: Definitive Edition run from "ATI™ Radeon™ X700 (256 MB)" to "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB)". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hitman GO: Definitive Edition?

Hitman GO: Definitive Edition lists ATI™ Radeon™ X700 (256 MB) or NVidia Equivalent (256 MB) or better and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB) or ATI Radeon HD 4870 (1Gb) or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hitman GO: Definitive Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB) or ATI Radeon HD 4870 (1Gb) or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Hitman GO: Definitive Edition need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
