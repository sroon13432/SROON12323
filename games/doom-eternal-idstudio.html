Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run DOOM Eternal: idStudio?

DOOM Eternal: idStudio lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB), AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB) or better and 32 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 3080 Ti (12GB), AMD Radeon RX 6750 XT (12GB) or better with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for DOOM Eternal: idStudio at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 3080 Ti (12GB), AMD Radeon RX 6750 XT (12GB) or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does DOOM Eternal: idStudio need?

32 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.
