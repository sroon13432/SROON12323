World of Warcraft

💾 128 GB free on an SSD (both tiers) ⚙️ World of Warcraft engine

World of Warcraft is Blizzard's long-running subscription MMORPG, installed through the Battle.net app and not sold on Steam. With the current Forever client Blizzard asks for Windows 10 64-bit (May 2019 Update or newer), a 4-core 3.0 GHz processor, a DirectX 12 card with 4 GB of VRAM, 8 GB of RAM and a 128 GB SSD; the recommended row moves to Windows 11, an 8-core 5.2 GHz boost CPU, a DirectX 12 8 GB card and 16 GB of RAM.

🚀 Recommended GPU: DirectX 12 GPU with 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 40 series / AMD RDNA 3 / Intel Arc B-Series)
Target: 60 FPS

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Blizzard added a hard GPU architecture floor for the Forever client: the card must be AMD GCN 1, NVIDIA Maxwell or Intel Skylake generation or newer, so a GeForce GTX 760/770/780 is below the line despite its model number.
  • 💡
    The storage row is free space on an SSD, not install size, and no hard-disk option is listed any more.
  • 💡
    Draw distance and raid/ground-effect density are the two settings that decide frame rate in group content; both can be lowered without changing the resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run World of Warcraft?

World of Warcraft lists DirectX 12 GPU with 4 GB VRAM (GeForce GTX 10 series / AMD RDNA 1 / Intel Iris Xe2-LPG) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 12 GPU with 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 40 series / AMD RDNA 3 / Intel Arc B-Series) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for World of Warcraft at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 12 GPU with 8 GB VRAM (GeForce RTX 40 series / AMD RDNA 3 / Intel Arc B-Series). That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended (Battle.net shop specification).

How much RAM does World of Warcraft need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does World of Warcraft need?

128 GB free on an SSD.
