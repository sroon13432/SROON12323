Hardware & Performance Analysis

BBY DON'T HURT ME asks for "GTX 1070" at minimum and "RTX 3060" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
