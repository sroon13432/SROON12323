Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Rise of the Tomb Raider™ runs from "NVIDIA GTX 650 2GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 980Ti 2560x1440" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 4.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
