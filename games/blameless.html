Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Blameless run from "Video card with 512 MB memory" to "Video card with 1024 MB memory". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.17 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
