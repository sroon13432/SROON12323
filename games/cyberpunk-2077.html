Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cyberpunk 2077 (Phantom Liberty)?

Cyberpunk 2077 (Phantom Liberty) lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB) / AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB) and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 SUPER / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cyberpunk 2077 (Phantom Liberty) at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 SUPER / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cyberpunk 2077 (Phantom Liberty) need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

70 GB SSD.
