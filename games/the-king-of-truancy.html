Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for The King Of Truancy list "NVIDIA GTX 960": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
