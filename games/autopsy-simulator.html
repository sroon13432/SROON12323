Hardware & Performance Analysis

Autopsy Simulator asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1650, 4 GB" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060, 6 GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a rise of roughly 71% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
