Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Peaceful Days runs from "Intel HD Graphics" in the minimum column to "GTX 1660 Super" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 22.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
