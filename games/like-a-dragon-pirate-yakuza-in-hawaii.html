Hardware & Performance Analysis

Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650,4GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060,6GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, roughly 192% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 56 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (Radeon VII bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
