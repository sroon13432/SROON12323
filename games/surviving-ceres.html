Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Surviving Ceres?

Surviving Ceres lists Nvidia GTX 650 Ti and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 650 Ti with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Surviving Ceres at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 650 Ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Surviving Ceres need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

3 GB available space.
