Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers I Am Fish moves from "GeForce GTX 960 (4096 VRAM)" to "GeForce RTX 2060 Super (6144VRAM)". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 3.4 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
