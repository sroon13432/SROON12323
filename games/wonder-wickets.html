Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Wonder Wickets?

Wonder Wickets lists 512 MB and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1 GB Discrete GPU with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Wonder Wickets at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1 GB Discrete GPU. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Wonder Wickets need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Wonder Wickets need?

600 MB available space.
