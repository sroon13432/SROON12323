Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Far Cry® 4 runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.5 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Far Cry® 4?

Far Cry® 4 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 or AMD Radeon HD5850 (1 GB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680 or AMD Radeon R9 290X or better (2 GB VRAM) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Far Cry® 4 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680 or AMD Radeon R9 290X or better (2 GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Far Cry® 4 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

30 GB available space.
