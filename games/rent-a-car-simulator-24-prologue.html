Hardware & Performance Analysis

Rent A Car Simulator 24: Prologue asks for "DirectX 11 compatible 4GB dedicated, GeForce GTX 970" at minimum and "DirectX 11 compatible 6GB dedicated, GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 10 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
