Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Dark and Light moves from "GeForce GTX 960" to "GeForce GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of about 164%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
