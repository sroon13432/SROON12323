Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Deadly Hunter VR?

Deadly Hunter VR lists NVidia GTX 970 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GTX 1070 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Deadly Hunter VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GTX 1070. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Deadly Hunter VR need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Deadly Hunter VR need?

4 GB available space.
