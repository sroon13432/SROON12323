Hardware & Performance Analysis

Toribash asks for "Intel HD 4000" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 950" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, 8.3 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (RX 460 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
