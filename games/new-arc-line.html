Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run New Arc Line?

New Arc Line lists Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 1660 6 GB VRAM or AMD® Radeon™ 5700 XT 6 GB VRAM and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3060 or AMD® Radeon™ 6600 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for New Arc Line at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3060 or AMD® Radeon™ 6600 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does New Arc Line need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does New Arc Line need?

15 GB available space.
