Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Ignition run from "3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 7" to "3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 9". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ignition?

Ignition lists 3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 7 and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and 3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 9 with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ignition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 9. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ignition need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
