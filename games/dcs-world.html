Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers DCS World moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 760" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1070 (1080 for VR)". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 184% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at Publisher minimum requirements; the recommended row is what makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 200 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Simulation titles like this lean on CPU-side systems, so the processor beside the listed GPU deserves as much attention.
