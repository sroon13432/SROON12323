Black Desert Online

💾 39 GB ⚙️ Black Desert Engine

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Black Desert Online asks for "Nvidia GeForce 9800 GTX" at minimum and "AMD Radeon RX 480" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, 9.5 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you Publisher minimum requirements, meeting the recommended row gets you Publisher recommended requirements, and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 39 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
