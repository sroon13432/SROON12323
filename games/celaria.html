Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Celaria?

Celaria lists DirectX® 9.0c compatible graphics card and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and dedicated DirectX® 9.0c compatible graphics card with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Celaria at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is dedicated DirectX® 9.0c compatible graphics card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Celaria need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Celaria need?

512 MB available space.
