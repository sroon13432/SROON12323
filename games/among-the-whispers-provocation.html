Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Among The Whispers - Provocation moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 8GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 3080 12GB". On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 55% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. The 11 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6800 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
