Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Lost Crown asks for "128 MB DX 9.0c compliant videocard" at minimum and "256 MB DX 9.0c compliant videocard" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.17 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
