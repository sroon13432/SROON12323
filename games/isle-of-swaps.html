Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Isle of Swaps?

Isle of Swaps lists 512 Mb Video Memory and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 512 Mb Video Memory with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Isle of Swaps at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 512 Mb Video Memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Isle of Swaps need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Isle of Swaps need?

600 MB available space.
