Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Xanadu Next?

Xanadu Next lists 32 MB VRAM, 3D accelerator compatible w/ DirectX 9.0c and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and 64 MB VRAM, 3D accelerator compatible w/ DirectX 9.0c with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Xanadu Next at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 64 MB VRAM, 3D accelerator compatible w/ DirectX 9.0c. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Xanadu Next need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Xanadu Next need?

2 GB available space.
