Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run One's Own Exile?

One's Own Exile lists Intel HD Graphics 2500 (Ivy Bridge), AMD Radeon R5 Graphics (Kaveri) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 (Kepler), AMD Radeon HD 7750 (GCN 1.0) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for One's Own Exile at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 (Kepler), AMD Radeon HD 7750 (GCN 1.0). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does One's Own Exile need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does One's Own Exile need?

150 MB available space.
