Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for SSTWL - Sector Breach run from "AMD Radeon R5 Graphics" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.
