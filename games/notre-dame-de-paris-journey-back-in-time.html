Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Notre-Dame de Paris: Journey Back in Time moves from "GTX 1070 - 8GB" to "GTX 1080 - 8GB". Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
