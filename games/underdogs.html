Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for UNDERDOGS runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 2070" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, a jump of about 104%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
