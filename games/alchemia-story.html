Hardware & Performance Analysis

Alchemia Story asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 1030 (DDR4)" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 (VRAM 3GB)" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
