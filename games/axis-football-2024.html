Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Axis Football 2024 are built around one card, "2 GB+", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
