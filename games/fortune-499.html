Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fortune-499?

Fortune-499 lists Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fortune-499 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
