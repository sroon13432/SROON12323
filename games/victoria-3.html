Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Victoria 3?

Victoria 3 lists Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 660 (2GB) | AMD® Radeon™ R7 370 (2GB) or Intel® Iris® Xe Graphics or AMD® Radeon™ Vega 8 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 (6GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB) | Intel® Arc™ A580 (8GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Victoria 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 (6GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB) | Intel® Arc™ A580 (8GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Victoria 3 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Victoria 3 need?

10 GB available space.
