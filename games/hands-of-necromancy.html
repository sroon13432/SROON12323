Can my PC run Hands of Necromancy?

Hands of Necromancy lists OpenGL 4.2 and 2048 MB RAM as its minimum, and Vulkan 1.2 with 8192 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is Vulkan 1.2. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2048 MB of memory at minimum and 8192 MB at recommended settings.
