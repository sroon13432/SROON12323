Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Horror moves from "GeForce RTX 2080" to "NVIDIA RTX 3080". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 82% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2026 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
