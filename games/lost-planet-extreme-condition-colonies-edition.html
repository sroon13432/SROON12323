Hardware & Performance Analysis

Lost Planet: Extreme Condition Colonies Edition names the same GPU, "DirectX 9.0c/Shader3.0 compatible, VRAM256MB, NVIDIA GeForce 6600 series", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
