Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Wardwell House list "AMD Radeon Graphics Processor (0x15DD)": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 1.51 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
