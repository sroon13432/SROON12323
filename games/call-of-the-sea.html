Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Call of the Sea?

Call of the Sea lists AMD Radeon HD 7750, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 or Equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD RX Vega 56, Nvidia GTX 1070/GTX1660Ti or Equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Call of the Sea at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD RX Vega 56, Nvidia GTX 1070/GTX1660Ti or Equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Call of the Sea need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Call of the Sea need?

15 GB available space.
