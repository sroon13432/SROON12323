Hardware & Performance Analysis

EVERYBODY'S GOLF HOT SHOTS asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 980/ Radeon RX Vega 56" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super/ Radeon RX Vega 64/ Intel Arc A580" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RX Vega 64 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
