Hardware & Performance Analysis

Pirate Ships asks for "Video Card DX9 256mb" at minimum and "Video Card DX9 512mb" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
