Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Might & Magic® Heroes® VII runs from "nVidia GeForce GTX460" in the minimum column to "nVidia GeForce GTX670" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, 3.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Might & Magic® Heroes® VII?

Might & Magic® Heroes® VII lists nVidia GeForce GTX460 or AMD Radeon HD5850 (1024 MB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and nVidia GeForce GTX670 or AMD Radeon HD7870 (2048 MB VRAM) with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Might & Magic® Heroes® VII at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is nVidia GeForce GTX670 or AMD Radeon HD7870 (2048 MB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Might & Magic® Heroes® VII need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Might & Magic® Heroes® VII need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
