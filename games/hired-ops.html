Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Hired Ops run from "Video card must be 1024 MB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
