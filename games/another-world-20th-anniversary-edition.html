Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Another World – 20th Anniversary Edition list "OpenGL 2.0 compatible": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 799 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.
