Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Diluvian Winds list "DirectX 11 and/or OpenGL 3.3 compatible video card": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Diluvian Winds?

Diluvian Winds lists DirectX 11 and/or OpenGL 3.3 compatible video card and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 and/or OpenGL 3.3 compatible video card with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Diluvian Winds at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 and/or OpenGL 3.3 compatible video card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Diluvian Winds need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
