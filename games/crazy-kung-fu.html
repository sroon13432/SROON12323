Hardware & Performance Analysis

Crazy Kung Fu asks for "AMD FX 8350" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
