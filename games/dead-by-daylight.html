Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dead by Daylight?

Dead by Daylight lists DX11 Compatible GeForce GTX 460 1GB or AMD HD 6850 1GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and DX11 Compatible GeForce 760 or AMD HD 8800 or higher with 4GB of RAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dead by Daylight at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DX11 Compatible GeForce 760 or AMD HD 8800 or higher with 4GB of RAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dead by Daylight need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
