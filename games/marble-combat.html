Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Marble Combat moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 2GB/AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050". Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (R9 270X bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Marble Combat?

Marble Combat lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 2GB/AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB or better and 4096 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon R9 270X with 8192 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Marble Combat at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon R9 270X. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Marble Combat need?

4096 MB of memory at minimum and 8192 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Marble Combat need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
