Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers inZOI moves from "NVIDIA RTX 2060 (6G VRAM), AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (6G VRAM)" to "NVIDIA RTX 3070 (8G VRAM), AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT (16GB VRAM)". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 77% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
