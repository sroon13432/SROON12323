Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Kujlevka moves from "GeForce GTX 650" to "Geforce GTX 1080". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Mid-range, 9.3 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
