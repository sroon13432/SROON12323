Hardware & Performance Analysis

Stronghold Crusader 2 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 8800GT 512MB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 1GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 3.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stronghold Crusader 2?

Stronghold Crusader 2 lists NVIDIA GeForce 8800GT 512MB or AMD Radeon HD 2900XT 512MB or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 1GB or AMD Radeon HD 5830 1GB or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stronghold Crusader 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 1GB or AMD Radeon HD 5830 1GB or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Stronghold Crusader 2 need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
