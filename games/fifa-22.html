Hardware & Performance Analysis

FIFA 22 asks for "NVIDIA GTX 660 2GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
