Can my PC run Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas – The Definitive Edition?

What GPU do I need for Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas – The Definitive Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 4GB / AMD Radeon RX 570 4GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
