Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Overwatch®?

Overwatch® lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 600 series, AMD Radeon™ HD 7000 series and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060/ GeForce® GTX 1650 or AMD R9 380/AMD RX 6400 or Intel® Arc™ A770 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Overwatch® at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060/ GeForce® GTX 1650 or AMD R9 380/AMD RX 6400 or Intel® Arc™ A770. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Overwatch® need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Overwatch® need?

50 GB available space.
