Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Culling 2?

The Culling 2 lists DX11 GPU with 1GB VRAM: GTX 750 / HD 7790 or equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and DX11 GPU with 1GB VRAM: GTX 750 / HD 7790 or equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Culling 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DX11 GPU with 1GB VRAM: GTX 750 / HD 7790 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Culling 2 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Culling 2 need?

8 GB available space.
