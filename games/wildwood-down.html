Wildwood Down lists NVIDIA Geforce GTS 450 / AMD Radeon HD 5570 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA Geforce GTX 750Ti / AMD Radeon R9 270x with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Wildwood Down at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA Geforce GTX 750Ti / AMD Radeon R9 270x. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
