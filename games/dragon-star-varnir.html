Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Dragon Star Varnir run from "ATI Mobility Radeon HD 5xxx, 1GB VRAM 5000" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, ATI Mobility Radeon R9 290". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 11 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Can my PC run Dragon Star Varnir?

Dragon Star Varnir lists ATI Mobility Radeon HD 5xxx, 1GB VRAM 5000 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, ATI Mobility Radeon R9 290 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dragon Star Varnir at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, ATI Mobility Radeon R9 290. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
