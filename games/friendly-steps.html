Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Friendly Steps runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, something like 53% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1.5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
