Hardware & Performance Analysis

RUNNING TRAIN | 走ル列車！ asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050M (4 GB VRAM)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (8 GB VRAM)" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
