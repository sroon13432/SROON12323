Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Kanon?

Kanon lists 512MB Video Memory and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1GB Video Memory with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Kanon at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1GB Video Memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Kanon need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Kanon need?

2 GB available space.
