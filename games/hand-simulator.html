Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hand Simulator?

Hand Simulator lists Intel HD 4000, GeForce 8800, ATI 1950 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Intel HD 4000, GeForce 8800, ATI 1950 or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hand Simulator at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel HD 4000, GeForce 8800, ATI 1950 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
