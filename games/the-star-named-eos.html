Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Star Named EOS?

The Star Named EOS lists 1GB RAM, OpenGL 3.3 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 4GB RAM, OpenGL 4.5 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Star Named EOS at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 4GB RAM, OpenGL 4.5. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
