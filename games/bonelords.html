Hardware & Performance Analysis

Bonelords asks for "GTX 1050ti (or equal)" at minimum and "RTX 2070 (or equal)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
