Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds?

Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1630, 4GB or AMD Radeon R9 380, 4GB or Intel Arc A380, 6GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT, 6 GB with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT, 6 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds need?

20 GB available space.
