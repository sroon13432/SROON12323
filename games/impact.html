Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for IMPACT list "GeForce 960": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

IMPACT lists GeForce 960 or equivalent with 4GB of video RAM and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 960 or equivalent with 4GB of video RAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for IMPACT at 60 FPS?
