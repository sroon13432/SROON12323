Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Midnight Monitor: Aldercourt moves from "Nvidia GTX 960" to "Nvidia GTX 1660". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, roughly 129% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
