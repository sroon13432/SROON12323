Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ashes of the Singularity: Escalation?

Ashes of the Singularity: Escalation lists 2 GB GDDR5 NVidia GeForce 660 / AMD R7 360 or better (GeForce 900+ / Radeon 290+ for Vulkan) and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and 4 GB GDDR5 NVidia GTX 970 / AMD R9 390 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ashes of the Singularity: Escalation at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 4 GB GDDR5 NVidia GTX 970 / AMD R9 390 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
