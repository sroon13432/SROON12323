Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dub Together asks for "Vulkan capable GPU with 1 GB, GeForce GTX 750" at minimum and "Vulkan capable GPU with 2 GB" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Can my PC run Dub Together?

What GPU do I need for Dub Together at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Vulkan capable GPU with 2 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
