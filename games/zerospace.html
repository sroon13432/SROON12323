Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for ZeroSpace runs from "Nvidia 1080 Ti" in the minimum column to "RTX 3060Ti" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, about 38% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run ZeroSpace?

ZeroSpace lists Nvidia 1080 Ti or RX 5700 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 3060Ti or RX 6800 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for ZeroSpace at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 3060Ti or RX 6800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does ZeroSpace need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
