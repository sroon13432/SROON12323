Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers GroundInFlames moves from "With support Vulkan 1.0" to "GTX 1650". Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 780 Ti bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
