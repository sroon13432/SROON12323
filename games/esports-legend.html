Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for eSports Legend runs from "Geforce 9600 GS, Radeon HD4000" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 260, Radeon HD 5770" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 3.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
