Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers WRC 4 FIA World Rally Championship moves from "NVIDIA® Geforce 8800 GT" to "NVIDIA® GeForce™ 9000 series". Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
