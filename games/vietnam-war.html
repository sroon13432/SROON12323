Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Vietnam War?

Vietnam War lists Integrated GPU and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidea or Radeon GPU Cards with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Vietnam War at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidea or Radeon GPU Cards. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Vietnam War need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

18 GB available space.
