Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Gothic 1 Remake moves from "8 GB VRAM, AMD RX 6700 XT" to "12 GB VRAM, Nvidia GeForce RTX 3070 Ti". Both rows sit in the Upper mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
