Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for DSS 2: War Industry list "128mb Video Memory, capable of Shader Model 3.0+": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 10 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
