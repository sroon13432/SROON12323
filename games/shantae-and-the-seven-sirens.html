Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Shantae and the Seven Sirens?

Shantae and the Seven Sirens lists AMD Radeon(TM) R7 250 or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon(TM) R7 250 or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Shantae and the Seven Sirens at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon(TM) R7 250 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Shantae and the Seven Sirens need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Shantae and the Seven Sirens need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
