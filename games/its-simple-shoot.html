Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Its Simple, SHOOT list "2047MB NVIDIA GeForce GT 750M": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 72 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
