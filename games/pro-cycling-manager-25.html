Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Pro Cycling Manager 25?

Pro Cycling Manager 25 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, 4GB or AMD Radeon RX 460, 4GB or Intel Arc A310, 4GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600, 6GB or Intel Arc A580, 8GB with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Pro Cycling Manager 25 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600, 6GB or Intel Arc A580, 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Pro Cycling Manager 25 need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

30 GB available space.
