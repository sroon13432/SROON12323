Hardware & Performance Analysis

Continent of the Ninth Golden asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GT730 Series" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 960" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, 6.4 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
