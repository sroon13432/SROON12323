Hardware & Performance Analysis

Chaser asks for "DirectX® 8.1 3D graphic card (32 MB)" at minimum and "DirectX® 8.1 3D graphic card (64 MB)" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1.35 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2010, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
