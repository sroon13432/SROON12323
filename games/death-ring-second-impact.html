Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Death Ring: Second Impact?

Death Ring: Second Impact lists NVDIA GTX1660 or AMD RX 6500 XT and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVDIA RTX2080 or RX 7600 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Death Ring: Second Impact at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVDIA RTX2080 or RX 7600. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Death Ring: Second Impact need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Death Ring: Second Impact need?

10 GB available space.
