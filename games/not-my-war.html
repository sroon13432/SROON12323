Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Not My War?

Not My War lists NVIDIA GTX 750 Ti / AMD R9 270 (2GB VRAM) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1650 / AMD RX 570 (4GB VRAM) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Not My War at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1650 / AMD RX 570 (4GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Not My War need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Not My War need?

2 GB available space.
