Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Trail: Frontier Challenge?

The Trail: Frontier Challenge lists GeForce 8500 GT and 1024 MB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 440 (1024MB) / Intel HD Graphics 530 (1792 MB) / Radeon HD 4570 (512MB) with 1024 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Trail: Frontier Challenge at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 440 (1024MB) / Intel HD Graphics 530 (1792 MB) / Radeon HD 4570 (512MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

1024 MB of memory at minimum and 1024 MB at recommended settings.
