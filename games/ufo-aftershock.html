Can my PC run UFO: Aftershock?

UFO: Aftershock lists nVidia® GeForceTM 5700 or ATI Radeon® 9500 and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and nVidia® GeForceTM 6600 or ATI Radeon® 9700 Pro with 768 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for UFO: Aftershock at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is nVidia® GeForceTM 6600 or ATI Radeon® 9700 Pro. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

512 MB of memory at minimum and 768 MB at recommended settings.
