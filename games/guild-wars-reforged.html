Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Guild Wars® Reforged?

Guild Wars® Reforged lists ATI Radeon 8500 or NVIDIA GeForce 3 Series Video Card with 64MB of VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and ATI Radeon 9600 or NVIDIA GeForce FX 5700 Series Video Card or above with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Guild Wars® Reforged at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is ATI Radeon 9600 or NVIDIA GeForce FX 5700 Series Video Card or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Guild Wars® Reforged need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Guild Wars® Reforged need?

4 GB available space.
