Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival runs from "AMD Radeon RX Vega 64 8GB, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660" in the minimum column to "AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT, NVIDIA RTX 3060" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a rise of roughly 47% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).
