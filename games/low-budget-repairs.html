Hardware & Performance Analysis

Low-Budget Repairs asks for "AMD Radeon RX 5700 8GB" at minimum and "AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT 12GB" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
