Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Max Manos are built around one card, "Integrated Graphics (with DirectX 11 compatibility)", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
