Hardware & Performance Analysis

Sugar Sweet Temptation asks for "TrueColor, 16bit" at minimum and "FullColor, 32bit" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sugar Sweet Temptation?

Sugar Sweet Temptation lists TrueColor, 16bit and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and FullColor, 32bit with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sugar Sweet Temptation at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is FullColor, 32bit. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sugar Sweet Temptation need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Sugar Sweet Temptation need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
