Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Beyond Space Remastered Edition?

Beyond Space Remastered Edition lists 256 MB OpenGL 3.2 or DirectX 10 compliant card and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 512 MB OpenGL 3.2 or DirectX 10 compliant card with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Beyond Space Remastered Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 512 MB OpenGL 3.2 or DirectX 10 compliant card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Beyond Space Remastered Edition need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Beyond Space Remastered Edition need?

2 GB available space.
