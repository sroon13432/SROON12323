Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Mizeria moves from "Intel UHD graphics 620" to "GeForce RTX 3050 - 8GB". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 22.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
