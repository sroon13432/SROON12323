Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Disney Planes list "256 MB DirectX® 9.0c-compatible (NVIDIA® GeForce 6600)": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Disney Planes?

Disney Planes lists 256 MB DirectX® 9.0c-compatible (NVIDIA® GeForce 6600 or better, ATI Radeon™ X800 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 256 MB DirectX® 9.0c-compatible (NVIDIA® GeForce 6600 or better, ATI Radeon™ X800 or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Disney Planes at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 256 MB DirectX® 9.0c-compatible (NVIDIA® GeForce 6600 or better, ATI Radeon™ X800 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Disney Planes need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
