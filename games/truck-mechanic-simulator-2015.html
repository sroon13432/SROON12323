Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Truck Mechanic Simulator 2015?

Truck Mechanic Simulator 2015 lists GeForce 9800GT or better w /512MB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 650 Ti or better w/ 1024MB VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Truck Mechanic Simulator 2015 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 650 Ti or better w/ 1024MB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Truck Mechanic Simulator 2015 need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Truck Mechanic Simulator 2015 need?

2 GB available space.
