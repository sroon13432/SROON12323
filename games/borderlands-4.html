Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Borderlands 4 runs from "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is about 45% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 100 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
