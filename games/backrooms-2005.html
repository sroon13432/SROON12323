Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Backrooms - 2005 runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeFroce RTX 3060 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 5.8 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
