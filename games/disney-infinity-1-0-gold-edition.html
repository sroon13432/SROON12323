Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition run from "Nvidia GeForce 8600 GT 512" to "Nvidia GeForce 960 GTX 2 GB". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition?

Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition lists Nvidia GeForce 8600 GT 512 / ATI Radeon HD 4650 1 GB and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce 960 GTX 2 GB with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce 960 GTX 2 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Disney Infinity 1.0: Gold Edition need?
