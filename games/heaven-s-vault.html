Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Heaven's Vault list "Nvidia Geforce GTX 660": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Can my PC run Heaven's Vault?

What GPU do I need for Heaven's Vault at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia Geforce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850, 2+ Gb of vram. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
