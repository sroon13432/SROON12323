Hardware & Performance Analysis

Jolly Battle names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Jolly Battle?

Jolly Battle lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Jolly Battle at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
