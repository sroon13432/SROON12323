Hardware & Performance Analysis

Kusan: City of Wolves asks for "GeForce GTX 660, 2 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, 4 GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, 4 GB or AMD Radeon RX 590, 8 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
