Hardware & Performance Analysis

SCARLET NEXUS asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 760, 2 GB" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, 4 GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 121% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
