Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Precinct?

The Precinct lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 660Ti / AMD Radeon R7 370 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Precinct at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Precinct need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
