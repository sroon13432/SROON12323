Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Mass Effect™ 3 N7 Digital Deluxe Edition (2012) run from "Supported chipsets: NVIDIA 7900" to "AMD/ATI Radeon HD 4850 512 MB". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
