Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Elements Divided runs from "NVIDIA GTX 970" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 1080" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, 100% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
