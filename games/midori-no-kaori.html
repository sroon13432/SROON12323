Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Midori no Kaori?

Midori no Kaori lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850 with 2GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850 with 2GB VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Midori no Kaori at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850 with 2GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Midori no Kaori need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Midori no Kaori need?

5 GB available space.
