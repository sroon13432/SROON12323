Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dandy Ace?

Dandy Ace lists Nvidia 450 GTS / Radeon HD 5750 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 460 / Radeon HD 7800 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dandy Ace at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 460 / Radeon HD 7800 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dandy Ace need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dandy Ace need?

3 GB available space.
