Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Chipmatic ("NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" and "NVIDIA GeForce GT 1030") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Chipmatic?

Chipmatic lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon 760M and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GT 1030 / AMD RX 550 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Chipmatic at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GT 1030 / AMD RX 550. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Chipmatic need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Chipmatic need?

2 GB available space.
