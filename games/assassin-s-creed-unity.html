Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Assassin's Creed® Unity moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780". Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Assassin's Creed® Unity?

Assassin's Creed® Unity lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680 or AMD Radeon HD 7970 (2 GB VRAM) and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 or AMD Radeon R9 290X (3 GB VRAM) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Assassin's Creed® Unity at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 or AMD Radeon R9 290X (3 GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Assassin's Creed® Unity need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
