Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Collective: the Community Created Card Game?

Collective: the Community Created Card Game lists NVIDIA® GeForce 8800 or ATI Radeon® X1900 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce 8800 or ATI Radeon® X1900 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Collective: the Community Created Card Game at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce 8800 or ATI Radeon® X1900. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Collective: the Community Created Card Game need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
