Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Three Kingdoms : Alias?

Three Kingdoms : Alias lists Intel HD 4400 / GeForce GT 730 / Radeon R5 230 and 4 MB RAM as its minimum, and Intel UHD 630 / GeForce GTX 750 Ti / Radeon RX 560 with 8 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Three Kingdoms : Alias at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel UHD 630 / GeForce GTX 750 Ti / Radeon RX 560. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Three Kingdoms : Alias need?

4 MB of memory at minimum and 8 MB at recommended settings.
