Can my PC run Dying Light: The Beast?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 TI / AMD Radeon 6750 XT / Intel ARC B580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
