Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Ancient Ruins are built around one card, "GPU with at least 512MB of VRAM", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ancient Ruins?

Ancient Ruins lists GPU with at least 512MB of VRAM and 2 MB RAM as its minimum, and GPU with at least 512MB of VRAM with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ancient Ruins at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GPU with at least 512MB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ancient Ruins need?

2 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Ancient Ruins need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
