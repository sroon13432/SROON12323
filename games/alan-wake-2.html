Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Alan Wake 2 moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1070" to "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060". Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1440p, Medium @ 30 FPS realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p, Low @ 30 FPS.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. Storage is 90 GB and the requirement asks for an SSD specifically, so a hard drive is out of the question here.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

What GPU do I need for Alan Wake 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1440p, Medium @ 30 FPS.
