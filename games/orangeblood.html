Hardware & Performance Analysis

Orangeblood names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
