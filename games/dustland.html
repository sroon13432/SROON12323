Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dustland asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 8GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 78% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dustland?

Dustland lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB / AMD Radeon RX 580 8GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 8GB / AMD Radeon RX 5700 8GB with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dustland at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 8GB / AMD Radeon RX 5700 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dustland need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.
