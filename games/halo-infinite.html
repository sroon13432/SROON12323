Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Halo Infinite moves from "AMD RX 570" to "Radeon RX 5700 XT". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of about 169%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
