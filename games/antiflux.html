Hardware & Performance Analysis

Antiflux names the same GPU, "Intel HD 4000 integrated", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
