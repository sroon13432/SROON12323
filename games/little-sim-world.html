Can my PC run Little Sim World?

Little Sim World lists AMD Radeon RX 570, NVIDIA GTX 1650 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT, NVIDIA RTX 3060 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Little Sim World at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon RX 6600 XT, NVIDIA RTX 3060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
