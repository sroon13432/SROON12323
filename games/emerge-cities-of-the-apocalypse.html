Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Emerge: Cities of the Apocalypse?

Emerge: Cities of the Apocalypse lists 256 MB VRAM (nVidia GeForce 8800 GT / ATi Radeon HD 3870 or better) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 256 MB VRAM (nVidia GeForce 8800 GT / ATi Radeon HD 3870 or better) with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is 256 MB VRAM (nVidia GeForce 8800 GT / ATi Radeon HD 3870 or better). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
