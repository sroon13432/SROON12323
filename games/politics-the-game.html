Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Politics The Game are built around one card, "Radeon HD 5000+", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
