Hardware & Performance Analysis

EA SPORTS™ Madden NFL 26 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti 4GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 8GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, roughly 175% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 10 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 65 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
