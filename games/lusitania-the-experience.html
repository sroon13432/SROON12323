Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Lusitania: The Experience are built around one card, "At least 2GB of video card memory", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it. As a casual title it is built to run on ordinary hardware, and the requirement is written for exactly that audience.
