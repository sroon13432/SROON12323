Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dungeons 2?

Dungeons 2 lists Intel HD4400, NVIDIA GeForce GT 440/GT 650M, AMD Radeon HD 7750/R5 255M and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon R7 265 or NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dungeons 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon R7 265 or NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dungeons 2 need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dungeons 2 need?

5 GB available space.
