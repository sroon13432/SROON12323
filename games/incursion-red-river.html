Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Incursion Red River runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForcc RTX 2080" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 72% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
