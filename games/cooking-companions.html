Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Cooking Companions runs from "Integrated graphics" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 2080 Ti 11GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Upper mid-range, 11.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
