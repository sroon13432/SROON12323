Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Night Drive: คืนหลอน ซ่อนทาง moves from "NVIDIA GTX 1050" to "Nvidia GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, roughly 185% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 451 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
