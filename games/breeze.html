Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Breeze?

Breeze lists IGP or better and 4 MB RAM as its minimum, and IGP or better with 4 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Breeze at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is IGP or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Breeze need?

4 MB of memory at minimum and 4 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Breeze need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
