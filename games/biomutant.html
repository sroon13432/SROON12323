Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers BIOMUTANT moves from "4 GB Direct3D 11 capable video card - GeForce GTX 960" to "6 GB Direct3D 11 capable video card - GeForce GTX 1660Ti". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, something like 71% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
