Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ad Infinitum asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti, 2 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060, 12 GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Mid-range, 5.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 21 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ad Infinitum?

Ad Infinitum lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti, 2 GB or AMD Radeon RX 7850, 2 GB or Intel Arc A380, 6 GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060, 12 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6650 XT, 8 GB with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ad Infinitum at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060, 12 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6650 XT, 8 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
