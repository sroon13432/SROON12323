Ultimate Auto-RPG: Tower Walker

★ Mostly Positive (74% of 67 reviews) 💾 3 GB available space

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ultimate Auto-RPG: Tower Walker?

Ultimate Auto-RPG: Tower Walker lists DirectX® 9 Compatible Graphics Card and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX® 9 Compatible Graphics Card with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ultimate Auto-RPG: Tower Walker at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX® 9 Compatible Graphics Card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ultimate Auto-RPG: Tower Walker need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
