Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Pure Pool?

Pure Pool lists NVIDIA GeForce 8800GT or AMD Radeon HD 3830 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or AMD Radeon HD 7750 with 3 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Pure Pool at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or AMD Radeon HD 7750. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 3 GB at recommended settings.
