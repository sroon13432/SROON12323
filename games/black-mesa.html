Hardware & Performance Analysis

Black Mesa asks for "2 GB Dedicated Video Card" at minimum and "4 GB Dedicated Video Card" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Can my PC run Black Mesa?

Black Mesa lists 2 GB Dedicated Video Card or Greater and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and 4 GB Dedicated Video Card or Greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is 4 GB Dedicated Video Card or Greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
