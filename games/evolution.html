Evolution

★ Mixed (49% of 113 reviews) 💾 500 MB available space

Evolution is a real time simulation of the evolutionary process. Creatures have DNA that defines their structure, traits, how they see the world around them, and how they react to it. Each creature is unique, most die quickly, some die a little slower, and others survive (some just like rocks, they usually die too).

🚀 Recommended GPU: GeForce 8800+

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Evolution?

Evolution lists GeForce 8800+ and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 8800+ with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Evolution at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 8800+. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Evolution need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Evolution need?

100 MB available space.
