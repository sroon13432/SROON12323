Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Chess Ultra moves from "NVIDIA Geforce GTS 450" to "NVIDIA Gerforce GTX 780". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, about 86% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
