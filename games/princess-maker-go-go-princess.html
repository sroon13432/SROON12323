Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Princess Maker Go!Go! Princess?

Princess Maker Go!Go! Princess lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 or AMD Radeon HD5850 (1 GB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 or AMD Radeon HD 7970 or better (2 GB VRAM) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Princess Maker Go!Go! Princess at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 or AMD Radeon HD 7970 or better (2 GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Princess Maker Go!Go! Princess need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

2 GB available space.
