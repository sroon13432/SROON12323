Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Theseus Protocol runs from "Intel HD 4600(AMD)" in the minimum column to "GTX 970(Dedicated 4GB AMD)" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, 10.5 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
