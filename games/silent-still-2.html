Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Silent Still 2 moves from "GTX 1050 ti" to "GTX 1080 ti". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, a jump of 3.1 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (GTX 1080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
