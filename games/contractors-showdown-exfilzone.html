Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Contractors Showdown : ExfilZone moves from "NVIDIA GTX 1070" to "NVIDIA RTX 3060". Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
