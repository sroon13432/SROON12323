Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Little Dungeon Stories moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GT 240" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 3.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
