Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Subliminal moves from "Nvidia GTX 1060" to "Nvidia RTX 3060". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 76% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 10 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
