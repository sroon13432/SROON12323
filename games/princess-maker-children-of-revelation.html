Hardware & Performance Analysis

Princess Maker: Children of Revelation names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 260, ATI Radeon 4870", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

What GPU do I need for Princess Maker: Children of Revelation at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 260, ATI Radeon 4870. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
