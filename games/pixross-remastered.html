Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Pixross Remastered are built around one card, "NVIDIA GeForce 450", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
