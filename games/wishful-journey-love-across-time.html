Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Wishful Journey:Love Across Time?

Wishful Journey:Love Across Time lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 780 or AMD HD 7950 with 3GB VRAM minimum (Support of Vulkan 1.1 required) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580 with 4GB VRAM minimum (Support of Vulkan 1.1 required) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Wishful Journey:Love Across Time at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580 with 4GB VRAM minimum (Support of Vulkan 1.1 required). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Wishful Journey:Love Across Time need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

30 GB available space.
