Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Call of Duty®: Advanced Warfare - Gold Edition runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTS 450 @ 1GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760 @ 4GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, about 36% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 55 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
