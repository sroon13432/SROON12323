Hardware & Performance Analysis

Z1 Battle Royale asks for "nVidia GeForce GTX 460 series" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
