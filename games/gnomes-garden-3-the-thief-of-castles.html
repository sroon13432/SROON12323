Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gnomes Garden 3: The thief of castles names the same GPU, "Intel GMA 3150", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
