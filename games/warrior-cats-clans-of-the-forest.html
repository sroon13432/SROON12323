Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest runs from "GTX 1060 (6GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (8GB)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, roughly 176% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
