Hardware & Performance Analysis

Emissary Zero asks for "NVIDIA GEFORCE GTX 750 Ti" at minimum and "NVIDIA GEFORCE GTX 1070 8 GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 3.2 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
