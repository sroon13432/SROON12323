Hardware & Performance Analysis

Modern Warships asks for "Compatible with DirectX 10.1: AMD Radeon HD 77XX" at minimum and "Compatible with DirectX 11: AMD Radeon HD 87XX" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.6 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 7 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 13 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
