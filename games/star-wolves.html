Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Star Wolves?

Star Wolves lists NVIDIA GeForce 6600 128MB or ATI Radeon 9800 128MB and 512MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 7800 512MB or ATI Radeon 1900XT 512MB Sound: Sound card with multi-channel sound support 5.1 with 1 GB or more RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Star Wolves at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 7800 512MB or ATI Radeon 1900XT 512MB Sound: Sound card with multi-channel sound support 5.1. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Star Wolves need?

512MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB or more at recommended settings.
