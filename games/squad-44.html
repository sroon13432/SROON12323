Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Squad 44?

Squad 44 lists Nvidia GTX 1080 or Radeon RX 6600 with at least 8GB of VRAM and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 3070 or Radeon 6800 with at least 8GB of VRAM with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Squad 44 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 3070 or Radeon 6800 with at least 8GB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Squad 44 need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Squad 44 need?

100 GB available space.
