Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Mafia III: Definitive Edition runs from "2GB of Video Memory & NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660, AMD Radeon HD7870" in the minimum column to "4GB of Video Memory & NVIDIA Geforce GTX 780" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, something like 86% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
