Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Townsmen?

Townsmen lists DirectX 9.0c compatible and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 9.0c compatible with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Townsmen at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 9.0c compatible. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Townsmen need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
