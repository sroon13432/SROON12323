Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Galaxy Burger are built around one card, "Nvidia GeForce 8600 GS", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 748 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
