StarCraft II

💾 30 GB available space ⚙️ StarCraft II engine

StarCraft II is Blizzard's real-time strategy game, free to play and installed through Battle.net rather than Steam. Its Battle.net specification still runs on modest hardware: Windows 10 with a Core 2 Duo or Athlon 64 X2 5600+, a GeForce 7600 GT, Radeon HD 2600 XT or Intel HD 3000 and 2 GB of RAM at minimum, and Windows 10 64-bit with a Core i5 or AMD FX CPU, a GTX 650 or HD 7790 and 4 GB of RAM when recommended, with 30 GB of storage either way.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790
Target: 60 FPS

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    StarCraft II is CPU-limited in large fights: the recommended row raises the processor more than the graphics card, so a faster CPU helps late-game frame rate most.
  • 💡
    In team games the unit count multiplies, so lower the graphics preset rather than the resolution to keep the interface readable at 1080p.
  • 💡
    The 30 GB figure covers the full game with all campaigns; the multiplayer client alone can be installed smaller from Battle.net.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run StarCraft II?

StarCraft II lists NVIDIA GeForce 7600 GT / ATI Radeon HD 2600 XT / Intel HD Graphics 3000 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for StarCraft II at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.

How much RAM does StarCraft II need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does StarCraft II need?

30 GB available space.
