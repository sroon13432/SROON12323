Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Hatsune Miku: Project DIVA Mega Mix+ moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 550 Ti, 1 GB" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 750 Ti, 2 GB". On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, 3.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
