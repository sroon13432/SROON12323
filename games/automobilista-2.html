Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Automobilista 2 moves from "GTX680" to "NVidia GTX 1080Ti". On our relative index that is Low-end to Upper mid-range, 4.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 120 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (GTX 1080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
