Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Task Force?

Task Force lists Geforce GTX 1060 or Radeon RX 580 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce RTX 3070 or Radeon RX 6700 XT with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Task Force at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce RTX 3070 or Radeon RX 6700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Task Force need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Task Force need?

35 GB available space.
