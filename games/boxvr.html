Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for BOXVR are built around one card, "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 901 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run BOXVR?

BOXVR lists NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970 / AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or greater and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970 / AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or greater with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for BOXVR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970 / AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does BOXVR need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
