Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Rejected Draft are built around one card, "Any integrated graphics card with WebGL support", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
