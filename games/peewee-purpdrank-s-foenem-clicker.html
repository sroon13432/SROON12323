Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for PeeWee Purpdrank's FOENEM Clicker are built around one card, "OpenGL 2.1 compatible graphics card, integrated graphics", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
