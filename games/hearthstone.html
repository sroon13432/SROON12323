Hearthstone

💾 12 GB available space ⚙️ Unity

Hearthstone is Blizzard's free-to-play card game, installed through Battle.net and not sold on Steam. The Unity engine move in August 2026 raised the official minimum to Windows 10 64-bit version 21H1 with a Core i3-6100 or A10-7850K, a GTX 660 or R9 280X and 8 GB of RAM; the recommended row lists a Core i5-10400F or Ryzen 7 1800X with 16 GB and a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 570, and both need 12 GB of disk space.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 570
Target: 60 FPS

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Since the Unity update the game no longer runs on Windows 7/8 or 32-bit Windows, so an older OS has to move to Windows 10 64-bit 21H1 first.
  • 💡
    Hearthstone is not frame-rate sensitive; the 8 GB memory minimum matters more, because deck trackers and browsers compete for the same RAM.
  • 💡
    The published install figure is 12 GB, so a small SSD still needs that much free before the Battle.net update can run.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hearthstone?

Hearthstone lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon R9 280X and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 570 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hearthstone at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 570. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.

How much RAM does Hearthstone need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Hearthstone need?

12 GB available space.
