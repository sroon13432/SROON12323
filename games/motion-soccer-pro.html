Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Motion Soccer PRO?

Motion Soccer PRO lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 970/AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or greater and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970/AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Motion Soccer PRO at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970/AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Motion Soccer PRO need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
