Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Super Pilot?

Super Pilot lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or equivalent and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 or equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Super Pilot at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Super Pilot need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Super Pilot need?

500 MB available space.
