Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Idle Demon Lord II list "Nvidia GTX 750": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
