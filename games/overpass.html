Hardware & Performance Analysis

OVERPASS™ asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 560 ou AMD Radeon HD 6870" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 3 Go ou Radeon RX 580 4 Go" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 7.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
