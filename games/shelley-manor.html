Hardware & Performance Analysis

Shelley Manor asks for "GTX1060" at minimum and "GTX2060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 72% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
