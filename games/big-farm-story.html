Hardware & Performance Analysis

Big Farm Story names the same GPU, "512MB", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1004 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
