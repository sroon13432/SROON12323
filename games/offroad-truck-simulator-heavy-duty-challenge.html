Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Offroad Truck Simulator: Heavy Duty Challenge® runs from "Nvidia GTX 1070 Ti" in the minimum column to "NVidia RTX 2080" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 50% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
