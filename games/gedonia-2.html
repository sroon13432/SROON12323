Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gedonia 2 asks for "Nvidia GTX960 4GB" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX2060 8GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.1 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 18 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Gedonia 2?

Gedonia 2 lists Nvidia GTX960 4GB or AMD 380X 4GB or Higher and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX2060 8GB or AMD 5700 8GB or Higher with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gedonia 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX2060 8GB or AMD 5700 8GB or Higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Gedonia 2 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Gedonia 2 need?

18 GB available space.
