Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for 港詭實錄ParanormalHK runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX1060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 163%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
