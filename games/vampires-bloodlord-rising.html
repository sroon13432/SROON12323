Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Vampires: Bloodlord Rising moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070, 8 GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Ti, 8 GB". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 100%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6700 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
