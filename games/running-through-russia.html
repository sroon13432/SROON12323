Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Running Through Russia moves from "Intel HD Graphics" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 28.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
