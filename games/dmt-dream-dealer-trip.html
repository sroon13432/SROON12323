Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for DMT: Dream Dealer Trip list "DX9 (modelo de shader 3.0) o DX11 con capacidades de funciones de nivel 9.3": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
