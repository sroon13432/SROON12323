Sudden Attack

💾 40 GB available space ⚙️ Nexon proprietary engine

Sudden Attack (서든어택) is Nexon's long-running free-to-play FPS and a fixture of Korean PC rooms, installed from sa.nexon.com and not sold on Steam. Nexon's official guide (last updated June 2026) asks for Windows 10 or newer, a Core 2 Duo E6300, a GeForce 7600 GT, 2 GB of RAM and 40 GB of storage at minimum, and a Core i5-2500 with 8 GB and a GeForce 9600 GT when recommended, on DirectX 9 or newer.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce 9600 GT
Target: 60 FPS
