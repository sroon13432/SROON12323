Hardware & Performance Analysis

MudRunner VR asks for "GTX 1060" at minimum and "RTX 3080/ RX 6800 XT - 10GB RAM" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of 3.1 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RX 7800 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
