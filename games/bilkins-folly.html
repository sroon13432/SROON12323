Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bilkins' Folly runs from "GeForce GT 640 (2048 MB)" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 660 (2048 MB)" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is roughly 133% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 799 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.
