Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run OutDrive?

OutDrive lists GTX560 and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and GTX560 with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for OutDrive at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GTX560. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does OutDrive need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
