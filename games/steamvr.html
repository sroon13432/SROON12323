Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for SteamVR runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run SteamVR?

SteamVR lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for SteamVR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does SteamVR need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does SteamVR need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
