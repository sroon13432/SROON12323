Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Monster Girls and the Mysterious Adventure 2?

Monster Girls and the Mysterious Adventure 2 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX1050 or over and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 or over with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Monster Girls and the Mysterious Adventure 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 or over. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Monster Girls and the Mysterious Adventure 2 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Monster Girls and the Mysterious Adventure 2 need?

4 GB available space.
