Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for F1® 25 runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1060 (6GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 2070 (+VR)" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 53% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 100 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
