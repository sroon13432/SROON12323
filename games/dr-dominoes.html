Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for DR Dominoes list "1 GB": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
