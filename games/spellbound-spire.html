Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Spellbound Spire moves from "NVIDIA GTX 970/ AMD Radeon RX 480" to "NVIDIA GTX 1060". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Spellbound Spire?

Spellbound Spire lists NVIDIA GTX 970/ AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Spellbound Spire at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
