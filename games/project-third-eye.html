Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Project Third Eye are built around one card, "NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti/AMD Radeon RX 470", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
