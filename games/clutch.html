Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Clutch?

Clutch lists 9.0s DirectX-compatible video card with 128 MB of memory, supports shader model 2 and 512 МB RAM as its minimum, and 9.0s DirectX-compatible video card with 128 MB of memory, supports shader model 2 with 512 МB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Clutch at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 9.0s DirectX-compatible video card with 128 MB of memory, supports shader model 2. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Clutch need?

512 МB of memory at minimum and 512 МB at recommended settings.
