Hardware & Performance Analysis

Succubus x Saint asks for "DirectX 9/OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU" at minimum and "OpenGL ES 2.0 hardware driver support required for WebGL acceleration. (AMD Catalyst 10.9…)" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
