Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Smart Farm?

Smart Farm lists GTX 1080TI and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 2080 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Smart Farm at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 2080. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Smart Farm need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

6 GB available space.
