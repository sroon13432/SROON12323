Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Dying Light: Bad Blood runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 560" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 780" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 4.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
