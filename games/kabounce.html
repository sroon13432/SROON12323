Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Kabounce?

Kabounce lists Intel(R) HD Graphics 4600 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD SAPPHIRE Radeon R9 280X Series equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Kabounce at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD SAPPHIRE Radeon R9 280X Series equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Kabounce need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Kabounce need?

4 GB available space.
