Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Space Battle Royale?

Space Battle Royale lists NVIDIA GTX 1050 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1650ti with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Space Battle Royale at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1650ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Space Battle Royale need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Space Battle Royale need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
