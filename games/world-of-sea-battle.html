Hardware & Performance Analysis

World of Sea Battle asks for "GeForce GTX 460 1GB" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 660 2GB" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is roughly 133% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run World of Sea Battle?

World of Sea Battle lists GeForce GTX 460 1GB or AMD Radeon HD 6850 1GB (must support shader 5 model) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 660 2GB or AMD Radeon HD 7850 2GB (must support shader 5 model) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for World of Sea Battle at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 660 2GB or AMD Radeon HD 7850 2GB (must support shader 5 model). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does World of Sea Battle need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

2 GB available space.
