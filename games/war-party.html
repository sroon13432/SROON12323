Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run WAR PARTY?

WAR PARTY lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 550 or ATI™ Radeon™ HD 6XXX or higher and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 or AMD R9 280 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for WAR PARTY at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 or AMD R9 280. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does WAR PARTY need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

1000 MB available space.
