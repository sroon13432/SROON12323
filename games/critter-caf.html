Hardware & Performance Analysis

Critter Café asks for "NVIDIA GEFORCE GTX 960" at minimum and "NVIDIA GEFORCE RTX 1070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 93%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
