Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Sovereign Elect are built around one card, "OpenGL 2.0 compatible video card with 256 MB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 604 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
