Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Killer: Infected One of Us moves from "GTX 750 ti 2GB" to "GTX 1050 2GB". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a rise of roughly 53% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

The recommended GPU is GTX 1050 2GB or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Killer: Infected One of Us need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
