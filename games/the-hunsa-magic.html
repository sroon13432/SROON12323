Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Hunsa Magic runs from "GeForce GTX 660" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1050Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a jump of about 129%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest.
