Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Onigiri Shop Simulator list "Nvidia Geforce GTX 650": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
