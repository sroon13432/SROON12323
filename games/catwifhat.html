Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Catwifhat ("Nvidia GeForce 400" and "GeForce 700") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Catwifhat?

Catwifhat lists Nvidia GeForce 400 / AMD Radeon HD 7000 and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 700 / AMD Radeon Rx 200 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Catwifhat at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 700 / AMD Radeon Rx 200. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

1 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
