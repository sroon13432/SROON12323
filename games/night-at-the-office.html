Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Night at the Office?

Night at the Office lists GTX 960 or slightly lower and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GTX 970 or slightly lower with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Night at the Office at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GTX 970 or slightly lower. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Night at the Office need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Night at the Office need?

5 GB available space.
