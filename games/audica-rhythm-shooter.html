Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run AUDICA: Rhythm Shooter?

AUDICA: Rhythm Shooter lists NVIDIA GTX 960 4GB / AMD Radeon R9 290 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for AUDICA: Rhythm Shooter at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does AUDICA: Rhythm Shooter need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does AUDICA: Rhythm Shooter need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
