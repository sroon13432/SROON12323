Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dead Rising 2: Off the Record?

Dead Rising 2: Off the Record lists NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800GTS or better, ATI Radeon™ HD 3850 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800GTS or better, ATI Radeon™ HD 3850 or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dead Rising 2: Off the Record at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800GTS or better, ATI Radeon™ HD 3850 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dead Rising 2: Off the Record need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
