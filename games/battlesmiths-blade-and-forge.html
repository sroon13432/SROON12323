Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Battlesmiths: Blade and Forge runs from "NV GeForce 8600 GT" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 66.7 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
