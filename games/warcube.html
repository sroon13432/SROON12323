Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Warcube?

Warcube lists 512MB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1GB VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warcube at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warcube need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

1500 MB available space.
