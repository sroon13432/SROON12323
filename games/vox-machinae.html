Hardware & Performance Analysis

Vox Machinae asks for "GPU with 2GB Memory" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 970 4GB" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Vox Machinae lists GPU with 2GB Memory and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 970 4GB / AMD R9 290 4GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Vox Machinae at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 970 4GB / AMD R9 290 4GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
