Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Sintopia moves from "RTX 2060 SUPER (8 GB)" to "RTX 4080 (8GB)". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and High-end, roughly 100% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is high-end territory (RTX 5070 Ti bracket), where the requirement says more about the game's ceiling than about the card you need to buy. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sintopia?

Sintopia lists RTX 2060 SUPER (8 GB) / RX 5700 XT (8 GB) and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 4080 (8GB) / RX 7900 XT (16GB) with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sintopia at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RTX 4080 (8GB) / RX 7900 XT (16GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sintopia need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Sintopia need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
