Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Outer Worlds: Spacer's Choice Edition runs from "GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1080 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, 108% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 62 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (GTX 1080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
