Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Alek - The Lost Kingdom?

Alek - The Lost Kingdom lists AMD Radeon HD 7750, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 or Equivalent and 8 MB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon HD 7750, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 or Equivalent with 8 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Alek - The Lost Kingdom at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon HD 7750, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 or Equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Alek - The Lost Kingdom need?

8 MB of memory at minimum and 8 MB at recommended settings.
