Hardware & Performance Analysis

It's A Wipe! names the same GPU, "Any Directx8 OpenGL Compatible card", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 246 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

The recommended GPU is Any Directx8 OpenGL Compatible card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
