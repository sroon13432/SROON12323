Can my PC run Toree 3D?

Toree 3D lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Toree 3D at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
