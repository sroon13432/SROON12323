Hardware & Performance Analysis

Bunhouse asks for "GTX 1050 (you probably can't run this on your momma's generic laptop)" at minimum and "RTX 2060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 3.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
