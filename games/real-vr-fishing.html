Hardware & Performance Analysis

Real VR Fishing names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 980", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
