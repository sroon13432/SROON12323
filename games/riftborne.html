Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Riftborne?

Riftborne lists Integrated graphics OK (DirectX 11-capable recommended for compatibility) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Integrated graphics OK with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Riftborne at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Integrated graphics OK. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Riftborne need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Riftborne need?

250 MB available space.
