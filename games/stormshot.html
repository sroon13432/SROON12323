Hardware & Performance Analysis

Stormshot names the same GPU, "Nvidia GeForce GTX 460", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stormshot?

Stormshot lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 460 or Radeon HD 4400 or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 460 or Radeon HD 4400 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stormshot at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 460 or Radeon HD 4400 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
