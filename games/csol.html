Hardware & Performance Analysis

CSOL lists "gtx 1050" at minimum and "gtx 2050" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (RX 460 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
