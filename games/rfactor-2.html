Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for rFactor 2 runs from "nVidia GeForce GTX 950" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 137%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Can my PC run rFactor 2?

rFactor 2 lists nVidia GeForce GTX 950 or AMD Radeon RX 550 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for rFactor 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
