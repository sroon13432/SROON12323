Hardware & Performance Analysis

Pax Autocratica asks for "1080p: Nvidia® GTX 1060 6G" at minimum and "Nvidia® GTX 2060 Super" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, something like 87% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
