Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Banner of the Maid?

Banner of the Maid lists Support 1280 x 720 or higher resolution, 512MB or above and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Support 1280 x 720 or higher resolution, 512MB or above with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Banner of the Maid at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Support 1280 x 720 or higher resolution, 512MB or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Banner of the Maid need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Banner of the Maid need?

2 GB available space.
