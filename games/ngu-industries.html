Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for NGU INDUSTRIES run from "Yes there are graphics, sometimes they even move. A toaster can display them probably" to "65,535 Bitcoin miners running in parrallel". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 2 GB at minimum against 69 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 420 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
