Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Cralon runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA Geforce RTX 4060" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 88% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 4060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cralon?

Cralon lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA Geforce RTX 4060 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cralon at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA Geforce RTX 4060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cralon need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Cralon need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
