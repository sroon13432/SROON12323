Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for One Finger Death Punch are built around one card, "512 MB VRAM with Shader Model 3.0 support", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

The recommended GPU is 512 MB VRAM with Shader Model 3.0 support and needs a directX 10 capable video card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
