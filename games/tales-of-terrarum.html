Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Tales of Terrarum list "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
