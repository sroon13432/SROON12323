Can my PC run Aquaculture Land: Fish Farming Simulation?

Aquaculture Land: Fish Farming Simulation lists OpenGL 3.0 compliant with 512MB of video RAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and OpenGL 3.0 compliant with 1.0GB of video RAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is OpenGL 3.0 compliant with 1.0GB of video RAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
