Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run CONTROL Resonant?

CONTROL Resonant lists Geforce GTX 1070 / Radeon RX 5600 XT / Intel Arc A580 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce RTX 3060 Ti / Radeon RX 6700 XT / Intel Arc B580 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for CONTROL Resonant at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce RTX 3060 Ti / Radeon RX 6700 XT / Intel Arc B580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does CONTROL Resonant need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does CONTROL Resonant need?

120 GB available space.
