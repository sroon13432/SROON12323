Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for CONTROL Resonant runs from "Geforce GTX 1070" in the minimum column to "Geforce RTX 3060 Ti" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is something like 45% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. At 120 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
