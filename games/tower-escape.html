Tower Escape

★ Very Positive (83% of 365 reviews) 💾 200 MB available space

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tower Escape?

Tower Escape lists Integrated Graphics and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Integrated Graphics with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tower Escape at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Integrated Graphics. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tower Escape need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
