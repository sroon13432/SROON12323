HELLBREAK lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 570 and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for HELLBREAK at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
