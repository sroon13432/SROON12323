Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Atlas Fallen: Reign Of Sand moves from "4 GB VRAM, GTX 1050 Ti/RX 470" to "8 GB VRAM, GTX 1070 Ti/RX 5700". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 150% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
