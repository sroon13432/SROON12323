Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Hardspace: Shipbreaker runs from "4 GB VRAM, GeForce GTX 770" in the minimum column to "6 GB VRAM, GeForce GTX 980 Ti" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 145%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
