Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Batman: The Enemy Within - The Telltale Series?

Batman: The Enemy Within - The Telltale Series lists Nvidia GTS 450+ with 1024MB+ VRAM (excluding GT) - LATEST DRIVERS REQUIRED and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTS 450+ with 1024MB+ VRAM (excluding GT) - LATEST DRIVERS REQUIRED with 3 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Batman: The Enemy Within - The Telltale Series at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTS 450+ with 1024MB+ VRAM (excluding GT) - LATEST DRIVERS REQUIRED. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Batman: The Enemy Within - The Telltale Series need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 3 GB at recommended settings.
