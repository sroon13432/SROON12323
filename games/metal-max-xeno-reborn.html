Hardware & Performance Analysis

METAL MAX Xeno Reborn asks for "Intel Core i5-2500K 3.3GHz" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 960" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
