Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Fire Burning City are built around one card, "NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 1.95 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
