Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Orebound run from "1050ti" to "Geforce 980ti". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Orebound?

Orebound lists 1050ti or higher and AMD equivalent and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce 980ti or RX 5600 XT or higher with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Orebound at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce 980ti or RX 5600 XT or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Orebound need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Orebound need?

6 GB available space.
