Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Märchen Forest: Mylne and the Forest Gift [Legacy ver.] are built around one card, "GTX750Ti/GTX 760+", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
