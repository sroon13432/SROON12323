Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for TransOcean: The Shipping Company run from "Nvidia Geforce 7600 Series/ATI Radeon X1600" to "ATI 4800 Series". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
