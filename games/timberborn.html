Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Timberborn runs from "GeForce GTX 950, Radeon R9 380" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 2070, Radeon RX 5700" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.8 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
