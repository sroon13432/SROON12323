Hardware & Performance Analysis

Blood of Steel lists "NVIDIA Geforce GT 430" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 Ti" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 60 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
