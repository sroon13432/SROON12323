Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Omega Crafter?

Omega Crafter lists Geforce 950 or Radeon HD 7970 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce 950 or Radeon HD 7970 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Omega Crafter at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce 950 or Radeon HD 7970. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
