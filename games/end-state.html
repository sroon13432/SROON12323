Hardware & Performance Analysis

End State asks for "GTX 1060 6GB" at minimum and "RTX 2060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 78% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
