Hardware & Performance Analysis

Pantheon: Rise of the Fallen names the same GPU, "GeForce RTX 2060", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
