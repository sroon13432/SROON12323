Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Anima : The Reign of Darkness run from "512MB NVIDIA GeForce 6800 series" to "1.5GB NVIDIA GeForce 500 series". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Anima : The Reign of Darkness?

Anima : The Reign of Darkness lists 512MB NVIDIA GeForce 6800 series or ATI Radeon X800 series or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1.5GB NVIDIA GeForce 500 series or ATI Radeon 6000 series or better with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Anima : The Reign of Darkness at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1.5GB NVIDIA GeForce 500 series or ATI Radeon 6000 series or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
