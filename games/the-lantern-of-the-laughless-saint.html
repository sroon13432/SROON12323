Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Lantern of the Laughless Saint?

The Lantern of the Laughless Saint lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Lantern of the Laughless Saint at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Lantern of the Laughless Saint need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Lantern of the Laughless Saint need?

8 GB available space.
