Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Moto Racer 4 list "Video card must be 1 GB": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
