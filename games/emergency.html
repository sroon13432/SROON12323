Hardware & Performance Analysis

EMERGENCY asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 4GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 1080 Ti" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.1 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
