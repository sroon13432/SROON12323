Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Fallout 4 VR runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1070" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1080" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RX Vega 64 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
