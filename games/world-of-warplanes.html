Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for World of Warplanes runs from "Shaders 3.0 (NVIDIA GeForce 9600 GT 512MB, Radeon HD 3870)" in the minimum column to "NVidia GeForce GTX 660 1GB, Radeon HD 7870" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 8.8 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 45 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run World of Warplanes?

World of Warplanes lists Shaders 3.0 (NVIDIA GeForce 9600 GT 512MB, Radeon HD 3870) or higher and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GeForce GTX 660 1GB, Radeon HD 7870 / higher with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for World of Warplanes at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GeForce GTX 660 1GB, Radeon HD 7870 / higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does World of Warplanes need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does World of Warplanes need?

35 GB available space.
