Hardware & Performance Analysis

Plush Parade names the same GPU, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX660", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
