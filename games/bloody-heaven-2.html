Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bloody Heaven 2 runs from "GeForce GTX 1660" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 3060" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a rise of roughly 38% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
