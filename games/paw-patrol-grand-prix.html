Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run PAW Patrol: Grand Prix?

PAW Patrol: Grand Prix lists AMD Radeon RX 550 4GB / Nvidia GTX 750 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon R9 280 / Nvidia GTX 960 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for PAW Patrol: Grand Prix at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon R9 280 / Nvidia GTX 960. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does PAW Patrol: Grand Prix need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does PAW Patrol: Grand Prix need?

8 GB available space.
