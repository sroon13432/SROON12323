Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Adorable Adventures ("NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6 GB" and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, 10 GB") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Adorable Adventures lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 580, 8 GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, 10 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6900 XTX, 16 GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Adorable Adventures at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, 10 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6900 XTX, 16 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
