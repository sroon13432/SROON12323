Hardware & Performance Analysis

Park Beyond asks for "1080p/30fps: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB" at minimum and "1080p/30fps: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 4GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 12 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Park Beyond?

What GPU do I need for Park Beyond at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1080p/30fps: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 4GB / AMD Radeon R9 290X 4GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
