Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for God's Challenge runs from "Nvidia GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 1070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 48% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at Publisher minimum requirements; the recommended row is what makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
