Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Wheelie Life moves from "GTX1650" to "RTX3050". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 43% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
