Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Grow Home?

Grow Home lists nVidia GeForce GTS 450 or AMD Radeon HD5670 (1024MB VRAM) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and nVidia GeForce GTX560Ti or AMD Radeon HD6870 (1024MB VRAM) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Grow Home at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is nVidia GeForce GTX560Ti or AMD Radeon HD6870 (1024MB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Grow Home need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Grow Home need?

1 GB available space.
