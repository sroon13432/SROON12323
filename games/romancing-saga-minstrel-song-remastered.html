Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Romancing SaGa -Minstrel Song- Remastered moves from "AMD Radeon™ R7 240" to "AMD Radeon™ RX 460". On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 3.5 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
