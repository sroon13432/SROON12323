Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for My Little Pony: A Zephyr Heights Mystery runs from "AMD Radeon RX Vega 10" in the minimum column to "AMD Radeon RX Vega 56" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, roughly 100% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 10 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
