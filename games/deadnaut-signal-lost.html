Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Deadnaut: Signal Lost are built around one card, "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 246 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
