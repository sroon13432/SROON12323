Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for WarriOrb list "Nvidia Geforce GTX 670": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
