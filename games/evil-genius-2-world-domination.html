Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Evil Genius 2: World Domination runs from "GeForce GT 1030 2GB, Radeon RX 550 2GB" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1660 Super 6GB, Radeon RX 5700 8GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.6 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 18 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
