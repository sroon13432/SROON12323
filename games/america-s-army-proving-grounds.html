Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run America's Army: Proving Grounds?

America's Army: Proving Grounds lists Direct X 9.0c compliant, Shader 3.0 enabled video card with 512MB RAM and 4096 MB RAM as its minimum, and Direct X 9.0c compliant, Shader 3.0 enabled video card with 1GB RAM with 8192 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for America's Army: Proving Grounds at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Direct X 9.0c compliant, Shader 3.0 enabled video card with 1GB RAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does America's Army: Proving Grounds need?

4096 MB of memory at minimum and 8192 MB at recommended settings.
