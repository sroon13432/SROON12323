Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for MagicCraft are built around one card, "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1GB VRAM", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run MagicCraft?

MagicCraft lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 1GB VRAM / AMD Radeon R0 Fury and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 1GB VRAM / AMD Radeon R0 Fury with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for MagicCraft at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 1GB VRAM / AMD Radeon R0 Fury. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does MagicCraft need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
