Hardware & Performance Analysis

BattleGroupVR asks for "GTX 970" at minimum and "GTX 980" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

BattleGroupVR lists GTX 970 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GTX 980 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is GTX 980 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
