Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for From Dust run from "256 MB DirectX 9.0c-compliant card with Shader Model 3.0" to "512 MB Geforce 9". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 1.5 GB to 3 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2011, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run From Dust?

From Dust lists 256 MB DirectX 9.0c-compliant card with Shader Model 3.0 or higher (*see supported list) and 1.5 GB (Windows XP) / 2 GB (Windows Vista) RAM as its minimum, and 512 MB Geforce 9 or Radeon HD 4000 (*see supported list) with 3 GB DDR3 RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for From Dust at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 512 MB Geforce 9 or Radeon HD 4000 (*see supported list). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does From Dust need?

1.5 GB (Windows XP) / 2 GB (Windows Vista) of memory at minimum and 3 GB DDR3 at recommended settings.
