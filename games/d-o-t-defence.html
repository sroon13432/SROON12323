D.O.T. Defence asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 6200" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.
