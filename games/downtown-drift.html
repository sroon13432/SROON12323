Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Downtown Drift?

Downtown Drift lists NVIDIA GTX 680 or AMD equivalent and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 980 or AMD equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Downtown Drift at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 980 or AMD equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Downtown Drift need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
