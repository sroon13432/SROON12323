Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for The House of Tesla: Definitive Edition are built around one card, "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
