Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Lord of the Rings: Gollum™?

The Lord of the Rings: Gollum™ lists Nvidia GTX 1060, 6GB / AMD Radeon R9 290X, 4GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070, 8GB (with DLSS Quality Setting) / AMD Radeon RX 6750 XT, 12GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Lord of the Rings: Gollum™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070, 8GB (with DLSS Quality Setting) / AMD Radeon RX 6750 XT, 12GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Lord of the Rings: Gollum™ need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Lord of the Rings: Gollum™ need?

45 GB available space.
