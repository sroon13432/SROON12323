Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run FPV Freerider?

What GPU do I need for FPV Freerider at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce GTX 960M / Radeon HD 7750 or higher, 1GB video card memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
