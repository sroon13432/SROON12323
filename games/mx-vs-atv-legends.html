Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers MX vs ATV Legends moves from "Geforce GTX 770" to "Geforce GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.4 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
