Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bronzebeard's Tavern runs from "AMD Radeon RX 550" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.6 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 6 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
