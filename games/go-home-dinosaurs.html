Hardware & Performance Analysis

Go Home Dinosaurs! asks for "512 MB, Shader Model 2" at minimum and "1 GB, Shader Model 3" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 451 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2013, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
