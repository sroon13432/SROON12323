Hardware & Performance Analysis

DOOM asks for "NVIDIA GTX 670 2GB/AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 970 4GB/AMD Radeon R9 290 4GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 122% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. A 55 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.
