Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Chains of Freedom moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Chains of Freedom?

Chains of Freedom lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Chains of Freedom at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Chains of Freedom need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

5 GB available space.
