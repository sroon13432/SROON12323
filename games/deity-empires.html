Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Deity Empires?

Deity Empires lists 1 Ghz and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 2 Ghz with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Deity Empires at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 2 Ghz. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Deity Empires need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
