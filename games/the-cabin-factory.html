Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Cabin Factory asks for "DirectX 11 Feature Level AMD" at minimum and "DirectX 12 Feature Level AMD" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Cabin Factory?

The Cabin Factory lists DirectX 11 Feature Level AMD or NVIDIA Card with 1 GB VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 12 Feature Level AMD or NVIDIA Card with 4 GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Cabin Factory at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 12 Feature Level AMD or NVIDIA Card with 4 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Cabin Factory need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
