Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Mushy?

Mushy lists Intel UHD 620 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Intel UHD 620 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Mushy at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel UHD 620. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Mushy need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
