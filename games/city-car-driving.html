Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers City Car Driving moves from "AMD Radeon R7 240" to "AMD Radeon R7 250X". Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 75% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

What GPU do I need for City Car Driving at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon R7 250X / nVidia GeForce GTX 750. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
