Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Vertical Shift list "NVIDIA GTX 970": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

What GPU do I need for Vertical Shift at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 970 / AMD R9 290 equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
