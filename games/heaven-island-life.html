Hardware & Performance Analysis

Heaven Island Life names the same GPU, "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Heaven Island Life?

Heaven Island Life lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Heaven Island Life at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
