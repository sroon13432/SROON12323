Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Night Janitor list "GTX770": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
