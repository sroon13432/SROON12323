Hardware & Performance Analysis

Moonstone Tavern - A Fantasy Tavern Sim! asks for "Intel HD Graphics 4000, Nvidia GeForce 8000, ATI Radeon HD 4800 Series" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 5xx series" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
