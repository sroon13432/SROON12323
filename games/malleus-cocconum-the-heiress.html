Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Malleus Cocconum: The Heiress list "Integrated graphics": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
