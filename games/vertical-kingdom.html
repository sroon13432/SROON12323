Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Vertical Kingdom moves from "AMD Radeon™ R7 260X (2GB VRAM)" to "Radeon™ RX 470(4GB VRAM)". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 193% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 470 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Vertical Kingdom?

Vertical Kingdom lists AMD Radeon™ R7 260X (2GB VRAM) / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 750(2GB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Radeon™ RX 470(4GB VRAM) / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 6 GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Vertical Kingdom at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Radeon™ RX 470(4GB VRAM) / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 6 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Vertical Kingdom need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

5 GB available space.
