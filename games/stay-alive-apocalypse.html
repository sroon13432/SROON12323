Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Stay Alive: Apocalypse run from "2 GB" to "4 GB". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
