Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Tree of Life ("Intel HD Integrated Graphics 530" and "GeForce GTX 650, Radeon 6770") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tree of Life?

Tree of Life lists Intel HD Integrated Graphics 530 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 650, Radeon 6770 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tree of Life at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 650, Radeon 6770. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tree of Life need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
