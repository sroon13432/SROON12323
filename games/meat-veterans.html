Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for MEAT Veterans runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 670" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce RTX 2070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 5.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
