Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ascend: Reborn?

Ascend: Reborn lists DirectX9 capable card supporting PS and VS 3.0 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX12 capable card with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ascend: Reborn at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX12 capable card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ascend: Reborn need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Ascend: Reborn need?

4 GB available space.
