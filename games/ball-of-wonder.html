Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ball of Wonder asks for "512 MB" at minimum and "1 GB" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 72 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
