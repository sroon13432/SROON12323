Hardware & Performance Analysis

Heaven Island - VR MMO asks for "Nvidia GTX 570" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 670" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, roughly 105% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 696 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
