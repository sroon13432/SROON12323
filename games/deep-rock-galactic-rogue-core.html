Hardware & Performance Analysis

Deep Rock Galactic: Rogue Core lists "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 1050Ti" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
