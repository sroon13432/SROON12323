Hardware & Performance Analysis

FORCED: Slightly Better Edition asks for "DirectX 9.0c-compatible, SM 3.0-compatible" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GTS" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2013, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
