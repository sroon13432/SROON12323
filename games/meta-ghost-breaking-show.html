Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Meta Ghost: Breaking Show runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1060 (6GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 3060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, 96% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
