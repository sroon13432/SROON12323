Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Resident Evil 6 moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800GTS" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 560". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of about 191%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Resident Evil 6?

Resident Evil 6 lists NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800GTS or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 560 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Resident Evil 6 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 560 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Resident Evil 6 need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
