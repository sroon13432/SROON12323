Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Beyond the Void?

Beyond the Void lists ATI Radeon HD 5770 or NVIDIA GeForce GTS450 with 1GB VRAM and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and Radeon HD 6850 or NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 with 1GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Beyond the Void at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Radeon HD 6850 or NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 with 1GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Beyond the Void need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Beyond the Void need?

2 GB available space.
