Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Alone in the Dark: Illumination™ run from "NVIDIA GeForce 460 GTX" to "NVIDIA GeForce 580 GTX". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2015, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Alone in the Dark: Illumination™?

Alone in the Dark: Illumination™ lists NVIDIA GeForce 460 GTX or AMD Radeon 6850 HD series card and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 580 GTX or AMD Radeon 6970 HD series card with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Alone in the Dark: Illumination™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 580 GTX or AMD Radeon 6970 HD series card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Alone in the Dark: Illumination™ need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Alone in the Dark: Illumination™ need?

15 GB available space.
