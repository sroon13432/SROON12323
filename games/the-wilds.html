Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The WILDS?

The WILDS lists GeForce 760 or equivalent with 4 GB of VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 980 or equivalent with 4GB of VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The WILDS at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 980 or equivalent with 4GB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The WILDS need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The WILDS need?

3 GB available space.
