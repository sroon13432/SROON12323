Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cassette Beasts asks for "2 GB VRAM, OpenGL 3.3" at minimum and "4 GB VRAM, NVIDIA GTX 970" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
