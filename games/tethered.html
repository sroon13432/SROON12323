Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Tethered ("NVIDIA GTX 970" and "NVIDIA GTX 980") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tethered?

Tethered lists NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD R9 290 equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 980 or AMD R9 390 equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tethered at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 980 or AMD R9 390 equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tethered need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Tethered need?

3 GB available space.
