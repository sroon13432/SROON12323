Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for 8Doors: Arum's Afterlife Adventure list "GeForce Gtx 560": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
