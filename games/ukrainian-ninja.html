Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ukrainian Ninja?

Ukrainian Ninja lists A graphics card with shader model 3.0 support and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and A graphics card with Shader Model 3.0 support, such as nVidia GeForce 7800 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ukrainian Ninja at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is A graphics card with Shader Model 3.0 support, such as nVidia GeForce 7800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ukrainian Ninja need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Ukrainian Ninja need?

2 GB available space.
