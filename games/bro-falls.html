Bro Falls

★ Mostly Positive (74% of 5,094 reviews) 💾 3 GB available space

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bro Falls runs from "NVIDIA GTX 660" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 1050Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a jump of about 129%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
