Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Imperivm RTC - HD Edition "Great Battles of Rome" run from "1280x800" to "1360x768". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Can my PC run Imperivm RTC - HD Edition "Great Battles of Rome"?

Imperivm RTC - HD Edition "Great Battles of Rome" lists 1280x800 or higher and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1360x768 or higher / 1920x1080 with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is 1360x768 or higher / 1920x1080. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
