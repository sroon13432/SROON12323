Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Call of Cthulhu®: Dark Corners of the Earth list "DirectX 9.0 3D compliant Video Card": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2009, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
