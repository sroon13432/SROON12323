Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Six Days in Fallujah runs from "Nvidia GTX 1060 (6GB)" in the minimum column to "Nvidia RTX 3070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 148%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 12 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 22 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
