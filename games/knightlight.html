Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for KnightLight runs from "Nvidia GTX 760" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 960" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 47% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
