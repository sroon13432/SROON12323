Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Starcom: Unknown Space run from "2 GB Direct3D 11 Capable video card" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers ask for 6 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Can my PC run Starcom: Unknown Space?

Starcom: Unknown Space lists 2 GB Direct3D 11 Capable video card or better (see additional notes) and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or better with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Starcom: Unknown Space at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

6 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
