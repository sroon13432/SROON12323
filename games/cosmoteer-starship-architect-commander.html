Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Cosmoteer: Starship Architect & Commander run from "1GB DirectX 10 Graphics Card" to "2GB DirectX 11 Graphics Card". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
