Hardware & Performance Analysis

Star Wolves 3: Civil War asks for "nVidia GF FX5700" at minimum and "nVidia GF 6800 GT" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5.5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2010, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Star Wolves 3: Civil War?

Star Wolves 3: Civil War lists nVidia GF FX5700 or ATI Radeon 9600 with 128MB VideoRAM and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and nVidia GF 6800 GT or ATI Radeon 1900XT with 512MB VideoRAM with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Star Wolves 3: Civil War at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is nVidia GF 6800 GT or ATI Radeon 1900XT with 512MB VideoRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Star Wolves 3: Civil War need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
