Teamfight Tactics

💾 16 GB (HDD minimum, SSD recommended) ⚙️ Unreal Engine 5 (since Set 18, August 2026)

Teamfight Tactics is Riot's auto battler, free on PC through the Riot client and not sold on Steam. Riot moved TFT from the League of Legends engine to Unreal with Set 18 in August 2026 and published a dedicated spec table: a Core i3-530, 2 GB of RAM and DirectX 11 hardware at minimum, a Core i5-8250, 4 GB of RAM and a 16 GB SSD install when recommended.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 / AMD Radeon HD 6950 / Intel UHD 630
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Teamfight Tactics runs from "NVIDIA GeForce 400 series" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a rise of roughly 60% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at Official minimum (1024x768, low settings); the recommended row is what makes Official recommended (1920x1080, high settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. Storage is 16 GB and the requirement asks for an SSD specifically, so a hard drive is out of the question here. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Strategy games spend their budget on turn logic and large maps, so single-core CPU speed matters as much as the GPU row here.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    TFT's own minimum is identical to League's apart from the AMD CPU example (A6-3650 instead of FX-4100), so a PC that runs League comfortably will run TFT.
  • 💡
    Since the Unreal migration the game uses the same DirectX 11 floor, so a DirectX 10-era graphics card is no longer usable.
  • 💡
    It is a menu- and board-driven game: frame rate matters far less than stable memory, so a 2 GB machine that meets the minimum should still close other browser tabs while playing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Teamfight Tactics?

Teamfight Tactics lists NVIDIA GeForce 400 series / AMD Radeon HD 6570 / Intel HD 4600 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 / AMD Radeon HD 6950 / Intel UHD 630 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Teamfight Tactics at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 / AMD Radeon HD 6950 / Intel UHD 630. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended (1920x1080, high settings).

How much RAM does Teamfight Tactics need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Teamfight Tactics need?

16 GB available space (HDD).
