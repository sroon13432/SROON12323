Hardware & Performance Analysis

Voidsayer names the same GPU, "1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable)", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Voidsayer?

Voidsayer lists 1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable) with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Voidsayer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
