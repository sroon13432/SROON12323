Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dungeons & Dragons Online®?

Dungeons & Dragons Online® lists GeForce 7XXX / ATI Radeon HD 3000 series with 512MB or more of dedicated video memory and 2GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia Geforce 8XXX / AMD Radeon HD 6### series or newer with 1GB or more of dedicated video memory with 4GB minimum RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dungeons & Dragons Online® at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia Geforce 8XXX / AMD Radeon HD 6### series or newer with 1GB or more of dedicated video memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dungeons & Dragons Online® need?

2GB of memory at minimum and 4GB minimum at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dungeons & Dragons Online® need?

32GB for high resolution*.
