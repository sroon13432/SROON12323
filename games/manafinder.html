Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Manafinder run from "OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU" to "Dedicated Graphics Card with OpenGL 4.1". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 604 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
