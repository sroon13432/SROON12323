Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for World of Warships: Legends runs from "NVidia GeForce GTX 1650 (4GB) /AMD Radeon RX 570 (4GB)" in the minimum column to "NVidia GeForce RTX 2060 (6GB) /AMD RX 5600 XT (6GB)" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 63% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 45 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
