Hardware & Performance Analysis

Sandustry asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 550 Ti (1 GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960 (4 GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, a jump of 5.4 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
