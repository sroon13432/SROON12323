PAGUI打鬼 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080" at its recommended settings. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.
