Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Frozenheim?

Frozenheim lists GeForce GTX 660, Radeon R7 265 or equivalent with 2 GB of video RAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1070 or equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Frozenheim at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1070 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Frozenheim need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Frozenheim need?

10 GB available space.
