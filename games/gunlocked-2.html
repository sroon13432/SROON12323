Can my PC run Gunlocked 2?

Gunlocked 2 lists Nvidia GT710/AMD RX550 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GT710/AMD RX550 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gunlocked 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GT710/AMD RX550. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
