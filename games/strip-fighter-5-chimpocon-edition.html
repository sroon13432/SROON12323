Hardware & Performance Analysis

Strip Fighter 5: Chimpocon Edition names the same GPU, "Integrated Graphics Chip", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
