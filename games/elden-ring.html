Hardware & Performance Analysis

ELDEN RING (Shadow of the Erdtree) asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (3GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB)" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p / 1440p @ 60 FPS (Maximum) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 45-60 FPS (Low / Medium).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. Storage is 60 GB and the requirement asks for an SSD specifically, so a hard drive is out of the question here.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Keep 'Ray Tracing' OFF unless using an RTX 3080 or better; Elden Ring's RT implementation has heavy FPS drops with subtle visual gains.
  • 💡
    Set 'Effects Quality' to Medium to prevent frame dips during dragon fire and massive spell casts.
  • 💡
    Disable Microsoft Device Association Root Enumerator in Device Manager if experiencing periodic micro-stutters.
