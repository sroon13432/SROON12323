Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Research Story list "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660Ti": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
