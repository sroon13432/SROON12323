Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Werewolves Within™ are built around one card, "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Werewolves Within™?

Werewolves Within™ lists NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Werewolves Within™ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970, AMD Radeon™ R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Werewolves Within™ need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Werewolves Within™ need?

5 GB available space.
