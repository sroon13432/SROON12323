Lex Mortis

★ Mixed (41% of 87 reviews) 💾 4 GB available space

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Lex Mortis runs from "Nvidia GTX 560" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 660" in the recommended one.
