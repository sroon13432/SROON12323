Path Of Wuxia

★ Very Positive (87% of 688 reviews) 💾 10 GB available space

Path of Wuxia combines classic wuxia world and youthful school life with anime art style, features simulation gameplay well known by Heluo fans. Play as a young Xia who join the academy known as the Hermitic Pavilions of Xia, grow strong along with various schoolmates and seek your own path of Xia.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Path Of Wuxia?

Path Of Wuxia lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050ti with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Path Of Wuxia at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050ti. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Path Of Wuxia need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Path Of Wuxia need?

10 GB available space.
