Hardware & Performance Analysis

Land Above Sea Below Prologue asks for "Dedicated graphics card, GTX 770 2GB" at minimum and "Dedicated graphics card, GTX 970" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 91%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 338 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
