Hardware & Performance Analysis

Call of Duty: Warzone asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 960" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a rise of roughly 61% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you Publisher minimum requirements, meeting the recommended row gets you Publisher recommended requirements, and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 125 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
