Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Touken Ranbu Warriors runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).
