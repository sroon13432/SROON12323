Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Gear.Club Unlimited 3 moves from "Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB, AMD RX 580 8GB, Intel ARC A380" to "Nvidia RTX 4070 8GB, AMD Radeon RX 6900 XT 16GB". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, 3.6 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. A 51 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 4070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Can my PC run Gear.Club Unlimited 3?

Gear.Club Unlimited 3 lists Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB, AMD RX 580 8GB, Intel ARC A380 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX 4070 8GB, AMD Radeon RX 6900 XT 16GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gear.Club Unlimited 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX 4070 8GB, AMD Radeon RX 6900 XT 16GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
