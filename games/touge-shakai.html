Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Touge Shakai runs from "Geforce GTX 750/Radeon RX 560" in the minimum column to "Geforce GTX 1060/Radeon RX 590" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 3.4 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
