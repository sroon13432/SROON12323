Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Rentlord run from "NVIDIA GeForce 840M" to "GTX 1050". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 113 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

The recommended GPU is GTX 1050. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
