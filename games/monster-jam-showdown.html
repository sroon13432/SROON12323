Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Monster Jam™ Showdown runs from "GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (4096 MB)" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 3080 Ti (12288 MB)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to High-end, 4.9 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is high-end territory (RTX 3080 Ti bracket), where the requirement says more about the game's ceiling than about the card you need to buy.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
