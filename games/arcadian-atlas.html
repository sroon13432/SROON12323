Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Arcadian Atlas are built around one card, "NVIDIA GT 630", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
