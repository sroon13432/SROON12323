Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Digimon World: Next Order?

Digimon World: Next Order lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560, 1 GB or AMD Radeon HD 6870, 1 GB and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 Ti, 1 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7850, 1 GB with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Digimon World: Next Order at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 Ti, 1 GB or AMD Radeon HD 7850, 1 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Digimon World: Next Order need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Digimon World: Next Order need?

7 GB available space.
