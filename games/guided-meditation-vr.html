Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Guided Meditation VR runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 980 Ti" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 Ti bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
