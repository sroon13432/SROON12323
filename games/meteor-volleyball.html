Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Meteor Volleyball! runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760, AMD Radeon R9 270X," in the minimum column to "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470," in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 116% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 470 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Meteor Volleyball!?

Meteor Volleyball! lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760, AMD Radeon R9 270X, or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470, or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Meteor Volleyball! at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 470, or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Meteor Volleyball! need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

200 MB available space.
