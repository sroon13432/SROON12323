Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Philna Fantasy list "Nvidia GTX 750 Ti": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory climbs from 5 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
