Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Gravebound?

Gravebound lists Any DirectX 11 or 12 compatible card and 8 MB RAM as its minimum, and Any DirectX 11 or 12 compatible card with 8 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Gravebound at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Any DirectX 11 or 12 compatible card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Gravebound need?

8 MB of memory at minimum and 8 MB at recommended settings.
