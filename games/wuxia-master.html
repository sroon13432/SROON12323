Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Wuxia Master moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 8600GT" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560". On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 5.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

What GPU do I need for Wuxia Master at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 / AMD Radeon HD 6870. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
