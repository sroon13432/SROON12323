Hardware & Performance Analysis

Rune Legacy Idle asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GT 730" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 (2GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, a jump of 9.5 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
