Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1080 equivalent" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 2080 equivalent" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, about 43% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. At 180 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond?

Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond lists NVIDIA GTX 1080 equivalent or greater and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 2080 equivalent or greater with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 2080 equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Medal of Honor™: Above and Beyond need?

180 GB available space.
