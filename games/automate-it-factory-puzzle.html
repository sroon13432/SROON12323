Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Automate It: Factory Puzzle moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, something like 73% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
