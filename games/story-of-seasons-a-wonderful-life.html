Hardware & Performance Analysis

STORY OF SEASONS: A Wonderful Life asks for "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 950" at minimum and "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, 114% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
