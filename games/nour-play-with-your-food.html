Hardware & Performance Analysis

Nour: Play with Your Food names the same GPU, "Intel HD Graphics 520", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
