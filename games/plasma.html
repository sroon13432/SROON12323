Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Plasma runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 Ti, 2 GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780, 3 GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, roughly 136% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
