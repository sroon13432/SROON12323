Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Sword Art Online: Lost Song moves from "GeForce GTX 650" to "GeForce GTX 670". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 100% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
