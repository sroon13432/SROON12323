Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cozy Builder?

Cozy Builder lists GeForce GTX 960 4GB or similar and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1060 or similar with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cozy Builder at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1060 or similar. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cozy Builder need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Cozy Builder need?

4 GB available space.
