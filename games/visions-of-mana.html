Hardware & Performance Analysis

Visions of Mana asks for "AMD Radeon™ RX 580" at minimum and "AMD Radeon™ RX 5700 XT" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 47% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. A 45 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
