Hardware & Performance Analysis

Liminal Universe asks for "NVIDIA GTX 1060" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, roughly 96% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Liminal Universe?

Liminal Universe lists NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580 or newer and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT or newer with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Liminal Universe at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT or newer. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Liminal Universe need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Liminal Universe need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
