Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Double Kick Heroes?

Double Kick Heroes lists Intel HD Graphics 5500 and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 2 Go with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Double Kick Heroes at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 2 Go. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Double Kick Heroes need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

5 GB available space.
