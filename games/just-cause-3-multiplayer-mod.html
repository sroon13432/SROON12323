Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Just Cause™ 3: Multiplayer Mod runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 (2GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 (3GB)" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 44% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
