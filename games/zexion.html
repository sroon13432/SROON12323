Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Zexion?

Zexion lists OpenGL 2.0 support required and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Zexion at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel HD Graphics 4000 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Zexion need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Zexion need?

50 MB available space.
