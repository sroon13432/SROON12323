Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for BandRoll list "GeForce 7600 GT (256 MB)": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run BandRoll?

BandRoll lists GeForce 7600 GT (256 MB) and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 7600 GT (256 MB) with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for BandRoll at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 7600 GT (256 MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does BandRoll need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does BandRoll need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
