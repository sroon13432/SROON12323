Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Rally Mechanic Simulator moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 with 4GB VRAM". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a jump of about 91%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Rally Mechanic Simulator?

Rally Mechanic Simulator lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580 Series with 4GB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 with 4GB VRAM or more with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Rally Mechanic Simulator at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 with 4GB VRAM or more. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Rally Mechanic Simulator need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Rally Mechanic Simulator need?

9 GB available space.
