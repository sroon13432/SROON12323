Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Binary Domain?

Binary Domain lists NVIDIA GeForce GT220 (512MB) / ATI Radeon HD 2600 XT (512MB) and 2GB (XP)/3GB (Windows 7 / Vista) RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 (1GB) / ATI Radeon HD 5750 (1GB) with 3GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Binary Domain at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 (1GB) / ATI Radeon HD 5750 (1GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Binary Domain need?

2GB (XP)/3GB (Windows 7 / Vista) of memory at minimum and 3GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Binary Domain need?
