Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Definitely Not Fried Chicken?

Definitely Not Fried Chicken lists Nvidia Geforce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 580 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia Geforce GTX 1080 6GB or AMD RX 5700 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Definitely Not Fried Chicken at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia Geforce GTX 1080 6GB or AMD RX 5700. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
