Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Tale of Bistun asks for "Nvidia GTX 950" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1050 ( 2048 MB); Radeon RX 580 (8192 MB)" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
