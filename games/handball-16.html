Hardware & Performance Analysis

Handball 16 asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800 GT" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® 750Ti" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 8.5 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
