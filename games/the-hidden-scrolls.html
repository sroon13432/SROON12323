Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Hidden Scrolls asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Upper mid-range, 4.6 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

The Hidden Scrolls lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Hidden Scrolls at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
