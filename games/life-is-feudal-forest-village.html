Hardware & Performance Analysis

Life is Feudal: Forest Village asks for "GeForce GTX 560/Radeon HD 6850" at minimum and "GeForce 760/Radeon HD 6970" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (R9 270X bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
