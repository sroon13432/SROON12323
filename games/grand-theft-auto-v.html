Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) runs from "NVIDIA 9800 GT 1GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 7.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 720p @ 30 FPS, meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (Very High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 110 GB install also names an SSD, and that part is not optional: texture streaming on a spinning drive is where this title falls apart. DirectX 10 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Grass Quality is the biggest FPS killer; drop from Ultra to Very High for an instant 20+ FPS gain in rural Blaine County.
  • 💡
    Use FXAA or MSAA 2x instead of MSAA 8x; 8x MSAA reduces performance by over 40% with little visible gain at 1080p+.
  • 💡
    Advanced Graphics settings (Extended Distance Scaling) should remain at default unless running an RTX 3080 or better.

Can my PC run Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5)?

Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) lists NVIDIA 9800 GT 1GB / AMD HD 4870 1GB and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB / AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 2GB / AMD Radeon HD 7870 2GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (Very High Settings).
