Meteor 60 Seconds!

★ Overwhelmingly Positive (96% of 1,748 reviews) 💾 Size: see Steam store page

Meteor 60 seconds! is a simple, fun, comic-like action game that simulates your life if there were a meteor heading towards Earth and you had 60 seconds to live. Do anything you want to do with your last 60 seconds, even if it's illegal! What about planting an apple tree?

🚀 Recommended GPU: Direct X 9.0c compatible video card
Target: 60 FPS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Meteor 60 Seconds!?

Meteor 60 Seconds! lists Direct X 9.0c compatible video card and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and Direct X 9.0c compatible video card with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Meteor 60 Seconds! at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Direct X 9.0c compatible video card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Meteor 60 Seconds! need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Meteor 60 Seconds! need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
