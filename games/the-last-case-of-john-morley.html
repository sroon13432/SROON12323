Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Last Case of John Morley asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 8600" at minimum and "GTX 1070 4GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Mid-range, 61.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 31 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
