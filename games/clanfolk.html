Hardware & Performance Analysis

Clanfolk asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 750 (2 GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 (2 GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a rise of roughly 86% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
