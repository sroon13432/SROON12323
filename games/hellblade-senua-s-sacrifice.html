Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice are built around one card, "GTX 770 with 2GB VRAM", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest.
