Hardware & Performance Analysis

Paquerette Down the Bunburrows names the same GPU, "DX10, DX11, DX12", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
