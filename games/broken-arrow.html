Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Broken Arrow runs from "GeForce GTX 1650 (4096 MB)" in the minimum column to "GeForce RTX 3080 (10240 MB)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, 3.3 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 85 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 7800 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
