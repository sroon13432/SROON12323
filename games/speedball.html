Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Speedball moves from "GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (or AMD equivalent)" to "GeForce RTX 2080 (or AMD equivalent)". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Upper mid-range, 3.8 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.
