Hardware & Performance Analysis

One day asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 260" at minimum and "Direct X 11.0 compliant video card with 2GB RAM (NVidia GeForce GTX 750)" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run One day?

One day lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 260 / AMD Radeon HD 5750. OpenGL 3.3 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Direct X 11.0 compliant video card with 2GB RAM (NVidia GeForce GTX 750 or AMD Radeon HD 7850) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for One day at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Direct X 11.0 compliant video card with 2GB RAM (NVidia GeForce GTX 750 or AMD Radeon HD 7850). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
