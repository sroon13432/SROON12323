Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for MeowCraft runs from "GTX 560" in the minimum column to "RTX 2080" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Upper mid-range, 18.8 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
