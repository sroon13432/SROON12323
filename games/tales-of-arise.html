Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Tales of ARISE runs from "GeForce GTX 760" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 970" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 111%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. A 45 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
