Hardware & Performance Analysis

LES MILLS XR BODYCOMBAT asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 3060 Ti" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
