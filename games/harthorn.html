Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Harthorn are built around one card, "GeForce GTX 1070", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
