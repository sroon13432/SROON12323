Hardware & Performance Analysis

Myth of Empires asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 105% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 85 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Can my PC run Myth of Empires?

Myth of Empires lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB / AMD Radeon R7 370 2GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB / AMD Radeon RX 580 4GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Myth of Empires at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB / AMD Radeon RX 580 4GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
