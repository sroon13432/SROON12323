Hardware & Performance Analysis

Enlisted asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 660" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1080" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 6.0 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. A 49 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
