Hardware & Performance Analysis

Soul Nomad & the World Eaters asks for "Radeon HD 5450" at minimum and "Geforce GT 640, Radeon HD 6450" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, roughly 150% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Soul Nomad & the World Eaters?

Soul Nomad & the World Eaters lists Radeon HD 5450 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce GT 640, Radeon HD 6450 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Soul Nomad & the World Eaters at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce GT 640, Radeon HD 6450. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Soul Nomad & the World Eaters need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Soul Nomad & the World Eaters need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
