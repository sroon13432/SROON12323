Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Company of Heroes 2?

Company of Heroes 2 lists 512MB Direct3D 10 capable video card (GeForce 8800 GT or Radeon HD 2900XT) and 2GB RAM as its minimum, and 1024 MB Direct3D 11 capable video card (GeForce GTX 470 or Radeon HD 5850) with 4GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Company of Heroes 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1024 MB Direct3D 11 capable video card (GeForce GTX 470 or Radeon HD 5850). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Company of Heroes 2 need?

2GB of memory at minimum and 4GB at recommended settings.
