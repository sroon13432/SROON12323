Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Planet S?

Planet S lists NVIDIA GTX 750 Ti, AMD Radeon R7 260X and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Planet S at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Planet S need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
