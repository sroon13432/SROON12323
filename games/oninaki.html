Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run ONINAKI?

ONINAKI lists AMD Radeon™ R7 260X / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 750 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon™ RX460 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX950 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for ONINAKI at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon™ RX460 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX950. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does ONINAKI need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does ONINAKI need?

5 GB available space.
