Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion® Game of the Year Edition (2009) names the same GPU, "128 MB Direct3D compatible video card", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4.6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2009, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
