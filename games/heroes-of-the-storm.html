Heroes of the Storm

💾 20 GB available space ⚙️ StarCraft II engine

Heroes of the Storm is Blizzard's free-to-play team brawler, installed through Battle.net and not sold on Steam. Battle.net lists Windows 10 with a Core 2 Duo E6600 or AMD Phenom X4, a GeForce 8600 GT, Radeon HD 4650 or Intel HD 3000 and 3 GB of RAM at minimum, and Windows 10 64-bit with a Core i5 or AMD FX CPU, a GTX 650 or HD 7790 and 4 GB of RAM when recommended, on 20 GB of storage.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Heroes of the Storm runs from "NVIDIA GeForce 8600 GT" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 7.5 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you Official minimum, meeting the recommended row gets you Official recommended, and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

    Team fights are the frame-rate test here: drop shadows and effects quality first, because hero outlines and health bars stay readable at any preset.
  • 💡
    The game runs on the StarCraft II engine, so it benefits from the same CPU headroom in crowded fights rather than from a faster GPU alone.

    20 GB of free space is required before a patch; Battle.net will refuse the update rather than shrink the install.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Heroes of the Storm?

Heroes of the Storm lists NVIDIA GeForce 8600 GT / AMD Radeon HD 4650 / Intel HD Graphics 3000 and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Heroes of the Storm at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7790. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.

How much RAM does Heroes of the Storm need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Heroes of the Storm need?

20 GB available space.
