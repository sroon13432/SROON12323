Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Awakening?

The Awakening lists 1Gb video graphic card and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and 2Gb video graphic card with 16 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Awakening at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 2Gb video graphic card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Awakening need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Awakening need?

1 GB available space.
