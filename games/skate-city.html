Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Skate City moves from "NVIDIA GT 460" to "NVIDIA GT 1050". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, roughly 132% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 594 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Skate City?

Skate City lists NVIDIA GT 460 or AMD Radeon HD 5550 w/ 1024 MB and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GT 1050 or AMD Radeon RX 560 w/ 2048 MB with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Skate City at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GT 1050 or AMD Radeon RX 560 w/ 2048 MB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Skate City need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Skate City need?

595 MB available space.
