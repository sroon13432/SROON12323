Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Computer Tycoon?

Computer Tycoon lists Radeon HD 6850 or similar and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Better than Radeon HD 6850 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Computer Tycoon at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Better than Radeon HD 6850. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Computer Tycoon need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Computer Tycoon need?

3 GB available space.
