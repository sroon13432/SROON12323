Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Spiritstead runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Low-end, a jump of 6.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
