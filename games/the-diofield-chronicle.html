Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The DioField Chronicle?

The DioField Chronicle lists AMD Radeon™ RX 460 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD Radeon™ RX 480 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 (3GB) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The DioField Chronicle at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD Radeon™ RX 480 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1060 (3GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The DioField Chronicle need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
