Hardware & Performance Analysis

A Castle Full of Cats asks for "Any with OpenGL 3.3 Support" at minimum and "GTX 1050" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, 10.8 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
