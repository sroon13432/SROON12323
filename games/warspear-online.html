Hardware & Performance Analysis

Warspear Online asks for "OpenGL 1.3-compatible; 128 Mb video memory" at minimum and "OpenGL 3.0-compatible; 256 Mb video memory" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Warspear Online?

Warspear Online lists OpenGL 1.3-compatible; 128 Mb video memory and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and OpenGL 3.0-compatible; 256 Mb video memory with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warspear Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is OpenGL 3.0-compatible; 256 Mb video memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warspear Online need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Warspear Online need?

100 MB available space.
