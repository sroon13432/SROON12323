Hardware & Performance Analysis

Aniimo lists "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Aniimo?

Aniimo lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6700 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Aniimo at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD Radeon RX 6700 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Aniimo need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
