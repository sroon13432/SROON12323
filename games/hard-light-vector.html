Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Hard Light Vector moves from "Nvidia GTX 970" to "Nvidia GTX 1070". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, something like 76% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
