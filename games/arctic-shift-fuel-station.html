Hardware & Performance Analysis

Arctic Shift: Fuel Station asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 12GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 86% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 10 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
