Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Terminal 2 asks for "Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with at least 512Mb" at minimum and "Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with at least 1024 Mb" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Terminal 2?

The Terminal 2 lists Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with at least 512Mb and 1024 MB RAM as its minimum, and Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with at least 1024 Mb with 2048 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Terminal 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Direct X 9.0c compliant video card with at least 1024 Mb. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Terminal 2 need?

1024 MB of memory at minimum and 2048 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Terminal 2 need?
