Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Last Days Motel?

Last Days Motel lists Nvidia 260 GTS or Radeon HD 4850 - 512 MB of VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia 460 GTX / Radeon 5770 - 1 GB of VRAM or better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Last Days Motel at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia 460 GTX / Radeon 5770 - 1 GB of VRAM or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
