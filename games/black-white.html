Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Black & White runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 129% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest.
