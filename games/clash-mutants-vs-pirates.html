Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Clash: Mutants Vs Pirates moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 580" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 119% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
