Hardware & Performance Analysis

Homeworld 3 asks for "Nvidia GTX 1060/AMD RX 480/Intel ARC A380" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1080 TI/AMD RX5700/Intel ARC A580" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, something like 83% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3050 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
