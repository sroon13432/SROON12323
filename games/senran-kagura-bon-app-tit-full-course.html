Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for SENRAN KAGURA Bon Appétit! - Full Course runs from "Intel HD3000+" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 950" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, 17.5 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
