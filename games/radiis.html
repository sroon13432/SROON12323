Hardware & Performance Analysis

Radiis asks for "GeForce GTX 460" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 670" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, 3.0 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
