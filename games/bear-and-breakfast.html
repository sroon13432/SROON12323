Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bear and Breakfast?

Bear and Breakfast lists GeForce GT 440 (1024 MB VRAM) or Radeon HD 6750 (1024 VRAM) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 460 (2048 MB VRAM) or Radeon HD 7770 (2048 VRAM) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Bear and Breakfast at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 460 (2048 MB VRAM) or Radeon HD 7770 (2048 VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Bear and Breakfast need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

3 GB available space.
