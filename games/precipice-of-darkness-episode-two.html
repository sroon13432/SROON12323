Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Precipice of Darkness, Episode Two list "ATI Radeon™ 9500": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2008, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
