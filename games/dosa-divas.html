Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dosa Divas asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti, 2 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, 4 GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, about 65% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
