Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Only False runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 (2 GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 2060 (6 GB)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, something like 73% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
