Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Nomad?

Nomad lists Graphics card with DX10 (shader model 4.0) capabilities and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Graphics card with DX10 (shader model 4.0) capabilities or higher with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Nomad at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Graphics card with DX10 (shader model 4.0) capabilities or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Nomad need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Nomad need?

2 GB available space.
