Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gaelic Football Laochra asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX1660 Ti (6 GB)" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3070 (8GB)" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, about 54% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6700 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
