Hardware & Performance Analysis

Once Human asks for "Nvidia GTX 750ti 4G" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1060 6G" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 112%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 100 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (HD 7970 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
