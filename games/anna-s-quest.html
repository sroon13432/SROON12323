Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Anna's Quest ("Nvidia GeForce 205, AMD Radeon HD 3400 Series with 256 MB VRAM, latest OpenGL driver" and "Nvidia GeForce 405, AMD Radeon HD 5400 Series") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.37 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Anna's Quest?

Anna's Quest lists Nvidia GeForce 205, AMD Radeon HD 3400 Series with 256 MB VRAM, latest OpenGL driver and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce 405, AMD Radeon HD 5400 Series or higher with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Anna's Quest at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce 405, AMD Radeon HD 5400 Series or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Anna's Quest need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Anna's Quest need?

1400 MB available space.
