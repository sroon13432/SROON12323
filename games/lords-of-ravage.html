Hardware & Performance Analysis

Lords of Ravage asks for "NVidia GTX 1060" at minimum and "NVidia GTX 1070" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lords of Ravage?

Lords of Ravage lists NVidia GTX 1060 / 3 GB or Radeon RX 580 / 4GB and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GTX 1070 / 8 GB or RX Vega 56 / 8GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lords of Ravage at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GTX 1070 / 8 GB or RX Vega 56 / 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Lords of Ravage need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Lords of Ravage need?

5 GB available space.
