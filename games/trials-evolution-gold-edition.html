Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Trials Evolution: Gold Edition?

Trials Evolution: Gold Edition lists DirectX® 10 compliant, 512 Mb video memory and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX® 11 compliant, 1024 Mb video memory with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Trials Evolution: Gold Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX® 11 compliant, 1024 Mb video memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Trials Evolution: Gold Edition need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Trials Evolution: Gold Edition need?

4 GB HD space Sound: DirectX 9.0c compatible.
