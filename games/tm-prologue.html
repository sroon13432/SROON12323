Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run TM - Prologue?

TM - Prologue lists 256 MB DirectX® 9.0–compliant card with Shader Model 3.0 or higher (see supported list) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 256 MB DirectX® 9.0–compliant card with Shader Model 3.0 or higher (see supported list) with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for TM - Prologue at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 256 MB DirectX® 9.0–compliant card with Shader Model 3.0 or higher (see supported list). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
