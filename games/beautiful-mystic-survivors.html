Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Beautiful Mystic Survivors moves from "GTX 580" to "GTX 1050". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a jump of about 171%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
