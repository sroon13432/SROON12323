Audio Factory

★ Very Positive (81% of 77 reviews) 💾 1 GB available space

Audio Factory is a VR experience (Daydream port) showcasing spatial audio capabilities of the Resonance Audio SDK by Google. Explore each floor to experience how spatial audio contributes to the VR immersion. Requires: headphones (Bluetooth not supported) and Vive/Oculus Touch controllers.

🚀 Recommended GPU: Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Audio Factory names the same GPU, "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Audio Factory?

Audio Factory lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Audio Factory at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Audio Factory need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
