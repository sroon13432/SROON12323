Hardware & Performance Analysis

Gods and Kings asks for "Geforce 9800GT" at minimum and "Geforce GTX 1050" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Low-end, 13.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2.93 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2026 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (R9 270X bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
