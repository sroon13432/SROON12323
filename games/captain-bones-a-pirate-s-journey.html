Hardware & Performance Analysis

Captain Bones : A Pirate's Journey lists "Intel HD Graphics 4600, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 630, Radeon HD 5670" at minimum and "Intel HD Graphics 5200, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750, Radeon HD 7800" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
