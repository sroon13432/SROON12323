Hardware & Performance Analysis

Close To The Sun asks for "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, 116% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 470 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
