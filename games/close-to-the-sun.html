Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Close To The Sun?

Close To The Sun lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 760 or AMD Radeon™ R7 260x with 3GB Video RAM and 8 GB GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 / AMD RX 470 with 8 GB GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Close To The Sun at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 / AMD RX 470. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Close To The Sun need?

8 GB GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB GB at recommended settings.
