Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Rumu?

Rumu lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7800 or greater and 3 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 770 GT / Radeon HD 7970 Or Greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Rumu at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 770 GT / Radeon HD 7970 Or Greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Rumu need?

3 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Rumu need?

1200 MB available space.
