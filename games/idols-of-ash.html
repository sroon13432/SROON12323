Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Idols of Ash run from "Intel HD 5000 (Shader Model 3)" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
