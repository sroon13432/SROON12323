Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Palworld is heavily RAM-dependent; having 32 GB RAM prevents game crashes during long multiplayer base sessions.
  • 💡
    Lowering 'View Distance' from Epic to High gives an instant 12-15 FPS boost near large pal bases.
  • 💡
    Turn off 'Motion Blur' and set 'Ride Camera Distance' to maximum for improved spatial awareness and smoother visuals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Palworld?

Palworld lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 (2GB) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Palworld at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (Medium / High Settings).

How much RAM does Palworld need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Palworld need?

40 GB available space.
