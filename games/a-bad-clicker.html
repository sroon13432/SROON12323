Hardware & Performance Analysis

A Bad Clicker names the same GPU, "RTX 4070 TI", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is high-end territory (RTX 4070 Ti bracket), where the requirement says more about the game's ceiling than about the card you need to buy. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
