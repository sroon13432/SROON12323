Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War runs from "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 670" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 970" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 122% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 175 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War?

Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 670 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon™ HD 7950 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 970 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon™ R9 390 / AMD Radeon™ RX 580 with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 970 / NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon™ R9 390 / AMD Radeon™ RX 580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.
