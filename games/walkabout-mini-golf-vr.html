Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Walkabout Mini Golf VR?

Walkabout Mini Golf VR lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Walkabout Mini Golf VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Walkabout Mini Golf VR need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Walkabout Mini Golf VR need?

2 GB available space.
