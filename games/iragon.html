Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Iragon moves from "Nvidia Geforce GTX 1060" to "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 76% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes Publisher recommended requirements realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at Publisher minimum requirements.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Unreal Engine 4 is well understood by current drivers, so the published numbers are realistic rather than optimistic.
