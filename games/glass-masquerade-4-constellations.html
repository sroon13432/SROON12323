Hardware & Performance Analysis

Glass Masquerade 4: Constellations asks for "Intel HD4000, AMD HD 6550D (or higier), 256Mb video memory" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 650, AMD HD7850 (or higher), 1GB video memory" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 3.8 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 650, AMD HD7850 (or higher), 1GB video memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
