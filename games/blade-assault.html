Hardware & Performance Analysis

Blade Assault names the same GPU, "Nvidia GTX 460", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
