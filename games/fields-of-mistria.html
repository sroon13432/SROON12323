Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fields of Mistria?

Fields of Mistria lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460, 1GB or AMD Radeon R7 240, 1GB and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and RX 480, 8 GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fields of Mistria at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is RX 480, 8 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Fields of Mistria need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
