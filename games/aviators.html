Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Aviators moves from "6 GB VRAM, GTX 1070" to "8 GB VRAM, RTX 2060". Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

The recommended GPU is 8 GB VRAM, RTX 2060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
