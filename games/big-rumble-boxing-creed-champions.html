Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Big Rumble Boxing: Creed Champions runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 980 Ti" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 Ti bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
