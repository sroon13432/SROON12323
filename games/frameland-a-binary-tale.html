Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Frameland: A Binary Tale list "GPU with 512MB of VRAM": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
