Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Othello Let's Go moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 8600GT" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, 5.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Othello Let's Go?

Othello Let's Go lists NVIDIA GeForce 8600GT or equivalent and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Othello Let's Go at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Othello Let's Go need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
