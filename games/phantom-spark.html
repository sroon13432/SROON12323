Can my PC run Phantom Spark?

Phantom Spark lists Nvidia 970 / AMD R9 390X and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia 970 / AMD R9 390X or better with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Phantom Spark at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia 970 / AMD R9 390X or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

6 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
