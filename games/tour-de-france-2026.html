Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Tour de France 2026 moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960, 4 GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 SUPER, 8 GB". On our relative index that is Low-end to Upper mid-range, 4.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 32 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 5060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
