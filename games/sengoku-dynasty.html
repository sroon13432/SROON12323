Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sengoku Dynasty?

Sengoku Dynasty lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650, 4 GB or AMD Radeon RX 570, 4 GB or Intel Arc A750, 8 GB and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 TI, 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT, 16 GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sengoku Dynasty at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 TI, 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT, 16 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sengoku Dynasty need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

15 GB available space.
