Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Dual Universe ("NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (3 GB)" and "AMD RX 580 (8 GB)") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Dual Universe lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (3 GB) / AMD Radeon RX 580 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and AMD RX 580 (8 GB) / NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 (8 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dual Universe at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is AMD RX 580 (8 GB) / NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 (8 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
