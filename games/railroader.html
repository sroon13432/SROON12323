Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Railroader are built around one card, "Nvidia GTX 1660 6GB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
