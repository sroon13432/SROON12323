Hardware & Performance Analysis

Assassin’s Creed Shadows asks for "NVIDIA® GEFORCE GTX 1650 4GB" at minimum and "Nvidia® GeForce RTX™ 3060Ti 8GB/AMD Radeon™ RX 6700 XT 12GB/Intel® Arc™ B580 12GB (REBAR ON)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a jump of about 109%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. At 115 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
