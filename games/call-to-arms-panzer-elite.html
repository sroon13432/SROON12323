Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Call to Arms: Panzer Elite runs from "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 -or- AMD Radeon RX 5700" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060Ti -or- AMD Radeon RX 6700XT" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, something like 35% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6700 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
