Hardware & Performance Analysis

ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut asks for "Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB GDDR5" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 2070 8GB GDDR6" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 104%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut?

ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut lists Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB GDDR5 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 2070 8GB GDDR6 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 2070 8GB GDDR6. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
