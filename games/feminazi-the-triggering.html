Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for FEMINAZI: The Triggering list "DirectX 9 compatible, at least 256MB video memory": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
