Hardware & Performance Analysis

Overpass 2 asks for "GeForce GTX 1050 (2048 MB)" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1660 Ti (6144 MB)/ Intel Arc A750 (8192 MB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 169% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 17 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
