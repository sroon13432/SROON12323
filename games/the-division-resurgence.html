Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers The Division Resurgence moves from "Intel UHD 630 Integrated GPU" to "Nvidia GTX 1650 (4GB)". Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is about 71% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Division Resurgence?

The Division Resurgence lists Intel UHD 630 Integrated GPU / AMD Vega 11 Integrated GPU and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 1650 (4GB) / AMD RX 5500 (4GB) / Intel ARC A380 (6GB) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Division Resurgence at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 1650 (4GB) / AMD RX 5500 (4GB) / Intel ARC A380 (6GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
