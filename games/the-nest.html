Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Nest runs from "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 1070" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Nest?

The Nest lists NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 970 / AMD Radeon™ R9 290 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon™ RX 480 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Nest at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce® GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon™ RX 480. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Nest need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Nest need?

6 GB available space.
