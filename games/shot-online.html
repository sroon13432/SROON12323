Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Shot Online?

Shot Online lists Geforce FX 5700 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce FX 8800 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Shot Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce FX 8800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Shot Online need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Shot Online need?

2 GB available space.
