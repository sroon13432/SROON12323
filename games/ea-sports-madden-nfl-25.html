Hardware & Performance Analysis

EA SPORTS™ Madden NFL 25 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti 4GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 8GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, roughly 175% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 10 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 65 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
