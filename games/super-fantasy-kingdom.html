Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Super Fantasy Kingdom moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® 8600 GT (512 MB)" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® 9500 GT (512 MB)". On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, 11.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.
