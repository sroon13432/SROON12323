Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Dark Light: Survivor moves from "Geforce RTX 2070" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070". Both rows sit in the Upper mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Dark Light: Survivor lists Geforce RTX 2070 or better and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 or better with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
