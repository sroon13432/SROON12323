Can my PC run Warlocks vs Shadows?

Warlocks vs Shadows lists NVIDIA GeForce 9400M 256MB and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 9400M 256MB with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warlocks vs Shadows at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 9400M 256MB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warlocks vs Shadows need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.
