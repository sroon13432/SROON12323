Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Tetraminos moves from "Intel HD Graphics 4000" to "Nvidia Geforce GT 730". Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is something like 83% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 317 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

The recommended GPU is Nvidia Geforce GT 730 / AMD Radeon R7 250. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
