Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run BABYLON'S FALL?

BABYLON'S FALL lists NVIDIA® Geforce® GTX1650 / NVIDIA® Geforce® GTX1060 6GB / AMD Radeon™ RX580 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® Geforce® RTX 3060 12GB GDDR6 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for BABYLON'S FALL at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® Geforce® RTX 3060 12GB GDDR6. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does BABYLON'S FALL need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does BABYLON'S FALL need?

20 GB available space.
