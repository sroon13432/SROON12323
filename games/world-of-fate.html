Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers World of Fate moves from "nVIDIA GeForce GTX 260 256mb" to "nVIDIA GeForce GTX 660, 1gb". Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 3.5 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run World of Fate?

World of Fate lists nVIDIA GeForce GTX 260 256mb or ATI Radeon HD 5670 256mb or Intel HD Graphics 4600 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and nVIDIA GeForce GTX 660, 1gb or AMD Radeon HD 7870, 1gb with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for World of Fate at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is nVIDIA GeForce GTX 660, 1gb or AMD Radeon HD 7870, 1gb. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does World of Fate need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does World of Fate need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
