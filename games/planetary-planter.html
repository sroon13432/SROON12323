Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Planetary Planter list "GTX 1650": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
