Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers GRANADO ESPADA moves from "NVIDIA® GeForce® 6600 GT" to "NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800 GT". Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is a jump of 3.3 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® 8800 GT or ATI/AMD RadeonTM HD3870 512MB equivalent or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
