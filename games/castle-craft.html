Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Castle Craft runs from "GeForce GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 2070" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, roughly 104% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RX 7600 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Castle Craft?

Castle Craft lists GeForce GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 580 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 2070 / AMD Radeon RX 6650 XT with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Castle Craft at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 2070 / AMD Radeon RX 6650 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Castle Craft need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Castle Craft need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
