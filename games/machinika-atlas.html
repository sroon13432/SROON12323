Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Machinika: Atlas?

Machinika: Atlas lists Graphics card with at least 1 GB of dedicated VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GT 440 / AMD Radeon HD 5750 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Machinika: Atlas at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GT 440 / AMD Radeon HD 5750. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Machinika: Atlas need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Machinika: Atlas need?

4 GB available space.
