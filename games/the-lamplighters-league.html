Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Lamplighters League asks for "AMD® Radeon™ R9 380 (4GB)" at minimum and "Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 Ti (6GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Lamplighters League?

The Lamplighters League lists AMD® Radeon™ R9 380 (4GB) or Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 950 (2GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 Ti (6GB) or AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Lamplighters League at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1660 Ti (6GB) or AMD® Radeon™ RX 5600 XT (6GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
