Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Tales from Toyotoki: Arrival of the Witch (The witch of the Ihanashi) list "1280ｘ720": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
