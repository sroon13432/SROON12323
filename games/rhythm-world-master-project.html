Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Rhythm World - Master Project moves from "Intel HD3000" to "Intel HD4000". Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is a jump of about 100%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 512 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
