Hardware & Performance Analysis

Tiny Birdtopia asks for "Nvidia GeForce GTX 750" at minimum and "Nvidia GeForce GTX 950" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, about 50% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 950. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
