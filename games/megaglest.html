Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers MegaGlest moves from "Geforce GTX 250, Intel 5200, Radeon HD 4850" to "Geforce 650ti, AMD HD 7850". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 3.7 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 696 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
