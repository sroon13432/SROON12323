Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for My Singing Monsters Playground list "GeForce GTX 660": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
