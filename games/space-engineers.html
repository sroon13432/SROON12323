Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Space Engineers moves from "Geforce 750/Radeon R9 270X" to "Geforce 1070 GTX". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 125%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
