Hardware & Performance Analysis

Myth of Lumi asks for "Integrated Graphics" at minimum and "GTX 2060" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Mid-range, a jump of 7.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
