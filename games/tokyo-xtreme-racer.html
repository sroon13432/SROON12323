Hardware & Performance Analysis

Tokyo Xtreme Racer lists "NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 Ti(VRAM 4GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 3060(VRAM 8GB)" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tokyo Xtreme Racer?

Tokyo Xtreme Racer lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 Ti(VRAM 4GB) or AMD Radeon™ RX 580 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 3060(VRAM 8GB) or AMD Radeon™ RX 6700 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tokyo Xtreme Racer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 3060(VRAM 8GB) or AMD Radeon™ RX 6700. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
