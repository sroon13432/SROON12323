Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Lost Skies runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8 GB VRAM) AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (6 GB VRAM)" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lost Skies?

Lost Skies lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 590 and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8 GB VRAM) AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (6 GB VRAM) or RX Vega 56 (8 GB HBM2) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lost Skies at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8 GB VRAM) AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (6 GB VRAM) or RX Vega 56 (8 GB HBM2). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Lost Skies need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Lost Skies need?

8 GB available space.
