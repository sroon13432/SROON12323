Hardware & Performance Analysis

Galaxy Control: 3D Strategy lists "ATI Radeon HD 3400 Series, GeForce 8600 GS" at minimum and "AMD Radeon HD 6670, GeForce GT 445M" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2016 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
