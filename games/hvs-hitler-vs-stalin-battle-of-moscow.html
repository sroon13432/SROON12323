Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for HVS: Hitler VS Stalin - Battle Of Moscow runs from "Nvidia GTX 950" in the minimum column to "AMD R9 290, Nvidia GTX 1060" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 90%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
