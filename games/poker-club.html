Hardware & Performance Analysis

Poker Club asks for "AMD Radeon HD 7700" at minimum and "GeForce RTX 2060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Mid-range, 6.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
