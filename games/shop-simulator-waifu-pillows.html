Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Shop Simulator: Waifu Pillows moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 590" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080, AMD Radeon RX Vega 64". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RX Vega 64 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
