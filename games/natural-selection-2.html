Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Natural Selection 2?

Natural Selection 2 lists DirectX 11 compatible dedicated video card with 2 GB VRAM and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 compatible dedicated video card with 4GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Natural Selection 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 compatible dedicated video card with 4GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Natural Selection 2 need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Natural Selection 2 need?

13 GB HD space.
