Hardware & Performance Analysis

GOD EATER 3 asks for "GeForce GTX 760" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 970" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 121% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
