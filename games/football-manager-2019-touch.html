Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Football Manager 2019 Touch?

Football Manager 2019 Touch lists Intel GMA X4500, NVIDIA GeForce 9600M GT or AMD/ATI Mobility Radeon HD 3650 – 256MB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Intel GMA X4500, NVIDIA GeForce 9600M GT or AMD/ATI Mobility Radeon HD 3650 – 256MB VRAM with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Football Manager 2019 Touch at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel GMA X4500, NVIDIA GeForce 9600M GT or AMD/ATI Mobility Radeon HD 3650 – 256MB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Football Manager 2019 Touch need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Football Manager 2019 Touch need?

7 GB available space.
