Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for RUNGORE: Beginner Experience run from "Intel graphics HD 620" to "Nvidia GeForce GT430". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 123 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
