Hardware & Performance Analysis

I'm Truely a Good Guy! asks for "GTX 1080" at minimum and "RTX 3080" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, something like 74% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
