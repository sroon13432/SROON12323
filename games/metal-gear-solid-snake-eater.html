Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers METAL GEAR SOLID Δ: SNAKE EATER moves from "RTX 2060 Super (8GB)" to "RTX 3080". Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, something like 52% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. At 118 GB this is a download that wants a dedicated drive rather than whatever is left on the system disk. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3080 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
