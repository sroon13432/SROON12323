Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run New Warfare?

New Warfare lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 960 / GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon™ RX 470 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1080Ti / RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon™ RX 6600XT with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for New Warfare at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1080Ti / RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon™ RX 6600XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does New Warfare need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does New Warfare need?

5 GB available space.
