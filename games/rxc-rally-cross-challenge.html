Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for RXC - Rally Cross Challenge runs from "Radeon HD 7750" in the minimum column to "AMD R9 290" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Entry-level gaming, 7.7 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
