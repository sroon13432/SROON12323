★ Mixed (54% of 192 reviews) 💾 5 GB available space

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Four Kings One War list "Nvidia GTX 1060 6gb": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
