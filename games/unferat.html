Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Unferat?

Unferat lists NVidia GeForce GT 730 or equivalent and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GTX 750 Ti or equivalent with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Unferat at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GTX 750 Ti or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Unferat need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

350 MB available space.
