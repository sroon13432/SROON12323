Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Hell Let Loose: Vietnam runs from "Nvidia RTX 2060 Super" in the minimum column to "Nvidia Geforce RTX 3060 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3060 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
