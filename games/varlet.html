Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for VARLET runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1050" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, 108% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
