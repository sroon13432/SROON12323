Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Hero's Hour ("AMD Radeon HD 6250" and "Intel HD 4000 integrated, NVIDIA GT 650m graphics card") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Hero's Hour?

Hero's Hour lists AMD Radeon HD 6250 or better DirectX11 compatible graphics card and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and Intel HD 4000 integrated, NVIDIA GT 650m graphics card or better DirectX11 compatible graphics card with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Hero's Hour at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Intel HD 4000 integrated, NVIDIA GT 650m graphics card or better DirectX11 compatible graphics card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Hero's Hour need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Hero's Hour need?

100 MB available space.
