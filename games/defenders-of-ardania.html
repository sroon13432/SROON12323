Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Defenders of Ardania are built around one card, "1GB, ATI HD3870, Shader Model, 512 MB (does not support integrated graphics card)", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2012, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
