Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Planet Coaster 2 runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB VRAM)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 Super (8GB VRAM)" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, about 60% more relative performance. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
