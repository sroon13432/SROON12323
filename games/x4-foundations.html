Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for X4: Foundations ("NVIDIA GTX 1060" and "NVIDIA RTX 3060") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run X4: Foundations?

X4: Foundations lists NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD RX 5300 or equivalent (Vulkan 1.4 support required) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 3060 or AMD RX 6700 or equivalent (Vulkan 1.4 support required) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for X4: Foundations at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 3060 or AMD RX 6700 or equivalent (Vulkan 1.4 support required). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does X4: Foundations need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does X4: Foundations need?

35 GB available space.
