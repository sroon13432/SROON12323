Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Plenty: Skyhearth are built around one card, "NVIDIA GTX 970", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 481 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
