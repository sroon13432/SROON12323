Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Spellsword Cards: Demontide runs from "GeForce 600M" in the minimum column to "GeForce GT 730 (1Gb)" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is a rise of roughly 83% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1.07 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.
