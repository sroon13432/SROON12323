Hardware & Performance Analysis

Asterix & Obelix XXL: Romastered asks for "2 GB (Geforce GTX 760/ Radeon R7 370)" at minimum and "4 GB (Geforce GTX 970/ Radeon R9 390)" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
