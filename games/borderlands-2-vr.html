Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Borderlands 2 VR?

Borderlands 2 VR lists NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 470 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Borderlands 2 VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 480 equivalent or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Borderlands 2 VR need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Borderlands 2 VR need?

5 GB available space.
