Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for 8-Bit Hordes runs from "Nvidia Geforce GTS 250" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce 550 Ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card.
