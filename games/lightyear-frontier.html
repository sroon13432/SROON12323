Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Lightyear Frontier moves from "NVIDIA Geforce GTX 1050" to "AMD Radeon RX 6600". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.5 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
