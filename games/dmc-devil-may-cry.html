Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run DmC: Devil May Cry?

DmC: Devil May Cry lists NVIDIA(R) GeForce(R) 8800GTS or better, ATI Radeon(TM) HD 3850 or better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and ATI Radeon(TM) HD 6950 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for DmC: Devil May Cry at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is ATI Radeon(TM) HD 6950 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does DmC: Devil May Cry need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does DmC: Devil May Cry need?
