Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run LEGO® Brawls?

LEGO® Brawls lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB / AMD Radeon R7 370 2GB and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB / AMD Radeon R7 370 2GB with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for LEGO® Brawls at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB / AMD Radeon R7 370 2GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does LEGO® Brawls need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does LEGO® Brawls need?

5 GB available space.
