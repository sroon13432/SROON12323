My Dirty Little Lake

★ Very Positive (84% of 57 reviews) 💾 250 MB available space

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run My Dirty Little Lake?

My Dirty Little Lake lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 / AMD Radeon RX 570, 1 GB VRAM, OpenGL 3.3 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT, 2 GB VRAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for My Dirty Little Lake at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT, 2 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
