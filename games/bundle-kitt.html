Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Bundle Kitt are built around one card, "NVIDIA GeForce 330", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 0 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
