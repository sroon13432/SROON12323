Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes are built around one card, "NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 420", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2020 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes?

Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 420 or ATI™ Radeon™ HD 6850 or Intel® HD Graphics 4000 or better with 2 GB VRAM and 4 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 420 or ATI™ Radeon™ HD 6850 or Intel® HD Graphics 4000 or better with 2 GB VRAM with 4 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 420 or ATI™ Radeon™ HD 6850 or Intel® HD Graphics 4000 or better with 2 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes need?

4 MB of memory at minimum and 4 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes need?

2 GB available space.
