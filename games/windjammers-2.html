Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Windjammers 2?

Windjammers 2 lists NVIDIA GTX 960 / Radeon HD 5750 or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 960 / Radeon HD 5750 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Windjammers 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 960 / Radeon HD 5750 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Windjammers 2 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Windjammers 2 need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
