Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Birth of an Artificial Intelligence asks for "Intel® HD Graphics 620" at minimum and "Intel® Iris® Plus Graphics 650" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 4 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
