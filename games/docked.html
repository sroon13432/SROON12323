Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Docked moves from "4 GB VRAM, Nvidia GeForce GTX 1650" to "8 GB VRAM, Nvidia GeForce RTX 3070". On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a jump of about 134%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 22 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
