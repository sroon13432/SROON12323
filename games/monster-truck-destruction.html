Can my PC run Monster Truck Destruction?

Monster Truck Destruction lists GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (4096 MB) / Radeon RX 570 (8192 MB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 3080 Ti (12288 MB) / Radeon RX 6950 XT (16384 MB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Monster Truck Destruction at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 3080 Ti (12288 MB) / Radeon RX 6950 XT (16384 MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
