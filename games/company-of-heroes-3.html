Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Company of Heroes 3?

Company of Heroes 3 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950, AMD Radeon R9 370, or equivalent performance and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660, AMD Radeon RX 5600, or equivalent performance with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Company of Heroes 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660, AMD Radeon RX 5600, or equivalent performance. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Company of Heroes 3 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Company of Heroes 3 need?

40 GB available space.
