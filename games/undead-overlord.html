Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Undead Overlord?

Undead Overlord lists Nvidia GT400 series with 512MB RAM or better, ATI 4870HD with 512MB RAM or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GT400 series with 512MB RAM or better, ATI 4870HD with 512MB RAM or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Undead Overlord at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GT400 series with 512MB RAM or better, ATI 4870HD with 512MB RAM or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Undead Overlord need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
