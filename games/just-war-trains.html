Hardware & Performance Analysis

Just War Trains asks for "NVIDIA GTX 960" at minimum and "NVIDIA RTX 2060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, a jump of about 186%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Just War Trains?

Just War Trains lists NVIDIA GTX 960 / AMD Radeon RX 470 with 4 GB VRAM and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 with 6-8 GB VRAM with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Just War Trains at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 with 6-8 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Just War Trains need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Just War Trains need?

2 GB available space.
