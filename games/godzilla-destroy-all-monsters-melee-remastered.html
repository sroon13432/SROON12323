Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered?

Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered lists NVIDIA GTX 1650 (4 GB VRAM) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1070 / GTX 1080 (8 GB VRAM) with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1070 / GTX 1080 (8 GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered need?

8 GB available space.
