StarCraft: Remastered

💾 8 GB available space ⚙️ Classic StarCraft engine, remastered assets

StarCraft: Remastered is the 2017 rebuild of the original StarCraft, installed through Battle.net and not sold on Steam. Blizzard's specification is deliberately light: Windows 10 with a Pentium D or Athlon 64 X2, a GeForce 6800 or Radeon X1600 Pro with 256 MB and 2 GB of RAM at minimum, and Windows 10 64-bit with a Core 2 Duo E6600 or Athlon 64 X2 5000+, a GeForce 8800 GT or Radeon HD 4850 with 512 MB and 4 GB of RAM when recommended.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB) / AMD Radeon HD 4850 (512 MB)
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

StarCraft: Remastered asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 6800 (256 MB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB)" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2017 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    The remaster keeps the original simulation, so it stays playable on hardware far below modern minimums; the 2 GB RAM row is the real floor.
  • 💡
    Switch between classic and remastered graphics with the in-game hotkey if the higher-resolution assets strain an old graphics card.
  • 💡
    It is a 4:3-era game at heart: 16:9 support is built in, but the UI scale option is what makes it readable on a 1080p or larger monitor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run StarCraft: Remastered?

StarCraft: Remastered lists NVIDIA GeForce 6800 (256 MB) / ATI Radeon X1600 Pro (256 MB) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB) / AMD Radeon HD 4850 (512 MB) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for StarCraft: Remastered at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GT (512 MB) / AMD Radeon HD 4850 (512 MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.
