StacKit

★ Very Positive (90% of 52 reviews) 💾 768 MB available space

StacKit is a turn-based puzzle of pure strategy. Load your gun in a precise order before you fight—this is your 'Stack'. Once in combat, your firing sequence is locked. Use Fire to strip armor, then hit with Corrosion to weaken foes and boost all other damage.

Hardware & Performance Analysis

StacKit asks for "GeForce GTX 750" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.6 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 768 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
