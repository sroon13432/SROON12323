Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Robot King Part I: Rebooted and Ready run from "128 MB" to "512 MB". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 3 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
