Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Disney•Pixar Cars: Radiator Springs Adventures?

Disney•Pixar Cars: Radiator Springs Adventures lists GeForce2/ATI 7500 with at least 32MB of VRAM and 256 MB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce2/ATI 7500 with at least 32MB of VRAM with 256 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Disney•Pixar Cars: Radiator Springs Adventures at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce2/ATI 7500 with at least 32MB of VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Disney•Pixar Cars: Radiator Springs Adventures need?

256 MB of memory at minimum and 256 MB at recommended settings.
