Hardware & Performance Analysis

Marauder asks for "DirectX compatible 64 MB" at minimum and "DirectX compatible 128 MB" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Marauder?

Marauder lists DirectX compatible 64 MB and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX compatible 128 MB with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Marauder at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX compatible 128 MB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Marauder need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Marauder need?

3 GB available space.
