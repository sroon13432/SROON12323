Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Nauticrawl ("2GB - Radeon R7 260 2GB" and "4GB - Radeon R7 260 2GB") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Nauticrawl lists 2GB - Radeon R7 260 2GB / GTX 750 Ti 2GB and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 4GB - Radeon R7 260 2GB / GTX 750 Ti 2GB with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Nauticrawl at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 4GB - Radeon R7 260 2GB / GTX 750 Ti 2GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Nauticrawl need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
