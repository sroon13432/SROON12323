Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Age of Mythology: Retold?

Age of Mythology: Retold lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 645 or AMD Radeon™ Vega 8 or Intel® Iris Graphics 550 or better and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce® GTX 970 or AMD Radeon™ RX 570 or better with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Age of Mythology: Retold at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce® GTX 970 or AMD Radeon™ RX 570 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Age of Mythology: Retold need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Age of Mythology: Retold need?

25 GB available space.
