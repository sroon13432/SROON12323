Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lil' Guardsman?

Lil' Guardsman lists GeForce GTX 660 or Radeon RX 460 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1080 Ti or Radeon RX Vega 64 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lil' Guardsman at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1080 Ti or Radeon RX Vega 64. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Lil' Guardsman need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

4 GB available space.
