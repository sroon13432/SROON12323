Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Mushroom Musume are built around one card, "DirectX", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
