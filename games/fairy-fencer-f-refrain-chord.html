Hardware & Performance Analysis

Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord asks for "Dedicated GPU with 2GB of VRAM" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 970" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord?

Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord lists Dedicated GPU with 2GB of VRAM and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 970 or AMD RX 560 2GB equivalent with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 970 or AMD RX 560 2GB equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Fairy Fencer F: Refrain Chord need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
