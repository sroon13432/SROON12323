Dakar 18

★ Mixed (69% of 831 reviews) 💾 Size: see Steam store page

Based on the world famous annual rally raid organized by Amaury Sport Organisation (A.S.O.) in South America, DAKAR 18 is a realistic simulation of the biggest cross-country rally in the world and features a variety of vehicles, including motorcycles, cars, trucks, quads and SSVs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dakar 18?

Dakar 18 lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD HD 7870 (2GB VRAM with Shader Model 5.0 or better) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB) or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dakar 18 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB) or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dakar 18 need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dakar 18 need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
