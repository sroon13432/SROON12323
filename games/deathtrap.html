Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Deathtrap?

Deathtrap lists GeForce 8800, Radeon HD4000, Intel HD4000 (min. 512 MB VRAM) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce 560 or Radeon HD5800 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Deathtrap at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce 560 or Radeon HD5800. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Deathtrap need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
