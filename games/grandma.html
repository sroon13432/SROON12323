Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for GRANDMA runs from "Radeon RX 580" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GTX 1080" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 87% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1080 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
