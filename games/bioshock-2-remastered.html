Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for BioShock™ 2 Remastered runs from "2GB AMD Radeon HD 7770" in the minimum column to "2GB ATI Radeon HD 7970, 2GB NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest.
