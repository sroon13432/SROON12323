Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bullet Sorrow VR?

Bullet Sorrow VR lists Geforce gtx970 and 4096 MB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce gtx1070 with 8192 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Bullet Sorrow VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce gtx1070. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Bullet Sorrow VR need?

4096 MB of memory at minimum and 8192 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Bullet Sorrow VR need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
