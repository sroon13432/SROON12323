Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Russian Fishing 4 moves from "GTX 760" to "GTX 1060". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 163% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
