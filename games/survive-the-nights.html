Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Survive the Nights moves from "AMD Radeon RX 470, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050, Intel Arc A580" to "AMD Radeon RX 6600XT, NVIDIA Geforce RTX 3060, Intel Arc B580". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.4 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
