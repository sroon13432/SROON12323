Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Samson moves from "Nvidia® 1070 GTX 8 GB" to "Nvidia® GeForce RTX™ 3060Ti 8GB". On our relative index that is Very low-end to Upper mid-range, 9.0 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6700 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Samson?

Samson lists Nvidia® 1070 GTX 8 GB / AMD Radeon™ RX 5600 6 GB and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce RTX™ 3060Ti 8GB / AMD Radeon™ RX 6700 XT 8GB with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Samson at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce RTX™ 3060Ti 8GB / AMD Radeon™ RX 6700 XT 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Samson need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Samson need?

15 GB available space.
