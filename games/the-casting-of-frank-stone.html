Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Casting of Frank Stone™ asks for "Nvidia GTX 1080" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX 3070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Mid-range to Upper mid-range, a rise of roughly 48% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 70 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
