Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Worms W.M.D moves from "Intel 4400, GeForce GTX 280, AMD Radeon HD 7750" to "GeForce GTX 750, AMD R7 370". Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 84% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Worms W.M.D?

Worms W.M.D lists Intel 4400, GeForce GTX 280, AMD Radeon HD 7750 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 750, AMD R7 370 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Worms W.M.D at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 750, AMD R7 370. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Worms W.M.D need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Worms W.M.D need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
