Hardware & Performance Analysis

GodsArena Online asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GT 710" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, 4.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Can my PC run GodsArena Online?

GodsArena Online lists NVIDIA GeForce GT 710 / Intel HD Graphics 530 and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 560 with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for GodsArena Online at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 560. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
