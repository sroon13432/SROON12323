Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Hive?

The Hive lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 650 / AMD Radeon HD 7750 or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX 560 or better with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Hive at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX 560 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Hive need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Hive need?

4 GB available space.
