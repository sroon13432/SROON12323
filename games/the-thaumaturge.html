Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Thaumaturge?

The Thaumaturge lists Radeon RX580 (8GB) or Nvidia GTX 1050 Ti (4GB) or Intel Arc A750 8GB and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and Radeon 6700xt (12GB) or Nvidia RTX 3060 TI (8GB) or Intel Arc A770 16GB with 32 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Thaumaturge at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Radeon 6700xt (12GB) or Nvidia RTX 3060 TI (8GB) or Intel Arc A770 16GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Thaumaturge need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 32 GB at recommended settings.

25 GB available space.
