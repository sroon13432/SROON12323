Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Hell Clock runs from "nVidia GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "nVidia RTX 3060" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 78% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.
