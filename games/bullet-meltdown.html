Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bullet Meltdown?

Bullet Meltdown lists Obsługa OpenGL 3.3 / 1 GB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Obsługa Vulkan / 2 GB VRAM with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Bullet Meltdown at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Obsługa Vulkan / 2 GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Bullet Meltdown need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Bullet Meltdown need?

1 GB available space.
