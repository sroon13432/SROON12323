Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Age of Wonders 4 moves from "Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 670 (2GB)" to "Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 1070 Ti (8GB)". On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 4.4 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
