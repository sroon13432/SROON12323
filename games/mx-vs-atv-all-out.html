Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers MX vs ATV All Out moves from "Geforce GTX 460" to "Geforce GTX 770". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, a jump of 3.7 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 26 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 770 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
