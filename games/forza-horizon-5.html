Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Forza Horizon 5?

Forza Horizon 5 lists NVidia GTX 970, AMD RX 470, OR Intel Arc A380 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GTX 1070, AMD RX 590, OR Intel Arc A750 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Forza Horizon 5 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GTX 1070, AMD RX 590, OR Intel Arc A750. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Forza Horizon 5 need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
