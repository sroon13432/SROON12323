Can my PC run Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition?

Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition lists OpenGL 2.0 compatible and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and OpenGL 2.0 compatible with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is OpenGL 2.0 compatible. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
