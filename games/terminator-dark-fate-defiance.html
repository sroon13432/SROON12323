Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Terminator: Dark Fate - Defiance runs from "Geforce GTX 750 (2GB)" in the minimum column to "Geforce GTX 1060 (6Gb)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of 3.6 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
