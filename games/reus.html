Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Reus run from "DirectX10(R) compatible card with 512MB of memory" to "DirectX10(R) compatible card with 1024MB of memory". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2013, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Reus?

Reus lists DirectX10(R) compatible card with 512MB of memory and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX10(R) compatible card with 1024MB of memory with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Reus at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX10(R) compatible card with 1024MB of memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Reus need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Reus need?

500 MB HD space.
