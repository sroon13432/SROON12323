Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lionheart: Legacy of the Crusader?

Lionheart: Legacy of the Crusader lists 3D graphics card compatible with DirectX 7 and 256 MB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 9 with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lionheart: Legacy of the Crusader at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 9. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Lionheart: Legacy of the Crusader need?

256 MB of memory at minimum and 512 MB at recommended settings.
