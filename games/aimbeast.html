Hardware & Performance Analysis

Aimbeast asks for "NVIDIA GeForce 470 GTX" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce 770 GTX" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Aimbeast?

Aimbeast lists NVIDIA GeForce 470 GTX or AMD Radeon 6870 HD series card or higher and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 770 GTX or AMD Radeon HD 7970 series card or higher with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Aimbeast at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 770 GTX or AMD Radeon HD 7970 series card or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Aimbeast need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Aimbeast need?

500 MB available space.
