Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Raintime names the same GPU, "1024x768", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 1 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Raintime?

The Raintime lists 1024x768 or better video resolution in High Color mode and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1024x768 or better video resolution in High Color mode with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Raintime at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1024x768 or better video resolution in High Color mode. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
