Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Death Delivery ("GEFORCE GTX 1080" and "GEFORCE GTX 1660 SUPER") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
