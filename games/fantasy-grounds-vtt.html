Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fantasy Grounds VTT?

Fantasy Grounds VTT lists Graphics card with DX10 (Shader Model 4.0) capabilities and a minimum of 2 GB of graphics memory and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Graphics card with DX10 (Shader Model 4.0) capabilities and a minimum of 3 GB of graphics memory with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fantasy Grounds VTT at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Graphics card with DX10 (Shader Model 4.0) capabilities and a minimum of 3 GB of graphics memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
