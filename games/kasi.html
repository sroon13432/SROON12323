Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Kasi moves from "Integrated" to "GTX 700 Series". Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 102 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
