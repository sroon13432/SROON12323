The two requirement rows for SHINONOME : Rainy Night Manor run from "1GB VRAM" to "2GB VRAM". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.
