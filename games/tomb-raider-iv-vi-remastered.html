Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered?

Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered lists GeForce GT 620 1gb / Radeon HD 8670D and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDA RTX 2080 / Radeon RX 6750 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDA RTX 2080 / Radeon RX 6750. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
