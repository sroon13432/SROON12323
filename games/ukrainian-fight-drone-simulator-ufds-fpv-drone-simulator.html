Hardware & Performance Analysis

Ukrainian Fight Drone Simulator (UFDS) - FPV drone simulator asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB VRam)/ AMD Radeon RX 5600 (6GB VRam)" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX 4060 (8GB VRam)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Mid-range, 6.7 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 28 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
