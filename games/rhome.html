Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Rhome moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.4 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 3.22 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
