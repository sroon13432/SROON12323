Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cult of the Yellow King asks for "Intel HD 530, GeForce GTX 750," at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1050, Radeon RX 460," at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a jump of 3.2 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 760 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
