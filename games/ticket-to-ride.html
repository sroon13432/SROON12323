Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Ticket to Ride® list "DirectX 11 class GPU [1280 x 720]": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
