Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Keep the Lights On list "Integrated AMD Radeon Vega": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
