Hardware & Performance Analysis

TinShift asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 2GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, 105% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

What GPU do I need for TinShift at 60 FPS?
