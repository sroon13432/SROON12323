Hardware & Performance Analysis

Snufkin: Melody of Moominvalley asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 185% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen.
