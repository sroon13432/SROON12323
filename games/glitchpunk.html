Hardware & Performance Analysis

Glitchpunk asks for "GeForce GTX 960" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1070" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, something like 71% more headroom. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 16 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
