Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III run from "NVIDIA GeForce 460" to "NVIDIA GeForce 770". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 50 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III?

Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III lists NVIDIA GeForce 460 or AMD Radeon 6950 or equivalent DirectX 11-card and 4 GB, 1 GB of V MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce 770 or AMD Radeon 7970 or equivalent DirectX 11 card with 8 GB, 2 GB of V MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce 770 or AMD Radeon 7970 or equivalent DirectX 11 card. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III need?

4 GB, 1 GB of V MB of memory at minimum and 8 GB, 2 GB of V MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War III need?

50 GB available space.
