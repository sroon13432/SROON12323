Can my PC run Battlevoid: Sector Siege?

Battlevoid: Sector Siege lists Graphics card: DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and Graphics card: DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is Graphics card: DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
