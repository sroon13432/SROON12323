Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers SONG OF HORROR COMPLETE EDITION moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 660 GTX, Radeon HD 7950" to "NVIDIA GeForce 980 GTX, Radeon RX 580". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 580 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
