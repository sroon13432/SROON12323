Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Phantom Brigade ("NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB)," and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB),") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Phantom Brigade?

Phantom Brigade lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB), or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8 GB) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB), or AMD Radeon RX 6700 (10 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Phantom Brigade at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB), or AMD Radeon RX 6700 (10 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Phantom Brigade need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Phantom Brigade need?

10 GB available space.
