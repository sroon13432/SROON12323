Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Phantom Brigade?

Phantom Brigade lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB), or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8 GB) and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB), or AMD Radeon RX 6700 (10 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Phantom Brigade at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB), or AMD Radeon RX 6700 (10 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Phantom Brigade need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Phantom Brigade need?

10 GB available space.
