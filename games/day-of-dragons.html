Hardware & Performance Analysis

Day of Dragons asks for "4GB nVidia GTX 960" at minimum and "8GB nVidia RTX 3070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Upper mid-range, 4.4 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
