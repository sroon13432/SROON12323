Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Inside the Labs runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 2060" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.3 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 2060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Inside the Labs need?
