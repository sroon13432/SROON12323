Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sword of the Sea?

Sword of the Sea lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070, 8GB or AMD Radeon RX 5600, 6GB or Intel Arc B570 and 12 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Ti, 11GB or AMD Radeon VII, 16GB or Intel Arc B580 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sword of the Sea at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Ti, 11GB or AMD Radeon VII, 16GB or Intel Arc B580. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sword of the Sea need?

12 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Sword of the Sea need?

15 GB available space.
