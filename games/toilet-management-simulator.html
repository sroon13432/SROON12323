Hardware & Performance Analysis

Toilet Management Simulator asks for "NVIDIA GTX 670 2GB" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 980" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 167% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
