Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Homeworld Remastered Collection moves from "NVIDIA GeForce 8800 GTS (512MB)" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560". On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of about 191%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
