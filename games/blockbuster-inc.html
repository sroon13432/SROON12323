Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Blockbuster Inc. moves from "GTX 660" to "GTX 1060". Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Entry-level gaming, 3.6 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

The recommended GPU is GTX 1060. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
