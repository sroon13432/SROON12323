Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Child of Lothian ("Nvidia GeForce GTX 1070" and "Nvidia GTX1660 TI 6GB") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Treat the pair as one hardware floor: a machine that clears the first row is not changed much by the second.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
