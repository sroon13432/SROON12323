Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Re-Legion runs from "Geforce GTX 760 2GB VRAM" in the minimum column to "Geforce GTX 1050 Ti 2GB VRAM" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 68% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.
