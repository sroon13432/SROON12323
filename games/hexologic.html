Hardware & Performance Analysis

Hexologic asks for "512MB" at minimum and "2GB" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 297 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2018 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
