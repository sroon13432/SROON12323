Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Mini Motorways?

Mini Motorways lists Shader Model 4.0 and RAM as its minimum, and Shader Model 4.0 with RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Mini Motorways at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Shader Model 4.0. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Mini Motorways need?

The store page does not state a memory requirement. 16 GB covers every game in this database at recommended settings.
