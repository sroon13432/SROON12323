Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Delta Force: Xtreme are built around one card, "DirectX 9.0 AGP with 32MB", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 819 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2009, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Delta Force: Xtreme?

Delta Force: Xtreme lists DirectX 9.0 AGP with 32MB or better with HW-T&L and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 9.0 AGP with 32MB or better with HW-T&L with 512 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Delta Force: Xtreme at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 9.0 AGP with 32MB or better with HW-T&L. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Delta Force: Xtreme need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 512 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Delta Force: Xtreme need?

820 MB.
