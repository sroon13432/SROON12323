Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Damascus?

Damascus lists Graphics card: OpenGL 3+ and DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Graphics card: OpenGL 3+ and DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Damascus at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Graphics card: OpenGL 3+ and DX9 (shader model 3.0) or DX11 with feature level 9.3 capabilities. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
