Hardware & Performance Analysis

Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 121% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
