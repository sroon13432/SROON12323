Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run It's Quiz Time?

It's Quiz Time lists 1024MB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and 4GB VRAM with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for It's Quiz Time at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 4GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does It's Quiz Time need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does It's Quiz Time need?

10 GB available space.
