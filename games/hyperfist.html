Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run HYPERFIST?

HYPERFIST lists Nvidia GTX 1660 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia RTX 2070 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for HYPERFIST at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia RTX 2070. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does HYPERFIST need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
