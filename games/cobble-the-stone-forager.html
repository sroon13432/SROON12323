Hardware & Performance Analysis

Cobble: The Stone Forager asks for "Nvidia GTX 760" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 960" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Low-end band on our index, and the step between them is something like 47% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 184 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
