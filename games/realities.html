Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Realities?

Realities lists Geforce GTX 970 or similar and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Geforce GTX 980 Ti or similar with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Realities at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Geforce GTX 980 Ti or similar. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Realities need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Realities need?

12 GB available space.
