Fortnite

💾 Size: see official requirements ⚙️ Unreal Engine 5

Fortnite is Epic Games' free-to-play battle royale, downloaded from Epic or the Fortnite site and not sold on Steam. Epic's support article splits the PC requirements into three tiers: Windows 10 22H2 64-bit with a Core i3-3225, Intel HD 4000 or Radeon Vega 8 and 8 GB of RAM at minimum; a GTX 960 with 16 GB at the recommended tier; and an RTX 3070 with 8 GB of VRAM for the Epic quality preset.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD R9 280
Target: 60 FPS

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Epic lists the Performance rendering mode separately from the three tiers; on hardware near the minimum it is the setting that keeps frame rate steady.
  • 💡
    The recommended tier now asks for 16 GB of RAM and an NVMe SSD, so a machine with 8 GB is at the minimum row even if its GPU is strong.
  • 💡
    Epic's older guidance still applies to high settings: lowering the quality preset one step restores most of the frame rate lost by a weaker GPU.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Fortnite?

Fortnite lists Intel HD 4000 / AMD Radeon Vega 8 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD R9 280 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Fortnite at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD R9 280. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended.

How much RAM does Fortnite need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Fortnite need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
