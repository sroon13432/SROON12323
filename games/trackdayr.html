Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run TrackDayR?

TrackDayR lists Nvidia GTX 770, Radeon HD 7970 and 8192 MB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB with 16384 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for TrackDayR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1070 8 GB, Radeon RX 580 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does TrackDayR need?

8192 MB of memory at minimum and 16384 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does TrackDayR need?

70 GB available space.
