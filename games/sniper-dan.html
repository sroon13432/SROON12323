Sniper Dan

★ Very Positive (95% of 232 reviews) 💾 1 GB available space

Sniper Dan is a delightfully funny, family-friendly 3D Hidden Object game with a big twist. Use a sniper rifle to solve hundreds of low-stakes jobs across six packed locations, discover surprising secrets, snap hilarious photo ops, and unlock costumes, upgrades, office décor, collectables and more!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sniper Dan?

Sniper Dan lists GeForce GTX 750 (2048 MB) or Radeon HD 7870 (2048 MB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 970 (4096 MB) or Radeon R9 270X (2048 MB) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sniper Dan at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 970 (4096 MB) or Radeon R9 270X (2048 MB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sniper Dan need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
