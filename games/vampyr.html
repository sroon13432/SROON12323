Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Vampyr moves from "2 GB, GeForce GTX 1050 (Legacy)" to "4 GB, GeForce GeForce GTX 970". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, a rise of roughly 54% in our index. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (R9 390 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
