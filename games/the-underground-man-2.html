Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Underground Man 2 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GT 320" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 960" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Legacy and Very low-end, roughly 175% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Underground Man 2?

The Underground Man 2 lists NVIDIA GeForce GT 320 or AMD Radeon HD 6670 or Intel HD 4600 and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 960 or AMD Radeon R7 250 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Underground Man 2 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 960 or AMD Radeon R7 250. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Underground Man 2 need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
