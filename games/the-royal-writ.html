Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Royal Writ?

The Royal Writ lists Intel 12500 Integrated graphics / AMD Ryzen 5 5600G integrated graphics and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 1080 / AMD RX 6600 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Royal Writ at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 1080 / AMD RX 6600. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Royal Writ need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Royal Writ need?

2 GB available space.
