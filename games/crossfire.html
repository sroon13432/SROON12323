CrossFire

💾 15 GB available space ⚙️ LithTech Jupiter

CrossFire is Smilegate's free-to-play FPS, one of the most played shooters in Asia, distributed outside Korea through Z8Games and Tencent rather than Steam. Z8Games lists a Core 2 Duo T6400 or Athlon 64 X2 4600+, a GeForce 9500 GT, Radeon HD 6450 or Intel HD 3000 and 4 GB of RAM at minimum, and a Core i5-3470 or Ryzen 3 1200 with a GT 630 or HD 6570 and 8 GB of RAM when recommended, on Windows 7/8/10 64-bit and DirectX 9.0c.

🚀 Recommended GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GT 630 / AMD Radeon HD 6570 / Intel HD Graphics 6000
Target: 60 FPS

Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for CrossFire runs from "NVIDIA GeForce 9500 GT" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GT 630" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Legacy band on our index, and the step between them is roughly 150% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes Official recommended (Z8Games) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at Official minimum (Z8Games).

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run CrossFire?

CrossFire lists NVIDIA GeForce 9500 GT / AMD Radeon HD 6450 / Intel HD Graphics 3000 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GT 630 / AMD Radeon HD 6570 / Intel HD Graphics 6000 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for CrossFire at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GT 630 / AMD Radeon HD 6570 / Intel HD Graphics 6000. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended (Z8Games).

How much RAM does CrossFire need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

15 GB available space.
