Hardware & Performance Analysis

Sword and Fairy 5 prequel names the same GPU, "NVidia GT440 (512M)", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers settle on 2 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sword and Fairy 5 prequel?

Sword and Fairy 5 prequel lists NVidia GT440 (512M) or Better and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GT440 (512M) or Better with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sword and Fairy 5 prequel at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GT440 (512M) or Better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sword and Fairy 5 prequel need?
