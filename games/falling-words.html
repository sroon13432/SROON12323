Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Falling words run from "256 MB" to "512 MB". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Both tiers settle on 0.5 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 154 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
