Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for 100 hidden rams list "Intel HD 4000": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 20 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That tier is integrated-graphics territory by our index, so the interesting questions are storage and operating system support rather than the graphics card. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
