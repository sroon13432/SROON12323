Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Police Simulator: Patrol Officers runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 (2 GB VRAM)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6 GB VRAM)" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 163%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 12 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
