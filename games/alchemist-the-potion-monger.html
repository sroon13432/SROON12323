Hardware & Performance Analysis

Alchemist: The Potion Monger asks for "GTX 760 2 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 970" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Entry-level gaming, roughly 121% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
