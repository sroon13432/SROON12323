Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Bish Bash Bots runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 870, 1 GB" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 960, 4 GB" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Very low-end and Low-end, 155% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 960 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
