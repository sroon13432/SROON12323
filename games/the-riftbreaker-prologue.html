Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Riftbreaker: Prologue runs from "Nvidia GTX 750 2GB" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GTX 1060 6GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 3.1 times the index, the widest kind of tier gap in this catalogue. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 8 GB to 12 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 570 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
