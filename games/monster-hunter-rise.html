Hardware & Performance Analysis

MONSTER HUNTER RISE asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GT 1030 (DDR4)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (VRAM 3GB)" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, a jump of about 126%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 36 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

That lands in mainstream territory (RX 570 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
