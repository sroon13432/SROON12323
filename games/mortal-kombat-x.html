Hardware & Performance Analysis

Mortal Kombat X asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is roughly 133% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 3 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 44 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
