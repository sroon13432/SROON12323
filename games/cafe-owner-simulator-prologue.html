Hardware & Performance Analysis

The store page for Cafe Owner Simulator: Prologue skips the minimum GPU entirely and states "GeForce GTX 970" for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings); plan around that card.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 3Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
