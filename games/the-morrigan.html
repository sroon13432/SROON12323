Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers The Morrigan moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290" to "NVIDIA GTX 1080/AMD Radeon RX 480". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Morrigan?

The Morrigan lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1080/AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Morrigan at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1080/AMD Radeon RX 480 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Morrigan need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Morrigan need?

10 GB available space.
