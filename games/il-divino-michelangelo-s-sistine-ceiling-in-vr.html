Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for IL DIVINO: Michelangelo's Sistine Ceiling in VR runs from "GTX 970" in the minimum column to "RTX 2080ti" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, 3.2 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 64 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 2080 Ti bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.
