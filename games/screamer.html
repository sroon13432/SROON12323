Hardware & Performance Analysis

Screamer asks for "GeForce GTX 1060 (6144 MB)/Radeon RX 5500 XT (8192 MB)" at minimum and "GeForce RTX 4070 (12288 MB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, a jump of about 156%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 35 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 4070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Screamer?

Screamer lists GeForce GTX 1060 (6144 MB)/Radeon RX 5500 XT (8192 MB) or equivalent and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce RTX 4070 (12288 MB) / Radeon RX 9070 XT (16384 MB) or equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Screamer at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce RTX 4070 (12288 MB) / Radeon RX 9070 XT (16384 MB) or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
