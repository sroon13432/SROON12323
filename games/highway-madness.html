Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Highway Madness are built around one card, "AMD RX 480 (or GeForce GTX 1060 3GB)", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. Between 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) and 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) the difference is preset and frame rate, and the same GPU carries both.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
