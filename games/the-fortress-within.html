Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Fortress Within asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 Ti 或 AMD Radeon R7 260X" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB 或 AMD Radeon RX 580 8GB" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Entry-level gaming, 194% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 4 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.
