Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run The Last Clockwinder?

The Last Clockwinder lists NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 470 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 470 or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Last Clockwinder at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 470 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Last Clockwinder need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does The Last Clockwinder need?

2 GB available space.
