Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Zen Aquarium?

Zen Aquarium lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680 or AMD Radeon HD 7970 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 570 (4GB VRAM) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Zen Aquarium at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 570 (4GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Zen Aquarium need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
