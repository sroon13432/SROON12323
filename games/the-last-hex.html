Hardware & Performance Analysis

The Last Hex names the same GPU, "VRAM 512MB", in both requirement columns, so the recommended tier is a settings target rather than a hardware upgrade. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
