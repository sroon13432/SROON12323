Hardware & Performance Analysis

Zumbi Blocks 2 Open Alpha asks for "Nvidia GT 1030" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1050 Ti" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
