Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Knights of Pen and Paper 3?

Knights of Pen and Paper 3 lists 1GB of VRAM, DirectX 10+ support and 1 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1GB of VRAM, DirectX 10+ support with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Knights of Pen and Paper 3 at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1GB of VRAM, DirectX 10+ support. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Knights of Pen and Paper 3 need?

1 GB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
