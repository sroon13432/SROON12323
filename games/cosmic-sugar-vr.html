Cosmic Sugar VR

★ Very Positive (93% of 417 reviews) 💾 50 MB available space

Cosmic Sugar is an elegant, GPU driven simulation space. Vive wands become attractors or repulsors which allow you to craft nebulas. The controls are very simple, but it takes some time to explore all of the possibilities. Thanks to East Forest for the trailer music.

🚀 Recommended GPU: GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 or better
Target: 60 FPS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Cosmic Sugar VR?

Cosmic Sugar VR lists GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 or better with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Cosmic Sugar VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Cosmic Sugar VR need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
