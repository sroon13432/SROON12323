Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Dinosaur Hunt runs from "Geforce 9600 GS, Radeon HD4000, Shader Model 3.0, 512 MB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670+" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Low-end, 7.5 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 3 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 dates the target hardware to cards from the late 2000s.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 670 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.
