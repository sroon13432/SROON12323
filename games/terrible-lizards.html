Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Terrible Lizards?

Terrible Lizards lists NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1050 2GB / AMD Radeon™ RX 560 2GB and 6 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2080 / AMD Radeon™ RX 6800XT with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Terrible Lizards at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce® RTX 2080 / AMD Radeon™ RX 6800XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Terrible Lizards need?

6 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Terrible Lizards need?

25 GB available space.
