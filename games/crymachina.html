Can my PC run CRYMACHINA?

CRYMACHINA lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon HD 7870 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Ti / AMD Radeon Vega 56 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for CRYMACHINA at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Ti / AMD Radeon Vega 56. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does CRYMACHINA need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does CRYMACHINA need?

8 GB available space.
