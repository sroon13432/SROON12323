Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Out of the Park Baseball 24 are built around one card, "OpenGL 3.1", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

The recommended GPU is OpenGL 3.1 or newer, min. 512 MB GPU memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
