Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Stormhill Mystery: Family Shadows run from "256 MB VRAM" to "512 MB VRAM". One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 1 GB to 2 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1.46 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2019 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2019 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
