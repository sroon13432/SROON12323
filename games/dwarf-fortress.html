Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dwarf Fortress?

Dwarf Fortress lists 1GB of VRAM: Intel HD 3000 GPU / AMD HD 5450 / Nvidia 9400 GT and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and 1GB of VRAM: Intel HD 3000 GPU / AMD HD 5450 / Nvidia 9400 GT with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dwarf Fortress at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is 1GB of VRAM: Intel HD 3000 GPU / AMD HD 5450 / Nvidia 9400 GT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dwarf Fortress need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dwarf Fortress need?

500 MB available space.
