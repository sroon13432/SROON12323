Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Kingshunt runs from "Radeon RX 580" in the minimum column to "Radeon RX 5700" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a rise of roughly 78% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
