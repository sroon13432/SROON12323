Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Gargadusa's Tower are built around one card, "Integrated graphics (Intel HD 4000)", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 4 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

In our index that recommended card sits in integrated-graphics territory, which is the honest reason this title turns up on low-end lists: the GPU is almost never the obstacle. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
