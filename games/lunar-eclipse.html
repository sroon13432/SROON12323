Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Lunar Eclipse?

Lunar Eclipse lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB VRAM) and 4096 MB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB VRAM) with 4096 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Lunar Eclipse at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon RX 580 (8GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4096 MB of memory at minimum and 4096 MB at recommended settings.
