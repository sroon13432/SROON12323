Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Rally Mechanic Simulator: Light Edition moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760" to "NVidia GeForce GTX 1070 8GB". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, 3.9 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. The 8 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Rally Mechanic Simulator: Light Edition?

Rally Mechanic Simulator: Light Edition lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 or AMD Radeon R9 270X with 2GB VRAM or more and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVidia GeForce GTX 1070 8GB with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Rally Mechanic Simulator: Light Edition at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVidia GeForce GTX 1070 8GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
