UMAMI

★ Very Positive (98% of 161 reviews) 💾 Size: see Steam store page

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run UMAMI?

UMAMI lists Nvidia GT740M | AMD Radeon R8 | Intel HD 630 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 850M | AMD Radeon R8 M435DX | Intel UHD 620 with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for UMAMI at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 850M | AMD Radeon R8 M435DX | Intel UHD 620. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does UMAMI need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does UMAMI need?

The store page does not state a storage requirement; check the Steam store listing before installing.
