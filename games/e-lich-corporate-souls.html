E-Lich: Corporate Souls lists GTX 1080 / RX 5700XT and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and RTX 3060 / RX 6600 XT with 12 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is RTX 3060 / RX 6600 XT. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does E-Lich: Corporate Souls need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 12 GB at recommended settings.
