Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Master Bladesmith moves from "NVIDIA GTX 970" to "GTX 980". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 980 bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Master Bladesmith?

Master Bladesmith lists NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD R9 290 or greater and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and GTX 980 or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Master Bladesmith at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GTX 980 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Master Bladesmith need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Master Bladesmith need?

3 GB available space.
