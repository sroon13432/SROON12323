Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War - Definitive Edition list "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950, AMD Radeon R9 370,": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
