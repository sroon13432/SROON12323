Hardware & Performance Analysis

Sledgehammer / Gear Grinder asks for "DirectX compatible 128 MB" at minimum and "DirectX compatible 256 MB" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 0.5 GB to 1 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. Released in 2014, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Sledgehammer / Gear Grinder?

Sledgehammer / Gear Grinder lists DirectX compatible 128 MB and 512 MB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX compatible 256 MB with 1 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Sledgehammer / Gear Grinder at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX compatible 256 MB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Sledgehammer / Gear Grinder need?

512 MB of memory at minimum and 1 GB at recommended settings.
