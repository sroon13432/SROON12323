Hardware & Performance Analysis

Jurassic World Evolution 3 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB VRAM)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 Super (8GB VRAM)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, roughly 92% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 16 GB in both columns, so a 16 GB machine has nothing to fix before installing. The 25 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 5060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
