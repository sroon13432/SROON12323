Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Tavern Keeper 🍻 runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1060 (3 GB)" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 (6 GB)" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a jump of about 100%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 6 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 2060 bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
