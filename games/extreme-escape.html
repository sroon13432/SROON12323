Extreme Escape

★ Very Positive (81% of 91 reviews) 💾 2 GB available space

Extreme Escape is an escape-room puzzle game with an action component. It combines realistic graphics with challenges at high altitudes. The player must find his way out of a critical situation by facing difficult weather conditions, technical problems and the fear of heights.

Hardware & Performance Analysis

Extreme Escape asks for "NVIDIA GTX 1060" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 1080" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, a rise of roughly 78% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 2 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2020 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Can my PC run Extreme Escape?

Extreme Escape lists NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 580 or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 1080 / AMD RX 5700 or greater with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 1080 / AMD RX 5700 or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
