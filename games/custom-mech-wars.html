Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers CUSTOM MECH WARS moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 8GB". Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 78% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 7 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look.
