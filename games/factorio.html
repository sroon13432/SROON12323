Hardware & Performance Analysis

Factorio asks for "DirectX 11 capable GPU with 1GB VRAM - GeForce GTX 750 Ti, Radeon R7 360" at minimum and "DirectX 11 capable GPU with 4 GB VRAM - GeForce GTX 1050 Ti, Radeon RX 570, Intel Arc" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Legacy to Low-end, a jump of 10.7 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 Ti bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Factorio?

Factorio lists DirectX 11 capable GPU with 1GB VRAM - GeForce GTX 750 Ti, Radeon R7 360 or Intel UHD Graphics 730 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 11 capable GPU with 4 GB VRAM - GeForce GTX 1050 Ti, Radeon RX 570, Intel Arc with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Factorio at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 11 capable GPU with 4 GB VRAM - GeForce GTX 1050 Ti, Radeon RX 570, Intel Arc. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Factorio need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Factorio need?

5 GB available space.
