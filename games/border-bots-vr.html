Hardware & Performance Analysis

Border Bots VR asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, 4 GB" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6 GB" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 0.5 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Border Bots VR?

Border Bots VR lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, 4 GB or AMD Radeon RX 470, 4 GB and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 480, 8 GB with 8 MB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Border Bots VR at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 480, 8 GB. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Border Bots VR need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 MB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Border Bots VR need?

3 GB available space.
