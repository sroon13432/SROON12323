Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Toram Online runs from "GeForce GT 1030 (GDDR5)" in the minimum column to "GeForce GTX 1660" in the recommended one. Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of about 146%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
