Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Costume Quest ("256 MB GeForce 7600GS, Radeon X1600," and "256 MB GeForce 220, Radeon 4550, Intel HD 3000 Graphics") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Both tiers settle on 1 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Read at face value the requirement sits in the lowest band of our index, which makes it a formality rather than a demand on the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2011, it predates the modern driver stack; if it refuses to launch, compatibility mode and a community patch are the usual first stops.
