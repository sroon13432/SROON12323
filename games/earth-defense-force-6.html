Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers EARTH DEFENSE FORCE 6 moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060, AMD Radeon RX 580" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070, AMD Radeon RX VEGA 56". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 45 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it.
