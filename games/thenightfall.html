Hardware & Performance Analysis

TheNightfall asks for "Nvidia®" at minimum and "Nvidia®" at its recommended settings. At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 6 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2018 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run TheNightfall?

TheNightfall lists Nvidia® / AMD® with 2048 MB memory and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® / AMD® with 4096 MB memory with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for TheNightfall at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® / AMD® with 4096 MB memory. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).
