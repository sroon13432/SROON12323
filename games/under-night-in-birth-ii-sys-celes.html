Hardware & Performance Analysis

Both rows of the requirement table for UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH II Sys:Celes list "Nvidia GeForce GTX 680": there is no faster card to buy for the recommended tier. The whole gap is spent on 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) versus 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), and the card it asks for does not change.

Both tiers settle on 4 GB of memory, which means memory is not the constraint here at all. The 20 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2024 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 950 bracket), where 1080p is comfortable as long as the preset stays modest. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
