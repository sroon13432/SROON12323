Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for PUMP IT UP RISE runs from "Nvidia® GTX 750" in the minimum column to "Nvidia® GTX 1050" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Very low-end to Low-end, a rise of roughly 86% in our index. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is low-end discrete-card territory (GTX 1050 bracket): 1080p works at low-to-medium settings, and a card above it simply gives you room to raise the preset. It is Steam Deck Verified, which means text size and controls were checked on the handheld rather than assumed.
