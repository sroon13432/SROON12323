VALORANT

💾 Size: see official requirements ⚙️ Unreal Engine 4

VALORANT is Riot's free-to-play 5v5 tactical shooter, installed through the Riot client rather than Steam. Riot splits its requirements into three official tiers: 30 FPS minimum (Core i3-540, Intel HD 4000, 4 GB of RAM), 60 FPS recommended (Core i3-4150, GeForce GT 730) and a 144 FPS high-end build (Core i5-9400F, GTX 1050 Ti). The game needs a CPU with SSE 4.2 or AVX, DirectX 11 and, on Windows 11, TPM 2.0 with UEFI Secure Boot because of Vanguard.

Best Graphic Settings & FPS Boost Tips

  • 💡
    Riot publishes three tiers, so pick the one that matches your monitor: the 60 FPS build targets a GeForce GT 730, and the 144 FPS build assumes a GTX 1050 Ti or Arc A310.
  • 💡
    Vanguard blocks cloud gaming and virtual machines outright, so a VM or a streaming box cannot be tuned into compatibility.
  • 💡
    On Windows 11 the game will not start without TPM 2.0 and UEFI Secure Boot enabled in firmware, which is a BIOS setting rather than an in-game option.
  • 💡
    The 30 FPS tier lists integrated Intel HD 4000; the recommended tier already assumes a discrete card, so an integrated-only laptop should expect the low tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run VALORANT?

VALORANT lists Intel HD 4000 / AMD Radeon R5 220 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GT 730 / AMD Radeon R7 240 with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for VALORANT at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GT 730 / AMD Radeon R7 240. That is the configuration the publisher targets for Official recommended (60 FPS).

How much RAM does VALORANT need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does VALORANT need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
