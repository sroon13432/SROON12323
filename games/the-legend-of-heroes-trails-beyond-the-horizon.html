The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
