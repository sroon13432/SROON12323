Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Tales of Seikyu ("Nvidia GeForce GTX 1080 [4 GB] \ AMD Radeon R9 380X [4 GB] \ Intel Arc A580 [8 GB]" and "Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 [12GB] \ AMD Radeon RX 6800 [16GB] \ Intel Arc A580 [8GB]") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 30 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2026 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 3060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
