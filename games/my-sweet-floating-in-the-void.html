Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for My Sweet Floating in the Void stops at "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1050 Ti" for Publisher minimum requirements, leaving no second tier to check an upgrade against.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. At 645 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Unity carries more of its cost on the CPU, which is why the processor row is worth reading beside the card. Released in 2024, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
