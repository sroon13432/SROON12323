Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Play Things runs from "NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Mid-range band on our index, and the step between them is something like 38% more headroom. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 502 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (RTX 4060 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Play Things?

Play Things lists NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super / AMD RX 5600 XT (6GB VRAM) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti / RTX 4060 (8GB+ VRAM) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Play Things at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti / RTX 4060 (8GB+ VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Play Things need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
