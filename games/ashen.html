Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Ashen runs from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 760" in the minimum column to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, 121% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Ashen?

Ashen lists Nvidia GeForce GTX 760 or AMD R7 270X (2GB VRAM) and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 or AMD R9 290X (4GB VRAM) with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Ashen at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 or AMD R9 290X (4GB VRAM). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Ashen need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Ashen need?

15 GB available space.
