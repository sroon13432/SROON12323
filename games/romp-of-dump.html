Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Romp of Dump?

Romp of Dump lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 or AMD Radeon RX 460 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 570 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Romp of Dump at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 570. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Romp of Dump need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
