Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Kingdom Ascension?

Kingdom Ascension lists Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 970 (4 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 480 (8 GB) and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3080 (10 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 6800 XT (16 GB) with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Kingdom Ascension at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia® GeForce™ RTX 3080 (10 GB) | AMD® Radeon™ RX 6800 XT (16 GB). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Kingdom Ascension need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Kingdom Ascension need?

1 GB available space.
