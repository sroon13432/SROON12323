Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for AMazing TD run from "DX10 (Shader Model 4.0), 512 MB VRAM" to "Nvidia GTX 1060". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 6 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2021 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run AMazing TD?

AMazing TD lists DX10 (Shader Model 4.0), 512 MB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GTX 1060 or equivalent, 3GB VRAM with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for AMazing TD at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GTX 1060 or equivalent, 3GB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does AMazing TD need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does AMazing TD need?

5 GB available space.
