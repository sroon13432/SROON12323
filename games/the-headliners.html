Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for The Headliners runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 30 Series" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Upper mid-range, 140% more index, which separates a card that copes from a card that does not. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6700 XT bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
