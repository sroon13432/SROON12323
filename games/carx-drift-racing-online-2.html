Hardware & Performance Analysis

CarX Drift Racing Online 2 lists "Nvidia GTX 1060" at minimum and "Nvidia RTX 3060" as recommended, and on our index both rows land in the same class, so the recommended column is about settings rather than a faster card. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 14 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
