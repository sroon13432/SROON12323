Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Hellsplit: Arena moves from "Nvidia GeForce GTX 970" to "Nvidia GeForce GTX 1070 8GB". Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a modest step, the same class of card with more room to spare. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 16 GB at minimum against 32 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 23.14 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
