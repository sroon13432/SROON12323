Hardware & Performance Analysis

Pistol Whip asks for "Geforce GTX 970" at minimum and "GeForce GTX 1070" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, about 76% more relative performance. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 799 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.
