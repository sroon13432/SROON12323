Hardware & Performance Analysis

Desert Blossom asks for "Hardware Accelerated Graphics with dedicated memory, 1GB memory recommended" at minimum and "Hardware Accelerated Graphics with dedicated memory, 4GB memory recommended" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 9 is unusual for a 2023 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
