Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run A Chair in a Room : Greenwater?

A Chair in a Room : Greenwater lists NVIDIA GTX 970 / AMD R9 290 equivalent or greater and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GTX 970 / AMD R9 290 equivalent or greater with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for A Chair in a Room : Greenwater at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GTX 970 / AMD R9 290 equivalent or greater. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does A Chair in a Room : Greenwater need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

3 GB available space.
