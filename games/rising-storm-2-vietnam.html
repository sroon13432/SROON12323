Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Rising Storm 2: Vietnam?

Rising Storm 2: Vietnam lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 or ATI Radeon HD 5850 and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 or AMD R9 270X or better with 6 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Rising Storm 2: Vietnam at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 or AMD R9 270X or better. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

4 GB of memory at minimum and 6 GB at recommended settings.
