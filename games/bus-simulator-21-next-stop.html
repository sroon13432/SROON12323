Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Bus Simulator 21 Next Stop moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 (2GB VRAM)" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB VRAM)". On our relative index that is Low-end to Entry-level gaming, roughly 184% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 18 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1650 Super bracket), where 1080p on medium to high is the realistic target and the card you already own is usually the question rather than the upgrade.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Bus Simulator 21 Next Stop?

Bus Simulator 21 Next Stop lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 (2GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon R9 280 (2GB VRAM) or higher and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon RX Vega 56 (8GB VRAM) or higher with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Bus Simulator 21 Next Stop at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8GB VRAM) / AMD Radeon RX Vega 56 (8GB VRAM) or higher. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
