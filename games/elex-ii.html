Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for ELEX II runs from "Radeon RX 5600 XT" in the minimum column to "Radeon RX 5700 XT" in the recommended one. Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 12 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. A 90 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.
