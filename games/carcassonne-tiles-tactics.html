Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics

★ Very Positive (80% of 1,099 reviews) 💾 2 GB available space

The official adaptation of the famous board game Carcassonne! A modern classic tile-placement game based on the award winning game in which the players draw and place a tile with a piece of southern French landscape on it. The player can then decide to place one of his followers, called Meeples.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics?

Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics lists DirectX 10 class GPU with 1024MB VRAM and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and DirectX 10 class GPU with 1024MB VRAM with 2 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is DirectX 10 class GPU with 1024MB VRAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 2 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Carcassonne - Tiles & Tactics need?

2 GB available space.
