Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Circle Empires runs from "Intel/AMD Integrated Graphics, 1GB VRAM" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce GT 1030 2GB" in the recommended one. Both rows sit in the Very low-end band on our index, and the step between them is a narrow gap, closer to a settings note than an upgrade order.
