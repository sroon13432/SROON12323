Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run PC Building Empire?

PC Building Empire lists NVIDIA GeForce RTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 570 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for PC Building Empire at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 6600. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does PC Building Empire need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does PC Building Empire need?

1 GB available space.
