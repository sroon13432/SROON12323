Hardware & Performance Analysis

Directive 8020 asks for "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 Ti" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Mid-range and Upper mid-range, about 62% more relative performance. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. A 40 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it.

That is upper mid-range (RX 6800 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card. Valve grades it Steam Deck Verified, so the interface, text and controls are all confirmed for the 1280x800 handheld screen. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
