Hardware & Performance Analysis

The minimum and recommended rows for Race Day Rampage: Streamer Edition are built around one card, "DX10, DX11,", which means the publisher scales settings rather than hardware. What separates 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings) from 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) is resolution and preset, not silicon.

Both tiers ask for 8 GB of memory, the common baseline rather than a reason to upgrade on its own. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2022 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
