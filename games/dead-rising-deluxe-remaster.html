Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070Ti" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Mid-range, something like 78% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. No storage figure is published, so check the store listing before dedicating a drive to it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames. On Steam Deck it is rated Playable rather than Verified: it runs, but small text or the default control layout may need adjusting.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster?

Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB) / AMD Radeon RX 580 and 16 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070Ti / AMD Radeon RX 5700 with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070Ti / AMD Radeon RX 5700. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster need?

16 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster need?

Neither requirement tier states a storage figure; check the game's official system requirements page before installing.
