Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Castle Woodwarf 2 runs from "Intel HD Graphics 3000" in the minimum column to "Geforce 400 series, Radeon HD 5000 series, 512 MB discrete RAM" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Legacy to Very low-end, a jump of 11.7 times in our index, which puts the two rows in different hardware eras. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. At 205 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
