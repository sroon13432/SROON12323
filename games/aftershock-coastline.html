Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Aftershock: Coastline?

Aftershock: Coastline lists NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 with Max-Q Design and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 with Max-Q Design with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Aftershock: Coastline at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 with Max-Q Design. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Aftershock: Coastline need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Aftershock: Coastline need?

1400 MB available space.
