Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Stars in Shadow run from "ATI Radeon HD 4000 Series" to "NVIDIA Geforce 400 Series". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 4 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2017 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Stars in Shadow?

Stars in Shadow lists ATI Radeon HD 4000 Series or better and 4 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA Geforce 400 Series or better, 1 GB RAM with 8 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Stars in Shadow at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA Geforce 400 Series or better, 1 GB RAM. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Stars in Shadow need?

4 GB of memory at minimum and 8 GB at recommended settings.
