Hardware & Performance Analysis

纵横麻将 Matrix Mahjong asks for "DirectX 11 compatible video card with at least 1GB of VRAM" at minimum and "DirectX 11 compatible video card with at least 4GB of VRAM" at its recommended settings. One of those rows names a category rather than a specific card, so it cannot be placed on a card index with confidence; treat the store's wording as the requirement and the rows above as the comparison.

Memory does not move between the tiers and stays at 2 GB, small even by laptop standards, so RAM is not where this title will strain a machine. At 1 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unsupported, usually because of anti-cheat, a launcher, or controls that do not map to the handheld. Released in 2026, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
