Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Aztecs: The Last Sun runs from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780, 6 GB" in the minimum column to "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 SUPER, 8 GB" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Low-end to Mid-range, 3.1 times the index at the top of the ladder, wide enough that the two rows describe different machines. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket): the recommended preset should hold, and a step below it starts trading settings for frames.
