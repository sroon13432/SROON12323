Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Valor of Man?

Valor of Man lists GeForce GTX 950, Radeon R7 360 and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and GeForce GTX 1070, Radeon RX Vega 64, or above with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Valor of Man at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is GeForce GTX 1070, Radeon RX Vega 64, or above. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Valor of Man need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.

How much storage does Valor of Man need?

4 GB available space.
