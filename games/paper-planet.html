Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Paper Planet?

Paper Planet lists NVIDIA® GeForce GTX 660 or newer (Integrated Video Cards not recommended) and 2 GB RAM as its minimum, and NVIDIA® GeForce GTX 660 or newer (Integrated Video Cards not recommended) with 4 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Paper Planet at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is NVIDIA® GeForce GTX 660 or newer (Integrated Video Cards not recommended). That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Paper Planet need?

2 GB of memory at minimum and 4 GB at recommended settings.
