Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for He is Coming ("NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 460 (1 GB)" and "NVIDIA® GeForce® GT 630 (2 GB)") come out in the same class on our index, which makes the recommended column a settings target. Read them as a single floor: the upgrade worth making here sits above both rows, not between them.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 399 MB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

The figure is in the lowest band of our index, so any working card of that generation meets it and the row is closer to a formality than a target.
