Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Find or be Found run from "Nvidia GeForce 1060, 4GB V-ram" to "Nvidia GeForce 2060, 8GB V-ram". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 12 is listed, which quietly excludes the pre-2015 cards that never implemented the API.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my PC run Find or be Found?

Find or be Found lists Nvidia GeForce 1060, 4GB V-ram or equivalent and 8 GB RAM as its minimum, and Nvidia GeForce 2060, 8GB V-ram or equivalent with 16 GB RAM as its recommended specification.

What GPU do I need for Find or be Found at 60 FPS?

The recommended GPU is Nvidia GeForce 2060, 8GB V-ram or equivalent. That is the configuration the publisher targets for 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings).

How much RAM does Find or be Found need?

8 GB of memory at minimum and 16 GB at recommended settings.
