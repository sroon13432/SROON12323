Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Deceit 2 moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 4GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce 3060". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, a jump of about 186%, enough to move the game from marginal to comfortable. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

The publisher states no memory figure, so plan on 8 GB as a safe floor and 16 GB if you play with other applications open. The 15 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1070 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2023 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
