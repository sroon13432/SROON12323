Hardware & Performance Analysis

Best Forklift Operator asks for "Nvidia GTX 970" at minimum and "Nvidia GTX 1060" at its recommended settings. Both rows sit in the Entry-level gaming band on our index, and the step between them is a step small enough that the recommended card mostly buys headroom rather than a different class of card. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That lands in mainstream territory (GTX 1060 6Gb bracket): 1080p at medium-to-high is what the figure aims at, and most two- or three-year-old gaming laptops sit close to it. Steam Deck marks it Unsupported, the grade Steam gives when anti-cheat or a launcher stands between the game and the handheld. A 2022 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
