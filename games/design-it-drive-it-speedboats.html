Hardware & Performance Analysis

The two requirement rows for Design it, Drive it : Speedboats run from "Shader Model 3.0 graphics card (AMD Radeon HD 5870, nVidia GTX 560)" to "AMD Radeon HD 7970, R9 280X, nVidia GTX 680". At least one of those rows describes a class of card, a VRAM figure or a DirectX level rather than a model, so our tier index cannot split them cleanly and the store's own wording is the precise requirement.

Memory climbs from 2 GB to 4 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 3 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2016 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
