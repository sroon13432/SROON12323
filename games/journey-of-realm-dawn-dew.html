Hardware & Performance Analysis

The requirement table for Journey of Realm：Dawn Dew runs from "GTX970" in the minimum column to "GTX1070" in the recommended one. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, something like 67% more headroom. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory climbs from 6 GB to 8 GB, a step a machine with 8 GB covers without leaning on the page file. At 5 GB the download is trivial, so storage planning does not enter the decision.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Ti bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. Released in 2025, it was built against current driver branches, so a supported card with an up-to-date driver is enough.
