Hardware & Performance Analysis

Dune: Awakening asks for "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6 GB)" at minimum and "NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (8 GB)" at its recommended settings. Our index places the two rows at Entry-level gaming and Upper mid-range, roughly 148% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. Meeting the minimum gets you 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings), meeting the recommended row gets you 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings), and the distance between the two rows is that difference.

Both rows ask for 16 GB of memory, the current baseline for a 1080p gaming PC rather than an unusual demand. A 75 GB install is a routine footprint, but check free space before the download rather than after it. The DirectX 12 row also sets a hardware floor: cards from before roughly 2015 do not implement it.

That is upper mid-range (RTX 3070 bracket), so the target settings are realistic but the upgrade conversation has to include the CPU beside the card.
