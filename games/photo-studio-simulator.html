Hardware & Performance Analysis

Between its two tiers Photo Studio Simulator moves from "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti, 4 GB 或 AMD Radeon R9 380X, 4 GB" to "NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super, 6 GB". Our index places the two rows at Low-end and Mid-range, roughly 127% more relative performance, which is a genuine change of class. The minimum is a launch condition at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings); the recommended row is what makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic.

Memory is the second place the tiers separate: 8 GB at minimum against 16 GB recommended, and the higher figure is the one that matters once a browser shares the machine. The 10 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 10 is unusual for a 2025 release and normally means a 2D or deliberately retro renderer.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for.

Every figure above comes from this title's published requirements. How the tiers and estimates are produced is explained in our editorial methodology.
