Hardware & Performance Analysis

SYNTHETIK 2 asks for "NVIDIA GTX 970" at minimum and "NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super, newer is better" at its recommended settings. On our relative index that is Entry-level gaming to Mid-range, a rise of roughly 62% in our index. Read the recommended row as the configuration that makes 1080p @ 60 FPS (High Settings) realistic, and the minimum as the one that only starts the game at 1080p @ 30 FPS (Low Settings).

Memory holds at 8 GB in both columns, which an 8 GB machine meets without swapping. The 9 GB install fits on any drive made this decade without a second thought. DirectX 11 keeps anything from the GTX 400 era onward in scope.

That is mid-range by our index (GTX 1660 Super bracket), the band the publisher's 1080p target is written for. Steam Deck lists it as Playable, so the game runs there while some text and inputs need a closer look. A 2021 release also means current drivers already carry a profile for it.
